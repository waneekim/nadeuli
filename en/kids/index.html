    Our Chuseok-holiday visit to Jeonju Dreamland, the small amusement park inside Jeonju Zoo: smaller, less scary rides that lower-elementary kids loved, no separate admission and no day pass — just cheap ride bundles — ticket prices, opening hours that end before the zoo's, and a heads-up for thrill seekers.

    Our family's Chuseok holiday visit to Jeonju Zoo with the kids: enclosures packed with animals, a full loop past elephants, snakes, bears, birds and monkeys, park-like paths with restrooms everywhere, meerkat and parrot GIFs, admission (adults 3,000 won) and hours from Jeonju City's official guide, and Dreamland inside the zoo for using up the kids' last bit of energy.
