Texas Roadhouse Columbia, SC Review: First Dinner Treated by a Colleague Posted There, Huge Portions and Amazing Steak, Ribs and Mashed Potatoes (US Business Trip Day 1)

Day 1 of our US business trip,
and this is the dinner story 🥩

In Columbia, South Carolina,
a colleague posted there by our company
said dinner was on them
and took us here.

It was Texas Roadhouse!
The portions were huge.

Texas Roadhouse Columbia — Texas flag on the roof

📍 Texas Roadhouse
(Columbia, South Carolina, USA
400 Columbiana Dr)
🥩 Steak · ribs ·
mashed potatoes
🍞 Bread and butter · peanuts
👥 Many staff, pleasant service
💵 Reasonable price for how much we ate

🤠 A Steakhouse Built of Wood

TEXAS ROADHOUSE neon sign above the entrance

Both the building and the inner walls
were made of wood,
and a moose head decoration
hung on the wall.

Moose head decoration on the inner wall

Near the entrance
there was a meat display case.

The handwritten sign said
”… and get a FREE
Cactus Blossom or Fried Pickles.”

Meat display case near the entrance — handwritten sign

👥 Many Staff, Pleasant Service

There were really a lot of staff.
Thanks to that, the service was
pleasant.

Inside the restaurant — staff bustling around

Once we sat down,
bread and butter came out first,
and there was also
a bag of peanuts on the table.

The bread and butter that came out first

A bag of peanuts on the table (salt-roasted)

🥩 Tender and Chewy Steak

The steak was
tender and chewy.

I was still adjusting to the time difference,
so it was hard to eat much,
but the ribs, steak and mashed potatoes
were amazing.

Steak and shrimp skewer, with mashed potatoes topped with bacon

A potato piled high with cheese and bacon

🔑 KICKThe ribs, steak and
mashed potatoes are a must!
But the portions are huge

🥤 The Dragon Fruit Coke Was a Letdown

For a drink,
I had the dragon fruit Coke.

But
it tasted worse than Welch’s.
I should have just ordered
a regular Coke.

Dragon fruit Coke — our companions behind it (faces blurred)

💵 US Prices Were More Reasonable Than Expected

For how much we ate,
the bill didn’t come to much,
which was nice.

US prices were more reasonable than I expected.

The burgers on the menu were
$12.49–$13.99.
(menu at the time of our visit)

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone in Columbia
who wants a big steak
✔ Anyone going with a group
and sharing
✔ A business trip where you’re
curious about an American steakhouse

Thanks to our colleague posted there,
we enjoyed our first dinner
in the US!
