Where to Eat at Magok One Grove (Near Gimpo Airport) — 4 Restaurants We Tried, Picked by Who You’re With

Our guide to eating at One Grove, Magok 🗺

From November 2025 to April 2026,
we tried
four places at One Grove.

Eulji Kkankkan's pho — thin slices of meat with bean sprouts and greens; croquettes in the back

📍 165 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu
(One Grove 원그로브, Magok)
🚇 Magok Station (Line 5), exit 1
about 3 min on foot
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
🍽 The four places in this post
Eulji Kkankkan · Matsunohana
Hoho Sikdang · Bonghyeja's Kitchen
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
(the same for all four)
🅿 One Grove underground parking
first 30 min free

Some days we went as a family,
and some days
it was just my wife and me.

One Grove lunch map,

We’ve also written

🍜 Eulji Kkankkan (을지깐깐) — with the whole family

In November 2025,
we had a meal here as a family.

The pho broth was
rich yet clean,
and the thinly sliced meat was so tender
our kids ate it happily.

The bean sprouts and coriander
were fresh and fragrant.

Croquettes — the croquettes and spring rolls we got on the side were crispy and generously filled

Rice topped with egg — the nutty flavor was the highlight

Generous portions
and friendly service —
we’d recommend it for a family meal out.

One small minus:
the seasoning is a little on the strong side,
so you may want to adjust it to taste.

🍤 Matsunohana (마츠노하나) — a date with my wife

In February 2026,
my wife and I
stopped here on a date.

The shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon
and the shoyu-salmon hormone bowl
blew us away on looks alone.

Shoyu-salmon hormone bowl — soy-marinated salmon and hormone (offal) topped with an egg yolk

Shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon — the shrimp tempura sticks out past the bowl

The wild conger eel tempura
we added on the side
was wonderfully crisp!

The savory hormone
and the soft umami of the soy salmon
went really well together.

Everything came neatly plated —
it felt like a real treat.

🍣 Hoho Sikdang (호호식당) — a meal for one

In April 2026,
we ordered the salmon rice bowl and a braised dish,
and both were neatly done and delicious.

The salmon was thick and fresh,
and the wasabi-and-soy combo was clean.

Salmon rice bowl — thick slices of salmon with wasabi on top

The braised dish had a deep, rich sauce
that went perfectly with rice.

The braised dish — a rich sauce that goes great with rice, topped with shredded green onion

Side dishes and soup come with it —
a filling Japanese home-style meal.
We’d happily go back.

When we reviewed it on Naver,
we ticked the keywords
’good for eating alone’
and ‘good for conversation’.

🍛 Bonghyeja’s Kitchen (봉혜자네주방) — with our two kids

In November 2025,
the four of us went —
my wife, our two sons and me.

The pork cutlet curry
was better than we expected!
Crispy outside, juicy inside,
with a rich curry sauce
and a generous portion.

Pork cutlet curry — a rich curry sauce over the crispy cutlet

The spicy stir-fry
was well seasoned through,
full of glass noodles and vegetables
for lots of texture,
but it’s a strong, grown-up flavor —
kids will need the heat toned down.

Spicy stir-fry — plenty of glass noodles and vegetables give it lots of texture

Side dishes and soup were fine,
the table setting was tidy,
and it was good value for money.

A spice-level option
or a note on how strong the sauce is
would make it easier
for families ordering again.

🚗 Parking at One Grove

For all four, just use
One Grove’s underground car park.

✔ First 30 min free
✔ More free time the more you spend
20,000 won or more: 1 hour
30,000 won or more: 2 hours
50,000 won or more: 3 hours
100,000 won or more: 5 hours
✔ After that, 2,000 won per 30 min
(max 30,000 won a day)

This is the parking notice
on Hoho Sikdang’s Naver Place page.
It may change,
so please check the signs on site too.
