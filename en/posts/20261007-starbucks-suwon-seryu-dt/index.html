Starbucks Suwon Seryu DT Drive-Thru, Ordering Ahead in the App and My Blonde Aerocano

A coffee I paid for myself, on my way to work ☕

Every morning on my commute
I cross the Suwon Station overpass.

The place I always stop at on that road:
Starbucks Suwon Seryu DT (스타벅스 수원세류DT점)!

The Blonde Starbucks Aerocano I picked up at Suwon Seryu DT

On weekday mornings, on my way to work,
this is where I pick up coffee
without getting out of the car —
a drive-thru (DT) store.

🛣 When I order: the overpass

About 5 minutes before I reach the store,
around the Suwon Station overpass,
is my moment to order.

Gwonseon-ro, the sign toward Suwon Station

The Suwon Station Overpass sign

I always order ahead in the Starbucks app.

I don’t touch my phone while the car is moving —
only when I’m stopped at a red light
do I put the order in.

It works because ordering
takes hardly any time.

🚙 What is My DT Pass?

According to Starbucks Korea’s terms,
once you register your car’s plate number,
the drive-thru entrance
recognizes your plate.

✔ Up to 2 plate numbers
✔ Register a payment method too,
and orders at the entrance are paid automatically
✔ For “MDP Siren Order” —
ordering ahead in the app at a DT store —
a registered plate number is enough
✔ Only for members who have
registered a Starbucks Card

☕ My usual: the Blonde Aerocano

The drink I always love is
the Blonde Starbucks Aerocano.

Just as I saved it in My Menu:
iced, venti, ristretto shots.

The Blonde Aerocano in the cup holder

A thick layer of foam on top,
with the coffee settled underneath.

The Starbucks Korea website describes it as
a drink with “a velvety, fine layer of foam
and a smooth finish.”

One cup with a green straw

The coffee at Suwon Seryu DT
is about the same quality as other Starbucks,
and feels a little better, even.

Above all,
the coffee smells wonderful here.

Most Starbucks stores taste much the same,
but to my taste, a few stores
came across a bit watery.

Starbucks is the café I use all the time,
so with coupons and passes
I can get good coffee
for even less than at other cafés.

Thanks to the discount that day,
a venti came to 5,040 won.

💚 The kind crew

And this store has
kind crew members.

I hope this place I stop at every morning

and that the kind crew
stay here for a long time.

🅿️ Parking and getting there

If you want to sit inside,
use the parking lot in front of the store.

✔ 12 spaces in front (1 accessible space)
✔ 1 hour free with a 10,000+ won purchase
(up to 4 hours a day, up to 2 cars)
✔ After that, 500 won per 10 minutes
✔ 9 min walk from Maegyo Station, Exit 3
✔ 1 min walk from the bus stop
”Yucheongyo · Seryu 1-dong Community Center Entrance” (유천교.세류1동주민센터입구)

(Starbucks Korea store page,
checked October 7)
