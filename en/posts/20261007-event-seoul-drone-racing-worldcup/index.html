Seoul Drone Racing World Cup 2026 — Schedule, Parking & Drone Building and Coding Activities at Gwangnaru Hangang Park

Over the Han River in cool autumn air,
two days of drones taking flight 🚁

Now in its second year!
The 2026 Seoul Drone Racing World Cup
(2026 서울 국제 드론 레이싱 월드컵)
runs Fri Oct 23 – Sat Oct 24
at Hangang Drone Park (한강드론공원)
in Gwangnaru Hangang Park.

We put this together from
Seoul city’s October event plan (September 30),
Seoul city’s press release (September 21)
and the organizer’s tournament website,
as of October 7.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🏁 What kind of competition is it?

Pilots watch the feed
from a camera on the drone
and race it around a course —
it’s an FPV (first-person view) drone racing competition.

Around 60 pilots
from 10 countries take part.
The 32 who get through qualifying
fight it out in a knockout tournament.

The winner takes home
the Seoul Mayor’s Award and a 5 million won prize!

There’s also a drone soccer tournament
at the same venue.
On the 23rd, the Seoul National Drone Soccer Championship;
on the 24th, the Open International Cup,
with players from Korea, China and Japan.

Seoul city says
the competition area and the activity area
are run separately.
On the site map, the Drone Experience Zone
is right next to the entrance.

The qualifying and final times there

🚇 Getting there & parking

On weekends, public transit is recommended

The Drone Park is inside Gwangnaru Hangang Park,
near Gwangnaru Parking Lot 2,
next to the Bicycle Park.
(351-1 Cheonho-dong, Gangdong-gu)

Subway The organizer points you to
Amsa Station (Line 8) and Cheonho Station.

From Cheonho Station (Lines 5 & 8), exit 1,
enter the park through
the Jeumeungil Nadeulmok (즈믄길 나들목) entrance.
Exits 2 and 10
lead to the stairs at Cheonho Bridge.

Parking The nearest lot, Gwangnaru Parking Lot 2
(in front of the Jeumeungil entrance), has 114 spaces;
the whole park has 4 lots with 389 spaces.

The first 30 min cost 1,000 won,
then 200 won for every 10 min,
up to 10,000 won a day.
With Seoul’s multi-child family card (다둥이 행복카드),
you get 50% off.

Parking outside the designated spots
means a 50,000 won fine.

Because weekends get crowded,
the Future Hangang Headquarters
recommends public transit.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The 23rd is a Friday,
so with school-age kids
Saturday the 24th is the realistic choice.

If you didn’t manage to book ahead,
ask on site
whether any spots are left.

The park has restrooms, convenience stores
and a children’s playground.
It’s outdoors by the river,
so a windbreaker is a good idea.

🔑 KICKOn the 24th, walk-in activities run until 16:00,
and the racing finals are in the afternoon
Do the activities first in the morning,
then watch the finals in the afternoon
