Gangdong Seonsa Culture Festival 2026 in Seoul — Schedule, Drone Show, Fireworks & Parking at the Amsa-dong Prehistoric Site

Cool October air,
and a festival that takes you
to Seoul 6,000 years ago 🍂

The 31st Gangdong Seonsa Culture Festival
(강동선사문화축제 — “seonsa” means prehistoric)
runs for three days, Fri Oct 16 – Sun Oct 18,
around the Amsa-dong Prehistoric Settlement Site
and Amsa History Park in Seoul.

Admission is free.

We put this together from
Gangdong-gu’s press release (September 30),
the festival website
and Seoul city’s listings.

Shall we go through the three days in order?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s theme is
’The Beginning of Seoul, Amsa’.

Gangdong-gu describes the Amsa-dong site
as the place where people
first settled in Seoul.

It’s the only festival in Seoul
chosen by the Ministry of Culture,
Sports and Tourism
as a 2026–2027 candidate festival.

The grounds are divided into ‘lands’.

Land of Fire · main stage (opening & closing)
Land of Water · second stage, water-gun play
Land of Earth · forest & round stages inside the site,
craft activities, the night light festival
Land of Wind · new this year:
the Time-Slip Handcrafted Market

The rest zone has the prehistoric BBQ,
and the food zone has street food.

📅 What’s on, and when?

Fri Oct 16 · Day 1
15:00–16:00 ‘Dream’ street parade (gilnori)
(festival entrance → main stage)
16:00–16:40 Jultagi tightrope walking
(National Intangible Cultural Heritage)
19:00–21:00 Opening ceremony & drone show
(Seol Woon-do, Wax, Space A)

Sat Oct 17
14:00–16:00 Bawijeol Village hosang-nori reenactment
(a folk funeral-procession performance)
14:30–16:00 ‘WHY’ prehistory talk concert
(archaeologist Kwak Min-su, webtoon artist Lee Jong-beom)
17:00 Electric Forest night DJ show
19:00–21:00 Gangdong Seonsa Singing Contest

Sun Oct 18 · Last day
13:00–15:30 Seonsa Rock (樂) Festival
14:00–15:00 Lee Sedol talk concert
17:00 Electric Forest night DJ show
19:00–21:00 Closing ceremony & fireworks
(Nam Jin, So Chan-whee, Shin Hyo-bum)

The day-one start time
differs between sources.
Seoul city’s listing says from 18:00 on the 16th,
but on the festival website,
programs start at 10:00 on the 16th too.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Subway Line 8 is the closest

Subway
Line 8, Amsa History Park Station
(암사역사공원역), exit 3 —
a 10-minute walk.
From Amsa Station (Line 8) exit 4, it’s 15 minutes.

Bus
Buses 340, 3411 and 3318:
get off at the Samsung Gwangnaru Apt. stop
(삼성광나루아파트).
Bus 3324: get off at the stop by
Amsa History Park Station exit 3.

Temporary parking
Seonsa Elementary School (837 Olympic-ro)
Seonsa High School (17 Godeok-ro 25-gil)
Sinam Middle School (65 Godeok-ro)
Amsa History Park lot

The high school isn’t certain yet:
the text notice says Seonsa High School,
but the map image on the website says Sinam High School.
Please check the website before you set off.

Last year (the 30th festival),
the road from the Seonsa Elementary School intersection
to the Seowon Village rotary was closed
and some buses were rerouted.
There’s no road-closure notice for this year yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The water-gun siege and hunt
can get clothes wet.
Bring a change of clothes and a towel.

Hut and costume making run all day,
so you don’t need to time your visit.

Night programs go on until 22:00,
so a jacket is a good idea.

For the Lee Sedol talk concert,
100 Gangdong-gu residents can sign up in advance
(Oct 12, 10:00 – Oct 13, 18:00),
and 30 more can register on site from 12:00 that day.
Anyone can watch from outside the barrier.

🔑 KICKDrone show: Fri 16th, opening ceremony
Fireworks: Sun 18th, closing ceremony!
Both are main-stage slots at 19:00–21:00,
so if you can go only one day, pick the date first
