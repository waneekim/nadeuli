Dinner by the Lake at Sunset? Liberty On the Lake’s Spinach Dip Is a Must (US Business Trip, Day 6)

Day 6 of our US business trip,
and this is the dinner story 🌅

Someone who lives here locally
said they’d treat us to something tasty
and took us to a spot
with a beautiful sunset.

Liberty On the Lake,
on the shore of Lake Murray!
It was a healing evening after a while.

Lake Murray at sunset, with yachts lined up

📍 Liberty On the Lake
(Irmo, South Carolina, USA
1602 Marina Rd, Lake Murray)
🌅 Night view and sunset from outside on the second floor
🥇 Nachos + white spinach dip is the best
🍗 Spicy wings · nachos with tomato on top
🍹 The non-alcoholic cocktail tasted like orange juice

🌅 On the Shore of Lake Murray

This is the lake that gave its name
to the Lake Murray Roll
we had on day 5.

Even right next to the lake,
there was no lake smell at all,
and the area felt so peaceful.

The Liberty On the Lake sign under the evening sky

The marina seen below, next to the vertical Liberty sign

The restaurant was split into
a first and second floor,
and we ate on the second floor.

It had a peaceful atmosphere,
with locals coming in
for chat and small talk.

Inside the restaurant — bar seats, high tables and American flag decorations

🛥️ A Path Between the Yachts

The night view from outside
on the second floor was
so beautiful.

Yachts stood in a row,
and a path like a bridge had been built
between them so you could walk through.
It was a sight I’d never seen before,
and very romantic.

A bridge-like dock path running between the boats

🥇 The Best: Nachos + Spinach Dip

The food was
very tasty too.

The best of all
was dipping nachos into the
white spinach dip!

White spinach dip and nacho chips

🔑 KICKNachos + white spinach dip,
this combination
was truly the best!

Next best were the nachos
topped with something like red tomato sauce.

Nachos topped with diced tomato, jalapeño and white sauce

🌶️ The Spicy Wings Were Great Too

The spicy wings were
very tasty too.

Spicy wings — served with celery and white sauce

A fried seafood basket
also came to the table.

Fried seafood basket — fried fish and shrimp, french fries and coleslaw

🍕 Pizza Tasted Korean-Style?

The pizza, unexpectedly,
tasted like Korean-style pizza,
not American-style.

🍹 How Were the Drinks?

I ordered a non-alcoholic cocktail,
and it just tasted like orange juice. 😅

The beer the local host drank
had a fighting rooster logo,
which they said is the
football team mark that symbolizes this region.

The University of South Carolina
football team in Columbia is called the
Gamecocks,
which are fighting roosters.

On the restaurant’s deck,
there was also a Statue of Liberty
sculpture.

The deck at night — a mosaic Statue of Liberty sculpture

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone who wants dinner
while watching the sunset on Lake Murray
✔ Anyone who wants to try
nachos + spinach dip
✔ Anyone on a business trip
who needs a little healing

I had a good day
at a restaurant that felt healing after a while!
