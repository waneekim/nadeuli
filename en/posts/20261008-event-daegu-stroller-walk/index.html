2026 Daegu Stroller Walk Festival Schedule, Route, On-site Registration, Duryu Park Outing for Young Children

The breeze is cool
It’s the perfect time for a stroller walk 👶

This Saturday!
The 13th Daegu Stroller Walk Festival
is happening on October 10 (Sat) 09:00~15:00
at the Duryu Park Inline Skating Rink area.

This event is for families with children aged 0 to 4
and expectant mothers.

I’ve organized the official information
from Daegu City’s press release (October 7)
and the event guide from Daegu Ilbo.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Event

This is a birth encouragement campaign hosted by Daegu City.

Families with young children and expectant mothers
walk together around Duryu Park,
share parenting concerns,
and relieve stress in this participatory festival.

This is the 13th year,
hosted by Daegu Metropolitan City, organized by Daegu Ilbo,
and sponsored by Daegu Mom 365.

Through advance registration in September,
about 300 families, over 800 people
have signed up.

🙋 Can I Go If I Didn’t Register in Advance?

Advance registration (September 7~October 2)
has already ended.

However, according to the city press release,
you can still join through on-site registration on the day of the event.

CategoryDetails
Eligible ParticipantsFamilies with children aged 0~4 · Expectant parents (pregnant women can participate)
Advance Registration9/7~10/2, first 500 teams (closed)
On-site ParticipationOn-site registration available
Inquiries053-757-5783

The organizer’s guide says the target is
’parents with a stroller.’

There is no official information
on the participation fee or the closing time for on-site registration.

✨ What’s the Schedule?

Here’s the event timetable
from the organizer, in order.

Prizes are drawn
for families who complete the walk.

🚶 Where Is the Walking Course?

The course starts and ends at the Inline Skating Rink,
a 1.5km round-trip course.
It takes about 40 minutes on foot.

🎨 What Activities and Play Are There?

CategoryProgram
Experience BoothsChildcare Support Center · CPR Experience · Infertility & Pregnancy Counseling Center · Writing Family Motto · Coloring & Postcard Drawing · Face Painting
EventsWater Balloon Event · Visible Radio · Prize Draw · Stage Recreation
Play ZoneCloud Playground · Bubble Play

According to the city press release, there will also be
an emergency care experience center by Dalseo Fire Station
and a booth promoting childbirth and childcare policies.

Psychological counseling booth requires separate registration.
Register by October 9 (Fri) 15:00,
first 40 people.
Registration is through the organizer’s Naver Form.

The types of experiences and events
may change.

🚗 Directions & Parking

No parking info for event day yet

The event is at the
Inline Skating Rink area inside Duryu Park.

According to Daegu tourism info, Duryu Park
is always free to enter
and has parking available.

However, there is no official notice
about parking or public transport for the event day.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

Registration starts at 09:00,
the walk begins at 10:30.
If you want to see the bubble show (09:45),
arrive a bit early.

Lunch is from 11:30 to 12:20,
bring your own lunch.
If you pack snacks and water for your child,
it’ll be easier to relax after the walk.

Experience booths are open until 15:00.
You can return early after the morning walk for nap time,
or stay and play until the afternoon.

🔑 KICKIf it rains, the event will be postponed
Check the organizer's notice
on Saturday morning first
