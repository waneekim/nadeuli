Cypress Gardens near Charleston, Part 1: Parrots and Peafowl at the Entrance, the Butterfly House and the Gift Shop (US Business Trip, Day 7)

Day 7 of our US business trip,
the place we stopped by
in the morning was
Cypress Gardens 🦋

There was so much to see
that we’re posting it in three parts.
Part 1 goes from the entrance to the gift shop!

The door of the Butterfly House, painted with butterflies

📍 Cypress Gardens
(Moncks Corner, South Carolina, USA
3030 Cypress Gardens Rd)
🦜 Entrance — parrots and peafowl greet you
🦋 A small butterfly garden (Butterfly House)
🐊 A small park — alligator warning sign
🛍️ Gift shop → past it is the main garden (Part 2)

🦚 The Parrots and Peafowl at the Entrance

When we first walked in,
parrots and peafowl
greeted us.

The aviary near the entrance — a peafowl on a branch

I heard that this aviary houses
African grey parrots
and a sulphur-crested cockatoo,
which used to be pets.

🦋 The Small Butterfly Garden

Nearby is
a small butterfly garden (Butterfly House).
I heard it’s an indoor butterfly garden
that opened in 1997.

Before going in,
there was a rules sign.

Don’t touch the butterflies,
don’t pick the flowers,
don’t let birds or butterflies get out,
and under 16 with an adult!

Butterfly House rules — under 16 with an adult

The inner garden and fountain — a sign says not to touch the fountain stones, and a Halloween ghost decoration

A mural of a caterpillar, a chrysalis and a monarch butterfly, with a bench

In the pond,
we also saw a turtle sitting on a rock
and some orange fish. 🐢

KICKA sign by the door —
dogs can't enter buildings,
including the Butterfly House!

🐊 A Small Park — Watch Out for Alligators!

Small parks continue
near the butterfly garden.

On the fence by the water,
there was a sign saying
alligators may live here.

The ALLIGATORS MAY LIVE HERE sign on the fence by the water

You shouldn’t approach alligators
or feed them,
and I heard that feeding them is illegal.

🛍️ The Gift Shop (GIFT SHOP)

Next up was the gift shop!
Under the tree in the middle of the courtyard,
there was a display of
Halloween pumpkins. 🎃

The courtyard decorated with Halloween pumpkins, and the GIFT SHOP

Cypress Gardens T-shirts and handmade crafts by local artists

Big-eyed grim reaper and turtle figures — a note about hand-painted bird feeders

Alligator and turtle figures and a shell ring dish ($65.00)

🗺️ Next Up: The Main Garden

Once you go past the gift shop,
the main garden appears.

We’ll pick up the story
of the main garden in Part 2!

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone looking for
a garden walk near Charleston
✔ Anyone who wants to see
parrots, peafowl and butterflies up close
✔ Anyone who wants to look at
local handmade crafts in a gift shop

Official site — cypressgardens.berkeleycountysc.gov (check admission and hours there)
