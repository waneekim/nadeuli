Haengju Hanwoo BBQ Festival 2026 — Dates, Free Tastings, Kids’ Activities & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Wondang, Goyang

At the tail end of autumn,
a charcoal-grilled hanwoo festival
comes to Wondang in Goyang 🔥

(Hanwoo is Korea’s
native breed of beef cattle.)

Now in its third year!
The 3rd Haengju Hanwoo BBQ Festival
(행주한우 숯불구이축제)
runs for two days,
Oct 31 (Sat) – Nov 1 (Sun),
next to Goyang Fire Station
(across from Jugyo No. 1 Public Parking Lot).

We’ve put together
the organizer’s festival website
and the festival listings of
the Gyeonggi Tourism Organization
and the Korea Tourism Organization.

First, let's see it all at a glance!

🎪 What is this festival?

Haengju Hanwoo is Goyang’s
signature hanwoo brand,
created by local hanwoo farmers
together with the Goyang Livestock Cooperative.
The festival website mentions
the brand’s launch in 2007
and a Presidential Award
at the national hanwoo quality contest
(전국한우능력평가대회).

This year’s theme is
’The Story of Haengju Hanwoo,
Goyang’s Specialty Farm Product’,
and the slogan is
’A Charcoal Gourmet Journey’.

It’s a food festival,
but there are also
free tastings and activities for kids.
The festival mascot is ‘Haengun-i’ (행운이),
a hanwoo with the spirit of
the Battle of Haengju (행주대첩).

The Korea Tourism Organization’s
festival listing says
admission is free.

✨ What’s on, and when?

Tastings and activities run on both days,
and the grill hall’s last entry time
is different each day.

the Gyeonggi Tourism Organization lists
for ‘free tastings and activities’.
Times for each show
haven’t been announced yet.

The Korea Tourism Organization’s listing
calls face painting and balloon art
’free activity booths’.

🥩 How do the free tastings and discounts work?

At the tasting zone
you can try Goyang’s local
livestock products for free.
The tasting categories are
hanwoo, dairy, handon (Korean pork),
beekeeping and poultry.

It’s one per person, first come, first served,
and it may end early
if supplies run out.

Here are the sample discounts
posted on the festival website’s front page.

The grill hall is where you
pick your own cuts of Haengju Hanwoo
to buy or to eat right there.
The Korea Tourism Organization’s listing
says it has 1,000 seats.

There are first-come giveaways too.
Add their KakaoTalk Plus Friend
to get Haengju Hanwoo bone broth,
or join the Naver Smart Store event
for a hanwoo nose-ring (코뚜레) craft kit.
Each round is limited to 100.

🚇 Getting there & parking

The organizer recommends public transit

Traffic gets congested,
so the notice asks visitors
to take public transportation if they can.

WayWhere to get on & off
SubwayWondang Station (Line 3), exit 5, 920m (13 min on foot)
BusNo. 11, 95, 850, 707, 771, 773 → get off at Sinwondang Maeul Complex 7 (신원당마을 7단지)
CarSet your navigation to ‘고양소방서’ (Goyang Fire Station) or ‘원당로 16’
ParkingJugyo No. 1 Public Parking Lot (주교제1공영주차장) is open

The parking fee and number of spaces
aren’t in the official notices.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids?

Face painting and balloon art
run all day on both days.

On Sunday (Nov 1), the grill hall’s last entry
is 16:00 — three hours earlier than Saturday.

The ‘festival site guide’ showing
where the activity zone is
is still being prepared on the website.
