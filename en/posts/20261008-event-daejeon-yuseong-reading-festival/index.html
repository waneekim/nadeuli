2026 Yuseong Book Festival Schedule · Author Lectures · Parking, Before Visiting Yurim Park with Kids

It’s the perfect season to spread out a mat on the lawn and read a book 📖

This weekend in Yuseong-gu, Daejeon, the 4th Yuseong Book Festival will be held at the east lawn plaza of Yurim Park from October 10 (Sat) to 11 (Sun).

Saturday runs from 11:00 to 21:00, Sunday from 10:00 to 17:00, and admission is free.

I’ve organized the official press release, Yuseong-gu Integrated Library notices, and leaflet info for you.

Want a quick overview?

🎪 What Kind of Event Is It?

This year’s theme is ‘Seorakseorak (書樂書樂) I Love Books~Seo!(書)’. There are 72 programs across 10 categories including lectures, performances, and exhibitions.

At the outdoor library on the lawn equipped with wooden shade, tents, and camping chairs, you can freely read 879 books, and 487 books—including past Newbery Medal winners—will be on display.

Yuseong-gu shared that last year, over 10,000 people attended.

✨ What’s Happening When?

There are three main stages: Main Stage, Fringe Stage, and Munhak Village Library. I’ve organized the leaflet schedule by date.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Bringing Kids

The children’s puppet show is on Saturday at 14:00, 15:00, and 16:00, and all three children’s book authors appear on Sunday at the Munhak Village Library.

The library notice suggests bringing a mat, parasol, personal cup, and reading light for the lawn.

The leaflet states that photos may be taken on site for event promotion and records.
