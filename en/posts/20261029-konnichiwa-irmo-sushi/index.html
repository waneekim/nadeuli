What to Order at a Japanese Restaurant? Konnichiwa’s Lake Murray Roll Is a Must (US Business Trip, Day 5)

Day 5 of our US business trip,
and this is the dinner story 🍣

A group of us on the business trip
decided to have a really good dinner,
and the place we found
was a Japanese restaurant.

Konnichiwa, in Irmo,
near Columbia, South Carolina!
In short,
it was very tasty.

🍣 My Top Pick: the Lake Murray Roll

What I had was
the Lake Murray Roll.
Lake Murray, I was told,
is the name of a lake nearby.

It was really tasty. Highly recommend it!

Lake Murray Roll — tuna and black tobiko on an avocado-wrapped roll

The online menu description says
the inside has shrimp tempura · spicy tuna,
