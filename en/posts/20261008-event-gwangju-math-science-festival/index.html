Gwangju Math & Science Festival 2026 — Dates, Activity Reservations and Parking with Kids

Explore maths and science hands-on at
the 2026 Gwangju Math & Science Festival
(광주 수학·과학축제),
October 17 (Sat)–18 (Sun),
at Gwangju National Science Museum.

Alongside the activity booths,
there are making workshops,
board games and performances.
We checked the education office’s festival website
on October 8 to sort out
reservations and parking.

Let's take a quick look!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s theme invites visitors
to “Log-in” to the future
through maths and science.
It is hosted by the Jeonnam–Gwangju
Integrated Special City Office of Education
and organized by the Gwangju Creative
Convergence Education Institute.

The audience includes children from preschool
through high school, parents
and members of the public.
Festival participation is listed as free.
We could not confirm whether that also covers
permanent exhibitions and paid museum facilities.

✨ When can we see what?

The booth guide gives 09:30–16:00.
The event overview also lists a 16:30 finish,
so plan activities around the booth closing time.

Date and timeProgramme
October 17–18, 09:30–16:00Maths and science activities, science play and exhibitions, maths games and missions
October 17, 09:30–10:30Performance block before the opening ceremony: instrumental music, Gwangtalpe performance team and male choir
October 18, 13:30–15:00Part 1 performance block: magic and balloon art, student dance

These performance times cover several acts.
Magic and balloon art is listed
as a 40-minute act within that block.
Check the running order on site
for its exact start time.

🧪 Which activities can kids choose?

Programmes take place in different areas.
If you were expecting only indoor activities,
remember that there are outdoor booths too.

ProgrammeActivities and location
Maths and science activity area130 outdoor activity booths
Science play area10 booths around the Children’s Science Museum: basic, sports, advanced and interdisciplinary science play
Science exhibition areaElectron microscopy, astronomy photographs and insect specimens; AI Hall, floor 2
Maths play areaIndoor maths playground in the Children’s Science Museum; board games in Byeolbit Nuri Hall, floor 2
Maths mission areaByeolbit Nuri Hall, floor 1; maths problem-solving for elementary and middle school students
Maths software and exhibition areaMain building, floor 2; maths software, structure-making and quizzes

Locations follow the detailed programme pages.
Some differ from the homepage labels,
so check the site map
on the day you visit as well.

📝 Book the making workshops first

The Together Science Inquiry workshops
(더불어과학탐구마당) offer six making programmes
for elementary school students and older.
Applications close October 9 (Fri), 21:00.
A team representative applies first come, first served
through the Naver Form linked
from the festival website.

If a guardian joins the team,
the guardian should apply.
Grade requirements vary by programme.

ProgrammeSchool grade and team size
Rolling ball / egg-protection structureGrade 4 or above; grades 1–3 may join with a guardian. Teams of 3–4
Punching robotGrade 5 or above; teams of 2
Foam-board aeroplaneGrade 4 or above; teams of 2
Kapla stackingGrade 3 or above; grades 1–2 may join with a guardian. Teams of 2
Stick-bomb dominoesGrade 3 or above; grades 1–2 may join with a guardian. Teams of 3–4

On the 17th, aeroplane, Kapla, domino
and egg workshops are listed
as three 90-minute sessions each.
Rolling ball and punching robot
have two 120-minute sessions each.
On the 18th, all six programmes
have two 120-minute sessions each.
Check the booking page
for remaining places and start times.

🚇 Getting there and parking

The address is 235 Cheomdangwagi-ro,
Buk-gu, Gwangju
(광주 북구 첨단과기로 235).
The regional map is an overview.
Use the museum’s location map
for actual directions.
The festival website lists these parking areas.

Parking locationOfficial guidance
Gwangju National Science MuseumParking gates remain open on festival days
Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)Walk to the museum through side gates near car parks 1 and 4
Gwangju Science High SchoolCar park and school grounds available; grounds excluded in rain
Additional listed locationsGwangju Industrial Complex outdoor car park, joint information office building and Samsung Electronics Green City Campus 3

Naver Place lists the museum’s usual
flat parking fee as 2,000 won,
while the festival guide says
the gates will remain open.
Confirm festival-day charges before setting off.

👨‍👩‍👧 Visiting with kids

For preschoolers, check the entry requirements
for science play and the maths playground
instead of immediately booking workshops
aimed at elementary students and older.
For elementary-school families, match the grade,
team size and 90- or 120-minute duration first.

🔑 KICKThe festival starts on the 17th,
but workshop applications close on the night of the 9th.
Check the grade requirement for your chosen activity first.

👍 Who might enjoy it?

✔ Families combining activities with Sunday’s magic and balloons
