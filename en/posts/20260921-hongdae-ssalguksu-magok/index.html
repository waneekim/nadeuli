Where to Eat Pho in Magok with Kids — Hongdae Ssalguksu Plus: Good-Value Vietnamese, and the Fried Rice Comes with Two Fried Eggs

Our Magok pho review 🍜

Early this January,
we took our two kids
to Hongdae Ssalguksu Plus, Magok
(홍대쌀국수 플러스) for a meal.

A bowl of pho — bean sprouts, onion and meat over a clear broth

📍 101 Magokseo-ro, Gangseo-gu
Unit 146, 1F
⏰ Mon–Sat 10:30–21:00
Last order 20:30
Closed Sundays
💰 Hongdae Ssalguksu (house pho) 7,900 won
🅿 Parking · takeout · delivery
🪑 High chairs available
📞 02-6958-5599

Eating out with two kids
is never easy,

but that day
we walked out
really happy with our meal!

🍽 A menu that makes kids easy to please

Pho, fried rice,

It was just right

On our table:

🍜 A broth that’s light yet rich

The pho broth was
light yet rich,

without any fuss.

The pairing of crunchy bean sprouts and meat
was great too.

That was the kids’
favorite by far —
they were fighting over it!
