US Business Trip, Day 1: Korean Air Incheon to Atlanta Review, Starlink In-Flight Wi-Fi, Meals and a 12-Hour Window Seat

US business trip,
Day 1 ✈️

This is the story of flying
from Incheon Airport
on Korean Air
to Atlanta airport in the US.

We left at 8:45 a.m.
and arrived safely
with no delay.

The best part was
having internet on the plane.

Gate 277 at Incheon Airport Terminal 2 — the board showed KE033 Atlanta 08:45

✈️ Korean Air KE033
(Delta DL7878 codeshare)
🛫 Incheon Airport Terminal 2
Gate 277 · departed 8:45 a.m.
🛬 Atlanta airport
(no delay)
⏱ Flight of over 12 hours
📶 Starlink in-flight Wi-Fi
🍱 Two meals

🛫 At Gate 277

We left from
Incheon Airport Terminal 2,
Gate 277.

The board showed
KE033 Atlanta 08:45.
The Delta DL7878 codeshare mark
was there too.

A sky-blue Korean Air plane outside the gate window

📶 There Is Internet in the Sky

The best part
of this flight
was the in-flight internet.

The internet service
was provided through Starlink.

The Starlink Wi-Fi connection screen

The connection screen said
that Wi-Fi could be temporarily limited
while passing over
China, India and Pakistan.

With the internet working,
I could also see
roughly where we were flying
on the map.

Location on Google Maps — over the sea east of Japan

🔑 KICKKorean Air Starlink Wi-Fi
Reportedly started free of charge on some planes
from September 15, 2026
(press reports)

According to the reports,
it was first applied to 3 A350-900s
and 1 777-300ER,
and you connect by entering
your name and seat number
and watching an ad.

🍱 Two Meals

I ate
two meals.

But my battery had died,
so I only took a photo of one.
I’m sorry I couldn’t photograph the second half.

The first meal — rice with a red-sauce stir-fry, wrap greens, side dishes and dessert

The meal was
better than I expected.

It was food eaten in the sky,
so the quality wasn’t very high,
and it was a bit hard
eating carefully in a tight seat,
but it was still tasty.

🪟 12 Hours in a Window Seat

For the over 12 hours of flying,
I sat in a window seat.

Clouds and the wing outside the window

I felt a little sorry
about getting out,
so I held it in and never went to the restroom.

Luckily, the person next to me
wasn’t a big person,
so the flight was comfortable.

Location on Google Maps — over Alaska

Outside the window before landing — blue sky and the wing

If you tend to go to the restroom a lot
on long flights,
an aisle seat
may feel more relaxing.

🛬 Arriving in Atlanta

We arrived at Atlanta airport
safely,
with no delay.

Arriving at Atlanta airport — the control tower and a row of planes

I’ll keep writing
the next part of the story
from Atlanta.

👍 Who I Recommend This For

✔ Anyone taking
Korean Air from Incheon to Atlanta
✔ Business trips where you need
internet on the plane too
✔ Anyone deciding on
a window seat for a flight of over 12 hours
