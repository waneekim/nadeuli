2026 Asia Culture Week Schedule · Art Market · Parking, Before Visiting ACC Asia Culture Plaza with Kids

A fall week in the heart of Gwangju
where you can meet many Asian countries 🌏

2026 Asia Culture Week
will be held from October 8 (Thu) to 11 (Sun)
at the National Asia Culture Center (ACC)
Asia Culture Plaza.

Admission is free,
no registration required—just drop by and enjoy.
Here’s a summary of the ACC event guide
and the September 30 press release.

Let’s take a quick look first!

🔴 Happening Now

It opens on the 8th
and continues until the 11th (Sun).
On the last day, it ends 2 hours earlier.

Date & TimeProgram
10/8·9·10 11:00~20:00Art Market · Food Market · Performances
10/11 (Sun) 11:00~18:00Last Day — Ends at 18:00
10/8·9 14:00~15:00Barista Mikael Jasin’s coffee brewing story
10/9·10 18:00~20:00Asia’s Table (Thai chef dining) — Registration closed

🎪 What Kind of Event Is It?

This year’s theme is ‘Asia’s Table.‘
Food and drinks from various Asian countries,
and the surrounding lifestyle culture,
all gathered in one place.

ACC introduces this event as
the only Asian cultural exchange festival in Korea.

🛍️ What Can You See?

ProgramDetails
Asia Art Market66 booths from 15 countries — handicrafts, daily goods, cultural products
Asia Food MarketFoods and food culture from various Asian countries
ExperiencesOpen participation, some require a fee
PerformancesFree viewing, times vary by performance

Countries participating in the art market include India, Nepal, Oman,
Indonesia, Syria, Mongolia,
Sri Lanka, Thailand, Türkiye, Korea, and more.
You can also chat with local artists and sellers.

The Thai wild tea research team ‘Miang Craft’
will introduce Thai tea culture.

🎶 What Performances Are There?

Asian traditional music will be performed on stage.
You can watch without separate registration

PerformanceDetails
Goseong NongyoNational Intangible Cultural Heritage from Goseong, Gyeongnam — rice planting songs expressed through singing, dance, and performance
Surbahar PerformanceIndian traditional string instrument with deep, low sounds
TORONSongs and music by a group of Uzbek and Korean performers

🚇 Getting There & Parking

Culture Jeondang Station on the subway is nearby

For subway, use Culture Jeondang Station exits 5 or 6.
For bus, get off at ‘National Asia Culture Center’ or
’Culture Jeondang Station’ stops.

During the same period, the Chungjang Festival is held on Geumnam-ro,
so traffic around the ACC is controlled according to the event schedule.

ACC Parking LotCapacity & Hours
A (near the Theater)275 cars · 08:00~22:00

Annex (near Sky Plaza)548 cars · 24 hours
Parking Fee (Small Cars)Amount

If you leave within 30 minutes of entry, it’s free.
Payment is by card only.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

The event is for ‘all ages.‘
However, there are no official children-only experiences.\

The market and performances are open for everyone
to enjoy freely without registration,
so you can stop by briefly if you wish.

Asia’s Table is a dining event with traditional liquor,
and registration is already closed.

The Chungjang Festival website also lists this event
as a linked program.
On the 9th (Fri), Chungjang Festival’s
’Asia Culture Day’ is held on Geumnam-ro.

🔑 KICKOn the last day, the 11th (Sun),
it ends at 18:00
That’s 2 hours earlier than other days
