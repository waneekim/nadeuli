🚗 Book and register before driving in

The entrance sign said
only vehicles registered
with an advance booking may enter.

I was able to walk around that day,
but this does not establish
an unrestricted visitor-entry policy.
Ask the management office
where walk-in visitors may go.

The sign restricting entry to pre-booked, registered vehicles

The official guidance also requires
online booking and vehicle registration.

For regular and deck sites,
you park in the campsite car park
and carry your equipment to the pitch.

Driving in does not mean parking beside your pitch.
Check the conditions for
the area you plan to book.
