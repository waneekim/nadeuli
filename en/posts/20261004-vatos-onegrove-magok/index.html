Vatos & Paulie’s at Magok One Grove (Near Gimpo Airport) — Four Moms, a Corona-Rita and Four or Five Hours of Talking

Our taco night at Magok One Grove 🌮

On a Sunday evening,
four of us moms got together
for drinks at Vatos & Paulie’s (바토스앤폴리스).

A Corona-Rita with a Corona bottle upside down in it

📍 165 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu
One Grove Building B, 1F
🚇 Magok Station (Line 5), exit 1
about 3 min on foot
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
last order 21:00
💰 Corona-Rita 18,500 won
🅿 Free parking based on what you spend
(100,000 won or more: 5 hours)
👶 High chairs · groups · reservations
📞 02-6244-9651

We chatted away
for four or five hours,
and it was such a fun place!

The menu cover reads
’VATOS & Paulie’s
