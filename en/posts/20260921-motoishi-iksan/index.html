Where to Eat Beef in Iksan — Motoishi (모토이시): Cuts Served with Name Tags, Grilled on Your Own Charcoal Brazier

Our Iksan beef review 🥩

This past January,
three of us friends
went to Motoishi (모토이시) in Iksan
to catch up.

A board of cuts with name tags — the marbling is dense

📍 Unit 102, 33-2 Seonhwa-ro 3-gil
Iksan, Jeonbuk
(Mohyeon-dong)
⏰ 17:00–24:00
Last order 23:20
Closed Sundays
💰 6-cut assorted set, 320 g
75,000 won
🅿 Small private lot
(public parking nearby recommended)
👶 High chairs · reservations · groups
📞 0507-1390-4406

We had the beef course,
and it beat our expectations —
all three of us left very happy!

🏷 Every cut comes with a name tag

The beef comes on one board,
laid out by cut,
with a name tag
stuck in each one.

Just the ones in our photo:
tomobara, riburosu,
zabuton, nakaochi-karubi
(Japanese names for beef cuts).

The marbling was a work of art,
and knowing exactly what you’re eating
adds one more kind of fun.

The meat
practically melted in our mouths,
which made the conversation even better.

A small plate of marinated beef — on a bamboo leaf with scallions and sesame

🔥 A little at a time on your own brazier

Every seat gets
its own charcoal brazier.

Grilling a few pieces at a time
over red-hot charcoal —
the atmosphere was fantastic!

Our own brazier — red-hot charcoal, with an extractor hood coming down above it

A few pieces on the grill — only as much as you'll eat right away

Fresh vegetables
with that charcoal aroma —
perfect with a drink.

A glass full of ice — a drink to go with the charcoal aroma

🔑 KICKRead the cut tags

knowing what you're eating

🙇 Treated like honored guests all meal

The owner and staff
were so kind that
we felt like honored guests
the whole meal.

Taste, atmosphere, service —
everything was great.

🚗 Parking

It’s down a side street,
so its own lot is small,
and the listing recommends
using a public lot nearby.

The Iksan Police Station car park
is reportedly open for free
after 19:00 on weekdays
and all weekend.

(per Diningcode’s listing)

📋 Menu & prices

6-cut assorted set (320 g) 75,000 won
(5 fresh wagyu cuts + 1 marinated cut)

Omakase set (320 g) 88,000 won
(three cuts picked by the chef)

Wagyu tongue (100 g) 23,800 won
Kkot-galbisal, boneless short rib (100 g) 27,800 won

(per the Naver Place menu)
We didn’t note which course we had,
so please treat the prices as a guide.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for
a place to meet friends in Iksan
✔ Anyone who likes beef
better when they know the cut
✔ Anyone who wants a drink
with that charcoal aroma

Taste, atmosphere and service —
all three were great,
so we’re planning to hold
our next get-together here, too.
