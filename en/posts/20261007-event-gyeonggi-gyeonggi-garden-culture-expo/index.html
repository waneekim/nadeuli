Gyeonggi Garden Culture Expo 2026 — Dates, Getting There & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Semiwon and Dumulmeori

A garden expo is coming to Dumulmeori,
where the Namhangang and Bukhangang rivers meet 🌿

This is the 14th one!
The 14th Gyeonggi Garden Culture Expo
(제14회 경기정원문화박람회)
runs for 4 days, Fri, Oct 16 – Mon, Oct 19,
around Semiwon and Dumulmeori in Yangpyeong.

We put this together from
Gyeonggi Province and Yangpyeong County press releases
and the overview on the expo’s official website.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of event is it?

It’s a garden expo held jointly
by Gyeonggi Province and Yangpyeong County,
organized by the Gyeonggi Environmental
Energy Promotion Agency.

This year’s theme is Dumulmeori Sa:saek.
‘Sa:saek’ holds two meanings at once:
four colors (四色)
and quiet contemplation (思索).

The stage is Semiwon,
‘local garden No. 1’ (지방정원 1호),
and the Dumulmeori area.
The plan is to keep the gardens
chosen in the open call
as open gardens even after the expo.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The main events are the opening ceremony,
garden exhibitions, cultural performances,
a conference and a garden industry fair.
Only the ones with dates are in the table.

DateProgram
Fri 10/16 – Mon 10/19Garden exhibitions — invited garden, artist gardens, imagination gardens, citizen & student gardens and more
Fri 10/16Expo starts — the competition gardens open to the public
Sat 10/17Opening ceremony — final ranking of the competition gardens and awards worth 20 million won in total
Mon 10/19Last day — it runs through Monday
Dates & times not announced yetGarden industry fair (59 companies) · garden culture activities · performances · busking (20 teams) · conference

The opening ceremony time
and the performance and activity schedules
aren’t in the official notices yet.

🌿 Which gardens can you see?

The invited garden is
landscape architect Jung Young-sun’s
’Untitled’ (무제), built inside Semiwon.

Jung Young-sun is the landscape architect
behind Seonyudo Park,
Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park
and Hee Won garden at the Ho-Am Art Museum.

There are also 10 competition gardens
chosen through an open call.

TypeWorks (titles translated by us)
Artist gardens · 5 (200㎡)Overlapping Grain · Dumul Yanggyeong (Two Views of Dumulmeori) · A Clearing in the Woods · The Garden of What Changes and What Doesn’t · Score of Stillness
Imagination gardens · 5 (60㎡)Splashed Ink in the Mist · Seeds of Contemplation, Growing Thought · Ipsang (Standing Thought) · Contemplation, Shedding the Weight · Mycelium of Contemplation

The artist gardens’ theme is
’Landscapes of Four Colors (四色)‘,
and the imagination gardens’ theme is
’Shapes of Contemplation (思索)’.

25 citizen garden docents
who finished their training
will tell the gardens’ stories
during the expo.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Yangpyeong County recommends public transport

Yangpyeong County’s transport department
expects severe traffic jams
around Yangsu-ri during the expo.

Here’s the public transport
Semiwon recommends.

HowWhere to get off
Gyeongui–Jungang LineYangsu Station (양수역) → about 700 m on foot
Bus 2000-1 (from Gangbyeon Station, Line 2) · 2000-2 (from Jamsil Station, Line 2)In front of Semiwon (세미원 앞) → about 100 m (limited weekday service)
G9311 express busYangsu Police Box (양수파출소) → about 600 m

During the expo, some G9311 (Yongmun–Jamsil) runs
won’t enter the Yangsu-ri section
and will stop temporarily at the
Sinwon Station stop (신원역 정류소) instead.

DateG9311 runs affected (departing Yongmun Terminal)
Fri 10/1610:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 15:00
Sat 10/17 · Sun 10/18All 6 weekend runs (07:00 · 10:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 15:00 · 18:00)
Mon 10/19Normal service

For parking, the Yangseo Culture
& Sports Park lot is closed for construction.
Semiwon points visitors to a public lot
a 5-minute walk away.

No temporary expo parking
or shuttle buses have been announced yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

If you came by train or bus,
show your ticket or transit-card history
at the operations booth.
You can get a gift from the
public transport check-in event
(while supplies last).

These are Semiwon’s regular admission fees.
Whether they stay the same during the expo
hasn’t been announced yet.

CategorySemiwon regular admission
General (19 and over)7,000 won (2,000 won back in Yangpyeong local gift vouchers)
Discount (children & teens 6 and over, etc.)4,000 won (1,000 won back in vouchers)
5 and underFree

Under Semiwon’s regular visitor rules,
you can’t bring in food — drinks only.
Kick scooters, bikes, balls
and inline skates aren’t allowed either.

severe traffic jams around Yangsu-ri
Check the walk from Yangsu Station
(Gyeongui–Jungang Line, about 700 m) first
