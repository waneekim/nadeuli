Seoul Silver Grass Festival 2026 (Eoksae Festival) at Haneul Park — Dates, Light Show Times, Parking & Tips for Kids

Autumn is here,
and silver grass is blooming at Haneul Park 🌾

This is the 25th edition!
The 25th Seoul Eoksae (Silver Grass) Festival
(제25회 서울억새축제 — “eoksae” means silver grass)
runs Sat Oct 17 – Fri Oct 23,
7 days, daily 10:00–21:00,
around Haneul Park (하늘공원) in World Cup Park.

We put this together from
the official notice that Seoul’s
Western Parks Leisure Center posted on September 23.

Shall we go through it one by one?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s theme is
’Silver Grass: Comfort from Nature’.

Haneul (“Sky”) Park is a grassland park
on Nanjido, a former landfill
that has returned to nature.
The park guide describes silver grass
shining in autumn and views of
Bukhansan Mountain and the Han River.

The festival grounds have five-senses gardens,
such as the ‘Garden of Comfort’
decorated with red kochia
and the ‘Garden of Hope’
with silver grass horse sculptures,
plus photo zones and a wish zone.
All are open 10:00–21:00.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The light show, busking and market run all 7 days.
Here’s everything by date and time.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 17 (Sat) 18:00–19:00Opening ceremony — a light show, performances by popera singer Volare and the Seongmisan Orchestra, and more
Daily 19:00–20:30Light show — starts at 19:00 · 19:30 · 20:00 (planned); extra 20:30 show on Oct 17 & 18 (planned)
Daily 14:00–17:30Busking — busking zone by the central stage (13:00–16:30 on Oct 17, planned)
Daily 14:30–18:30Re-born Market — plants potted in recycled containers · permaculture produce · upcycled silver grass & ornamental grass goods

The notice says the light show brings
music and colorful lights together.

🌾 What hands-on activities are there?

The walk-up activities (sign up on site) are Haneul silver grass bouquet making,
seed bombs, beeswax wraps and four-leaf clovers from sock loops,
‘Eoksae-ya! Go, Haneul Park’, the park photographer’s photo printing booth,
festival letter writing and the Silver Grass & Sky Library.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Seoul city recommends public transport

The notice says traffic
will be very congested.

Subway Line 6
From World Cup Stadium Station (월드컵경기장역)
or Mapo-gu Office Station (마포구청역),
it’s about a 40-minute walk.

Maengkkongi electric shuttle
(맹꽁이 전기차, named after a little toad)
Board at the Nanjicheon Park parking lot
(Nanjicheon Park ↔ top of Haneul Park)
October–November hours 09:00–19:00,
running as needed or every 10–20 minutes.

RiderShuttle fare
Adultsone way 2,000 won · round trip 3,000 won
Children (ages 4–12)one way 1,500 won · round trip 2,200 won
Infants 48 months and underfree

which rate applies during the festival.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Activity sign-up opens at 13:30,
so if you arrive after lunch
you can go activities → busking & market → light show
in one go.

The Haneul Park nursing room
is in the visitor information center,
open 09:00–18:00.

If you’ll ride the shuttle with a stroller,
check the size rule first:
folded, its three sides must add up to 115 cm or less,
and it must weigh 10 kg or less.

The light show starts after sunset,
so pack an extra layer for the kids.

🔑 KICKPer the park guide, the electric shuttle
stops running at 19:00
(last ride up: 18:30)
If you're staying for the light show,
plan your way back down first
