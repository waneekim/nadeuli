Wonchau in Anyang: Springy Hand-Pulled Jjajangmyeon, Jjamppong and Tangsuyuk (Menu Prices, Parking)

Very springy noodles,
and both the black bean sauce and the jjamppong broth were delicious.

On the evening of Saturday, October 10,
we went to Wonchau (원차우),
a hand-pulled noodle place on the Gwangmyeong–Anyang side.

We had actually planned to eat at
a very famous red-bean noodle soup place
right across the street,
Hwangok Jakpum Jeolla-do Yetmatson Pat-kalguksu
(황옥작품 전라도옛맛손팥칼국수).

But it had closed early that day,
so we walked into the hand-pulled jjajangmyeon place
in front of it instead.

It was better than we expected, and delicious!

Wonchau's sign at night: "CHINESE RESTAURANT, course meal specialist"

🏮 A hand-pulled noodle place on the Sammak food street

The sign says “course meal specialist” (코스요리전문점),
and the glass door says
”Wonchau, hand-pulled jjamppong and jjajang specialist.”

Just inside the entrance were signs for
”Sammak food street, excellent dining district,"
"Model Restaurant” and “Safe Restaurant.”

The grand-prize panel from the Sammak village festival cooking contest, with stone-plate jjajang and jjamppong posters

Inside hung a panel reading
”Anyang Sammak Food Street Food Festival,
Sammak Village Festival Cooking Contest,
Grand Prize (최우수상).”

Hall tables under red lanterns

Dish photos on the wall and the "Mokryeon" room

The hall had several rows of tables,
and along the wall there were also rooms
with name plates, “Dongbaek” and “Mokryeon.”

The restaurant menu on October 10, 2026

At the bottom of the menu it said
a large portion costs 2,000 won extra,
and jjamppong can be switched to a rice version.

There were also seafood udong and ulmyeon,
seasonal noodles like cold noodles and kongguksu,
fried rice and rice bowls, and dumplings.
The tables only include prices I could read clearly on the menu.

🥢 Hand-pulled jjajangmyeon with very springy noodles

Pickled radish, onion, black bean paste, seasoned zha cai and soy sauce

The basic side dishes were pickled radish, onion,
black bean paste, seasoned zha cai and soy sauce.

The jjajangmyeon we ordered came with
the sauce served separately.
We poured it over the noodles and mixed it in.

Pouring the separately served black bean sauce over the noodles (GIF)

Jjajangmyeon mixed with the sauce

Once we tasted it,
the jjajangmyeon noodles were really springy.

The black bean sauce
was very tasty, too.

Among the jjajangmyeon places I’ve been to, this one still ranks among the few I’d single out.

🍖 Tangsuyuk that was chewier than expected

White tangsuyuk before the sauce goes on

The tangsuyuk also came with the sauce on the side.
We poured it over the white fried pieces.

The separately served tangsuyuk sauce

The tangsuyuk was also

From jjajangmyeon and jjamppong to tangsuyuk,

🅿 Park in front, and move your car after eating

Parking spaces marked along the side of the restaurant

In front of and beside the restaurant
there were marked parking spaces.
Naver Place also lists “parking”
among its amenities.

The door reading "hand-pulled jjamppong specialist" and a notice asking guests to move their cars after eating

On the door was a notice:
“Please be sure to move your car after your meal.”

Once you’ve finished eating,
it’s a good idea to move your car.

👍 Who I’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone looking for jjajangmyeon with springy hand-pulled noodles
✔ Anyone looking for a place with tasty jjamppong broth
✔ When you can’t decide on dinner on the Sammak food street

We went in instead of the place we’d planned,
and it turned out better than expected.

When I crave hand-pulled jjajangmyeon
around Sammak in Anyang,
I think Wonchau will come to mind.
