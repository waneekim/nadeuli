2026 Jeju Citrus Expo Dates, Admission & Advance Booking – Seogwipo Agricultural Technology Center with Kids

In November, when the tangerines turn deep orange,
a citrus expo opens in Seogwipo 🍊

The 2026 Jeju Citrus Expo (제주감귤박람회)
runs for five days, November 19 (Thu) to 23 (Mon),
at the Seogwipo Agricultural Technology Center’s
Agro-Ecological Garden area in Namwon-eup, Seogwipo.

I checked the Jeju International Citrus Expo Organizing Committee’s
official website – its visitor guide and notices –
and a D-100 news report (August 13)
on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Expo Is It?

It’s an expo that shows Jeju citrus
from growing to processing, distribution and export,
all in one place.
It is hosted by Jeju Special Self-Governing Province
and organized by the Jeju International Citrus Expo Organizing Committee.

According to the D-100 report, this year’s theme is
”Jeju Embracing Citrus! Jeju Opening the Future!”
The organizers said they are preparing smart farming
and high-quality citrus growing technology,
the citrus processing industry, export consultations
and academic sessions.

For visitors, they plan to run
cultural performances and a citrus direct market,
along with family hands-on activities.

🎟️ How Much Is Admission?

These are the admission fees in the visitor guide.

CategoryAdmission
On-site ticket2,000 won
Advance booking1,000 won
Jeju residents1,000 won
Under 13 · 65 and overFree
National (independence) merit recipients · basic livelihood recipientsFree
People with disabilities (plus 1 caregiver for severe disabilities)Free

For two adults with two kids under 13,
it’s 4,000 won with on-site tickets,
or 2,000 won with advance booking.

⏰ What Are the Opening Hours?

ItemDetails
Gates open–close10:00~17:00
Exhibition halls10:00~16:30
Advance bookingUntil November 18, book your visit date on the website
On-site salesNovember 19–23

Advance booking is allowed once per account,
for up to 20 people.

Even with a booking,
you buy your ticket on site at the discounted price
after your booking is confirmed.
The website says to be sure to bring
ID to confirm your booking.

To change the date,
cancel under “My Page > Reservation check”
and book again.

🍊 What Exhibition Halls Are There?

Here is the hall layout
from this year’s exhibitor guide.

HallContents
Citrus direct marketCitrus · other Jeju fruit
Citrus Industry HallProcessed citrus products (food · cosmetics) · packaging
Farm Equipment & Materials HallCitrus and farm machinery · farm materials
Partner Agencies HallAgricultural organizations · public agencies

The organizing committee announced that it will separately collect
citrus entered in this year’s citrus contest (early-season · eco-friendly)
for display at the expo.

🚌 Getting There & Parking

Set your navigation to '서귀포농업기술센터'!

The address is Namwon-eup, Seogwipo-si,
7413 Jungsangan-dong-ro (중산간동로 7413).
Here are the driving times
from the official directions page.

FromDriving time
Jeju Airport (via 5.16 Road)Via Harye-ri entrance, about 57 min
Jeju Airport (via Pyeonghwa-ro)Via Sangchang and Topyeong intersections, about 80 min
Seogwipo City Hall, 1st BuildingVia Topyeong intersection, about 15 min
Jeju World Cup Stadium (Seogwipo)About 24 min

By bus, go from Jeju Airport or Jeju Bus Terminal
to Harye-ri Entrance (하례리입구) and transfer
to reach “Seogwipo Agricultural Technology Center (Expo).”
City buses from downtown Seogwipo (Jungang Rotary · Wimi area)
also pass the expo site.
The website says to check route numbers and times
on the Jeju Bus Information System.

There is no information yet
on parking capacity or shuttle buses.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

Kids under 13 get in free,
so admission costs little for families.

The exhibition halls close at 16:30
and the grounds at 17:00.
If you arrive late in the afternoon,
visit the exhibition halls first.

Of the five days, the weekend falls on
the 21st (Sat) and 22nd (Sun),
and the last day, the 23rd, is a Monday.

🔑 KICKAdvance booking closes on November 18.
With a booking, adults pay 1,000 won.
Show your ID on site.
