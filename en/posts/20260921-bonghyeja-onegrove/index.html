Bonghyeja’s Kitchen at Magok One Grove (Near Gimpo Airport) — Pork Cutlet Curry Beat Our Expectations, the Spicy Stir-Fry Is for Grown-Ups

Our family meal out at One Grove, Magok 🍛

In November 2025,
the four of us —
my wife, our two sons and me —
went to Bonghyeja’s Kitchen (봉혜자네주방).

Pork cutlet curry — a rich curry sauce over the crispy cutlet, served with rice and salad

📍 165 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu
One Grove Building A, 1F
🚇 Magok Station (Line 5), exit 1
about 3 min on foot
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
(last order 21:00)
💰 Stir-fries, per person
13,000–15,000 won
🅿 One Grove underground parking
(free time based on what you spend)
👶 High chairs available
📞 02-6106-0053

It’s one of the places
we went to as a family at One Grove,
and the pork cutlet curry
is what stuck with us.

🍛 Pork cutlet curry — better than expected

To cut to the chase,
it was better than we expected!

The cutlet was crispy outside
and juicy inside.

The rich curry sauce
went beautifully with it.

With rice and salad,
the whole plate felt well balanced,

and the toasty, savory topping
in the middle
was the highlight.

Generous portions, too —
just right for a family meal out.

🌶 Spicy stir-fry — a grown-up taste

The spicy stir-fry
we ordered second
was well seasoned through
and perfect with rice.

Lots of glass noodles and vegetables
gave it plenty of texture.

Spicy stir-fry — plenty of glass noodles and vegetables give it lots of texture

But it’s a strong flavor
that grown-ups will love,
so for kids
you’ll need to tone down the heat.

🍚 Side dishes, soup and table setting

The side dishes and soup were fine,
and the table setting was tidy.

Overall,
it’s good value for money,
so we’d recommend it for a family meal.

😅 One small wish

We wish there were a spice-level option
or a note on how strong the sauce is.

When we order again with our two kids,
it would help to know in advance
how spicy each dish is.

Still, we’d happily go back!
