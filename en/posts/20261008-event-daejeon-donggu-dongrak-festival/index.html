2026 Daejeon Donggu Dongrak Festival Schedule, Timetable, Parking — Before Visiting Soje-dong Daedongcheon with Kids

During the Hangeul Day holiday, the alleys behind Daejeon Station
are filled with delicious aromas for three days 🍞

It’s the 4th year!
The 4th Daejeon Donggu Dongrak Festival
will be held from October 9 (Fri) to 11 (Sun)
every day from 11:00~22:00
at Soje-dong Donggwangjang-ro and Daedongcheon area.
Admission is free.

I’ve organized Daejeon Dong-gu’s press releases (October 6 & 7)
and the official website’s schedule and leaflet.

Shall we take a quick look first?

🎪 About the Festival

This year’s festival is all about gourmet food.
Dong-gu has added night tourism to local specialties
like bread and flour.

It’s right behind Daejeon Station Donggwangjang,
so you can walk to the festival after getting off the train.

On the first day, the opening ceremony at 19:00
is held together with the Dong-gu 50th Anniversary of District Establishment celebration.

✨ What’s Happening When?

Here’s the main stage schedule by date.
Every night, there’s the Donggu Dongrak Concert.

Busking stages also run from noon.
On the 9th from 12:00, the 10th from 11:00,
and the 11th from 12:00, different teams perform.
At 17:00 on the 11th, there’s the Dopamine Zumba Dance Challenge.

🍴 What to Eat and Experience

At the World Food Street,
you’ll find food from 14 countries.
Vietnamese pho, pad thai, Japanese takoyaki,
Turkish kebab, Portuguese egg tart,
Korean street food and banquet noodles are featured.

All three experiences are first-come, first-served on site.

ExperienceTime & Registration
Daejeon Flour Expedition11th 16:00~17:00 · On-site registration for 30 teams (up to 4 people per team) at the main stage entrance
Donggu Dongrak Adventure10th & 11th 14:00~16:00 Alley Tour · On-site registration for 20 people per day from 11:00 to 13:30
Soje Hidden Restaurant10th & 11th 12:00~13:00 Chef Dessert Cooking Show · 10 on-site spots out of 30 per day

The Flour Expedition is a hands-on experience
where you make and taste gnocchi (similar to Korean sujebi)
using potatoes and flour.

The Adventure is a 2-hour walking course
from World Food Street → Art Site Soje
→ Railroad Official Village → Daedongcheon Book Picnic,
and you get a disposable film camera.

The chef for the cooking show is Oh Se-deuk on the 10th,
and Shin Dong-min on the 11th.
Advance registration (closed October 2) is over.

At the venue, you’ll also find a retro Soje game zone,
bakery pop-up store, flea market and experience booths,
and a university-linked flea market.

🚇 Directions & Parking

It's a 7-minute walk from Daejeon Station

Subway: Exit Daejeon Station via Exit 1
and walk 7 minutes from Hoguk Railroad Plaza.

Bus: At Daejeon Station Donggwangjang (garage)
bus stop, routes 102, 107, 317, 616, 707, 711,
30, 33-1, 33-2, 52, and 62 stop here.

During the festival, Donggwangjang-ro and Daedongcheon riverside parking area
will be closed to traffic.
This is from October 6 (Tue) 21:00
to October 12 (Mon) 05:00.

Here are the official free parking lots.
The Daedongcheon riverside parking lot is listed
as the section between Cheolgabgyo and 2nd Chisugyo bridges.

Free Parking LotLocation

Dongdaejeon Sungkyul Church Annex Parking4 Mirae-gil · 9th 09:00~21:00

Dongdaejeon Sungkyul Church parking
is open only on the 10th from 09:00 to 17:00,
and on the 11th from 16:00 to 21:00.

Wondong and Indong street parking, Daejeoncheon area parking,
and the Urban Industry Support Platform parking lots
are paid, but are free on weekends.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

The nursing room is next to the main stage
by the General Situation Room.

With busking and experiences during the day,
and concerts in the evening,
if you plan to stay late,
it’s a good idea to bring an extra jacket for your child.

🔑 KICKThe Flour Expedition allows up to 4 people per team
If you're a family of 4, you can join as one team
On the 11th, register on-site at the main stage entrance
30 teams available
