Balhae Lamb Skewers Yeongtong, Suwon: Lamb Skewers and Ribs on Charcoal the Owner Makes Himself — My Favorite Dish Is Liu Rou Duan

A lamb skewer review from Yeongtong, Suwon 🍢

One Friday evening,
I stopped by
Balhae Yangkkochi (발해양꼬치)
in a shopping building in Maetan-dong, Yeongtong-gu.

It’s a place
we often go to
when a group gets together.

The lamb is tender and has no gamey smell.

The 'Balhae Yangkkochi · lamb skewer specialty' signboard

🏪 A lamb skewer specialty shop in Maetan-dong

The signboard has
’Balhae Yangkkochi’
and ‘lamb skewer specialty’
written large.

On Naver Place,
it’s listed as ‘Balhae Yangkkochi’
written as one word.

The shop front with a sheep-illustration sign

On the window out front,
there’s a notice saying
’Closed on Sundays’.

Naver Place also shows
regular closing every Sunday.

👫 A shop run by a Chinese couple

The shop is run
by a Chinese couple
themselves.

The owner has been running
this place for a very long time.

The Naver Place introduction says
it opened on December 16, 2013 and has been
run in the same spot
without changing owners.

It has been quite Koreanized,
but since it’s run by people from there,
you can taste something different.

A little of the taste
of authentic Chinese cooking
is still alive.

🔥 Charcoal the owner makes himself

The lamb skewers and lamb ribs
are grilled over charcoal
that the owner makes himself.

So you can taste
real craftsmanship.

The charcoal stove in front of the shop

At the table,
a small grill
for cooking the skewers is set out.

The table setting with the skewer grill and side dishes

Lamb cooking on the charcoal grate

The lamb is
tender and has no gamey smell.

Table spices — cumin and chili powder

Tsingtao and side dishes of onion, chili pepper and peanuts

🍖 My favorite dish, Liu Rou Duan

The dish I like most
at this place
is Liu Rou Duan.

On the menu board,
it’s written ‘Liu Rou Dwan’.

The owner said he couldn’t
put this dish into Korean,
so he just wrote it in Chinese.

Liu Rou Duan (menu board name 'Liu Rou Dwan')

In Chinese characters,
it’s written 溜肉段 (Liu Rou Duan).

On the Chinese recipe site
’Xiachufang (下厨房)‘,
it’s presented as a signature dish
of restaurants in Northeast China.

The usual way to make it is
to coat the meat in a starch batter, fry it,
and toss it in a thick sauce.

Liu Rou Duan tossed with onion, carrot and bell pepper

The dish is thick and sticky,
and the tasty aroma lingers.

A closer look at the Liu Rou Duan

🔑 KICKA dish with no Korean name
'Liu Rou Dwan' on the menu board
(溜肉段 · Liu Rou Duan)
It's my favorite item

👍 Who we’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone looking for tender lamb
with no gamey smell
✔ Anyone who likes
charcoal-grilled lamb skewers and ribs
✔ Anyone curious about
Chinese food made by people from there

If you’re looking for lamb skewers in Yeongtong,
go to Balhae Yangkkochi
and try the Liu Rou Duan too!
