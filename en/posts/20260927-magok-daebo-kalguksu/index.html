Kalguksu in Magok, Seoul: Dalin Daebo Kalguksu Main Branch — Potato Pancake with Lettuce & Chili, Jang-kalguksu Deeper Than Ramyeon

Our kalguksu dinner in Magok 🍜

One Sunday evening,
we took the kids to
Dalin Daebo Kalguksu (달인대보칼국수),
the main branch in Magok.

It was our first time at this branch,
and it was so good for eating with kids.

The star of the night, the shredded potato pancake — seasoned lettuce and chili sauce on the same plate

🏠 A kalguksu place in West Bldg #211

It’s in unit 211
of Botanic Prugio City’s West Building.

Through the glass door
you can see the kitchen
labeled ‘in-house noodle workshop’ (자가제면소).

The entrance from the mall corridor — the in-house noodle workshop shows through the glass door

On the green wall inside
hangs the Dalin Daebo Kalguksu sign.

The Dalin Daebo Kalguksu sign on the green wall

You order on the tablet
fixed to each table.

We got the potato pancake,
jang-kalguksu and clear kalguksu!

The tablet ordering device on the table, with a payment and parking notice

🥔 The best: the shredded potato pancake

Today’s number one
was the potato pancake.
On the menu it’s ‘gamjachae-jeon’
(shredded potato pancake).

Thinly shredded potato
is pan-fried into a wide, flat pancake,
and it comes on one plate
with a seasoned lettuce salad
and a sauce of finely chopped chilies.

Topping the crispy pancake
with the lettuce and chili sauce together
made it absolutely amazing!

Tearing off some potato pancake with the lettuce (GIF)

🌶 Jang-kalguksu, deeper than ramyeon

The jang-kalguksu
(knife-cut noodles in a spicy paste broth)
was incredibly good too.

It has a far deeper flavor than ramyeon.

It’s topped with a boiled egg, seaweed flakes, sesame,
chopped green onion and chili.
On the menu it’s ‘Eolkeuni Jang-kalguksu’.

Jang-kalguksu — boiled egg, seaweed flakes, sesame, green onion and chili

The moment the jang-kalguksu noodles are lifted (GIF)

🍜 Clear kalguksu, mild and a little salty

The clear kalguksu
had a mild, slightly salty flavor
that was superb.

The clear broth, topped with seaweed,
sesame and green onion,
is full of wide, flat noodles.

It comes with danmuji
(yellow pickled radish) and kimchi.

Clear kalguksu — seaweed, sesame and green onion

Clear kalguksu noodles (GIF)

📜 Anchovy broth and house-made noodles

A board on the wall
lists the two things
this place is proud of.

Broth — anchovies from Korea’s south coast
and vegetables are simmered
for over 3 hours,
then finished with a special sauce, it says.

House-made noodles — fresh noodles made in-house
are aged at the right temperature and humidity,
with no coloring, preservatives
or chemical additives, it says.

The anchovy broth and house-made noodle board with its TV history; visitors' autographs on the left

Further in, there really is a kitchen window
labeled ‘in-house noodle workshop’.

The board also says
it started in 1992 in Banghwa-dong, Gangseo-gu,
and has been carried on by three generations,
and lists its TV appearances,
including SBS’s ‘Saenghwal-ui Dalin’
(a show about everyday masters) in 2005.

Inside the restaurant, with the in-house noodle workshop window

The nationwide branch board — the Magok main branch tops the Seoul/Gyeonggi list

🚗 Parking & payment

If you come by car, park in the building’s lot
and take the West Building elevator
up to the 2nd floor.

The notice on the table
asks you to tell the counter to register your parking.

‘Cash payment (Payco · Zero · Kakao)‘
and earning or using coupons
are also done at the counter.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for a kalguksu place
to eat at with kids
✔ Anyone looking for a crispy pancake
to go with kalguksu

deeper than ramyeon
