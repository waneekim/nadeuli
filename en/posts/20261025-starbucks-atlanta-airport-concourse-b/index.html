Starbucks Atlanta Airport Review: The Concourse B Store I Visited on a Layover, Tastes Like Korea but Costs More

A review of the Starbucks
at Atlanta airport ☕

On my way to the US
for a business trip,
I stopped by the airport Starbucks
during my layover in Atlanta.

I was curious how Starbucks coffee
in the US would taste,
and it was almost the same as in Korea.

The Starbucks next to the 'Gates B1–B14' sign on Concourse B — the line was long

📍 Atlanta airport (ATL), Concourse B
(near gate B14, next to Popeyes)
⏰ Daily 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.
(store sign)
☕ Grande iced Americano
💰 $6.50 + 15% tip
🟤 No Blonde roast
(at the time of our visit)

✈️ On a layover, on Concourse B

The Starbucks was
on Concourse B.

Right in front of it
was a ‘Gates B1–B14’ sign,
and the shop next door
was Popeyes.

The Siren logo next to the entrance — Popeyes is right next door

The store sign said
it’s open
daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.

☕ No Blonde, just Americano

In Korea, my taste is
already set on the
Blonde Americano.

But here,
there was no Blonde
and only the
regular Americano.

The menu board under the STARBUCKS sign — a fall drinks screen was up

So I ordered a
grande
iced Americano.

The taste was
almost the same as in Korea.

Grande iced Americano — the name and time on the cup label are blurred

🔑 KICKThere may be no Blonde roast
This store only had the regular Americano
(at the time of our visit)

💵 Adding the tip

The price was
higher than in Korea.

A grande Americano was
$6.50,
and I added a 15% tip on top.

ItemAmount
Grande Americano$6.50
15% tipabout $1

In Korean won,
that’s roughly 10,000 won including the tip.

⏳ Coffee that came out later than expected

There was a
long line in front of the store.

The coffee also
came out later than I expected.

If your layover is tight,
leave yourself some extra time.

👍 Who I’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone on a layover at Atlanta airport
who needs a familiar
cup of coffee
✔ Anyone curious about
Starbucks in the US
✔ Anyone who’s fine with
the regular Americano
instead of Blonde

Starbucks in the US
didn’t taste
all that different.

Next time, I’d like to try
a Starbucks somewhere else
too!
