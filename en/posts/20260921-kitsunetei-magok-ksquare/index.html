Kitsunetei, Magok K-Square — Crispy-Outside, Juicy-Inside Hirekatsu, Salmon and Pork Rice Bowls, and Easy with Kids

Our Magok hirekatsu review 🍽

In October 2025, we took our kids
to Kitsunetei
in the basement of K-Square, Magok.

Hirekatsu — served sliced on a wire rack, with a pale pink center

📍 105-7 Magokjungang-ro
Gangseo-gu, Seoul

💰 Hirekatsu 15,500 won

👶 High chairs · reservations · takeout

We ordered the hirekatsu,
a salmon rice bowl
and a pork rice bowl —
and, to cut to the chase,
we were really happy!

🐷 Hirekatsu — crispy outside, juicy inside

The hirekatsu was crispy outside
and moist inside,
bursting with juices.

It comes in thick slices
on a wire rack,
with a pale pink cross-section.

Lemon, wasabi
and a cabbage salad with dressing
come on the side.

On the Naver Place menu
it’s the hirekatsu set at 15,500 won:
aged Korean pork tenderloin,
deep-fried.

🍣 Salmon rice bowl — fresh and tender

The salmon bowl
was fresh and tender.

The salmon comes topped
with salmon roe and chopped scallions.

Salmon rice bowl topped with salmon roe (back) and pork rice bowl topped with shredded green onion and bok choy (front)

🌶 Pork rice bowl — sweet, salty, spicy

The pork bowl
has a sweet-and-salty soy flavor
with a spicy kick.

Thin slices of pork
topped with shredded green onion,
bok choy and a single chili.

On the Naver Place menu
it’s the butadon at 12,000 won:
pork belly braised
in a spicy sauce.

🔑 KICKCan't decide? Get these three!
Hirekatsu + salmon bowl

🧸 Cozy and easy with kids

The atmosphere was cozy,
and it was easy

Naver Place’s amenities list
