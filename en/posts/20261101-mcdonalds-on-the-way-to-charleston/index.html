Ordered an Iced Coffee at a US McDonald’s and Got a Latte? How to Use the Kiosk (US Business Trip, Day 7)

Day 7 of our US business trip,
and this is a morning story ☀️

On the way from our lodging
to Charleston,
we stopped at a rest stop McDonald’s.

I ordered an iced coffee,
but instead of a Korean-style iced americano,
a latte came out, and I was surprised. 😅

The McDonald's at the rest stop — the Welcome sign above the entrance

📍 McDonald's at a rest stop on the way to Charleston
(next to a Mr. Fuel gas station)
☕ Order: Iced Coffee, medium, $2.00
🧾 $2.16 with tax
🥛 Not a Korean-style iced americano, but a latte-like coffee
📱 Kiosk in 9 steps — follow the photos below in order

🚗 On the Way to Charleston

We drove the highway
from our lodging to Charleston.

The highway to Charleston — a sky full of clouds

Autumn leaves turning color outside the car window

The rest stop we pulled into
was a Mr. Fuel gas station,
and right next to it
was a McDonald’s.

The McDonald's sign seen past the Mr. Fuel gas station

The counter — the mobile and delivery order pickup shelf, the kitchen and Halloween pumpkin decorations

📱 How to Order at the Kiosk

We ordered at the kiosk.
Just tap the red marks
in the photos, in order.

① First screen — Continue as Guest

If you’re not an app member (My McDonald’s Rewards),
tap Continue as Guest.

① If you're not an app member, Continue as Guest

② Menu screen — McCafé®

We went in the morning, and the
Good morning screen showed
the breakfast menu first.
For coffee, tap McCafé®
in the left menu.

② McCafé® in the left menu

③ Choosing a coffee — Iced Coffee

From the McCafé list,
I tapped Iced Coffee ($2.00, 190cal).

③ Iced Coffee in the McCafé list

④ Pick a size — Add to Order

You choose from M, L and S,
and that day all three were $2.00.
After choosing, Add to Order!

④ Choose a size and tap Add to Order

⑤ Your cart — View My Order

When the amount at the bottom changes to $2.00,
tap View My Order.

⑤ When the amount changes, View My Order

⑥ Suggested items — Maybe next time

Try these, too! It suggests
items to buy along with your order.
If you don’t want any, tap Maybe next time.

⑥ If you don't need the suggested items, Maybe next time

⑦ Donation — Not Today

It asks about a Ronald McDonald House donation.
(Round up $0.84 · $1 · $3 · $5)
If you don’t want to, tap Not Today.

⑦ If you don't want to donate, Not Today

⑧ Where to eat — Here / To Go

Tap Here to eat in,
or To Go to take it with you.

⑧ Here to eat in, To Go to take it with you

⑨ Check and finish — Complete Order

Medium Iced Coffee $2.00
plus $0.16 tax makes a total of $2.16.
Tap Complete Order
and pay, and you’re done!

⑨ Check the total and Complete Order

🥛 Why the Iced Coffee Was a Latte

I’d had a Korean-style
iced americano in mind
when I ordered the Iced Coffee,
but a latte-like coffee came out.

Looking at the screen again,
the black Premium Roast Coffee was 10cal,
and the Iced Coffee was 190cal.

KICKIf you want it black,
check the Americano category at the top
or Customize Ingredients
first!

✔ Anyone looking for

✔ Anyone stopping for a short rest
