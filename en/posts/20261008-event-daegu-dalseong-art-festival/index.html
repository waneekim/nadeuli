2026 Dalseong Daegu Contemporary Art Festival Hours, Artworks, Directions – Free Outdoor Exhibition at Gangjeongbo The ARC

Walking among the artworks, feeling the Nakdong River breeze—it’s autumn 🎨

The 2026 Dalseong Daegu Contemporary Art Festival is happening right now.

It’s open until Sunday, October 11,
with four days left as of October 8.

The venue is the Gangjeongbo The ARC Plaza area in Dasa-eup, Dalseong-gun,
and admission is free.

I’ve summarized the official festival website
and Dalseong-gun press releases.

Want a quick overview?

🔥 Happening Now – Remaining Schedule

You can see the outdoor artworks daily from 11:00 to 22:00.
Here’s a table of what’s left for the final four days.

Date & TimeWhat’s On
Each of the last 4 days30+ outdoor artworks — 11:00~22:00
Until 10/11 (Sun)The ARC Cultural Center 1F Resident Artist Special & Exchange Exhibition
10/10 (Sat) 14:00 · 16:00Citizen participation: ‘My Own Imagination Block’ (ages 5+, free) — Registration closed

The October 10 activity is artist Jung Jin-kyung’s
’My Own Imagination Block’ workshop.
It takes place in the Vital Room at The ARC Cultural Center
and lasts 90 minutes.

As of October 8, registration via the Dalseong Cultural Foundation
was already closed.
For inquiries, call 053-668-4252.

The small concert and three other activities held in September
have already ended.

🎪 About the Festival

This is the 15th year of the festival.

It’s introduced as Daegu’s leading outdoor art festival
set along the riverside at Gangjeongbo.

This year’s theme is
”Movement: Flow–Pause–Flow.”
Like light reflecting on the river
and wind stirring the artworks,
it presents movement and stillness
through contemporary art.

It’s also one of the ten autumn festivals
that make up Fantasia Daegu Festa.

🖼️ What Artworks Are There?

26 teams of Korean artists
are presenting over 30 sculptures, installations, kinetic art, and media art along the riverside.

The ARC is a building inspired by the river, water, and nature.
According to Daegu tourism guides, the surrounding riverside area
and bike paths are green spaces for relaxation,
and the 3rd-floor observatory is a nice spot to take a break.

👨‍👩‍👧 Visiting with Kids

The artworks are spread out along the riverside,
so it’s an exhibition you experience as you walk.

The official Dalseong-gun blog
suggests, rather than looking for the “right answer” with kids,
to ask, “What does this artwork look like to you?“
and have a conversation.

The same blog also recommends
Dalseong Wetlands as a place to walk after the festival.

🔑 KICKOutdoor exhibits are open until 22:00,
but indoor exhibition hours are not listed.
The ARC Cultural Center is open
10:00–18:00 (per Daegu tourism).
If you want to see indoors, visit during the day.
