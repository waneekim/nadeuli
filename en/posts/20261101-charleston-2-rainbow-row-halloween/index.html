Charleston Part 2: Rainbow Row and a Halloween Walk Through a Wealthy Neighborhood, the Listing Prices Are Wild! (US Business Trip, Day 7)

After the pineapple fountain,
this time we walked around Rainbow Row 🌈

It’s pretty and charming,
but it’s a wealthy neighborhood.
It happened to be Halloween,
so there was a lot to see.

Rainbow Row — pink, purple and mint-colored houses in a row

📍 Around Rainbow Row
(Charleston, South Carolina, USA)
🌈 A street lined with 13 colorful houses
🏠 Real estate window listings up to $12,000,000
🎃 Halloween day: people in costume, decorated houses
🌳 A quiet, peaceful neighborhood

🏠 Jaw-Dropping Listings in the Real Estate Window

In a real estate window by the street,
listing ads were posted,
and the prices were jaw-dropping.

The most expensive listing on the bottom row
was $12,000,000!
It’s a house with 5 bedrooms and about 608 m² (about 184 pyeong; 1 pyeong is about 3.3 m²).
In Korean money, that’s well over 10 billion won.
Next to it, houses listed at $7,250,000
and $3,495,000 sat side by side.

Listing ads in the real estate window — bottom-left is $12,000,000

🎃 Halloween Day, People in Costume

It was Halloween day, October 31,
so there were people in costume on the street.

Someone in old-fashioned clothes,
and even someone in a chicken costume!

People in costume on a balcony — old-fashioned clothes and a chicken costume (faces blurred with mosaic)

🌈 Colorful Rainbow Row

Rainbow Row is a stretch of 13 colorful houses
in a row on East Bay St.

The old houses were renovated in the 1930s–40s
and painted in pastel colors,
and that’s apparently how it got its name.

A quiet lane with pointed trees — water visible at the end

A sidewalk in the shade of trees — a skull decoration peeking out on the pink wall

🏛️ Halloween Decorations on Every House

On the street lined with big houses,
every house had Halloween decorations.

Pumpkins piled up on the whole staircase,
a doll on a swing, skeletons,
even scary characters guarding the door!

A street lined with big houses — a skeleton decoration on the balcony of the house on the left

A house with pumpkins piled up on the whole staircase and a doll on a swing

Halloween decorations on a big house with columns — an 'I SMELL CHILDREN' sign at the door

KICKSince it was Halloween,
looking at the decorations
on every house was a real treat! 🎃

🌳 A Quiet, Peaceful Wealthy Neighborhood

The big house with tall columns was under renovation work on an old house.

It’s pretty and charming,
but it’s definitely a wealthy neighborhood.
And yet it was quiet and peaceful.

A big house with tall white columns — under renovation work on an old house

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone who wants photos
of colorful old houses
✔ Anyone curious about
the feel of a wealthy US neighborhood
✔ Anyone who likes looking at
Halloween decorations

It was a pretty, charming,
quiet, peaceful wealthy neighborhood!
