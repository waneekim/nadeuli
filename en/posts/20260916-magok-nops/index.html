NOPS Steakhouse in Magok, Seoul — Tomahawk and L-Bone Steak Lunch for Six, and Bread That Was a Work of Art

Our steak lunch in Magok 🥩

Six of us went at lunchtime
to a steakhouse
called NOPS (놉스).

Bone-in steak, grilled and sliced — piled onto a cast-iron oval platter

📍 105-7 Magokjungang-ro
Gangseo-gu, Seoul
K-Square Magok, B2
⏰ Daily 11:00–21:00
Weekday break 16:00–17:00
Last order 20:00
💰 About 300,000 won for 6
(we ordered the lunch special)
🅿 Parking: first 90 min free
👥 Groups and reservations OK
📞 0507-1356-1655

🍽 What the six of us ordered

It’s a steakhouse,
so steak was the main event, of course!

A tomahawk steak
and an L-bone steak, 1.5 kg,

plus myeongnan (pollock roe) gambas pasta
and truffle fettuccine on the side.

For dessert,
ice sweet potato and tiramisu.

We ordered the lunch special,
and for six it came to about 300,000 won.

🥖 The bread was a work of art

Before the steak,
I have to tell you about the bread.

Three rolls on a wooden board
and a pat of herb-flecked butter.

Three rolls with herb butter — the first thing that stuck with us here

The crust was so crisp,
it was truly a work of art!

This bread kept us going
until the steak arrived.

🥩 Served sliced on a cast-iron platter

The steak is grilled on the bone,
sliced in advance
and served on a cast-iron oval platter.

The gravy comes
separately in a sauce boat.

The sliced steak — with tomato-onion salad and mashed potatoes alongside

🍟 A table full of sides

The sides come out with it.

Fries with cheese dip
on newspaper-print paper,
creamed spinach and mashed potatoes.

Fries with cheese dip; creamed spinach and gravy in the back

And even a salad
of tomato and onion tossed in sauce!

The space around the steak
filled up in no time.

Tomato-onion salad — something fresh between bites of steak

😅 But there wasn’t enough

For six of us, though,
there wasn’t enough steak.

Even the tomahawk
plus the 1.5 kg L-bone,
split among six adults,
doesn’t give anyone a generous share.

It was really the two plates of pasta
that filled us up.

🔑 KICKFor six people,
two steaks aren't enough
With a big group,
add one more steak!

🍠 Two desserts

For dessert, ice sweet potato
and tiramisu.

A roasted sweet potato, cut in half,
topped with ice cream
and drizzled with caramel sauce.

Ice sweet potato — ice cream on a roasted sweet potato, with caramel sauce and a mint leaf

The tiramisu comes
with the name NOPS
stenciled on top in powdered sugar.

Tiramisu — NOPS stenciled over the cocoa

📋 Menu and prices

Lunch Combo (T-bone) 189,900 won
Lunch Combo (Porterhouse) 241,900 won
(soup, salad, pasta, sides and dessert)

Benefit Steak 38,900 won
Choice Lunch Course 29,000 won
(per person, book in advance)

(as listed on Naver Place)

The tomahawk and L-bone
lunch special we ordered
isn’t on Naver Place under that name now.
Please check the menu before you order.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone who loves
crusty bread before a meal
✔ Groups planning a steak lunch
(order plenty!)
✔ Anyone who wants dessert
in the same sitting

From the bread to dessert,
a meal that leaves you full —
that’s this Magok steakhouse.

But if there are six of you,
order plenty of steak!
