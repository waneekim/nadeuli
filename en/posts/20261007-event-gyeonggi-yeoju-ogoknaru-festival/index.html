Yeoju Ogoknaru Festival 2026 at Silleuksa — Dates, Fireworks Times, Parking & Tips for Kids

Along the Namhangang River,
autumn is deepening 🍂

The 2026 Yeoju Ogoknaru Festival
(2026 여주오곡나루축제)
runs Fri, Oct 23 – Sun, Oct 25,
three days, daily from 10:00 to 21:00,
around the Silleuksa Temple tourist area in Yeoju.

The Ministry of Culture’s regional festival listing
says it’s free.

We put together the 2026 schedule
from the festival website
run by the Yeoju Sejong Culture & Tourism Foundation.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

The festival tells the story of Jopo Naru (조포나루),
one of the four great ferry landings of the Joseon era,
where Yeoju’s farm produce
was sent as tribute to the king.

The grounds are split into three areas:
Naru Madang (ferry yard), Ogok Madang (five-grain yard)
and Janchi Madang (feast yard).

This year, two night programs —
‘Naru Nanjang’ (a ferry-side night party)
and ‘Yeogang Goedam’ (Yeogang River ghost stories) —
are making their debut.

There’s also tteokme-chigi (pounding rice cake with a mallet)
with injeolmi and sikhye,
and the Dalbit Naru photo zone
with a night view of the suspension bridge.

The bibimbap is only described as ‘affordable’;
the price isn’t in the official info yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The straw playground and Joseon playground
are open until 18:00.
Hit the playgrounds first in the daytime,
catch the sweet potato ‘Guinness’ roast at 17:00,
then carry on into the night programs.

Yeogang Goedam is a horror experience,
so give easily scared kids a heads-up.

🔑 KICKFireworks: the 23rd at 20:30, the 25th at 20:00
The 24th has the falling-fire show at 19:00
