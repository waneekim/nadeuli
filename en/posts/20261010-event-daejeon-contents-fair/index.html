2026 Daejeon Contents Fair Dates, Free Admission, Parking – DCC Characters & Games Before You Go with Kids

This autumn, characters, games and cosplay
gather at Daejeon Convention Center 🎮

The 2026 Daejeon Contents Fair (대전콘텐츠페어)
runs from October 16 (Fri) to 18 (Sun)
for three days at Daejeon Convention Center (DCC) Exhibition Hall 2.

Admission is free.
With online pre-registration, you can enter every day of the three days.

I’ve summarized the official Daejeon Contents Fair website
and the Daejeon Tourism Organization press release (October 7).

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What kind of event is it?

It’s a content exhibition built on three themes:
public characters, gamification and subculture.
The website describes it as “a content exhibition everyone can enjoy together,
regardless of age or taste.”

It’s hosted by Daejeon Metropolitan City
and organized by Daejeon Tourism Organization and Daejeon Information & Culture Industry Promotion Agency.

Industry programs such as investment and business meetings
also take place,
so below I picked only what families might enjoy.

🎟️ How do I get in?

TypeOfficial info
Online pre-registrationFree entry every day, 16th–18th
On-site registrationAvailable · one entry per day, no re-entry
Admission tableGeneral · elementary/middle/high school · university (graduate) students · 65+ · people with disabilities · national merit recipients: free
16th (Fri) · 17th (Sat)10:00–18:00
18th (Sun)10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00)

Visitors aged 65+, people with disabilities and national merit recipients
need proof (disability card, ID, etc.).
Outside food, smoking, and pets other than guide dogs are not allowed.

🧸 Things to do with kids on all three days

These are listed on the website’s program pages
as running every day, 16th–18th.

ActivityOfficial info
Cube Builder PlaygroundA family and kids’ play space for stacking magnetic cube blocks
Games for EveryoneFree play of serious games in education, healthcare, defense and barrier-free · collect stamps for a souvenir
Living Character HallTurn a public character or your own character into goods
Minecraft block experience16th–18th (times not on the website)
AI Art School · Cosplay experience zone16th–18th
Da-da-da! Nerf gun rangeHit targets with a rapid-fire Nerf gun · during event hours
Photo dioramaFree photos in front of a 3D diorama and large cosplay figures

At “Games for Everyone,” you play a game
and leave a star rating by QR code.
The website says it’s recommended for the whole family, kids to adults.

The Public Character Promotion Hall is also open all three days,
with the Kkumssi Family showroom and goods market.

🗓️ What’s on each day?

DateProgram (official website)
17th (Sat)Cosplay Runway on the special stage · 15–20 teams compete
17th (Sat) 13:00Brawl Stars esports tournament (players aged 13+, teams of 3)
17th (Sat) 14:00–15:00Influencer “Neo” signing · first 100 people
17th (Sat) · 18th (Sun)B-Sports Zone (Yu-Gi-Oh! · Nibel Arena)
18th (Sun) 15:00–16:00Daejeon Anime Song Contest finals · singing game and anime OSTs

For the Brawl Stars tournament date,
the notice (September 28) says the 17th at 13:00,
while the program table on the website’s front page says the 18th.
This article follows the notice.

For the costume parade, the website says
”details to be confirmed later.”

📸 Itasha car exhibition

Itasha are custom cars
wrapped with large images of favorite characters.

30 itasha cars stand in the indoor and outdoor exhibition zones
all three days.
The website introduces them as a photo zone for great shots.

🚗 Getting there · Parking

DCC parking is free on weekends

The address is 87 Expo-ro, Yuseong-gu, Daejeon (대전 유성구 엑스포로 87),
Daejeon Convention Center Exhibition Hall 2.
These are the fees from the website’s “Transport & Parking” section.

Parking lotFee
DCC (Halls 1 & 2) weekendsFree
DCC weekdaysFirst hour free · 500 won per 30 min · max 10,000 won per day
Tongilgwan outdoor lot (Expo Science Park) weekdaysFirst hour free · 500 won per 30 min · max 5,000 won per day
Discounts50% for national merit recipients, disabled drivers, compact and hybrid cars

At the DCC lot, entry and exit are restricted from 22:00 to 7:00.
The website has no public transit directions.

👨‍👩‍👧 If you’re going with kids

On the weekend of the 17th & 18th, the 2026 Daejeon Bread Festival
is held right next door around Hanbit Tower in Expo Science Park.
The Daejeon Tourism Organization notice lists its hours as 13:00–20:30.

Try the hall activities in the morning
and continue to the bread festival in the afternoon.

🔑 KICKThe 18th (Sun) ends at 17:00
Last entry is 16:00
On-site registration allows one entry per day

✔ Kids who love block and game activities
✔ Families who want to see cosplay and character goods
✔ Families combining it with the bread festival on the weekend

an exhibition to see characters, games and cosplay for free.
