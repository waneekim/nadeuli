2026 Geumbyeokjeong Pungnyu ‘Oh My Gat Party’ Dates & Show Times – Before Visiting with Kids in Sejong

A cool autumn afternoon
is perfect for resting in a pavilion 🍂

At Geumbyeokjeong Pavilion in Janggun-myeon, Sejong,
Geumbyeokjeong Pungnyu ‘Oh My Gat Party’
(금벽정 풍류 오 마이 갓 파티) runs
October 24 (Sat)–25 (Sun)
for two days.

Both days run 13:00~19:00.

I rechecked the Sejong Cultural Foundation notice (October 6)
and Sejong City’s ‘October Sejong Night Out Week’ guide
on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Event Is It?

The theme on the poster is
’The pungnyu and healing of Confucian culture’.
(Pungnyu means refined, leisurely enjoyment of art and nature.)

The foundation describes it as
’a time to rest while gazing at Cheongbyeok,
enjoy music and performances,
and meet the local flavors and charms.’

The program has three parts:
Seonbi Pungnyu Reflection · Oh My Gat Party · Pungnyu Market

It is also on the main program list
of Sejong City’s ‘October Sejong Night Out Week’,
held from October 21 to November 1.

🍵 What Is Seonbi Pungnyu Reflection?

A program for slowing down
while taking in the scenery around Geumbyeokjeong.

ProgramDetails
Tea timeSit in the Geumbyeokjeong pavilion and enjoy tea while gazing at Cheongbyeok
Music listeningPut on headphones and enjoy the view with music of your choice
Coloring & calligraphyTry coloring and brush writing yourself
Folk gamesJegichagi (shuttlecock kicking), spinning tops, biseokchigi (stone tossing), gonggi (jacks), ddakji
Eoulling neighborhood rideRide an Eoulling bike around Geumbyeokjeong
Barefoot walkWalk on the white sand (백사장) and feel nature

Sejong City’s leaflet lists music listening as ‘LP listening’
and coloring as ‘coloring book’.

Eoulling is the name of Sejong City’s public bike service.
The notice doesn’t say whether bikes are lent at the event
or whether there is a fee.

🎶 When Are the Performances?

Residents and local artists perform
against the autumn scenery of Geumbyeokjeong.

Date & TimeStage
10/24 (Sat) 13:00Opening stage (residents’ performance & opening ceremony)
10/24 (Sat) 16:00·17:00·18:00Local artist performances
10/25 (Sun) 14:00·16:00·18:00Local artist performances

The names of the performing artists
are not in the notice yet.

🛍️ What’s at the Pungnyu Market?

MarketDetails
Residents’ food stallsFood prepared by local residents
Brewery tasting & salesDrinks from breweries in Janggun-myeon
Nearby shops marketA market with local shops

The brewery tasting and sales involve alcohol,
so that part is for grown-ups.
With kids, you can browse
the food stalls and the shops market.

🚗 Getting There & Parking

No parking info yet

Geumbyeokjeong is in Janggun-myeon, Sejong.
Its Naver Place address is
342-9 Geumam-ri, Janggun-myeon (장군면 금암리 342-9).

Neither the foundation notice nor the city guide
has any parking or public transport information.
It’s a good idea to ask the foundation before you set out.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

With kids, head to the folk games first.
Jegichagi, spinning tops, biseokchigi,
gonggi and ddakji are listed in the notice.

There are also sit-down coloring and calligraphy,
and a barefoot walk on the sand.

Performances are at 16:00, 17:00 and 18:00 on Saturday
and 14:00, 16:00 and 18:00 on Sunday.
Play the games, then move over
in time for a show.

🔑 KICKBoth days end at 19:00
The last performance is at 18:00
There's no parking info, so ask the foundation before you go
