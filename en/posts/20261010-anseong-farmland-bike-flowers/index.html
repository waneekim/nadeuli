Anseong Farmland Electric Bike Tips: Tickets First, Then a 30-Minute Cosmos & Pink Muhly Ride

The first thing to do after entering?
Buy electric bike tickets!

We visited Anseong Farmland.
This first part of our story goes
from the entrance and the electric bikes
to the cosmos and pink muhly fields.

The animals will come
in the next part.

A cosmos hill spreading out beyond the pink muhly

📍 Anseong Farmland · Gongdo-eup, Anseong
🚲 Buy electric bike tickets right after entering
⏳ About a 1-hour wait if you're late · we waited 30 minutes
💰 Large 20,000 won · Small 14,000 won
🌸 30-minute route: cosmos field → pink muhly

🎟️ Buy electric bike tickets first

Once you’re inside the farm,
the first thing to do is
buy tickets for the electric bikes.

The electric bikes stay popular to the end,
so the wait gets longer as time goes on.
If you go late, plan on about an hour’s wait.

We also waited a good 30 minutes.

The Experience Farm (체험목장) WELCOME entrance

A queue seen through the lattice · only the lower part, so no faces show

🔑 KICKRight after you enter,
buy electric bike tickets first!
The later it gets, the longer the wait.

🚲 Large or small? How we chose

The electric bikes come in small and large.
The signs where you line up
showed how many can ride and the fare.

Large bike queue · 3 adults and 2 preschoolers, 20,000 won

Small bike queue · 2 adults and 2 preschoolers, 14,000 won

Here is what the signs say, as a table.

TypeRiders on the signFare
Large3 adults · 2 preschoolers20,000 won
Small2 adults · 2 preschoolers14,000 won

If you have two children
and at least one is school-age,
the large bike is the better choice.

🌸 30-minute route: straight to the cosmos, then pink muhly

Once you have tickets,
ride straight to the flower fields!

You get 30 minutes on the bike,
which is shorter than you’d think.

The English paper map · No. 19 is the ticket office for electric bikes and rides; the cosmos fields and pink muhly hill are on the right

Go straight to the cosmos field and look around,
then take photos at the pink muhly field.

Even just that
made 30 minutes really tight.

A dirt path through the cosmos field

The cosmos field
was truly vast.

🩷 Pink muhly, fluffy like cotton

After the cosmos field,
we went on to the pink muhly field.

The pink muhly was
soft and fluffy, like cotton.

Pink muhly up close

Compared with the pink muhly in Jeju,
the color seemed a little deeper.

A pink muhly field under the open sky

A lone pine at the end of a path through the pink muhly

🅿️ No reverse, so park with care!

The electric bikes can’t go in reverse.
So when you stop by the flower fields,
you need to park carefully.

So you can turn and ride out to the side,
think about the direction before you park.

🐾 Animals later, on foot

Spend the 30 bike minutes on the flowers,
and see the animals later on foot,
feeding them as you walk.

We’ll cover the animals
separately in the next part.
