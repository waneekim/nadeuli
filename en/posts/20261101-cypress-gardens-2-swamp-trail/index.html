🎃 The Lawn Greeted Us with Halloween Decorations

Past the gift shop,
a wide lawn opens up.

With Halloween coming up,
there was a skeleton crawling out of the ground,
a black cat in a BOO hat,
and even a tree balloon with a scary face!

Halloween decorations on the lawn — a skeleton, a pumpkin-headed skeleton and a BOO cat

🌳 The Dreamlike Swamp Trail

In the middle of the garden
is a black-water swamp.

The trail winds between
bald cypress trees
growing straight up out of the water.

The trail and a bench along the swamp — bald cypress roots at the water's edge

The bald cypress swamp in the sunlight

The trees were reflected on the water
and sunlight came down
through the branches,
so the whole walk was healing.

Bald cypress trees turned orange, and the swamp covered with leaves

I heard the swamp here
is 80 acres (about 320,000 m²),
and the trail is 4.5 miles (about 7 km).
I also heard you can look around
by boat.

🎬 Like the Lead in “The Notebook”

As we walked,
it felt like we were
the leads in “The Notebook.”

I heard that Cypress Gardens
is a place where more than 16 films and series
were shot,
including “The Notebook.”

🦇 The Bat Hotel and the Fairy House

On the walk,
we saw a snake and a squirrel. 🐍🐿️

On a lawn in the woods,

CYPRESS GARDEN BAT HOTEL — a bat house put up by the Berkeley Soil and Water Conservation District

Inside a hollow of a big tree,

A fairy house with a green door in a tree hollow
