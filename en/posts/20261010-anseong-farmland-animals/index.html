🐶 Dog tricks and an animal parade

The first thing we saw at the farm
was dogs doing tricks
and a line of different animals passing by.

Animals passing in a line across the grass show field

Matching our photo times to the official event timetable,
it was the weekend 11:30 Farm Animal Play Show (가축놀이한마당) slot.

Here are the weekend and holiday shows on the official timetable.

TimeWeekend & holiday showMap no.
11:30Farm Animal Play Show (가축놀이한마당)25
13:00Farm Animal Talent Show (가축장기자랑)27
14:00Farm Animal Parade (가축퍼레이드)26
14:30Farm Animal Play Show (가축놀이한마당)25
15:30Sheep Herding (양몰이)39
16:00Mini Games (설렘 한가득 미니게임)21
16:30Farm Animal Play Show (가축놀이한마당)25

On weekdays there is the Farm Animal Play Show (11:30 · 13:30)
and Little Animal Explorer (꼬마 동물 탐구생활, 13:00 · 15:00).

The timetable notes that shows may change or be cancelled
depending on the weather and the animals’ health.
It has applied since September 14, 2026,
and we checked it on the official website on October 11.

After the show,
we fed the baby animals.

Feeding a guinea pig with chopsticks

On the official fee page, feeding costs
2,000 won per serving.

FeedAmountPrice
Hay1 basket2,000 won
Pig feed1 basket2,000 won
Rabbit feed1 plate2,000 won
Horse feed (carrot sticks)42,000 won
Bird seed1 bag1,000 won

The official Rabbit Village (토끼마을) page says
rabbit feed can be paid by card only.

You can only give feed bought inside the farm.
The farm says outside feed such as carrots or hay
cannot be brought in.

🐐 The animal talent show

Next,
we watched the animal talent show.

A goat came out on an indoor stage.

A goat and a staff member on the indoor stage · the staff member's face is covered with a sticker

A goat crouching down in front of the staff member

Its name on the official timetable is the Farm Animal Talent Show (가축장기자랑).
Weekends and holidays at 13:00, map no. 27,
and our photo times matched that slot too.

🥕 Horse feed is a carrot stick

Next, at the stable,
we fed the horses.

Horse feed is a carrot stick:
pieces of carrot on a wooden stick.

Holding out a carrot stick to a horse in its stall

A horse munching on a carrot

A black pony in front of the HORSE HOUSE and the carrot feeding sign

These are the carrot feeding prices on the sign.

PriceCarrots
2,000 won4
3,000 won6
5,000 won10+2
10,000 won20+4

The official fee page also lists horse feed
as 4 carrot sticks for 2,000 won,
sold at Cafe Sosweet (카페 쏘스윗) and Farm Picnic (팜피크닉).
It says on weekends and holidays the riding arena sells them too.

🔑 KICKHold the carrot stick tight!
If a horse eats the stick, it gets hurt.
Don't let go while you feed it.

That is what the caution notes on the sign say.
The sign also asks parents
to feed together with young children.

🐴 Horse riding in the indoor arena

After feeding the horses,
we went horse riding.

In the indoor arena,

Here is the horse riding experience, per the official guide.

ItemOfficial guide
Price8,000 won per person
Course3 laps of a set indoor track · big or small horse
Who can ride36 months and older · 90 cm tall or more
Who cannot rideAnyone in a skirt · adults 90 kg or more
Upper age limitUp to 60 (fee page) · 58 in full years (Riding Center fee page)
HoursHorse riding until 17:30 · break 12:00–13:00
Mini horse riding8,000 won · preschoolers only

You can’t ride if you’re wearing a skirt.
The upper age limit differs from page to page
even on the official website,
so please check again at the riding arena.

On the arena wall
there was also a sign saying
”Horses can be easily startled.”

🐄 Last of all, feeding cows and pigs

Last of all,
we fed the cows and pigs.

And that wrapped up our whole farm day.

A tan cow on a lead rope · faces of visitors in the back are covered with a sticker

According to the official guide, at the Experience Farm (체험목장)
you can try feeding activities
such as hay, pig feed and bird seed.
Hay and pig feed are 2,000 won per basket.
