Hangang Festival Autumn 2026 (Seoul) — Dates, Parking & Weekend-by-Weekend Programs at Jamwon, Gangseo and Ttukseom

It’s autumn outing season
at the Hangang parks 🍂

Hangang Festival – Autumn 2026
(2026 한강페스티벌_가을)
runs October 17 (Sat) – 25 (Sun)
on the Han River itself
and in seven Hangang parks.

Jamwon, Gangseo and Ttukseom
are the main venues, and
each park has its own dates and programs.

We put this together from
the festival’s official website
and its leaflet (September).

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It’s the autumn edition
of the Hangang Festival,
Seoul’s festival for all four seasons.

This year’s theme is
’Hup-two, hup-two Hangang —
taking care of body and mind.’

Eleven programs run
in three strands —
challenge, culture and rest —
and times and fees vary by program.

Let's sort it by park and weekend
🗓 First weekend: Oct 17 (Sat) – 18 (Sun)
Jamwon · workouts, cooking, free shows
Jamwon · Hangang Silent Yoga (17th)
Jamsil · Sagak Sagak Art Festival (17th)
🗓 Second weekend: Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun)
Ttukseom · young chefs' food contest
Gangseo · kids' forest playground (24th–25th)
Banpo · Hangang Silent Yoga (25th)

🏃 First weekend: what’s on at Jamwon?

October 17 (Sat) – 18 (Sun),
at the multipurpose sports field
in Jamwon Hangang Park
(잠원한강공원).

O.Un.Han — 5,000 won per class
(‘Today’s workout, done at the Hangang’)
Rhythm training 10:00–11:00
POUND fit 12:00–13:00
Sliding board 14:00–15:00

Hangang Hankki 16:00–17:30
(‘a meal at the Hangang’)
a meal you make without fire, 5,000 won

ON Stage 17:30–19:00
free — just turn up.

The stage opens with
the children’s musical team
’Iruda Dream’ (이루다 드림).

After a team of
Dankook University musical theatre students,
a musical-star gala starts at 18:15 —
Seo Beom-seok (서범석) on the 17th
and Lisa (리사) on the 18th.

October 17 (Sat)
Hangang Silent Yoga on the Jamwon lawn
15:30–18:00, 9,000 won
Sagak Sagak Art Festival at Jamsil
13:00–20:00

The leaflet’s page
’Things to enjoy in the Hangang parks in October’
also lists the Hangang Family Festival
at Gwangnaru the same weekend
(13:00–20:00, free).

🍜 Second weekend: Ttukseom and Gangseo

The Road Chef Hangang
(더 로드셰프 한강)
Ttukseom Hangang Park swimming pool
23rd (Fri) 15:00–21:00
(the leaflet says it starts at 17:00)
24th (Sat) 13:00–21:00
25th (Sun) 13:00–20:00

A contest where 20 teams of young chefs
compete by selling their food.
Entry is free; you pay for the food.
The final awards ceremony
is at 19:00 on the 25th.

Hangang Forest Adventure
(한강 포레스트 어드벤처)
next to the children’s playground
in Gangseo Hangang Park
24th (Sat) – 25th (Sun)
13:00–17:00, free

It’s a forest playground for children,
with monkey climbing, a zipline,
rope play
and even tree climbing.

Just turn up — only the activity zone
’Autumn Forest Workshop’ (가을 숲 공작소)
needs booking ahead
(400 people a day, until 17:00 on the 21st).

October 25 (Sun)
Hangang Silent Yoga at the Banpo picnic area
session 2, 15:30–18:00

All festival long (17th–25th)
Hangang Autumn Eco Classes
at six spots, incl. Gangseo Wetland Ecological Park
free, booking required.

On the water (paid):
yachts, kayaks and Tubesters
at Sebitseom (세빛섬), Banpo,
and the Hi Seoul Cruise at Yeouido.

The cruise has a seagull-feeding sailing
at 16:00 on the 17th, 18th, 24th and 25th.
Festival discount price:
adults 22,500 won,
children 15,300 won.

🚇 Getting there and parking

They recommend public transit on weekends

The festival offers no parking support.

Jamwon Jamwon Station (Line 3), exit 4
about 15 min on foot
(or Sinsa Station exit 5, 15–20 min)

Gangseo Banghwa Station (Line 5), exit 2
then village bus Gangseo 07 to the last stop
(about 20 min on foot from
Magongnaru Station, Line 9 / AREX)

Ttukseom Jayang Station (Line 7)
exits 2 & 3 lead straight into the park.

By Hangang Bus (the river bus):
Apgujeong pier for Jamwon,
Magok pier for Gangseo,
and Ttukseom pier.

Hangang park car parks
1,000 won for the first 30 min
then 200 won per 10 min
max 10,000 won a day

Jamwon 722 spaces · Gangseo 99
Ttukseom 674
50% off with Seoul’s Dadungi Happiness Card
(for families with several children).

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Check the age rules first.

Rhythm training & POUND fit: age 6+
Sliding board: age 12+
Hangang Hankki has no age limit,
and kids 12 and under
come with a guardian.

At Forest Adventure,
monkey climbing & the zipline are 6+
and tree climbing is 9+.
Silent yoga is for ages 8 and up
(no preschoolers).

At Jamwon, Hangang Hankki (16:00)
flows straight into ON Stage (17:30)
on the same field.

Forest Adventure has no changing rooms
and only 20 hammocks and mats,
so the organizers ask you
to bring your own mat or tent.

🔑 KICKBooking for O.Un.Han & Hangang Hankki
closes Oct 14 (Wed) at 17:00
Leftover spots are posted Oct 15 (Thu) 17:00
on the Urban Sports (어반스포츠) website

☔ What if it rains?

ON Stage is cancelled
if rain is forecast
12 hours before it starts.

Silent yoga and the Sagak Sagak Art Festival
are also cancelled in the rain,
and Forest Adventure
may be postponed or cancelled.

The Road Chef goes ahead in the rain
and is only cancelled
for heavy rain or a typhoon.

O.Un.Han and Hangang Hankki
may also be postponed or cancelled,
with a full refund if cancelled.
