Hangang Festival Autumn 2026 (Seoul) — Dates, Parking & Weekend-by-Weekend Programs at Jamwon, Gangseo and Ttukseom

It’s autumn outing season
at the Hangang parks 🍂

Hangang Festival – Autumn 2026
(2026 한강페스티벌_가을)
runs October 17 (Sat) – 25 (Sun)
on the Han River itself
and in seven Hangang parks.

Jamwon, Gangseo and Ttukseom
are the main venues, and
each park has its own dates and programs.

We put this together from
the festival’s official website
and its leaflet (September).

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It’s the autumn edition
of the Hangang Festival,
Seoul’s festival for all four seasons.

This year’s theme is
’Hup-two, hup-two Hangang —
taking care of body and mind.’

Eleven programs run
in three strands —
challenge, culture and rest —
and times and fees vary by program.

Let's sort it by park and weekend
🗓 First weekend: Oct 17 (Sat) – 18 (Sun)
Jamwon · workouts, cooking, free shows
Jamwon · Hangang Silent Yoga (17th)
Jamsil · Sagak Sagak Art Festival (17th)
🗓 Second weekend: Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun)
Ttukseom · young chefs' food contest
Gangseo · kids' forest playground (24th–25th)
Banpo · Hangang Silent Yoga (25th)

🏃 First weekend: what’s on at Jamwon?

October 17 (Sat) – 18 (Sun),
at the multipurpose sports field
in Jamwon Hangang Park
(잠원한강공원).

O.Un.Han stands for
’Today’s workout, done at the Hangang’.
Here’s everything in time order.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 17 & 18, 10:00–11:00O.Un.Han rhythm training — 5,000 won
Oct 17 & 18, 12:00–13:00O.Un.Han POUND fit — 5,000 won
Oct 17 & 18, 14:00–15:00O.Un.Han sliding board — 5,000 won
Oct 17 & 18, 16:00–17:30Hangang Hankki (‘a meal at the Hangang’) — a meal you make without fire, 5,000 won
Oct 17 & 18, 17:30–19:00ON Stage — free, just turn up
Oct 17, from 18:15Musical-star gala — Seo Beom-seok (서범석)
Oct 18, from 18:15Musical-star gala — Lisa (리사)

The ON Stage opens with the children’s musical team
’Iruda Dream’ (이루다 드림).
After a team of Dankook University musical theatre students
comes the musical-star gala.

A few things also run elsewhere
the same weekend.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 17 (Sat) 13:00–20:00Sagak Sagak Art Festival — Jamsil
Oct 17 (Sat) 15:30–18:00Hangang Silent Yoga — Jamwon lawn, 9,000 won
Same weekend 13:00–20:00Hangang Family Festival — Gwangnaru, free (from the leaflet’s page ‘Things to enjoy in the Hangang parks in October’)

🍜 Second weekend: Ttukseom and Gangseo

Here’s the second weekend,
plus what runs all festival long,
by date and time.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 23 (Fri) 15:00–21:00The Road Chef Hangang (더 로드셰프 한강) — Ttukseom Hangang Park swimming pool (the leaflet says it starts at 17:00)
Oct 24 (Sat) 13:00–21:00The Road Chef Hangang
Oct 25 (Sun) 13:00–20:00The Road Chef Hangang — final awards ceremony at 19:00
Oct 24 (Sat) – 25 (Sun) 13:00–17:00Hangang Forest Adventure (한강 포레스트 어드벤처) — next to the children’s playground in Gangseo Hangang Park, free
Oct 25 (Sun) 15:30–18:00Hangang Silent Yoga session 2 — Banpo picnic area
All festival long (Oct 17–25)Hangang Autumn Eco Classes — six spots incl. Gangseo Wetland Ecological Park, free, booking required
Oct 17, 18, 24 & 25, 16:00Hi Seoul Cruise seagull-feeding sailing — festival discount price: adults 22,500 won · children 15,300 won

The Road Chef is a contest where 20 teams of young chefs
compete by selling their food.
Entry is free; you pay for the food.

Forest Adventure is a forest playground for children,
with monkey climbing, a zipline,
rope play
and even tree climbing.

Just turn up — only the activity zone
’Autumn Forest Workshop’ (가을 숲 공작소)
needs booking ahead
(400 people a day, until 17:00 on the 21st).

On the water (paid):
yachts, kayaks and Tubesters
at Sebitseom (세빛섬), Banpo,
and the Hi Seoul Cruise at Yeouido.

🚇 Getting there and parking

They recommend public transit on weekends

The festival offers no parking support.

How to get to each park:

ParkGetting there
JamwonJamwon Station (Line 3), exit 4, about 15 min on foot (or Sinsa Station exit 5, 15–20 min) · Hangang Bus (the river bus): Apgujeong pier
GangseoBanghwa Station (Line 5), exit 2 → village bus Gangseo 07 to the last stop (about 20 min on foot from Magongnaru Station, Line 9 / AREX) · Hangang Bus: Magok pier
TtukseomJayang Station (Line 7), exits 2 & 3 lead straight into the park · Hangang Bus: Ttukseom pier

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Check the age rules first.

ActivityAge rule
Rhythm training & POUND fitage 6+
Sliding boardage 12+
Hangang Hankkino age limit; kids 12 and under come with a guardian
Forest Adventure monkey climbing & ziplineage 6+
Forest Adventure tree climbingage 9+
Hangang Silent Yogaages 8 and up (no preschoolers)

At Jamwon, Hangang Hankki (16:00)
flows straight into ON Stage (17:30)
on the same field.

Forest Adventure has no changing rooms
and only 20 hammocks and mats,
so the organizers ask you
to bring your own mat or tent.

🔑 KICKBooking for O.Un.Han & Hangang Hankki
closes Oct 14 (Wed) at 17:00
Leftover spots are posted Oct 15 (Thu) 17:00
on the Urban Sports (어반스포츠) website

☔ What if it rains?

ON Stage is cancelled
if rain is forecast
12 hours before it starts.

Silent yoga and the Sagak Sagak Art Festival
are also cancelled in the rain,
and Forest Adventure
may be postponed or cancelled.

The Road Chef goes ahead in the rain
and is only cancelled
for heavy rain or a typhoon.

O.Un.Han and Hangang Hankki
may also be postponed or cancelled,
with a full refund if cancelled.

👍 Recommended for families who…

✔ have kids aged 6+ and are looking for
workouts or forest play
✔ want to catch a free evening musical stage
(Jamwon, 17th–18th)
✔ want to watch young chefs
battle it out with food (Ttukseom, 23rd–25th)

Jamwon is the first weekend,
Gangseo and Ttukseom the second.
Start by picking
the dates of the park you want.

Check the official notice once more before you go!
