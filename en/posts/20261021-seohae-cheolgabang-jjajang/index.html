Taepungeun Bureodo Cheolgabangeun Ganda, a Chinese Restaurant near Seohae-gu Office, Incheon — Chewy-Noodle Jjajangmyeon, Jjajangmyeon + Tangsuyuk Set for 21,000 Won

A review of a Chinese restaurant near Seohae-gu Office, Incheon 🥢

On a Wednesday at lunchtime,
we had an errand at the district office
and looked for a good place nearby,
so we went to
태풍은 불어도 철가방은 간다
(Taepungeun Bureodo Cheolgabangeun Ganda).

What we really liked was
the jjajangmyeon with chewy noodles.

Chewy-noodle jjajangmyeon, sprinkled with sesame seeds

🍜 Jjajangmyeon with Chewy Noodles

What we really liked here was
the jjajangmyeon.

The noodles were really chewy.

It was better than we expected,
a little tastier than
your average jjajangmyeon place.

Sesame seeds on top of the black bean sauce.
The pickled radish and onions are self-service.

🍖 A Generous Portion of Tangsuyuk

We ordered tangsuyuk too
(Korean-style sweet-and-sour pork).

The sauce isn’t poured on top.
It comes in a separate bowl.

Fried dumplings
came on the same plate.

Tangsuyuk and fried dumplings, with the sauce in the bowl at the back

The sauce comes in a separate bowl (GIF)

This is what it looks like
once you pour the sauce on.

Vegetables like onion and carrot
go on top with the sauce.

Tangsuyuk with the sauce poured on

A piece of tangsuyuk with the sauce on (GIF)

The portion was bigger than we expected.

We wanted to finish it all,
but we ended up leaving a little.
