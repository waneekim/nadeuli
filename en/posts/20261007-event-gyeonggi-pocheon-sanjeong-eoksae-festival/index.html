Myeongseongsan Silver Grass Festival 2026 at Sanjeong Lake (Pocheon) — Schedule, Lake Fireworks Times, Parking & Tips for Kids

It’s autumn, and Myeongseongsan’s silver grass
is turning a shimmering silver 🌾

This is the 29th edition!
The 29th Pocheon Sanjeong Lake Myeongseongsan Silver Grass Festival
(제29회 포천 산정호수 명성산 억새꽃축제 — “eoksae” means silver grass)
runs Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, Oct 18,
three days, around Sanjeong Lake (산정호수)
and Myeongseongsan Mountain (명성산).

We put this together from
the press release Pocheon City issued on October 7
and the stage schedule and event notice
on the Pocheon Culture & Tourism Foundation’s official blog.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s theme is
’Autumn: Our Moments
Shining Brightest with Silver Grass’.

Myeongseongsan has a silver grass field
of about 150,000 m².
Sanjeong Lake was built in 1925
as a farm reservoir,
and this year marks
101 years since it was made.

Performances, exhibitions, hands-on activities —
about 30 programs in all.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The stage program changes every day.
Here’s the foundation’s schedule in date order.

Date & timeProgram

Oct 17 (Sat) 20:00–20:30Sanjeong Lake Fireworks Show on the water

The opening ceremony is on the special stage
at Sanjeong Lake Plaza, and the schedule notes
that times may change depending on conditions on site.

🎆 What is the lake fireworks show?

It’s the festival’s signature evening program.
The theme is ‘Lighting Up Memories with Silver Grass
— 101 Years of Sanjeong Lake’.

From boats moving across the lake,
a story is told through
performance and fireworks
together.

All seats are free, with open seating.

🌾 What’s on during the day?

In the daytime, hands-on programs run
all around Sanjeong Lake and Myeongseongsan.

ProgramWhen · where · how
Legend of Gungye (궁예) guided tourDaily 11:00 · 13:00 · 14:00 · 15:00, departs from the Sangdong Parking Lot at Sanjeong Lake (상동주차장), first 20 people per session
Myeongseongsan ESG plogging (picking up litter as you walk)Daily 11:00–17:00, along the Myeongseongsan hiking trails, sign up online or on site, biodegradable bags and gloves provided
On-site activitiesRed Mailbox (a letter you receive a year later), photo studio, silver grass wish path & fortune draw, silver grass art workshop, silver grass calligraphy, Pocheon Korean chrysanthemum (구절초) activity
Trail exhibitions & stamp tourPoem-and-painting exhibition, winning entries from the national Pocheon tourism photo contest, trail stamp tour

If you book the guided tour in advance
and come on the day,
you get a limited-edition activity kit, one per person
(at the main information center on Sanjeong Lake Plaza).
The booking link is in the foundation blog’s
special events post.

Join the plogging
and you can also try the ESG roulette event.

On Sanjeong Lake Plaza there will be a rest area,
the ‘Silver Grass Garden Recharge Zone’,
plus food booths.

The guided tour starts from
the Sangdong Parking Lot at Sanjeong Lake.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Pocheon City Theater Company’s family play ‘Fox Pass’
is a fresh take on
the Fox Pass folktale of Myeongseongsan.
There are two shows: the 16th at 15:00 and the 18th at 16:00.

On Saturday the 17th, the evening runs straight
from the 18:00 opening ceremony
to the concert and the lake fireworks show.
On Sunday the 18th, you can see the family play
and then stay for the evening fireworks.

The fireworks show is on the lake after dark,
so pack an extra layer for the kids.
