2026 Gwangju Memories of Chungjang Festival Schedule, Parade Times, Parking — Before Going to Geumnam-ro with Kids

The autumn festival is in full swing on Geumnam-ro 🎺

Now in its 23rd year! The 23rd Gwangju Memories of Chungjang Festival is happening for 5 days from October 7 (Wed) to 11 (Sun) across Geumnam-ro, Chungjang-ro, May 18 Democracy Square, and Art Street.

Admission is free. Here’s a summary based on Dong-gu Office press releases and the official festival website.

Let’s take a quick look first!

🔴 Happening Now

The festival kicked off on the 7th and continues until the 11th (Sun). There will be four parades over three days starting from the 9th!

Here’s a collection of the remaining main stage (in front of Jeonil Building 245) and Geumnam-ro events.

On the 9th, at Asia Culture Street, immigrants and international students will showcase their hometown foods. Dishes like nasi goreng, Hokkien mee, and wagashi are on the menu.

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

This is a downtown street festival with a ‘memories’ theme, starting from Chungjang-ro.

This year’s theme is ‘Songs of Memories.’ The festival is decorated to evoke the past through songs cherished by each generation.

It has been named an Honorary Culture and Tourism Festival by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism for four consecutive years since 2024.

On the 10th and 11th, from 13:00 to 19:00, the citizen clown troupe ‘Memory Wanderers’ will also be around.

🚇 Getting There & Parking

Geumnam-ro is a car-free street

The section from Jeonil Building 245 to Geumnam-ro Park, where the main stage is located, will be operated as a car-free street until 06:00 on the 12th.

Car access will also be restricted according to the schedule around Geumnam-ro 4~5-ga, Art Street, and the National Asia Culture Center. Bus stops will have information on detour routes.

According to the festival website, there are 19 free/discount parking lots with 1,248 spaces for visitors.

Four parking lots near Geumnam-ro 4-ga Station and Art Street offer a 30% discount, and two near Wedding Street offer a 20% discount.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

On the 10th (Sat) from 13:00 to 17:00, the Miro Gathering Market will be held at Miro Center 2F Miro Lounge — a kids’ family flea market where children can freely buy and sell items themselves.

On the 10th and 11th, the 2nd floor of Miro Center will also have a digital drawing experience and the Miro Garden, which serves as both a rest area and performance space (11:00~18:00).

On the 9th, the W Parade will feature the same performance across three sections of Geumnam-ro. There will be temporary audience seating in front of the Fountain of Light in section 3.

The rice ball making activity on the 10th was a pre-registered program for teams, with sign-ups closing on October 6.

🔑 KICK For free parking at Honam-dong Public and 3 other lots, you need a parking stamp. Stamps are available at the festival information booths.
