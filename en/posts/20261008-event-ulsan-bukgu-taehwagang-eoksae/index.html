2026 Taehwagang Silvergrass Wetland Experience Center Hours · Silvergrass Bubble Blowing · Migratory Bird Badge, Under Naehwanggyo Bridge in Myeongchon

It’s the season when the silvergrass at the Taehwagang estuary
waves in the wind 🌾

Ulsan City is running the Taehwagang Silvergrass Wetland Experience Center
from September 28 to November 22
under Naehwanggyo Bridge in Myeongchon-dong, Buk-gu.

Here’s a summary of the press release
(from September 28) by Ulsan City’s Environmental Policy Division.

Let's take a quick look first!

🔴 Happening Now

Including today, Thursday, October 8,
there are 46 days left until November 22 (Sunday).

Here’s a table summarizing how it operates.

OperationDetails
Days OpenEvery day from 9/28 (Mon) to 11/22 (Sun), no holidays
Hours10:00~17:00
ReservationNot required (open to everyone on site)
On rainy daysNot open

Since it’s open every day,
it’s also open during the Hangeul Day holiday (9~11).
But it closes if it rains.

There are seven weekends left,
from October 10·11 to the last days, November 21·22.

🎪 What Kind of Place Is It?

The press release introduces the Taehwagang estuary as
a representative autumn ecological tourism spot
where wetland plants like silvergrass, water silvergrass, reeds, and wild rice naturally grow.

The experience center is held
with the silvergrass colony as its backdrop.

Ulsan City explains that silvergrass is
a representative water-purifying plant
that helps clean river water
and provides a habitat for many creatures.

It’s not a festival with a stage,
but an ecological experience center
where you can enjoy the same activities every day.

The subtitle of the press release is
”Enjoying Taehwagang’s Autumn Ecological Experience Amid Silver Waves.”
Ulsan City hopes citizens and tourists
will directly feel the ecological value of silvergrass.

✨ What Can You Do?

Here are the activities listed in the press release.

ActivityDetails
Nature InterpretationA guide is always present to explain the features of wetland plants and the role of river wetlands
Fruit ObservationObserve seeds and fruits with a magnifying glass
Silvergrass Bubble BlowingMake bubbles using silvergrass and reeds
Ecology Quiz · Bird BadgeSolve a quiz and make a round winter bird badge (pin button)
Polaroid Photo ZoneTake instant camera photos with the silvergrass colony as the background

There is no information in the press release
about activity fees, number of materials, or time per activity.

🚶 Where Is It Held?

The location is under Naehwanggyo Bridge

The location is under Naehwanggyo Bridge
in Myeongchon-dong, Buk-gu, Ulsan.
The press release introduces this spot
as the Taehwagang estuary.

On Naver Maps (as of October 8),
there is also a separate listing for the Taehwagang Silvergrass Colony
in Myeongchon-dong.

There is no information in the press release
about parking or public transportation.
Check the location first using the map card below.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

No reservation is needed,
so you can drop by on a nice day.

There are hands-on activities like
solving a quiz and making a winter bird badge,
and blowing bubbles with silvergrass.

A guide is always present, so
you can ask together with your child
how silvergrass and reeds are different.

🔑 KICKClosed on rainy days
The program ends at 17:00
So don't go too late in the afternoon
Check the weather before you go
