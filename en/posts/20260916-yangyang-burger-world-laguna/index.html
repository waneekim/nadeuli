Burger World Laguna Beach, Yangyang — Yangyang Burger vs New York Burger with a View of the Sand Sculptures (Menu, Prices, Parking)

Our Laguna Beach burger review, Yangyang 🍔

A handmade burger shop
right in front of the beach sand sculptures:
Burger World, Laguna Beach!

Two burgers on an orange tray — the Stars and Stripes marks the New York Burger, the Korean flag the Yangyang Burger

📍 170 Hajodaehaean-gil
Hyeonbuk-myeon, Yangyang, Gangwon
(하조대해안길 170, inside Laguna Beach)
⏰ Daily 10:00–19:00
💰 Yangyang Burger 9,900 won
New York Burger 10,900 won
🅿 Free parking
🐶 Pets allowed
📞 0507-1438-7826

🏖 After looking at the sand sculptures

In our Laguna Beach post
we only wrote
”the sand sculptures are a permanent display
in front of a burger shop”
and moved on.
This is that shop.

The beach where they’ve set up
mermaid, sailing-ship and lion
sand sculptures!

We wandered among the sculptures,
then walked in here
and had dinner.

🪟 You’re paying for the window

One whole wall is open,
so from your seat
the sand sculptures and the sea
are right in front of you.

Inside it’s pink neon
and an orange-striped ceiling —
pretty loud colors,

but turn toward the window
and past the ship and the mermaid
the waves are rolling in.

The view from our seat — the sea beyond the ship, mermaid and horse sand sculptures

We’d just seen the sculptures on the beach,
and once we sat down
we got to see them all over again.

The kids kept stopping mid-meal
to look out the window.

🔑 KICKWhat you pay for here: the window
One whole wall is open
sand sculptures and sea
straight ahead from your seat!

🍔 Yangyang Burger vs New York Burger

We ordered two.

Each burger comes
with a little flag stuck in it.
The Korean flag is the Yangyang Burger,
the Stars and Stripes the New York Burger!

No chance of mixing up
which is which.

Yangyang Burger — beef patty, cheese and a rich onion-chutney sauce on frill-ice lettuce

The Yangyang Burger is 9,900 won.

Onion, tomato and frill-ice lettuce,
a beef patty, cheese, mayonnaise
and an onion chutney sauce.

That dark brown sauce
running down over the patty
is the chutney.
It’s the cheapest burger here.

The New York Burger is 10,900 won.

A double patty,
‘snow cheese’ and cheddar,
and a house cheese sauce.

If the Yangyang Burger is about
veggies and sauce,
the New York Burger is about
meat and cheese!

Order both
and you can compare
two very different burgers in one go.

To drink, we had two Pepsis
and one yellow ‘ade’ (fruit soda).

📋 Menu & prices

Yangyang Burger 9,900 won
New York Burger 10,900 won
Chester Burger 10,900 won
(the Yangyang Burger + fried egg & demi-glace)
Beureuno Burger (브르노) · Kentucky Burger 11,900 won each
[Pepsi combo] Nyubeon Burger (뉴번버거) 12,900 won

(per the Naver Place menu)

At around 10,000 won each,
it didn’t feel pricey
for a beachfront spot.

🚗 Getting there & parking

300 m on foot
from Hajodae Beach and Surfyy Beach,
10–15 min by car
from Ingu Beach and Yangyang town.
(per the restaurant’s directions)

Parking at Laguna Beach is easy —
you could even pull over briefly on the beach road —
and there’s a separate lot too.

Pets are welcome too.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone going to see
the Laguna Beach sand sculptures
✔ Anyone who wants a meal
with a sea view from the table
✔ Families on a beach day
looking for a burger around 10,000 won

More than the burgers themselves,
it’s the seat
we remember.

If you’re going for the sand sculptures,
don’t just snap a photo and leave —
have a meal here
and enjoy them once more through the window!
