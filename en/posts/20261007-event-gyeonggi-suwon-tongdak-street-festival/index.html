Suwon Chicken Street Festival 2026 — Schedule, Concerts & Parking; With Kids, Start at Hwaseong Haenggung by Day

It’s the season when the area
in front of Hwaseong Haenggung
fills up with autumn outings 🍂

The 2026 Suwon Chicken Street Festival
(2026 수원 통닭거리 축제)
runs for three days, Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, Oct 18,
at Hwaseong Haenggung Plaza
and around Suwon’s Chicken Street
(Tongdak-geori — ‘tongdak’ is whole fried chicken).

We put this together from
Suwon city’s festival portal,
its September 30 press release
and Suwon Tourism’s pages.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Suwon Tourism introduces it as
Suwon’s signature food festival.

It celebrates Chicken Street’s
50-year tradition and is tied in with
’Visit Suwon Year 2026’ (2026 수원방문의 해).

Suwon Special City hosts it,
and the Haenggung Culture Street
Merchants’ Association runs it.
The festival is free.

There are two festival areas.

PlaceWhat’s there
Hwaseong Haenggung PlazaOpening ceremony & concerts · promo booths · citizen events
Suwon Chicken StreetYajang (outdoor dining zone) · hands-on booths · busking

Suwon Tourism’s page
also lists a flea market.

✨ What’s on, and when?

So far, only the opening ceremony
and two concerts have times.

Fri 10/16, 19:00Concert — Son Tae-jin (손태진), Kyungseo (경서), Biboiz (비보이즈), Lee Ha-rin (이하린)

The event-hours field on the portal
says ‘01:00–01:00’,
so the festival’s hours aren’t known yet.

🏯 Pair it with Hwaseong Haenggung by day

Hwaseong Haenggung Palace (화성행궁),
right next to the festival grounds,
is open daily 09:00–18:00.

At Sinpungnu Gate (신풍루) of the palace,
the Suwon City Performing Arts Company
stages its regular Muye 24gi show
(무예24기, ‘the 24 martial arts’).
It shows the martial arts practiced
by the Jangyongyeong (장용영) soldiers
in King Jeongjo’s time.

The show is free,
but palace admission is separate.

🚇 Getting there & parking

There's no festival parking lot

The portal lists parking facilities as ‘none’
and road closures as ‘none’.

from Suwon Tourism’s
’How to get to Paldalmun’ page.

It’s open 24 hours and takes cards only.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

In the evening, the main draws are
the opening ceremony and concert on the 16th
and the yajang (outdoor dining zone)
on Chicken Street.
Yajang means seats out on the street
for eating and drinking outdoors.

So if you’re bringing kids,
we suggest daytime.
Start with the 11:00 Muye 24gi show
and a palace visit,
and once booth hours are announced,
browse those next.

The Saturday (17th) 16:30 concert
is a show that starts before sunset.

If you’d like to see more of the palace
in the evening, there’s also
the Hwaseong Haenggung night opening:
Fri, Sat & Sun 18:00–21:30
(tickets & entry close at 21:00),
at the same prices as daytime.
