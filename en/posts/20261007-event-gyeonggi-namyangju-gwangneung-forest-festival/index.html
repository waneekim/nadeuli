Gwangneung Forest Festival 2026 — Dates, Shuttle Bus & Parking Before You Walk the Once-a-Year Forest Trail with Kids

News for an autumn forest-trail outing 🌳

A forest that opens just once a year:
the 21st Gwangneung Forest Festival
(제21회 광릉숲축제)
runs Sat, Oct 24 – Sun, Oct 25,
two days, 10:00–17:00,
around Gwangneung Forest
in Jinjeop-eup, Namyangju.

We put this together from
Namyangju City’s festival website
(directions page updated October 6)
and the city’s press release (October 6).

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

The festival website calls it
’Korea’s only forest-culture festival’
and says it aims to be
an eco-friendly festival.

Access to Gwangneung Forest
is normally restricted,
so the website’s front page reads:
a forest trail that opens just once a year.

It’s the forest around Gwangneung,
the royal tomb of King Sejo of Joseon
and Queen Jeonghui,
protected for some 550 years.
In 2010 it became a UNESCO
Biosphere Reserve,
and some 6,251 taxa
of living things live here.

The official poster lists
Namyangju City as the host,
with the National Institute of Forest Science,
the Korea National Arboretum
and Bongseonsa Temple as partners.

✨ What happens in the forest?

These are the programs listed in
the website’s ‘Program Preview’
and on the official poster.
They all run on both days, around Gwangneung Forest.

ProgramWhat it is
Forest Walkers (광릉숲뚜벅이)The festival’s main program: a walk along the thousand-year-old forest trail
Forest Stage (광릉숲테이지)Gugak (Korean traditional music), jazz and instrumental shows; busking along the forest trail
Forest Photo Studio (광릉숲사진관)Photo zones set in the forest
Forest Yoga (광릉숲요가원)Yoga in the forest
Forest Library (광릉숲도서관)Reading in the forest
Forest Post Office (광릉숲우체국)Letter writing
Forest Teacher (광릉숲선생님)Guided forest talks
Forest Rest Stop (광릉숲멍쉬멍)Rest area

Food and the market
run separately as side events.

Side eventWhat it is

Forest Village Market (광릉숲마을장)A citizen-run flea market on the lotus path at Bongseonsa Temple
Forest Info (광릉숲알리미)Promotion booths
Forest Eats (광릉숲먹거리)Food booths

The timetable for each program
and the opening ceremony’s date and time
aren’t in the notices yet.

🚌 Getting there & parking

Shuttle buses run from Jinjeop Station (Line 4)

The website’s directions page
lists a festival shuttle bus.

The same page gives the interval
as both 15 minutes and about 20 minutes,
and says the buses will run flexibly
when traffic is heavy.

For the festival address,
the website’s directions page gives 193 Bongseonsa-gil,
while the city’s cultural events listing
gives 32 Bongseonsa-gil — different numbers.
This post follows the website,
and the map shows the Bongseonsa Temple
parking lot next to No. 193.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

It’s a festival about
walking forest trails,
so pack comfortable walking shoes
for the kids.

If you take the shuttle,
it’s another 15 minutes or so on foot
from the military base parking lot
to the festival grounds.

There are restful programs too,
like reading in the forest,
letter writing and a rest area.
If the kids get tired, drop in for a break.

🔑 KICKThe festival ends at 17:00
Parking lots close after 18:00
Shuttles run until 18:00 too
If you came by car, head out in good time

✔ Families who want to walk a forest trail that’s usually off-limits
✔ Families looking for a restful outing, like forest yoga and reading
✔ Families who’d like to catch forest busking and a flea market together
