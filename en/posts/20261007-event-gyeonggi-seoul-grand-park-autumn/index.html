Seoul Grand Park Autumn Festival 2026 — Dates, Programs & Parking Before You Take the Kids to ‘Pinocchio’s Forest’

Late autumn, when the leaves turn,
is coming to Seoul Grand Park
in Gwacheon 🍁

The 2026 Seoul Grand Park Autumn Festival
(2026 서울대공원 가을축제)
runs Sat, Oct 31 – Sun, Nov 8,
9 days around Seoul Grand Park.

This year’s theme is
’Pinocchio’s Forest, Tinted with Art’.

We put this together from
Seoul city’s October event plan (September 30),
Fun Seoul and Seoul Grand Park’s notices.

The festival’s hours and fees
haven’t been announced yet,
so we’ve set them apart below.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Fun Seoul describes it as
”a festival built around
Seoul Grand Park’s distinctive
autumn nature (autumn leaves, fallen leaves, etc.)”.

It’s made up of programs
that families — the park’s main visitors —
can join in and try for themselves.

The park is run by
the Seoul Metropolitan Government,
but its address is in
Gwacheon, Gyeonggi-do.

Last year it ran as the
’Autumn Foliage Festival:
Autumn-Light Grand Park’
from October 25 to November 2, 2025,
around the Meeting Plaza (만남의 광장).

✨ What programs are there?

Forest Art Gallery (숲속 미술관)
Installation art that blends
with the autumn-leaf forest

Artist-linked exhibition
There’s also a large installation artwork
that visitors can take part in.

Performances
Acoustic concerts,
dance performances and more

Participation events & children’s drawing contest
Only the names are out so far —
no details on how to enter yet.

🌿 What are the eco craft booths?

The eco craft booths
(친환경 체험부스) are making activities
Seoul Grand Park picked
through a public call for ideas.
The themes are upcycling
and crafts from natural materials.

Four winning programs
were announced on October 7.

Endangered-animal
’bag safety reflector tags’
made from old banners

‘Jewels That Hold the Sea’,
made from discarded glass

Mini terrazzo mirrors
made from waste plastic

Making enrichment items
for the animals

The conditions in the call:
two weekend days during the festival,
up to 30 minutes per session,
5 hours a day.

Which weekend it’ll be,
and whether there’s a materials fee,
isn’t out yet. The notice says details
may change as the plans are worked out.

💰 Festival fees vs. zoo admission

The festival’s hours, admission and activity fees
are not announced yet.

What follows is not the festival fee —
it’s Seoul Grand Park’s regular admission.
(Checked on the park’s fee page on October 7)

Only two places charge admission:
the Zoo and the Theme Garden.
Whether the festival grounds
are inside them hasn’t been announced.

Zoo individual tickets
Adults 5,000 won · Youth 3,000 won
Children 2,000 won

Theme Garden individual tickets
Adults 2,000 won · Youth 1,500 won
Children 1,000 won

Free for ages 5 and under
and 65 and over;
tickets are valid on the day only.

The Zoo and Theme Garden open at 09:00
and close at 18:00 in October
and 17:00 in November.
Last entry is 1 hour before closing.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Subway Line 4, Seoul Grand Park Station
(대공원역), exits 1, 2 and 3.

Seoul Grand Park recommends public transport,
calling itself “10 minutes by subway
from Sadang Station”.

Apart from the parking lot,
regular cars and taxis
can’t drive into the park.
There’s no taxi stand inside either,
so get in and out outside the park.

Elephant Train (코끼리열차)
Loops from the main information center
→ Stop 1 (Zoo & Theme Garden)
→ Stop 2 (Seoul Land)
about every 5 minutes.
One way: adults 2,000 won,
youth & children (36 months+) 1,500 won

Parking Seoul Grand Park lot
(about 6,616 spaces)
Paying in the Kakao T app:
3,000 won for the first 2 hours,
then 1,000 won per 30 min (max 5,000 won a day)
At the pay machine:
4,000 won for the first 2 hours,
then 1,500 won per 30 min (max 6,000 won a day)

The daily maximums are a special discount
for the ‘parking lot stabilization period’.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

If you have a multi-child family card:
family members listed on Seoul’s
Dadungi Haengbok Card (다둥이 행복카드)
or Gyeonggi’s i-Plus Card (아이플러스 카드)
get into the Zoo and Theme Garden free
after an ID check.
(Cards from other cities and provinces don’t count)
Multi-child (Dadungi) cars
also get 50% off parking.

The Elephant Train’s doors are narrow.
With a large stroller,
the park suggests the Zoo’s stroller rental
(02-500-7193).

No pets, kick scooters, bikes,
inline skates or balls
in the Zoo and Theme Garden.
Strollers and wheelchairs are fine.

🔑 KICKFrom November 1, the Zoo
closes at 17:00
Last entry is 16:00!
If you want to see the Zoo too,
set out in the morning
