Anseong Matchum Land Waterside Walk: Ducks, Fish and an Evening Sky

Food for the fish—
so why are the ducks here first?

I took a walk around the waterside park
inside Anseong Matchum Land.

I tossed some food for the fish,
but the ducks came over and ate it.
That unexpected turn
made the walk a lot of fun.

🌿 A walk beside the water

Looking back at the photos,
I can see the pond covered in lotus leaves
and the path running alongside it.

I photographed the scenery,
but the funniest part of the visit
was the little commotion over fish food.

Lotus leaves beside the path and railing

🦆 Fish food, but the ducks got there first

I tossed some food into the pond
for the fish.

But the fish were not alone.
There were ducks there too,
and they came over and ate the food.

It was meant for the fish,
yet the ducks got to it first.
That was such a funny, unexpected sight.

A short clip of fish gathered by the edge

Ducks encountered beside the pond

So I tried tossing the food
here and there, away from the ducks,
hoping to get it to the fish.

Trying to feed the fish
just gave me more reasons
to laugh at the ducks.

A walk made funnier by ducks eating the fish food.
That is the moment I remember most
from this waterside park.

🌇 Evening light and flowers in the photos

Evening sky reflected beside the wooden walkways

The photos show the sky reflected in the water
and wooden walkways stretching across it.
I also photographed a path edged

Putting the fish and duck clips
among those landscape photos
brings back the laughter from that walk.
