Singal Ogeori Street Festival 2026 in Yongin — Schedule, ALi & Shin Yong-jae Concert and Kids’ Activities Along Singalcheon Stream

Autumn is here —
perfect for a walk along Singalcheon Stream 🍂

The 2026 Singal Ogeori Urban Regeneration Street Festival
(신갈오거리 도시재생 거리축제 —
ogeori means a five-way junction)
runs for one day, Sat Oct 24, 11:00–18:00,
along Singalcheon Stream in Giheung-gu, Yongin,
on the stretch from Giheunggyo Bridge
to Galcheongyo Bridge.

We put this together from
the press release and festival poster
Yongin city posted on October 7.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It’s a street festival that shares
the results of the Singal Ogeori
urban regeneration project with residents.

Local residents, shop owners and artists
are putting together shows, activities and food
to bring new energy
to Singal Ogeori and Singalcheon Stream.

The poster’s slogan is
’A Story We Draw Together’ (함께 그리는 이야기).

Yongin Special City hosts it,
and the Yongin Cultural Foundation
and the Singal Urban Regeneration Support Center
organize it together.

✨ What’s on, and when?

By day, hands-on activities, exhibitions
and street shows run all along the stream,
and the ceremony and celebration concert start at 17:00.

TimeProgram
During 11:00–18:00Daytime programs — arts & culture activity booths, urban regeneration promotion, activities & exhibition, local food shops & craft studios, a kids’ flea market, I’m Busker street shows
From 17:00Ceremony & celebration concert — singers ALi and Shin Yong-jae close the festival
18:00Festival ends

The exact time of each program
hasn’t been announced yet.
Yongin city says to check
the Yongin Cultural Foundation website
for the detailed schedule.

🎵 What performances are there?

From local artists to teens, university students
and neighborhood hobby clubs, everyone takes the stage.

WhoWhat
I’m Busker (아임버스커)Artists from the Yongin Cultural Foundation’s street performance support program — instrumental music, traditional performing arts, magic and performance art, all around Singalcheon
Youth Busker (유스버스커)Shows by teen and young adult artists
Shared-school studentsNanta drumming (the Yongin Office of Education’s Gongyu Hakgyo “shared school” program)
Dankook Univ. & local clubsShows by musical theater students and local hobby clubs
Gugak Inside (국악인사이드)A traditional pungmul street parade (Korean folk percussion) through the festival grounds
ALi & Shin Yong-jae (알리·신용재)Celebration concert along with the 17:00 ceremony

Plenty of programs
put kids center stage.

Some activities are paid.
Which ones, and how much,
isn’t in the notice yet.

🍢 Food and places to rest

Local merchants run
food booths and food trucks,
and there’s a picnic zone for a break.

To cut down on disposables,
it’s run as an eco-friendly festival with reusable dishes,
they say.

🚇 Getting there & parking

No transit or parking info yet

The festival runs along Singalcheon Stream
from Giheunggyo Bridge to Galcheongyo Bridge.
Both bridges are in Singal-dong, Giheung-gu.

The press release and poster
don’t include public transit, parking
or road-closure information.
It’s a good idea to call the inquiry line before you go.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The art wall, crafts and flea market
are listed as ‘daytime programs’ on the poster,
so if you arrive during the day
you can start with the activities.

ALi and Shin Yong-jae take the stage
with the 17:00 ceremony,
and the festival ends at 18:00.
If you want to catch the concert,
leave plenty of time in the afternoon.

Since some activities are paid,
ask ahead about how to pay
and you’ll fumble less on site.

🔑 KICKAt the kids' flea market
children take part as 'sellers'
There's no sign-up info yet,
so if your child wants to sell,
ask the inquiry line first
