Cheap Coffee at Magongnaru Station: Mammoth Express, Exit 5 — a 1,600-Won Americano for the Morning Commute

My Magongnaru Station commute-coffee review ☕

Right by Magongnaru Station exit 5,
on the ground floor of Lotte Castle Le West,
I stopped by Mammoth Express.

An Americano is 1,600 won!
Given what coffee costs these days,
that’s really cheap.

Mammoth Express, Magongnaru Station Exit 5 branch — a white sign and a brown awning

📍 111 Magokjungang-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul
Lotte Castle Le West, Bldg 104
1F, Unit 163
(1 min from Magongnaru Station exit 5)
⏰ Weekdays 06:30–17:00
Weekends & holidays 10:00–17:00
💰 Americano M 1,600 won
(hot; iced 1,800 won)
🥤 Takeout only
📞 0507-1323-3299

🏠 Mammoth on the ground floor of Le West

The sign says ‘MAMMOTH COFFEE’,
the awning ‘MAMMOTH EXPRESS’.

The lettering above the window reads
’매머드커피 익스프레스 956호점
마곡나루역5번출구점’ —
Mammoth Coffee Express store no. 956,
Magongnaru Station Exit 5.

It’s a good place to grab a cup
on the way to work.

☕ Americano prices by size

These are the prices on the wall menu
and the menu screen above the counter.

Hot (HOT)
S 1,200 · M 1,600 · L 3,000 won

Iced (ICE)
S 1,400 · M 1,800 · L 3,000 won

The 1,600 and 1,800 won
on the kiosk’s first screen
are for size M.

I had a large (3,000 won)
with an extra shot, for 3,500 won.

Even a large with an extra shot is 3,500 won,
so on days you want it strong,
it’s still easy on the wallet.

The banner out front says
”괜찮아, 커피는 살안쪄”
(“It’s okay, coffee won’t make you fat”),
with a takeout Americano
at S 1,400 won (iced).

The banner out front — ‘괜찮아, 커피는 살안쪄’ (It's okay, coffee won't make you fat), takeout Americano S 1,400 won (iced)

📋 Other coffees are cheap too

Above the wall menu:
‘TAKE MORE, PAY LESS’!

Coffee and cold brew,
non-coffee, frappés and blended drinks,
tea and ades — all on one board.

The wall menu — COFFEE · COLD BREW · NON COFFEE · FRAPPE · TEA/ADE, with a Mammoth Order notice beside it

You can switch to decaf, too
(except the Korean-style ‘mix coffee’).

The sign next to the kiosk says
the decaf coffee is
’100% Colombian single origin’,
and the decaf cold brew is made
with ‘the Swiss Water Process’.

📱 Kiosk & Mammoth Order

The kiosk is inside the shop.
It has buttons for other languages,
and the menu is split into
coffee, cold brew, non-coffee and decaf.

✔ Payment — N Pay, Apple Pay
and Samsung Pay logos,
a ‘pay with Kakao Pay’ sticker,
and Zero Pay and Sikgwon Daejang
stickers on the glass door
✔ Mammoth Order — order ahead in the app;
search ‘마곡나루역 5번출구점’

The kiosk — (HOT) Americano 1,600 won, (ICE) Americano 1,800 won, with a decaf option notice beside it

🥤 A grab-and-go spot

The shop is small.
The counter and pickup counter face you,
and pickup numbers appear
on the far right of the menu screen.

The store’s opening notice
also describes it as
takeout-only and cashless.

Inside — the counter and the menu screen, with pickup numbers on the far right

The same menu is on the glass door,
so you can choose
before you even walk in.

⏰ For the morning commute, not the trip home

On weekdays it’s open
from 6:30 am to 5 pm;
on weekends and holidays,
from 10 am to 5 pm.

It opens early
but closes at 5 pm,
so it’s for the way to work,
not the way home.

The shop opened
on February 16, 2026.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone near Magongnaru Station

✔ Anyone who wants to save
