Gwangmyeong Cave Light Festival 2026 — Drone Show Times, Free Shuttle Buses & Parking Before You Take the Kids

1,000 drones lighting up
the autumn night sky —
the light festival is back ✨

The 4th Gwangmyeong Cave Light Festival 2026
(제4회 광명동굴 빛축제)
runs for two days, Fri Oct 16 – Sat Oct 17,
from 11:00 to 21:00
around Gwangmyeong Cave (광명동굴).

We put this together from
the notices on Gwangmyeong Cave’s website
and the Ministry of Culture’s
regional festival listing.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s theme is
’Forest of Light, Awakening Life’.

It’s a hands-on light festival
hosted by Gwangmyeong City,
with drone shows, performances,
activities and a market,
plus an eco quiz show and a film festival.

The festival is free.
But admission to the cave itself
and other facility fees are separate.

✨ When are the drone shows?

There are 4 drone shows over the two days.
We’ve listed them together
with the opening-day ceremony.

In the notice’s own words,
it’s a dazzling light show
with 1,000 drones filling the sky.

The quiz show is held at the Media Tower,
and taking part gets you
Gwangmyeong Cave goodies.

The craft zones open at 11:00 on both days,
and the busking and film festival
are at the Plaza of Light (빛의 광장).

🚌 Getting there: shuttle buses & parking

From 17:00, cars can't get in

The notice warns of heavy traffic
and asks visitors to use public transport.

From 17:00, all vehicles are barred
from entering Gwangmyeong Cave.

but they say they won’t leave
before the listed times.

Parking fees are the regular rates.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

To catch the daytime shows too,
Saturday the 17th is the day.
There’s a magic show at 11:00
and a bubble show at 17:00.

You can go straight from
bubble show → food zone → 19:00 drone show.

The cave itself is open until 18:00 (last entry 17:00),
and admission is paid separately.

Cave admission (individual)Adults · youth · children
Standard10,000 won · 5,000 won · 3,000 won
Gwangmyeong residents5,000 won · 2,500 won · 1,500 won
Infants under 3Free

The cave isn’t closed on either festival day.

🔑 KICKThe drone shows start at 19:00 or later,
but cars can't enter the cave grounds from 17:00
Decide where you'll catch the shuttle first
The last buses back leave at 22:20 and 22:30
