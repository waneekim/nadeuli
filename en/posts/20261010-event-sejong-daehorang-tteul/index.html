2026 Daehorang Tteul Dates & Archery – Gim Jongseo Historical Theme Park, Sejong, Before You Go with Kids

In cool autumn weather,
a playground of the Tiger General is open
in Janggun-myeon, Sejong 🐯

Daehorang Tteul (대호랑 뜰), the history playground at Gim Jongseo Historical Theme Park,
runs from August 27 (Thu) to November 29 (Sun),
every Thursday to Sunday, 10:00–17:00.

I’ve summarized the Sejong Cultural Foundation notice (August 26)
and the park page on the Sejong City tourism website.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎉 Open Right Now

It opened on August 27
and continues from today (the 10th) until November 29 (Sun).

Counting only the Thursday-to-Sunday open days,
there are 30 days left, including today.
That’s eight weekends to go.

🐯 Who Was the Tiger General, Kim Jong-seo?

Gim Jongseo Historical Theme Park
is the tomb of General Kim Jong-seo (Jeoljae, 1383–1453),
a loyal official of King Danjong’s reign in the Joseon Dynasty.
It is Sejong City Monument No. 2.

The Sejong City tourism page explains that
in the 16th year of King Sejong (1434) he pioneered the Six Garrisons,
and that Korea’s current border was settled at that time.
He also took part in compiling the Goryeosa and the Annals of King Sejong.

The tomb area covers about 200 pyeong,
surrounded by a round wall with three gates,
and a red-arrow gate (hongsalmun) stands at the entrance.
An old tombstone erected under King Yeongjo
and a ‘Chungsin Jeongnyeo’ (loyal subject) plaque are also there.

The park is open year-round, and admission is free.

✨ What Can You Do at Daehorang Tteul?

The foundation describes it as a history experience space to enjoy with kids.
Here are the six experiences listed in the notice.

The poster’s title is
’A history experience with the Tiger General.’

🎁 There’s a Survey Event, Too

The EVENT box at the bottom of the poster says
that if you take the program satisfaction survey,
you can win a mobile gift voucher worth 30,000 won
in a prize draw.
The survey is linked through the QR code on the poster.

🚗 Getting There & Parking

It's in Daegyo-ri, Janggun-myeon

The Sejong City tourism page lists the location as
San 45 and 7 other lots, Daegyo-ri, Janggun-myeon, Sejong.
On Naver Map it appears as ‘Gim Jongseo General Historical Theme Park’ (김종서장군역사테마공원)
(245 Bamsil-gil).

Neither the foundation notice nor the tourism page
gives parking or public transport information.
Before you set off, call the park number on the tourism page,
044-857-1898.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

Sand play and archery
are hands-on, active experiences.
A change of clothes and wet wipes will help.

You receive the goodies only
after finishing the stamp mission,
so check the mission first when you arrive.

Hearing the general’s story first
through the board game and story exhibition,
then taking photos at the photo zone, is a nice order.

It’s open until 17:00,
so arriving late in the afternoon leaves little time for the experiences.

🔑 KICKOpen only Thursday to Sunday
Closed Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday
On a weekday, go on Thursday or Friday!
