Dodong Seowon Digital Art 2026 — Ginkgo Light Shows, Activities and Shuttles

An academy’s ginkgo tree, over 400 years old,
becomes a canvas of autumn-night light. 🌳

Dodong Seowon Digital Art 2026: Dodong Yeonhwa
(도동서원 디지털아트·도동연화)
was underway at the October 8 check.

It runs October 2 (Fri)–November 14 (Sat)
around Dodong Seowon
in Guji-myeon, Dalseong-gun, Daegu.

This guide follows the county’s October 1 press release
and the official event website.

Let's take a quick look!

🔥 What dates remain?

As of October 8, there were 38 days remaining,
including that day.

The ginkgo media projections run
only on Friday and Saturday evenings.
Here are the remaining dates from that reference point.

🎪 What is the event about?

Dodong Seowon is a Confucian academy
honouring the Joseon scholar Kim Goeng-pil,
known as Hanhwondang.
It became UNESCO World Heritage in 2019
as part of the Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies.

The theme is “Dodong Yeonhwa (道東姸畵)”:
“Foremost among academies, reborn through light.”

✨ What light displays can we see?

There are three lighting programmes.

DisplayTime
Ginkgo media projectionsFriday and Saturday, 18:30 and 19:30; 30 minutes each
Lighting art on Suwollu Pavilion, walls, pine grove, steles and treesDaily sunset, around 18:00, until 21:00
Miracle Stones, around 100 illuminated sculptures on the lawnDaily sunset, around 18:00, until 21:00

The official guide says the first and second
projection screenings have the same content.

Four scenes are introduced on the official website.

SceneContent
Golden EnlightenmentGolden blossoms bloom on the ginkgo tree
Sea of LightJellyfish, turtles and whales swim between branches
Guardian of Dodong SeowonThe ginkgo awakens as a guardian character
Ginkgo Dressed in TraditionFive traditional colours, mother-of-pearl, fan dance and azaleas

🏮 When are crafts and food available?

Activities take place at the Dodong Confucian Culture Hall.

Date and timeActivities and food
Friday and Saturday, 17:00–20:00Traditional games
Friday and Saturday, 17:00–20:00Dodong Cooperative food and local market
Saturday, 17:00–20:00Mini cheongsachorong lanterns and ginkgo-leaf bookmarks; six Saturdays from 10/10
Saturday, 18:00–20:30Moonlight guided tour; twice a day on six Saturdays from 10/10; reservation required

The moonlight tour explores the academy at night
with a guide. Apply using the reservation button
on the official website’s activities/food-zone page.

🚇 Getting there and parking

Shuttles run on Friday and Saturday evenings.

On Fridays and Saturdays, a shuttle bus
connects the temporary car park to the academy entrance.

ShuttleDetails
BoardingTemporary car park A, 358-1 Dodong-ri, Guji-myeon
ArrivalBus stop in front of Dodong Seowon’s pavilion, 55 Dodong-ri
OperationFriday and Saturday, 17:00–21:00
JourneyAbout 5–7 minutes each way
VehiclesThree 25-seat buses; two on Fridays

The layout also marks Dodong Seowon’s own car park.
Daegu Tourism’s regular listing says parking is free,
but event-period capacity and fees
are not separately announced.

The official website gives these public-transport options.

TransportRoute
Metro and busDaegok Station, Line 1 → Express 8 → Guji-myeon Administrative Welfare Centre
Next legDalseong Happy Taxi, between that centre and Dodong Seowon, or a transfer
City busDalseong 3, toward Dodong Seowon

Dalseong 3 has long intervals and may follow
market-day schedules on the 5th and 10th;
check current operation before travelling.

Dalseong Happy Taxi enquiries:
Dalseong County Office, 053-668-3012.

👨‍👩‍👧 What helps with kids?

Activities and the market start at 17:00,
with the first projection at 18:30.
Arriving around five can allow crafts,
the projection and a Miracle Stones walk in sequence.

The layout marks a projection viewing area
on the lawn in front of the ginkgo,
and toilets near the Confucian Culture Hall.

For the academy buildings by day,
Daegu Tourism lists regular hours of 10:00–17:00.

🔑 KICKProjections, activities and shuttles
are limited to Friday and Saturday.
Sunday–Thursday nights have lighting art
and Miracle Stones only.
