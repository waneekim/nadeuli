2026 Sejong Science Jiphyeonjeon Schedule, Experience Booths, Parking – Before Visiting Sejong Jungang Park with Kids

During the Hangul Day holiday, a science and drone experience zone opens at Sejong Jungang Park 🚀

2026 Sejong Science Jiphyeonjeon will be held from October 9 (Fri) to 11 (Sun), daily from 10:00 to 17:00 at Sejong Jungang Park Eoullim Garden 1. All programs are free.

This post summarizes the Sejong City press release (October 8) and the attached poster.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Event

This is a science culture event where anyone can easily and enjoyably experience the principles of science and technology.

It is linked with the 2026 Sejong Hangul Festival held during the same period. The Hangul Festival’s kids’ program guide also lists ‘science technology, drone, and other experiences.’

The poster says, ‘At Sejong’s Jiphyeonjeon, the world of future space, robots, and drones unfolds.’ The Ministry of Science and ICT, Korea Foundation for the Advancement of Science and Creativity, and Sejong Technopark are also listed.

✨ Event Schedule

Experience booths run all three days, with special programs on the first day, the 9th.

Date & TimeProgram
9, 10, 11th 10:00~17:00Science Technology Experience Booths · Drone Experience Booths
9th (Fri)Science Magic Show · Four-legged Robot Show (Special Program)

The shows are described as a performance explaining science principles through magic and a walking four-legged robot show. Start times are not listed in the press release.

🔬 What Experiences Are There?

There are two types of booths. At the drone booth, you can pilot a drone yourself.

Experience BoothProgram
Science TechnologyAir Rocket Flight Team · AR Racer · Robot Omok (Gomoku)
Science TechnologyRobot Soccer Academy · Make Your Own Solar Car · Lunar Rover Mission
Drone ExperienceDrone Claw Machine · Coding Drone Soccer Challenge · Virtual Flight Simulator

Only two programs required advance registration.

ParticipationProgram
Advance Registration (Closed)Air Rocket Flight Team · Make Your Own Solar Car
On-site Free ParticipationAll other experiences

As of October 8, the advance registration form was marked ‘closed.’ All other programs can be enjoyed freely on the day at the venue.

🎆 On the Same Day at the Hangul Festival

After the science experiences, performances for the Hangul Festival continue at the main stage in the Urban Festival Plaza of Jungang Park.

Date & TimeHangul Festival Main Stage
9th (Fri) 12:00Black Eagles Air Show (about 5 minutes above the park)
9th (Fri) 20:40Hangul Drone Grand Show (2,000 drones)
10th (Sat) 20:40Fireworks & Media Art Grand Show
11th (Sun) 18:00Closing Concert National Symphony Orchestra

The drone and fireworks shows last about 10 minutes and may change depending on on-site conditions.

🚇 Getting There & Parking

It's the same park as the Hangul Festival

Eoullim Garden is inside Sejong Jungang Park. Since the Hangul Festival is held on the same day, follow the Hangul Festival’s transportation and parking guidance.

Bus: From Sejong Express Intercity Bus Terminal, take bus 203 or 205 and get off at Sejong Arts High School. It’s about a 2-minute walk.

During the festival, the park is pedestrian-only, so bicycles and kickboards are not allowed.

Parking & TransportInformation
Parking Lots11 lots listed in the Hangul Festival guide · Search by parking lot name in navigation apps
Morning of the 9thEntry/exit restricted at Sejong Arts Center, Jungang Park LH Temporary, and North Ieung Bridge parking lots (for Hangul Run, until 11:40)
Hangul Train (Paid)Board near the Urban Festival Plaza main stage · Adults 4,000 won, Children 3,000 won

The Hangul Festival organizers note that parking lots will be very crowded and ask visitors to use public transport or walk if possible.

👨‍👩‍👧 If Visiting with Kids

In the same Eoullim Garden (2), there is an Eco-friendly Playground for the Hangul Festival. It features a jungle gym and spinning swings, open from 10:00 to 18:00.

On the 9th and 10th, the Rose Garden hosts the Day Goblin Playground (traditional games).

Strollers can be borrowed at the Lakeside Park Management Center. For lost children or lost items, go to the General Information Center at Jangnamdeul Square in Jungang Park.

The National Children’s Museum is a 3-minute walk from Jungang Park. It is open from 09:30 to 17:30.
