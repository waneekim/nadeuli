Cheorwon Heritage Media Art 2026 — Light Shows, Pinkfong Cinema and Visiting with Kids

Light washes over the stone walls
of Cheorwon’s former Labour Party Headquarters
on autumn evenings ✨

2026 Cheorwon Heritage Media Art
(국가유산 미디어아트 철원 노동당사) runs
September 11 (Fri)–October 11 (Sun),
19:00–21:00, at the headquarters
and Cheorwon History and Culture Park.
Admission is free; Tuesdays are closed.

This guide brings together the culture ministry’s
festival listing, the official website
and Cheorwon County announcements.

Let's take a quick look!

🎉 The festival is under way

The festival opened on September 11.
At the October 8 source check,
four days remained, through October 11 (Sun).

There are no scheduled closure days
between October 8 and 11.

Here is the programme for those final days.

Day and timeProgramme
Daily, 19:00–21:00Headquarters facade projections, park light installations and Moeul Night Market
Daily, 17:30 and 19:30Pinkfong Cinema at Cheorwon Theatre; a different film from the 9th
10 (Sat), 17:00Cheorwon Busking Busking, in front of Ojeongpo; 60 minutes
10 (Sat), 19:00 and 20:00Peace Robot street performance; 30 minutes each
10 (Sat), 20:00Harmony of Light, Cheorwon arts troupe at Sundial Square; 40 minutes

🎪 What kind of event is it?

The headquarters building bears memories
of the Korean War and the country’s division.
Its facade and the history park become
the stage for an evening arts event,
telling heritage stories through light and images.

This year’s theme is “Peace Soaring”:
from memory to empathy, beyond healing towards the future.

✨ When do the facade projections start?

The headquarters projections run
every 30 minutes, four times a night.
Two works play in sequence.

TimeWhat to see
19:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30Peace Bundle, about 10 minutes, followed by Pro PACE, about 5 minutes
19:15, 19:45, 20:15, 20:45Ojeongpo Light Time Signal, lasers and lighting; about 5 minutes
Between screeningsPeace Courtyard: striking a gong or small drum sends light across the headquarters

“Peace Bundle” unfolds a colourful cloth
wrapped around the building.
Its three acts follow stories
of mine flowers, a zelkova tree and cranes.

“Pro PACE” is by French artist Yann Nguema.
The Cheorwon version adds scenes
of the headquarters building
for its first presentation in Korea.

🔦 What can we try in the park?

The official website lists 30 light installations,
video works and activities.
Here are some options to explore with children.

Activity or attractionLocation
Wriggling Blackboard: drawings come to life on a large boardCheorwon Public School
Headquarters Embracing Peace: write peace messages on balloonsBeside Ojeongpo
Chichi and Popo: a cat-and-train character animationHistory and Culture Exhibition Hall exterior
Real World: smartphone AR treasure hunt, from 09:00Cheorwon Financial Cooperative
Peace Fireflies: lights respond to your footstepsIndependence Activists’ Path
Moeul Night Market: local products and foodTteokjeon Street and in front of Cheorwon Literature Museum

There are also “Peace Soaring”
and “Moon and Rabbit” photo spots.

At Cheorwon Station and the Literature Museum,
media art draws on local writer Lee Tae-jun’s work
and the children’s story Waiting for Mom.

🎬 How do we see Pinkfong Cinema?

Cheorwon Theatre screens Pinkfong films
twice daily, at 17:30 and 19:30.

Tickets are handed out at the information zone
one hour before each screening,
to the first 50 people.

DateFilm
8 (Thu)Pinkfong Cinema Concert 2: Wonderstar Concert Adventure; 71 minutes
9, 10, 11Pinkfong Cinema Concert 3: Catch the Gingerbread Man!; 65 minutes

The official notice says films and schedules
may change depending on operations.

🚗 Getting there and parking

You can visit independently

The headquarters is outside the civilian restricted area,
so no advance application is needed to visit.

The official directions suggest searching for
“철원 노동당사” or “금강산로 265”
in your navigation app.

Event parking locations were not given
in the guidance checked.
Traffic and parking arrangements
may change with operations.

👨‍👩‍👧 Visiting with kids

After the 17:30 Pinkfong screening,
you can head to the first
headquarters projection at 19:00.

On Saturday the 10th, the Peace Robot
and Cheorwon arts troupe performances
are also scheduled.

The light attractions start at 19:00.
The official outdoor performance notice
also recommends a coat for chilly evenings.

🔑 KICKPinkfong tickets: first 50 people
at the information zone, one hour before the screening.
For the 17:30 film,
arrive for ticket distribution at 16:30.

👍 Who might enjoy it?

✔ Families looking for a free autumn light festival
✔ Families with young Pinkfong fans
✔ Families introducing children to the history of Korea’s division

From projections on stone walls to park activities,
this is an autumn evening light festival
ending over the Hangeul Day holiday.

Check the official notice once more before going!
