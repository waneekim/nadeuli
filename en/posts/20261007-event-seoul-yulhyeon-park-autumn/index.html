Yulhyeon Park Autumn Festival 2026 in Gangnam, Seoul — Schedule, Evening Lineup & Parking, Plus a Kids’ Café and Puppet Show

A one-day festival
is coming to Yulhyeon Park,
beside the Tancheon stream,
on an autumn Saturday 🍂

Yulhyeon Park Autumn Festival 2026
(2026 율현공원 가을축제),
‘Yulhyeon Sings the Autumn Night’,
runs Sat Oct 17, 10:00–20:00
around the park’s lawn plaza.

The shows and activities are all free —
just turn up and join in.

We put this together
from the official notice (September 29)
by Seoul’s Eastern Parks Leisure Center.

First, the key facts at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Yulhyeon Park sits
at the far edge of Gangnam-gu,
right beside the Tancheon stream.

It opened in November 2016
on some 47,000 pyeong
(about 155,000 m²),
with a rose garden, a sand playground
and a multipurpose sports field.
(per Seoul city’s 2022 introduction)

It’s organized by Seoul’s
Eastern Parks Leisure Center (동부공원여가센터),
and it’s also listed in Seoul’s
October event plan (September 30).

The festival comes in three parts.

‘Here & There’ Seoul Kids Café
(여기저기 서울형 키즈카페)
10:00–15:00, lawn plaza

Book lounge programs
10:00–18:00, Yulhyeon Park’s book lounge (책쉼터)

Performances
18:00–20:00, from magic to trot

🎈 What’s on during the day?

First up, the kids’ café.

Session 1: 10:00–12:00
Session 2: 13:00–15:00

It’s for ages 4 to 9
(international age, born 2017–2022),
with a parent or guardian.
Sign up on site.

With an Olympic theme,
they’ve prepared bouncy castles, sports bounces,
swings, sand play, block play,
crafts and group games.

The book lounge programs
are open to anyone who drops by.

10:00–13:00
Flower frame making

14:00–15:00
Hand-puppet show ‘Ttongdwaeji Wangbanggu’
(똥돼지 왕방구, roughly ‘The Pig’s Big Fart’)

15:30–18:00
’Memories of Yulhyeon’ clear bookmark making

Only the puppet show takes some seats in advance.
From 9:00 am on Thu, Oct 8,
you can pre-book on Seoul’s
public service reservation website
(서울시 공공서비스예약),
and the remaining seats
are open to walk-ins on the day.

🎤 What time are the evening shows?

For two hours from 18:00,
five acts follow the opening.

18:00 Opening (MC Kim Byung-chan 김병찬)
18:05 Lian (리안) · Magic
18:30 Rimpak (림팍) · Popera
18:50 Yoon Ye-won (윤예원) · Fusion gugak / trot
19:10 Namgoong Ok-bun (남궁옥분) · Folk pop
19:30 Shin Yu (신유) · Trot
(until 20:00)

Gugak is traditional Korean music,
and trot is a classic Korean pop style.

The notice lists both the shows
and the activities as
’free, just turn up and join’.

Magic comes first (18:05),
so the order works well with kids.

🚌 Getting there & parking

There's no parking
inside the park

That’s why the organizer
recommends public transport.

From Suseo Station (Line 3 / Suin-Bundang Line), bus 402 or 3426
From Jangji Station (Line 8), bus 402, 440 or 3426
Get off at
’Gangnam Hanyang Sujain Apartment’ (강남한양수자인아파트).
(The notice image also spells it ‘수지안’)

🅿 If you’re driving,
Bamgogae-ro 21-gil Public Parking Lot
(밤고개로21길 공영주차장)
is 1,200 won per hour,
about a 4-minute walk to Yulhyeon Park.
(Seoul city’s 2022 introduction
says it’s open 24 hours, year-round)

On the official map,
the festival area is in the northern part of the park,
east of Gangnam Hanyang Sujain Apartment.

Across the road to the south
is the park’s Nest Garden (둥지정원).

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The kids’ café is only for ages 4 to 9.
If a younger sibling is coming along,
pair it with the flower frame making
(10:00–13:00), which is open to everyone.

If you’re making a day of it,
this order has no time clashes.

10:00 Kids’ café, session 1
14:00 Puppet show
15:30 Clear bookmark making
18:05 Magic show

Flower frame making runs until 13:00,
so you can still pop in
after kids’ café session 1 ends.

After the bouncy castles and sand play,
kids tend to get sandy.
Wet wipes and a change of clothes help.

The shows go on until 20:00,
so bring a jacket for the kids too.

🔑 KICKThe puppet show (14:00–15:00)
overlaps with kids' café
session 2 (13:00–15:00)
To do both,
pick morning session 1 for the kids' café!

👍 Who we’d recommend it for

✔ Families looking for a place where kids aged 4–9 can run around
✔ Families looking for free crafts and a puppet show
✔ Families who’d like to stay for the evening trot and folk shows

Hands-on fun for the kids by day,
live shows in the evening —
a festival that fits nicely into a full day out!
