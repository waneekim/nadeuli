Our older one and our younger one
both caught the flu,
so we took them to
Magok Tuntun Pediatrics (마곡튼튼소아청소년과).

So many little kids
come to this clinic,
and after our visit
we could see why.

🏥 A clinic that takes up a whole floor

It uses almost the entire floor,
so inside it’s really spacious.

Waiting room — children's song videos on a big screen, picture-book shelves below

In the waiting area,
a big screen plays children’s song videos,
and there’s a shelf of picture books.

It makes the wait
a little easier
for kids who are feeling sick.

We also liked that
the waiting room for infant checkups and vaccinations
is separated off with glass.

Sick kids
and kids there for their shots
don’t end up mixed together.

Waiting room for infant checkups and vaccinations — separated off with glass

Compared with other pediatric clinics nearby,
the facilities were clearly better.

🛏 The big thing: an inpatient ward

What makes this clinic stand out
is that kids can be admitted here
when they need to be.

For us as parents, it’s a big plus
that an admitted child can feel more settled
and get more focused care.

Entrance to the inpatient ward — a banner saying 'admissions 365 days a year'

Not many pediatric clinics
have inpatient rooms,
so just knowing it’s there is reassuring.

According to Naver Place,
the ward has 29 beds.

🌙 Open until 11 pm on weekdays

According to the sign,
it runs evening and late-night hours
until 11 pm on weekdays.

On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays
it’s open until 6 pm,
and the lunch break is 13:00 to 14:00.

Opening hours sign — weekdays 09:00–23:00, evening and late-night clinic

Besides pediatrics,
the sign also lists ENT,
internal medicine, dermatology
and psychiatry.

When your child suddenly spikes a fever at night,
having somewhere to go
is such a relief —
any parent who’s been there knows.

There’s also a plaque
from Korea’s disease control agency (KDCA)
naming it an influenza
sentinel surveillance site.

The 2026 influenza sentinel surveillance site plaque

🚗 Parking & getting there

If you drive,
park in K-Square’s underground car park
(B3–B6)
and go up to Tower 3, 2F.

The directions say to park
near pillars 02, 03 or 04,
or 21, 22 or 23.

The inpatient ward banner lists
parking support:
4 hours on admission or discharge day,
2 hours for visits.

By subway, it’s between
Magok Station (Line 5)
and Magongnaru Station (Line 9, AREX),
about 3 minutes on foot underground.
(per Naver Place)

🍀 How our kids did

Our two kids
were diagnosed with the flu
and were given flu medicine
through an IV drip,
and within two days they were almost better.

That’s just how it went for our kids.
Every child’s course can be different,
so for diagnosis and treatment,
please always talk to a doctor.

A place close by to go
when the kids get sick at night,
where they can even be admitted if needed —
that was a real comfort.
