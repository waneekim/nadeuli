Mapo Naru Salted Shrimp Festival 2026 (Saeujeot Festival) — Dates, Fireworks & Parking, Plus Shrimp Catching for Kids

Right before kimchi-making season,
the festival where people go
to buy salted shrimp
is back for the 19th time 🦐

The 19th Mapo Naru Saeujeot Festival
(마포나루 새우젓축제)
runs for three days,
Oct 16 (Fri) to Oct 18 (Sun),
at Peace Plaza in World Cup Park.

(Saeujeot is tiny shrimp
salted and fermented —
a key seasoning for kimchi.)

We’ve put together
Mapo-gu’s press release (October 7)
and Seoul’s Fun Seoul festival page.

First, what kind of festival is it?

🎪 What is this festival?

In the Joseon era,
salted shrimp and goods
from all over the country
came in at Mapo Naru,
the old Mapo ferry landing.
This signature Mapo-gu festival
brings that history back to life.

This year’s slogan
is a pun on ‘Mapo-gu’:
‘Saeujeot-jom matbogu-ga!
Junbihaesseo Mapo-gu-ga!’
(“Have a taste of saeujeot!
Mapo-gu has it all ready!”)

During the festival,
LED hwangpo sailboats
(traditional yellow-sail boats)
will float on Nanji Pond,
and a special exhibition,
‘Mapo Naru Memory Tunnel’,
is planned too.

🦐 How does the salted shrimp market work?

There are 12 salted shrimp booths in all,
four more than last year,
coming from Ganggyeong, Gwangcheon,
Sinan, Buan and Sorae.

You can compare yukjeot,
ojeot and chujeot side by side
(shrimp salted from the June,
May and autumn catches),
with the origin labeled.

Mapo-gu says it set prices
10–15% cheaper than regular markets.

There are also 14 specialty produce stalls
(from sister cities such as Gochang,
Sinan, Yecheon, Namhae,
Cheongyang and Ongjin,
plus local farm co-ops)
and 12 food stalls.

The new shrimp food booth
sells shrimp pasta
and shrimp fried rice,
and there’s also barbecue,
janchi-guksu (banquet noodles)
and bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes).

The market stalls are open
10:00–20:00 on all three days.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The main stage events
from the Fun Seoul program schedule.

Oct 16 (Fri), opening day
10:30 Satto Haengcha (magistrate’s parade)
& reenactment of a hwangpo boat arriving
(about 200 people march
from Mapo-gu Office Plaza to Peace Plaza)
17:00 Opening ceremony part 1: culture concert
18:00 Opening ceremony part 2
(Jung Dong-ha, Hong Kyung-min Band, Ali, Maya)
19:30 TBS live broadcast
’Autumn Love Concert: Rest’ (가을愛 콘서트: 쉼)

Oct 17 (Sat)
11:30 Jultagi (tightrope-walking) show
14:00 ‘Rolling a 19 m Shrimp Gimbap with Big Mama!‘
17:30 Nationwide Moms & Dads Singing Contest
(guest singers Kim Sung-hwan, Jadu, Yoon Su-hyun)
20:00 Classical & jazz concert (waterside stage)

Oct 18 (Sun), closing day
11:30 Hongdae K-indie band relay stage
13:30 ‘Dwae-Sae-Gim’ bossam feast
(pork, saeujeot and kimchi;
first come, first served, about 200 people)
18:00 Closing concert (Park Hyun-bin, Hong Jin-young and more)
followed by fireworks and a laser show 🎆

🚗 Getting there & parking

Oct 17–18 overlap with the Silver Grass Festival

The festival notice gives no
subway, bus or shuttle information.

Peace Park parking lot: 1,162 spaces
Nanjicheon Park parking lot: 438 spaces
(World Cup Park information page)

Cars normally pay 150 won per 5 minutes;
‘for football matches and events’
it’s a flat 5,000 won (per entry, 4 hours).
The notice doesn’t say
which rate applies during this festival.

Also, on the festival site map,
the markets go up in Parking Lot F
and the farmers’ market (농수산물시장) parking lot.

At Haneul Park, in the same park,
the Seoul Silver Grass Festival (서울억새축제)
starts on Oct 17 (Sat).
Seoul’s notice for that festival says
”traffic will be very congested,
so we recommend public transportation.”

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids?

Shrimp catching, saeujeot-making and auction experiences
run throughout the festival.

There are also bouncy castles for kids,
‘Little Satto’ (little magistrate)
and a bubble show.

The play area with bouncy castles
and traditional games
(UNICEF Plaza on the site map)
is open 10:00–18:00.

For the shrimp gimbap rolling on the 17th,
38 children who sign up on site
take part in two teams.

The nursing room is at
Byeoljari Rest Area in Peace Park,
open 09:00–18:00.

🔑 KICKThe play area closes at 18:00
and the fireworks on Oct 18 (Sun)
follow the closing concert (18:00–20:30)
Play in the daytime, stage at night —
plan your day in that order
