Mapo Naru Salted Shrimp Festival 2026 (Saeujeot Festival) — Dates, Fireworks & Parking, Plus Shrimp Catching for Kids

Right before kimchi-making season,
the festival where people go
to buy salted shrimp
is back for the 19th time 🦐

The 19th Mapo Naru Saeujeot Festival
(마포나루 새우젓축제)
runs for three days,
Oct 16 (Fri) to Oct 18 (Sun),
at Peace Plaza in World Cup Park.

(Saeujeot is tiny shrimp
salted and fermented —
a key seasoning for kimchi.)

We’ve put together
Mapo-gu’s press release (October 7)
and Seoul’s Fun Seoul festival page.

First, what kind of festival is it?

🎪 What is this festival?

In the Joseon era,
salted shrimp and goods
from all over the country
came in at Mapo Naru,
the old Mapo ferry landing.
This signature Mapo-gu festival
brings that history back to life.

This year’s slogan
is a pun on ‘Mapo-gu’:
‘Saeujeot-jom matbogu-ga!
Junbihaesseo Mapo-gu-ga!’
(“Have a taste of saeujeot!
Mapo-gu has it all ready!”)

During the festival,
LED hwangpo sailboats
(traditional yellow-sail boats)
will float on Nanji Pond,
and a special exhibition,
‘Mapo Naru Memory Tunnel’,
is planned too.

🦐 How does the salted shrimp market work?

The market stalls are open
10:00–20:00 on all three days.
Here are the booths, market by market.

MarketBooths & details
Salted shrimp12 in all (four more than last year) — from Ganggyeong, Gwangcheon, Sinan, Buan and Sorae
Specialty produce14 — sister cities such as Gochang, Sinan, Yecheon, Namhae, Cheongyang and Ongjin, plus local farm co-ops
Food12 — the new shrimp food booth (shrimp pasta, shrimp fried rice), plus barbecue, janchi-guksu (banquet noodles) and bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes)

You can compare yukjeot,
ojeot and chujeot side by side
(shrimp salted from the June,
May and autumn catches),
with the origin labeled.

Mapo-gu says it set prices
10–15% cheaper than regular markets.

On the last day, the closing concert
is followed by fireworks and a laser show 🎆

🚗 Getting there & parking

Oct 17–18 overlap with the Silver Grass Festival

The festival notice gives no
subway, bus or shuttle information.

Parking lots and car fees
are on the World Cup Park information page.

Parking & feesSpaces & fees
Peace Park parking lot1,162 spaces
Nanjicheon Park parking lot438 spaces
Cars, normally150 won per 5 minutes
Cars, ‘for football matches and events’Flat 5,000 won (per entry, 4 hours)

The notice doesn’t say
which rate applies during this festival.

Also, on the festival site map,
the markets go up in Parking Lot F
and the farmers’ market (농수산물시장) parking lot.

At Haneul Park, in the same park,
the Seoul Silver Grass Festival (서울억새축제)
starts on Oct 17 (Sat).
Seoul’s notice for that festival says
”traffic will be very congested,
so we recommend public transportation.”

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids?

Here’s what there is to do with kids,
and when.

Activity & facilityTime & who
Shrimp catching, saeujeot-making and auction experiencesOngoing throughout the festival
Play area (UNICEF Plaza on the site map) — bouncy castles and traditional games10:00–18:00
Shrimp gimbap rollingOn the 17th — 38 children who sign up on site take part in two teams
Nursing room (Byeoljari Rest Area in Peace Park)09:00–18:00

There are also bouncy castles for kids,
‘Little Satto’ (little magistrate)
and a bubble show.

🔑 KICKThe play area closes at 18:00
and the fireworks on Oct 18 (Sun)
follow the closing concert (18:00–20:30)
Play in the daytime, stage at night —
plan your day in that order
