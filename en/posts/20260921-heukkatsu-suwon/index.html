Spicy Pork Cutlet in Suwon: Heukkatsu (흑카츠), Sin-dong — A Bit Hotter Than Shin Ramyun, with Sauce to the Last Bite

My Suwon spicy pork cutlet review 🌶

This past June,
at Heukkatsu in Sin-dong,
Yeongtong-gu, Suwon,
I had the spicy pork cutlet.

I only have one photo,
so this one is short.

Spicy donkatsu — a crispy pork cutlet heaped with red sauce, with cabbage salad and rice on the side

📍 Sin-dong, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon
50 Gwonseon-ro 908beon-gil, 2F
(Sin-dong café street)
⏰ Daily 11:00–21:00
Break 15:30–17:00
Lunch last order 15:00
💰 Spicy donkatsu 12,000 won
🅿 8 free spaces behind the building
📞 0507-1343-9706

🔥 Intense from the first bite

On top of the crispy cutlet,
a heap of sweet-and-spicy sauce!

So it hits hard from the very first bite.

There’s so much sauce
that it stayed moist right to the end.

The cabbage salad and rice
tame the heat just enough.

🌶 A bit hotter than Shin Ramyun

The heat felt
a little stronger than Shin Ramyun,
Korea’s classic spicy instant noodles.

If you like spicy food,
it’s worth taking on at least once,

and it’s the dish
that comes to mind
when I need to blow off stress.

🔑 KICKA little hotter
than Shin Ramyun
If spicy is too much,
go loin, tenderloin or half-and-half

📋 Menu & prices

Spicy donkatsu 12,000 won
Loin katsu 11,000 won
Tenderloin katsu 12,000 won
Half-and-half katsu 13,000 won
Cheese roll katsu 12,000 won
Katsudon 9,000 won

(per the Naver Place menu)

There are pasta dishes too.

🚗 Parking

The surface lot behind the building
is free. (8 spaces)

If it’s full, there’s Sin-dong
Public Parking Lot 1 (paid),
a 3-minute walk away,

and Sin-dong Public Parking Lot 2,
a 5-minute walk away,
is said to be free
until 6 pm on weekdays.

(per the Naver Place notice)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone who loves spicy food
✔ Anyone craving a pork cutlet
on a stressful day
✔ Anyone after a cutlet place
with private rooms
(the rooms are nicely set up)

Confident with spice? Spicy donkatsu.
If not, half-and-half!

A pork cutlet for the days you need to let off steam.
