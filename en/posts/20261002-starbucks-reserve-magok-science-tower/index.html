Starbucks Reserve in Magok, Seoul — Magok Science Tower R Store: My First Iced Pour-Over with Reserve Brazil Beans

My first visit to a Starbucks Reserve, in Magok ☕

I go to Starbucks all the time,
but a Reserve store?
This was my first.

An iced pour-over and its bean card on a Reserve tray

📍 62 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul
1F Reserve · 2F regular Starbucks
⏰ Weekdays 07:00–22:00
Weekends & public holidays
09:00–22:00

(Brazil São João, per the menu board)

📞 1522-3232

🏢 Reserve on 1F, regular Starbucks on 2F

The sign at the entrance says it too:
1F is the Reserve brewing bar,
2F is a regular Starbucks.

Walk in, and under
the swirl of wood covering the ceiling,
the long Reserve bar stretches out.

The 1F Reserve bar — the swirling wood decoration on the ceiling

By the windows,
leather armchairs and small round tables
stand in a row.

I sat by the window.

Leather armchairs and round tables by the window

📱 Ordering in the app, as the store suggested

I ordered in the app,
as I always do.

I like coffee with some acidity,
and I like it iced,
so I went with the Brazil beans
the store recommended.

I asked for it
brewed as a pour-over.

The app order screen — ICED · POC · Grande · for-here cup

The bean is called
’Sun-Dried Brazil
Fazenda São João’.

In the app’s order details,
the brew method showed up as ‘POC’.

It was my first time ordering like this,
so I was really looking forward to it!

It came in a glass with the Reserve logo,
on a wooden tray carved with the same logo,
together with a bean card.

An iced pour-over (GIF)

☕ How it tasted

The aroma was amazing.

Even though I’d ordered it iced,
it was an aroma I’d never smelled before.

The glass with the Reserve logo

Compared with my usual
iced Blonde Aerocano,
it tasted a little heavier,
with a slightly bitter edge.

I’d have liked
a bit more acidity.

The bean card puts acidity
at the 4th of 5 dots,
and the menu board says MEDIUM-HIGH,
but to me the body and the bitterness
came through first.

📇 Reading the bean card

Front of the bean card — SUN-DRIED BRAZIL FAZENDA SÃO JOÃO

The flavor note:
apricot and hazelnut chocolate

Body: the middle of 5 dots,
acidity: the 4th of 5

Sul de Minas, Brazil,
natural process

Brew methods: pour-over,
Chemex, espresso

Back of the bean card — body, acidity, region, process, brew methods

To be fair, the card’s description
does also mention a flavor that’s
”pleasantly bitter yet fragrant”.

Next time I’m thinking
of trying a different brew method.

👃 Smelling the beans first at the aroma station

Next to the bar is an aroma station
where you can smell
four kinds of beans before you order.

A note there says,
“Feel free to smell them —
you can also order them as drip coffee,“
so you can take a sniff without any pressure.

The aroma station — four beans under glass lids

The poster beside it introduces
a newly arrived Brazil bean, ‘Fazenda Recanto’
(semi-sweet chocolate and nut butter).

It’s also a sun-dried Brazil,
but a different bean
from the ‘São João’ I got.

The new-bean poster — BRAZIL FAZENDA RECANTO

🔑 KICKThere are two Brazil beans!
São João vs Recanto
When you order,
ask which one you're getting
