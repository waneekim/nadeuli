Where to Eat in Siheung with Kids: Mikkodam, Mulwang-dong — Grilled Mackerel, Seaweed Soup and Beef Bulgogi in a Tidy Korean Spread

A Korean set meal in Mulwang-dong, Siheung 🍚

Back in late March,
we went as a family to
Mikkodam (미꼬담),
Siheung Mulwang branch.

Grilled mackerel — it comes out savory, with no fishy smell

📍 1F, 867 Dongseo-ro
Siheung, Gyeonggi-do
(Mulwang-dong)
⏰ Daily 10:30–21:00
(last order 20:30)
💰 Seaweed soup from 15,000 won
Set for two 46,000 won
🅿 Parking lot by the entrance
(room for nearly 40 cars)
📞 0507-1367-1427

It’s a Korean set-meal place,
and it was
better than we expected.

All three main dishes
pulled their weight.

🐟 All three mains pass the test

The grilled mackerel
was savory,
with no fishy smell.

With grilled fish,
if that part goes wrong,
nothing else matters —
and this came out cleanly grilled,
right down to the skin.

The seaweed soup (miyeok-guk)
had a deep flavor,
and the beef bulgogi
was sweet and salty —
perfect with rice.

All three tasted like
’good home cooking’,
so nothing felt heavy.

Japchae, a meat side dish and salad — every plate neatly done

🥢 Side dishes fit for guests

Every side dish
was set out
neatly and cleanly,

so rather than just eating,
it felt like
being treated as guests.

Gim (seaweed), namul greens and kimchi — each in its own small dish

There was also a plate
of yukjeon (pan-fried beef)
topped with seasoned chives.

Seasoned chives on yukjeon — a plate that feels like a dish in itself

👶 Why it works with kids

The seasoning isn’t harsh
and it tastes wholesome,
so the kids ate well.

The portions were generous, too —
enough to clear a bowl of rice
in no time.

Rolled omelet with a ketchup lattice — a welcome side on a kid's plate

The rolled omelet
with ketchup drizzled in a lattice
was a welcome sight
on the kids’ plates.

The restaurant was pleasant
and the seats comfortable,
so sitting there with kids was easy.
