Where to Eat Haejangguk with Kids in Magok, Seoul — Oneul Hantang, a Hangover-Soup Spot with Plenty of Healthy Choices

Our haejangguk spot in Magok 🍲

In February 2025,
we took the kids
to Oneul Hantang (오늘한탕) in Magok-dong
for the first time.

Haejangguk — full of green vegetables, with a fine powdered topping

📍 Gangseo-gu, Seoul

Plenty of dishes that suit kids
and lots of healthy options,
so choosing what to order was easy.

🍺 Even if you drank the night before

and they’re on the healthy side.

So even if you drank the night before,

you can come to with your kids.
