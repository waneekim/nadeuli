Gamjabat Market Potato Bread in Seoul (Across from the Constitutional Court) — We Came for One Each and Left with a Set

We paid our own way — here’s our review 🥔

Bread that really looks like a potato:
Gamjabat Market’s potato bread!

It really looks like a potato — put it on a plate and you'd do a double take

📍 1F, 16 Yunboseon-gil
Jongno-gu, Seoul
(across the road from
the Constitutional Court)
⏰ Closes at 7:00 pm
💰 Potato bread 3,800 won each
3-flavor set 9,900 won
📦 Original, box of 10: 29,700 won
🛍 Takeout available
📞 +82-70-8808-1716

On our way back
from a family visit
to the Constitutional Court,
we popped across the road
to Gamjabat Market (감자밭마켓),
thinking we’d just grab
a snack for the kids.

🥔 A wall of potato boxes

Step inside
and boxes marked ‘Chuncheon Gamjabat’
are stacked up like a wall.

Chuncheon Gamjabat boxes — Original, 10 per box, 29,700 won

On the shelves,
individually wrapped potato breads
in every flavor!

You’ll spot other breads too,
like sweet potato bread
and corn bread.

The shelves — everything is wrapped one by one, so it's easy to pick

Individually wrapped potato bread

😋 We only meant to buy one each

It was our first time there,
so to start with we just got
one each for the kids.

Our older one had a tummy ache,
so we got the plain Original
with nothing inside;
for our younger one,
the Dujjonku potato bread
(두쫀쿠, short for
’Dubai chewy cookie’).

And it was just so good!

Our older one, tummy ache and all,
ate half,
and Mom polished off the rest.

I had one bite myself
and we ended up
buying a set of three more.

Cheese and Gorgonzola potato bread — 3,800 won each

The set of three we went back for — even the bag has that potato-field feel

🔑 KICKFirst time? Start with the 3-flavor set!
Original · Tomato Basil ·
Gorgonzola, all 3 for 9,900 won
(11,400 won if bought one by one)

📋 Prices (in store, on the day we visited)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for a kids’ snack
after visiting the Constitutional Court
✔ First-timers

✔ Anyone taking home a whole box

‘Just one, since it’s our first time’
doesn’t work here.
You’ll end up going back in anyway!

As a snack for the kids
or a treat for the grown-ups,
this potato bread hits the spot.
