Jeonju Dreamland with Kids — The Small Amusement Park Inside Jeonju Zoo: No Day Pass, Just 1-, 3- or 5-Ride Tickets

Our Jeonju Dreamland day with the kids 🎡

After a full loop of Jeonju Zoo,
we headed on to Dreamland,
tucked inside the zoo grounds.

The Viking ship — with the Ferris wheel and the Jeonju Dreamland sign behind it

📍 68 Sori-ro, Deokjin-gu, Jeonju
(inside Jeonju Zoo)
⏰ 10:00–18:00
(summer weekdays)
🎟 No separate admission fee
No day pass — instead
1-, 3- or 5-ride tickets
💰 1 ride: adults 3,000 won
5 rides: adults 10,000 won
📞 063-275-4900

It’s a small amusement park
run together with the zoo.

To cut to the chase,
for kids in the early grades
of elementary school,
it was a really satisfying place to play,
and it’s perfect for the time left after the zoo.

Getting around with our kid in a folding wagon

🎢 Small, not-so-scary rides

The rides inside
are a bit smaller and less scary
than the ones at the big amusement parks
we all know.

The haunted house has the same feel.

The haunted house — skull and cobra decorations, with roller coaster rails behind

✈️ Round and round in a plane

There’s also a ride
where airplane-shaped seats
spin round and round
as they rise.

The airplane-shaped spinning ride

The planes rising as they spin (GIF)

⛵ The Viking ship and the Ferris wheel

There’s a Viking ship, too.
It swings right up into the sky!

The Viking ship swinging up into the sky (GIF)

From inside the Ferris wheel,
you look down over
the zoo’s woods and the city.

Inside the Ferris wheel — the zoo's woods outside the window

According to the Korea Tourism Organization,
there are about 12 rides,
including the Cheongnyong (Blue Dragon) coaster,
the Viking, the Ferris wheel and the Super Rider.

🎟 Ride bundles instead of a day pass

Dreamland has
no separate admission fee.
You just buy tickets for the rides you want.

There’s no day pass, either.
Instead, you buy ride tickets
in bundles of 1, 3 or 5 rides.

And since they’re really cheap,
riding everything
didn’t look like a problem at all.

🔑 KICKNo separate admission fee!
Instead of a day pass,
buy 1-, 3- or 5-ride bundles
for just what you'll ride

😅 The downside

The rides are pitched at younger kids,
so if you want something really thrilling,
you may be a little disappointed.

If your kid is expecting
a big roller coaster,
it’s worth telling them beforehand.

💰 Ride ticket prices

1-ride ticket
Adults 3,000 won
Youth 2,500 won · Children 2,000 won

3-ride discount ticket
Adults 7,000 won
Youth 6,500 won · Children 5,000 won

5-ride discount ticket
Adults 10,000 won
Youth 9,000 won · Children 8,000 won

7-ride discount ticket
Adults 12,000 won
Youth 11,000 won · Children 10,000 won

“Youth” means middle and high school students;
“Children” means 25 months to elementary school.

(per the Jeonju Zoo website)

⏰ Opening hours

Summer: weekdays 10:00–18:00
weekends & holidays 10:00–18:30

Winter: weekdays 10:00–17:00
weekends & holidays 10:00–17:30

(per the Korea Tourism Organization; open year-round)

It closes earlier
than the zoo (open until 19:00 in summer).
We shot our videos
around 5:30 pm over the Chuseok holiday.
