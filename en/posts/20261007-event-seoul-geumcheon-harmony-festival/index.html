Geumcheon Harmony Festival 2026 in Seoul — Dates, Venue & Parking, Plus Fireworks and the Wappen Patch Tour with Kids

On an autumn weekend,
the area around Geumcheon-gu Office
fills up with a music festival 🎶

The 2026 Geumcheon Harmony Festival
(금천하모니축제)
runs for two days, Sat Oct 17 – Sun Oct 18,
around Geumcheon-gu Office
and Geumnarae Central Park.

It’s open to everyone,
and admission is free!

We put this together on October 7
from the Geumcheon Cultural Foundation’s website,
the festival’s official blog
and Seoul’s Fun Seoul (펀서울) listing.

First, the key facts at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Held every year since 2003,
for more than 20 years,
it’s Geumcheon-gu’s signature festival.

It’s built around ‘music’ —
a chance for residents to relax,
have fun and get together.

This year’s slogan is
”Harmony that resonates, a melody that connects hearts”.

The official poster calls it
’2026 Lively Geumcheon Harmony Festival’
(2026 활기찬 금천하모니축제).

Last year it was held around
Geumcheon-gu Office Plaza
and the Anyangcheon multipurpose plaza.
This year it centers on Geumcheon-gu Office,
using Geumnarae Central Park (금나래중앙공원),
the plaza in front of the office and more.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The main programs listed on Fun Seoul
are the citizen show choir ‘Sing for Harmony’,
guest singer performances, and fireworks.

Hands-on booths, a flea market,
a book festival and food trucks
run alongside as side programs.

Only two things have dates so far.

Sat Oct 17
’Sing for Harmony’ stage,
a show choir of local residents
(time not announced yet)

Sun Oct 18, 11:00–17:00
’Harmony Wappen Tour’
at Geumnarae Central Park

On the Wappen Tour, you collect stamps
around the festival grounds
and take a music-taste test
to find the wappen (embroidered patch)
that suits you.

With the patches you collect,
you decorate a pouch or name tag.

You’re also welcome to bring
a fabric item from home,
like a bag or a cap.
It may end early
if the prepared materials run out.

As of October 7, the guest singers
and the fireworks date and time
haven’t been announced yet.

In the week before the festival,
Mon Oct 12 – Fri Oct 16,
the tie-in ‘Harmony Week’
brings library programs
across Geumcheon-gu.

Doksan Library (독산도서관) says
it will run drop-in crafts
like mother-of-pearl keyrings and bookmarks
throughout that week.

🍢 Market, food & busking?

These hours come from
the call for booth participants.
They were application terms,
so the real hours may differ.

Hands-on booths 11:00–17:00
about 10 booths

Flea market · food · food trucks
11:00–21:00
about 12 flea-market and 10 food booths,
plus about 5 food trucks

Busking 13:00–17:00
about 10 teen and young-adult teams,
up to 15 minutes per team

Food vendors were asked to list
the price per 100 g next to the selling price,
and reusable containers
are planned to be provided.

The call recruited groups
offering free hands-on activities,
but it also lists terms for paid ones.
Check each booth’s cost on site.

🚇 Getting there & parking

No festival transport info yet

So this follows Geumcheon-gu Office’s
’Directions’ page.

Geumcheon-gu Office Station (Line 1), Exit 1
3 minutes on foot.

From Guro Digital Complex Station (Line 2),
take Exit 1 and transfer to a bus
toward Geumcheon-gu Office,
get off at the Geumcheon-gu Office stop
and walk 5 minutes.

Buses include trunk (blue) lines 150 and 500
and branch (green) lines 5530, 5531 and 5617.

Geumcheon-gu Office parking lot
First hour free, then 500 won per 10 minutes
50% off for compact cars, eco-friendly cars
and Dadungi Card holders
(Seoul’s multi-child family card)
260 regular parking spaces.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

If hands-on activities for kids
are the goal,
Sunday the 18th is the day.

The Wappen Tour runs only that day,
from 11:00 to 17:00.
According to the call for booths,
the hands-on booths also close at 17:00.

Geumnarae Central Park (16,331 ㎡)
has a community center, restrooms and more.

With a Dadungi Card,
parking at the office is 50% off.

Just so you know:
‘I’m an Alien Looking for Friends’
and the ‘Just Resting? Youth’ wellness program
are pre-registration programs for young adults,
not events to join with kids.

🔑 KICKThe Wappen Tour is Sun Oct 18 only,
and ends early if materials run out
Bring a bag or cap
to decorate with patches

✔ Families who want to see performances

✔ Families looking for an autumn weekend outing
