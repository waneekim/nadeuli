2026 I♥FEsta Yeongjong Schedule, Lineup, Dessert Experience — Before Visiting Seaside Park with Kids

At the autumn seaside park in Yeongjongdo,
you’ll hear both rock and Korean traditional music 🎸

2026 I♥FEsta Yeongjong
(I♥FEsta Yeongjong)
will be held on October 10 (Sat) 14:00~21:00
at Yeongjong Seaside Park
Haneulgureum Square.
Admission is free.

I’ve summarized the festival information,
poster, and the September 2 press release
from the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

I♥FEsta is the signature festival
of the Incheon Free Economic Zone,
combining the three city festivals of Songdo, Yeongjong, and Cheongna.
It has been held since 2025.

This year’s official name is the 2026 IFEZ Global
Festival, with the theme ‘The Rhythm of K-Culture,
Connecting the World’, and it takes place in three parts.

Date · VenueTheme
9/12 (Sat) Songdo Central ParkK-STAGE · Hanbok Fashion (Past Event)
10/10 (Sat) Yeongjong Seaside ParkK-BEAT · K-Dessert
10/24 (Sat) Cheongna Lake ParkK-OST · K-Content & Wine

The theme for the Yeongjong event is
K-BEAT, where rock meets Korean traditional music.
The food section will feature K-desserts
special to Yeongjong International City.

✨ What Programs Are There?

The festival is a one-day event, from 14:00 to 21:00.
Here are the programs listed on the poster.

CategoryProgram
K-BEAT PerformanceRock & Korean music crossover — Second Moon · Jung Dong-ha · Ensemble Hwasumok · Yuk Joongwan Band
K-FOODK-Dessert Brand Zone · Yeongjongdo Local Dessert Market
Fun ActivitiesDessert making experience · Snack Olympics guerrilla event · Photo zone
RelaxationYeongjong Local Flea Market · Picnic Zone · Food Truck Zone
Special ProgramYeongjong Live Busking · Music Fireworks

The official schedule for each performer
and the music fireworks show
has not been announced yet.

📱 What Is the Mobile Pass?

This year, to help you enjoy all three city festivals,
the I♥FEsta Mobile PASS is available.

According to the Incheon Free Economic Zone Authority,
visitors to Yeongjong are encouraged
to register for the mobile pass.

If you visit and check in at local attractions,
you can receive a chair and table set.
There are also discount coupons
for nearby businesses connected to tourist spots.

🚌 Getting There & Parking

Walk 680m from the bus stop

The Seaside Park management office address is
6, Haneuldalbit-ro 2beon-gil, Yeongjong-gu, Incheon.

According to the Incheon Facilities Corporation park guide,
public transportation options are as follows:

DepartureBus · Arrival
Airport Railroad Yeongjong StationBus 223 · 202 → Dongbo Nobility Stop
Airport Railroad Unseo StationBus 223 · 203 · 320 · 307 → Dongbo Nobility Stop

From the bus stop,
it’s a 680m walk to the park.

For those driving, here are the directions:

DirectionRoute (Distance)
From Yeongjong BridgeGeumsan IC → Jayeon-daero → Haneuldalbit-ro (5.4km)
From Incheon BridgeTurn right toward Gueup Ferry 6·7 Complex → Yeongjong-daero → Haneuldalbit-ro (8.0km)

There is no official information yet
on parking or traffic control for the festival day.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Bringing Kids

The dessert making experience and
the Snack Olympics guerrilla event
are great programs to enjoy with children.
There is no information yet on
reservations or material fees for the experience.

With picnic and food truck zones,
it’s a nice place to relax while waiting for performances.

According to the Seaside Park facility guide,
there are also a rail bike and a sea observatory.
For festival day operations, contact the Yeongjong Park Project Team
at 032-456-2973.

If you plan to stay for the music fireworks,
be sure to bring an extra layer for your child
as the sea breeze can get chilly in the evening.
