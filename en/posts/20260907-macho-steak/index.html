Macho Steak Review (Suwon & Jangan, Seoul) — Four Visits in Six Months, from Work Lunches to a Family Dinner

Our review of a steak place
we went to four times in six months 🥩

From July to December 2025:
three times at the Suwon branch,
once at the Jangan branch —
and we paid every time.

A pat of butter on the grill marks — the signature look of this place

It started as a lunch spot

and ended as a family dinner.

This isn’t a one-visit review —
it’s a review tested by going back!

🥩 First visit — the Suwon branch

My first visit was in July,
a lunch with coworkers.

The steak comes out
still in its pan,
with corn, pickles
and three sauces.

First visit — the steak served in its pan, with corn, pickles and sauces

Use the sauces however you like —
for me, the soy-based sauce
went best with the meat.

🧈 Why we keep going back

Butter on a thick cut of meat,
plus crisscross grill marks.

As the butter melts,
it really brings out the flavor.

October visit — the crisscross grill marks are sharp and clear

The sizzle and the smell
coming off the iron plate
hit you first.

Because it comes in the pan,
it stays warm for a long time,
and they cooked it
just the way we asked.

The corn on the side
is sweet and nutty —
a perfect side dish.

When I went in October
with four coworkers,
the staff were friendly
and the food came out fast,
so we finished with time to spare
within our lunch break.

👨‍👩‍👧 The Jangan branch, a family dinner

Our fourth visit
was with the family,
at the Jangan branch.

Here you cook the steak
right on the hot plate,
so it stayed really juicy.

Jangan branch — juicy steak on the hot plate

We mixed the corn, rice and meat
together on the hot plate,
and it was exactly what our kids like.

Rice + meat + corn on the hot plate — the crispy bits are the best part

The rice sticks a little to the plate
and turns nutty,
like nurungji (crispy scorched rice).

Meat, corn and rice dished out onto the kids' plate

🔑 KICKThe hot plate heats up fast

If you go with kids,

📝 Notes on each branch

The Suwon branch
is good for work lunches and small groups.
Parking was easy
and the restrooms were clean.
It’s a small place, though,
so it gets crowded at peak times.

The Jangan branch
is good for a family meal.
Portions were big,
and we ate comfortably with the kids.

🚗 Parking at the Jangan branch

At the Jangan branch,
you can’t park in front of the restaurant.

They point you to
the ‘Amano North Korea Research Institute
parking lot’ (21 Janghan-ro),
a 2-minute walk away.

It’s 3,000 won for the first 30 min;
on holidays, a 24-hour pass
is 5,000 won
through the Modu Parking app (모두의주차장).
The restaurant doesn’t cover parking.

(Per the notice on Naver Place)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for
a steak lunch with coworkers
✔ Anyone looking for
an easy steak dinner with kids
✔ Anyone who wants a steak place
for everyday, not just special days

For the price,
all four visits landed
somewhere between ‘decent’ and ‘good’.

Not a special-occasion steak,
but an easy, go-anytime steak —
four visits in six months says it all.
