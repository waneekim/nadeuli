Seoul Animal Happiness Festa 2026 at Yeouido Hangang Park — Schedule, Parking & a Free VR Zoo for Kids (No Pet Needed)

It’s October, and the breeze
off the Han River is turning cool 🍂

A Seoul city festival
marking ‘Seoul Animal Protection Day’
on October 4,
Seoul Animal Happiness Festa 2026
(2026 서울동물행복페스타)
runs October 17 (Sat) – 18 (Sun)
on the plaza in front of the Mulbit Stage
at Yeouido Hangang Park.

We put this together from
Seoul’s October event plan (September 30)
and the official website.

With a pet or without,
everyone is welcome

🎪 What kind of event is it?

An animal-protection festival
held by the Seoul Metropolitan Government
together with the Hankook Ilbo newspaper —
this is its 3rd year.

The official website says
”anyone can visit,
whether or not they bring a pet.”

It’s also the first event of
’Seoul Animal Happiness Week 2026’,
a series the city has grouped together.
Here are the dates and venues of all three.

DateEvent & venue
Oct 17–18Seoul Animal Happiness Festa — Yeouido Hangang Park
Oct 24–25Seoul Pet-lympics (서울 펫-림픽) — World Cup Park
Oct 31Seoul Daengnyang Days (서울 댕냥데이즈) — Oil Tank Culture Park

Join all three events
and, they say, you’ll get a gift
at the Daengnyang Days HQ booth.

🙆 No pet? Is that OK?

Yes! The FAQ on the official website
says you can join even without a pet.

What you can enjoy without a pet:
the Animal Protection PLAY Zone,
the Pet Culture O/X Quiz
and the adoption campaign
for abandoned animals.

The PLAY Zone runs 10:00–17:00 both days.
It’s free,
with no sign-up — just join on the spot.

The PLAY Zone’s 5 activities
① Wild animals: a team game
② Farm animals: find the code
stamped on eggshells
③ Lab animals: a game about
alternatives to animal testing
④ Service animals: an etiquette O/X quiz
⑤ Zoo animals: a VR zoo

The VR zoo lets you meet animals
and their habitats digitally,
with no live animals on display.

Collect a QR stamp at each activity —
gather all 5 for a souvenir.
No app needed; it runs in your phone’s browser.
When it’s busy, you may have to wait.

The O/X Quiz and Pet Talk Live
are open to people without pets, too.

🐶 Going with your dog

The organizers ask you
to be sure to bring
a leash and poop bags.

Keep a safe hold on the leash
as you move around,
and be considerate of animals
that are sensitive to noise
and unfamiliar surroundings.

On the 17th, a pet-owner skills test
experience (반려인능력시험)
runs 6 times from 11:00,
and there are consultation and
pet registration booths both days.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Children can join the PLAY Zone
together with a parent or guardian.

The Animal Happiness Run alone
has 500 teams walking with their dogs.
If your child is scared of dogs,
it’s worth talking it over beforehand.

At the adoption show,
instead of clapping,
greet the animals as the host directs,
then lower yourself to their eye level
and watch calmly, the organizers ask.
If you’re hoping to adopt,
you can talk on site
directly with the foster families.

The venue has medical & operations booths
and a Seoul 119 rescue team,
and a bag storage area is planned.

🔑 KICKWhen you arrive, head first
to the main info & on-site registration desk!
Register by QR and get a wristband
It may be used to check stamps and prizes
