2026 Fall in Gwangalli Remaining Schedule · Drone Show · Performance Timetable, Before Visiting Gwangalli Beach with Kids

An autumn festival has opened 🍂
on the sandy beach in front of the Gwangalli sea.

2026 Fall in Gwangalli
is happening right now.
It runs for three days,
from October 8 (Thu) to 10 (Sat),
across the sandy area of Gwangalli Beach.

On the final day, Saturday the 10th,
there will be the Gwangalli Drone Light Show
and celebratory performances in the evening!

Here’s a summary of the official festival website
and Suyeong-gu’s drone show information.

Shall we take a quick look first?

🔥 Happening Now, Remaining Schedule

The experience booths and stamp tour run all three days,
but the main stage program changes daily.
Here’s a quick overview by date.

DateMain Stage Highlights
10/8 (Thu)Song Festival 10:00~15:00 → Busking → 19:00 Woody
10/9 (Fri)Dance Contest 10:00~16:00 → Dance Performances → 19:00 Ali
10/10 (Sat)Dance & Busking from 13:00 → Creative Play → Drone Show Link → 20:00 Hwang Chi-yeul

Festival hours are until 20:00 each day,
and only on the last day, the 10th, until 21:00.
The schedule may change slightly
as noted in the official info.

🎪 About the Festival

It’s described as a ‘cultural harvest festival’
where the culture and tastes grown throughout Suyeong-gu
come together in Gwangalli.

It’s based on four stages:
finding, connecting, sharing, and spreading
the seeds of culture like people, spaces, and tastes.

The main programs are
live performances and cultural experiences,
as well as competition programs
like the song festival and dance contest.

🎤 Daily Stage Schedule

On the first day, the 8th (Thu),
the song festival is followed by busking,
and at 19:00 there’s a celebratory performance by singer Woody.

The 9th (Fri) stage focuses on dance.
The dance contest features participants
who registered in advance on the website.

with performances by the winning teams

followed by a creative play.

🎫 Experience Booths and Stamp Tour

On the sandy beach,
local businesses and brands from Suyeong-gu
will run themed experience booths all three days.

ExperienceDetails
Themed Experience Booths 10:00~20:00Cafes, bakeries, workshops (leather, rattan), gardening, fashion, accessories, hobbies
Stamp Tour 11:00~20:00Get a sheet at the on-site operations HQ → Visit booths and collect stamps → If you get 8 or more, receive a souvenir

Anyone visiting can join the stamp tour.
Souvenirs are given first-come, first-served each day,
with examples like eco bags, pens, keyrings, and mugs.

Participating businesses and detailed programs
are still listed as ‘to be announced.’

🚁 Saturday Night Drone Show

Gwangalli M Drone Light Show
is a regular drone performance
held every Saturday by Suyeong-gu.

From October, winter hours apply,
so there will be two shows at 19:00 and 21:00.
The October 10th show is titled
’When You Visit Busan in October.’

You can watch from anywhere
along Gwangalli Beach.
However, it may be canceled or delayed
on the day due to rain, strong winds, or communication issues.

🚇 Getting There & Parking

The organizers recommend public transport

The official website expects heavy crowds
and recommends using public transportation.

TransportWhere to Get Off
Subway Line 2Get off at Gwangan Station or Geumnyeonsan Station and walk
BusBus stops near Gwangalli Beach

Parking info is limited to a single line
recommending public transport.

The drone show website announced that
the Suyeong-gu Office underground parking lot (100 Namcheondong-ro)
has been closed indefinitely since August 6.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

If you’re bringing children,
be sure to check out the stamp tour.
Pick up a sheet at the operations HQ
and collect stamps at the booths.

Since souvenirs are first-come, first-served,
it’s best to start before sunset.

On Saturday the 10th, you can enjoy
the afternoon stage, evening drone show,
and celebratory performance all in one place.

🔑 KICKThe drone show is held twice at 19:00 and 21:00
The festival stage wraps up with the 19:00 drone show link
and the 20:00 Hwang Chi-yeul performance
The drone show may be canceled on the day if it’s rainy or windy
