Where to Eat Pho at Magok One Grove (Near Gimpo Airport): Eulji Kkankkan — Family Pho, Croquettes, Spring Rolls and Egg Rice, Seasoning a Bit Strong

Pho at One Grove, Magok 🍜

In November 2025,
we had a family meal
at Eulji Kkankkan (을지깐깐).

Pho — rich broth with bean sprouts, chives and thinly sliced meat; a plate of croquettes behind

📍 165 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu
One Grove, B1
🚇 Magok Station (Line 5)
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
(last order 21:00)
💰 Beef pho
13,000 won
🅿 One Grove underground parking
(free time depends on spend)
👥 Reservations · groups OK

We ordered pho, croquettes,
spring rolls and egg-topped rice,
and it’s one we’d
recommend for a family meal out.

We’ll say this up front, though:
the seasoning runs a little strong.

🍜 Pho — the kids ate it happily

The broth was
rich yet clean.

The thinly sliced meat was tender,
so our kids ate it happily, too!

The bean sprouts and coriander
were fresh and fragrant.

The coriander comes in its own bowl,
so add as much as you like.

Pho, a bowl of coriander and the croquette plate — the kids ate from small side plates

🥟 Croquettes & spring rolls — crisp and well filled

On the side,
we ordered croquettes
and spring rolls.

Both were
crispy and generously filled —
great as sides.

Three croquettes — a crisp breadcrumb coating, served with a little salad

🍳 Egg-topped rice — it’s all about the nuttiness

It comes as a soft fried egg
with a crispy fried topping,
and meat on the side.

The nutty flavor is the highlight.

Egg-topped rice — a soft fried egg with a crispy topping, meat on the side

🧂 The seasoning runs a bit strong

One minus.
The seasoning is
a little on the strong side,
so you may want to adjust it to taste.

Still, the portions are generous
and the service is friendly,
so for a family meal out,
we’d recommend it.

🚗 Parking at One Grove

Just use One Grove’s
underground car park.

✔ First 30 min free
✔ More free time the more you spend

30,000 won or more: 2 hours
50,000 won or more: 3 hours
100,000 won or more: 5 hours

(max 30,000 won a day)
