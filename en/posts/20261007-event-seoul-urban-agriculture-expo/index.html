Seoul Urban Agriculture Expo 2026 at Seoul Forest — Dates, Activities & Parking (Threshing and Grasshopper Catching with Kids)

Two days of green, hands-on fun
come to Seoul Forest this autumn 🌱

The 15th Seoul Urban Agriculture Expo
(서울도시농업박람회)
runs October 16 (Fri) – 17 (Sat)
around the East Park Leisure Center
in Seoul Forest.

There are hands-on activities
like grasshopper catching and threshing,
and admission is free (per Fun Seoul).

We put this together from
Seoul’s October event plan (September 30)
and the organizer’s website.

Shall we take a closer look?

🎪 What kind of event is it?

An expo where you can see, smell,
taste and try ‘urban agriculture’ —
growing plants in the city.

Seoul’s event plan describes it as
”a range of exhibits and activities
to experience the green senses of urban farming
and connect with living things.”

This year’s slogan is
”Companion plants, a green pause for my heart!”
(organizer’s website)

Last year (the 14th) it was held
in May at Boramae Park,
and in 2024 (the 13th)
in May at Yeouido Hangang Park.
This year it moves to Seoul Forest in October.

It’s the first autumn edition
in three years, since 2023
(the 12th, at DDP’s Eoullim Plaza).

✨ What can you see and do?

Both days run 09:00–18:00.
(Start times differ between notices → see ⏳ below)

We’ve put what’s in each zone,
per the organizer’s website, in a table.
The Theme Zone has four farms
plus an exhibit of crops grown in Seoul.

ZoneWhat’s there
Theme Zone · Scent FarmSniff a variety of herbs
Theme Zone · Companion FarmSolve a plant quiz and get a companion-plant card
Theme Zone · Color FarmA color palette of small plants
Theme Zone · Gourmet FarmBite-size garden rice and a tasting
Hands-on ZoneGrasshopper catching · threshing · making egg bundles (gyeran kkureomi) · insect activities, and more

🏡 Garden models to try at home

Four model gardens are on display,
each designed for a school, an office,
a home or a care setting.

ModelWhat kind of garden
① Education (school) typeA garden for observing and recording how plants grow
② Office typeCompanion plants on your desk and a vertical garden
③ Home-life typeA balcony garden that grows ornamental plants together with vegetables and herbs
④ Care & welfare typeA wheelchair-accessible garden and a hydroponic smart farm

🚇 Getting there and parking

The address is
273 Ttukseom-ro, Seongdong-gu, Seoul
(Seoul Forest).
Calling it an eco-friendly event,
the organizers recommend taking public transit.

Here’s how to get there by subway and bus.

TransportStop · exit · walk
Subway Seoul Forest Station (Suin–Bundang Line)Exits 3 & 4 → 2-minute walk
Subway Ttukseom Station (Line 2)Exits 7 & 8 → 11-minute walk
Bus 121Seoul Forest Gate 1
Bus Seongdong 13Gate 2
Bus 123 · 2014 · 2224 · 2413Gate 3

Parking: both lots are open 24 hours.

Parking lotSpaces · fees
Seoul Forest Parking Lot211 spaces · 200 won per 5 min, 2,400 won per hour
Ttukseom Reservoir Public Parking Lot (뚝섬유수지 공영주차장)333 spaces (11 accessible, 34 family-priority) · 150 won per 5 min, 1,800 won per hour

The Ttukseom Reservoir lot has more spaces
and is 600 won cheaper per hour.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Grasshopper catching, threshing
and the insect activities
are all hands-on.
Wet wipes and a change of clothes
will come in handy.

There are also activities
where you look, smell and guess,
like sniffing herbs and the plant quiz.

Also at Seoul Forest,
the Seoul International Garden Expo
(서울국제정원박람회)
continues until October 27.
After the expo, you could carry on
with a walk through the gardens.

🔑 KICKThe companion-plant giveaway is
first come, first served — 2,500 people
(per Fun Seoul)
The giveaway time hasn't been announced yet
Bring a bag to carry your plant pot home
