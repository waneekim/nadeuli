Gimpo Laveniche Festival 2026 — Schedule, Fireworks Time & Performers Before You Take the Kids

It’s the season when autumn sunsets
settle over the Geumbit Canal 🍂

The 2026 Gimpo Laveniche Festival
(김포 라베니체축제)
takes place for one day,
Sat, Oct 17, 13:00–21:00,
around Laveniche
and Hangang Central Park.

Street parades and busking by day,
shows on the floating stage in the evening,
and it all wraps up with
fireworks at 20:30.

We’ve put together
Gimpo City’s press release from October 1
and its culture & tourism festival page.

First, let's see it all at a glance!

🎪 What is this festival?

Gimpo City introduces it as
’Gimpo’s signature waterfront festival’ —
music, fireworks
and things to do at Laveniche,
all in one.

The press release’s headline
says it too: “Enjoy Laveniche to the fullest.”

Laveniche (라베니체)
is a waterfront shopping street
on the Geumbit Canal (금빛수로),
a man-made canal running through
Gimpo’s Hangang New Town.
The canal is 2.68km long in all
and is made up of Laveniche,
a waterside park and the Riverwalk.

Hangang Central Park (한강중앙공원)
is a park whose wide lawn
leads into Laveniche’s waterside park.
The evening’s main floating stage is here.

✨ What’s on, and when?

Laveniche’s streets by day,
the floating stage at Hangang Central Park by evening.
Here it is in time order.

TimeProgram
13:00–18:00Parades & busking — all along Laveniche: marching band and percussion (라퍼커션) parades, acoustic and magic busking
18:00Opening event — main floating stage at Hangang Central Park, pre-show and opening ceremony
After the openingCelebration concert — jazz band Jo-un Trio (조운트리오), John Park, Bada and more
From 20:30, about 15 minFireworks show — launched from inside Laveniche
21:00Festival ends — people leave in turns, sector by sector

🚇 Getting there & road closures

Nearby roads close for the fireworks

The festival is held around Laveniche
and Hangang Central Park.
The address is 40 Chodang-ro, Gimpo.

Full closures for the fireworks
Nearby roads and footbridges
where debris could fall,
measured from the launch site inside Laveniche,
will be closed from before the fireworks start.
Gimpo Police Station, the local model drivers’ association
and others will manage the closures together.

Which roads will be closed
isn’t in the notice yet.

SituationGimpo City’s measures
When the festival endsLeaving sector by sector · shorter intervals on the Gimpo Goldline · safety staff on hand
When crowds build upAbout 500 safety staff · joint control room (at Hangang Central Park)
If someone gets sick or hurtMedical booths — separate ones at Hangang Central Park and Laveniche
If phone signals dropMobile base stations from all three carriers, in place from the day before the festival

There’s no parking information
in the official notices yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids?

The parades and magic busking
run 13:00–18:00 on Laveniche’s streets.
You could see the street shows
and the flea market in the daytime,
then move to the opening stage at 18:00.

The fireworks start at 20:30 and last about 15 min,
and the festival ends at 21:00.
It’ll be after dark,
so pack an extra layer for the kids.

Gimpo City says it’s preparing
for big crowds, so agree in advance
on a place to meet
in case you get separated from your kids.

🔑 KICKBefore the fireworks (20:30) start
nearby roads and footbridges close
and afterwards you leave sector by sector
Plan your way home first
