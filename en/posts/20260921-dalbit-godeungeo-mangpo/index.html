Where to Eat Grilled Fish in Mangpo, Suwon — Dalbit-e Gu-un Godeungeo: Crispy Mackerel and a Self-Serve Side-Dish Bar with Rolled Omelet and Kimchi Pancakes

Our grilled-fish review from Mangpo, Suwon 🐟

This past March,
a coworker and I
went without a reservation to
Dalbit-e Gu-un Godeungeo (달빛에구운고등어),
the Suwon Mangpo branch
(the name means
’mackerel grilled in the moonlight’).

A platter of grilled fish — mackerel and several other fish on one plate

📍 2F, 28 Deogyeong-daero
1499beon-gil, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon
⏰ Daily 11:00–21:30
Last order 20:40
💰 Grilled mackerel, half a fish
13,000 won
Mixed grill for 2: 39,000 won
🅿 1 hour 30 min free parking
📞 +82-31-204-3392

To cut to the chase,
it was a meal
better than we expected!

The grilled fish was good,
but this is the kind of place
where you want to talk
about the side dishes first.

🐟 Crispy outside, moist inside

First, the main event:
the grilled fish
came out nicely done.

Crispy outside, moist inside!

Savory, with no fishy smell,
so the rice disappears fast.
A true “rice thief,” as Koreans say.

The portion was really generous, too.

White flesh under golden, crispy skin

🥢 The real draw: self-serve side dishes

What we remember most
is the self-serve side-dish corner,
run buffet-style.

You can help yourself
to neatly laid-out side dishes
as much as you like,
without feeling awkward about it.

Great value for money
and real satisfaction, both at once!

Side dishes from the self-serve corner — six kinds, a little on each plate

Even the labor-intensive ones,
like rolled omelet
and kimchi pancake,
were excellent.

My coworker and I
kept marveling as we ate.

Kimchi pancake and rolled omelet from the self-serve corner

🔑 KICKSide dishes from the self-serve corner
with unlimited refills!
Rolled omelet and kimchi pancake too —
help yourself, no need to hold back

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone who wants to load up
on side dishes without feeling awkward
✔ Anyone looking for savory
grilled fish with no fishy smell
✔ Anyone after a filling meal

Fish that’s crispy outside and moist inside,
