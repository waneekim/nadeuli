Suwon Arboretum Autumn Flower Festival 2026 — Schedule, Parking & the Night Garden Before You Take the Kids to Ilwol Arboretum

News of an autumn arboretum festival
with cosmos and chrysanthemums in bloom 🌸

The 2026 Suwon Arboretum Autumn Flower Festival
(2026 수원수목원 가을꽃축제)
is on for one day, Sat, Oct 24,
at Ilwol Arboretum in Suwon.

That same evening, the after-dark opening
’Night Garden’ (밤빛정원) carries on.

We put this together from
Ilwol Arboretum’s notice (October 1)
and Suwon city’s press releases.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It celebrates Ilwol Arboretum
being picked as one of the
’10 Arboretums You Must Visit in 2026’
in a Korea Forest Service contest.

Autumn flower displays, hands-on activities,
a flea market and photo zones
are planned.
The exhibitions come in three parts.

ExhibitionWhat’s on show
With a partner institutionRose, chrysanthemum and succulent variety displays by the Gyeonggi-do Agricultural Research & Extension Services
With local partnersGwanggyo Chrysanthemum Bonsai Club · Suwon Agricultural Life Science High School
Autumn flowersCosmos variety display · ornamental grass exhibition ‘The Colors the Grasses Left, Autumn’ · autumn flower path

Here’s the timetable for Saturday, October 24.
We’ve put the daytime festival
and the Night Garden in one table.

On the garden concert line-up:
Park Seung-hwa of Yurisangja (유리상자 박승화),
Ijel (이젤), a brass quintet
from the Suwon Philharmonic Orchestra,
Kim Ji-hyun (김지현, gugak — Korean traditional music)
and Baek Da-na (백다나, vocals).
Announcer Yun Mi-ji (윤미지) hosts.

🌙 The Night Garden is the same day

The Night Garden is the arboretum’s
after-dark opening every Friday
and Saturday in October.
Festival day is one of those days,
so you can see the gardens until 21:00.

Ilwol Arboretum decorates its gardens
with pink flowers such as garden mums,
asters and pink muhly grass,
and in the Grass Garden more than 130 kinds —
fountain grass, silver grass, pampas grass and more —
grow side by side.

In the exhibition glasshouse
you can also see the exhibition
’Thumbelina, the Girl Born from a Flower’.

Night programWho · how to join
Night Garden evening walkFamilies, 20 people · book on the website · 5,000 won (admission included)
‘Bambitna’ glowing flowerpot makingAnyone · book on the website · 5,000 won (admission not included)
Hanji pressed-flower lantern makingAnyone · pay 1,000 won on site at the info desk · 50 a day
Stamp tourAnyone · Free · finish it to get an arboretum sticker

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The festival notice doesn’t mention admission,
so here are the arboretum’s regular fees.

VisitorIndividual (Suwon residents · multi-child families)
Adults (19 and over)4,000 won (2,000 won · 1,000 won)
Youth (13–18)2,500 won (1,500 won · 500 won)
Children (7–12)1,500 won (1,000 won · 500 won)
6 and underFree

A multi-child family means
two or more children,
with the youngest 18 or under.
You need to show proof
for discounts and free entry.

No outside food (drinks are OK),
and pets can’t come in.
Strollers are allowed,
and there’s a nursing room
on the 1st floor of the Visitor Center.

🔑 KICKThe evening walk and Bambitna pots
need advance booking on the website
Last entry to the gardens is 20:30
