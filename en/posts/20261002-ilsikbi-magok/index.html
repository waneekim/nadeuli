Where to Eat in Magok: Ilsikbi’s Lunch Special at 29,000 Won a Person — Sashimi, Sushi, Fried Shrimp and Stone-Pot Rice in One Course

Our Japanese lunch review from Magok 🍣

Near LG Science Park,
on the 3rd floor of the Ace Plaza building,
we had the lunch special at Ilsikbi (일식비).

The star of the lunch special — sashimi in a wooden box, with small side dishes

📍 61 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu
Ace Plaza 3F
(5 min walk from Balsan Station)
🚇 Balsan is on Line 5,
3 stops from Gimpo Airport
⏰ 11:20–22:00
Break 15:00–17:00
Last order 21:00
Closed Sundays
💰 Lunch special 29,000 won
(per person)
🅿 2 hours free in the building
📞 02-2658-3555

Bottom line first:
it’s a place we’d definitely recommend.

It’s especially right
for a workday lunch
or a team dinner (hoesik).

🚪 Entrance and dining room

The sign has a red ‘日食’ (Japanese food) seal,
‘비’ (bi) in brush calligraphy,
and ‘SASHIMI & SUSHI’.

Ilsikbi's entrance — the '日食 비 SASHIMI & SUSHI' sign

The poster at the door says,
“Japanese food that changes
the standard for get-togethers.”

Its four icons:
private rooms available,
a generous spread,
ideal for gatherings & team dinners,
and best value for money.

The entrance wall and poster — 'Japanese food that changes the standard for get-togethers'

Inside: green walls, wooden partitions
and leaf decorations on the ceiling.

Partitions and blinds
divide the tables,
so even with a big group
it feels less crowded.

The dining room, divided by green walls and wooden partitions

🍱 The lunch special, in the order it came

It doesn’t all arrive at once —
it’s a course, served dish by dish.

Here it is
in the order we took the photos.

① Salad and porridge

A salad with a yellow dressing
and crushed peanuts,
and a bowl of warm porridge (juk)

Salad — yellow dressing and crushed peanuts

A bowl of warm porridge

② Sashimi — the star of the day

Several kinds of sashimi
come in the compartments
of a wooden box,
with side dishes in
three small bowls in the middle.

On a plate beside it:
kimchi for wrapping the sashimi!

The paper on the table explains
’How to enjoy sashimi even more.’

A slice of sashimi with wasabi (GIF)

A sheet of kimchi on your plate,

③ Croquettes

Two crispy croquettes

Two croquettes with sauce

④ Grilled dish and sushi

Next came grilled food with vegetables
and a board of sushi,
including shrimp and salmon.

Grilled dish with vegetables

Sushi — shrimp, salmon and white fish

⑤ Fried shrimp and soup

Breaded fried shrimp

Fried shrimp and spicy soup

⑥ Stone-pot rice

Last came stone-pot rice (dolsot-bap)
topped with crumbled gim (seaweed), sesame,
yellow pickled radish and kimchi.

Stone-pot rice to finish

We sat down a little before noon,
and it took just over 40 minutes
to reach the stone-pot rice.

It didn’t stop at a box of sashimi —
warm dishes and rice followed,
so lunch left us really full!

💼 Just right for a workday lunch or team dinner

For the two of us, it came to 58,000 won
(29,000 won per person).

With sashimi, sushi, fried food,
grilled food, soup and rice all coming,
even a group with different tastes
can all eat well.

There are lots of partitioned seats,
and the poster says
’private rooms available’
and ‘ideal for gatherings & team dinners’.

Naver Place says
group dining and reservations
are available.

🚗 Parking

Parking in the building
is free for 2 hours.

After that it’s 1,000 won per 10 min,
says the sign at the entrance.

Another notice says
that when the car park is busy,
you can park at Magok NY Tower
across the street.
(Naver Place: when full, NY Tower parking
is covered for 3 hours —
tell the counter when you pay)
