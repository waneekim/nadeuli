My First Walmart in the US: Much Bigger Than Emart Traders, a Good ChatGPT Snack Pick, and the Laptop Charger I Bought Instead of a Power Bank (US Business Trip, Day 1)

Day 1 of my US business trip,
the last story 🛒

On the first night of my business trip
to Columbia, South Carolina,
I stopped by a US Walmart.

A real Walmart was
an unbelievably huge store.

Walmart at night — the Grocery entrance is on the right

📍 Walmart, USA
(on a business trip to Columbia, South Carolina)
🏬 Much bigger than
Emart Traders
👥 Quiet, not crowded
💵 Prices about the same as Korea
🛍 Dark Chocolate Almonds ·
Starbucks Blonde Roast iced coffee ·
(mistake) a 100W laptop charger

🏬 Much bigger than Emart Traders

It seemed to be
much bigger than
Emart Traders, the big Korean warehouse store.

But the place was
quiet, which was nice.

The bakery and dessert corner right inside the entrance — muffins $3.98 (Rollback)

There were more items
and lower prices than I expected.
I felt that prices were about the same as in Korea.

The bottled coffee fridge — Stok cold brew $6.48

Monster energy drinks — $2.68–$2.78 per can

Bottled water was

Cheetos Puffs Party Size, bigger than a hand

The Halloween decor corner — skeleton and pumpkin decorations

Already Christmas — the Holiday Shop and red ribbons

🤖 Two things I bought on ChatGPT’s recommendation

While looking at all kinds of things,

① bettergoods

bettergoods Dark Chocolate Almonds (340 g)

🔌 I thought it was a power bank…

I needed a power bank
and bought one,
but it turned out
I had bought a laptop adapter (charger)
by mistake.

What I bought, spread out in the lodging — the wrongly bought 100W charger is at the bottom left

It really hurts to waste the money.
Since it’s 100W,
if it turns out useful at home,
I’ll try to put it to good use.

🔑 KICKIn the US, look for a power bank
under 'Power Bank'
If the box only says 'Charger'
it's a charger that plugs into the wall

🌎 The world is wide and the stores are big

Having come to Walmart in the US,
I felt that the world is wide and the stores are big.

👍 Recommended for

✔ Anyone going to buy water
and snacks on the first day
of a US business trip or vacation
✔ Anyone curious about
US supermarket prices
✔ Anyone who needs to buy a power bank
(check the name carefully!)
