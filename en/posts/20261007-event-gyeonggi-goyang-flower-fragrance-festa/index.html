Goyang Flower Fragrance Festa 2026 at Starfield Goyang — Dates, Stamp Tour & Free Parking Before You Go with Kids

October is here,
full of the scent of autumn flowers 🌸

The 2026 Goyang Flower Fragrance Festa
(2026고양꽃향기페스타)
runs Sat Oct 17 – Sun Oct 25,
daily 10:00–20:00 for 9 days,
on the 1F east outdoor plaza of Starfield Goyang.

We put this together from
Goyang city’s September 29 press release,
the festival poster
and Starfield Goyang’s visitor information.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Now in its 2nd year,
the Flower Fragrance Festa is
a ‘pop-up flower festival’
where flower farmers meet shoppers directly.

This year’s theme is
’Blooming Planet’ (블루밍 플래닛).
It imagines the one planet
in a barren universe
that’s full of flowers and plants.

Goyang city puts it this way:
the Ilsan Lake Park
Autumn Flower Festival (Oct 1–11)
is about walking and looking at flowers,
while the Flower Fragrance Festa
is about meeting flowers through play and hands-on fun.

Goyang Special City
and the Goyang International Expo Foundation
put it together.
They held the two events back to back
so residents can meet flowers and plants more often
and enjoy them in everyday life.

✨ What’s on?

It runs at the same hours all 9 days.
Here are the programs
from the press release in a table.

ProgramDetails
Space Flower CollectionStamp tour — find the ‘space flowers’ hidden in 12 flower-farm booths
Photo zonesPhoto spots that combine space and flowers
Pet spaceA spot to make memories with your pet
Flower-farm boothsSee flowers and plants up close, pick one you like and take it home

The poster also lists keywords like
Flower Market, companion plants,
Bloom Garden and Flower Explorers.
There are no details yet
on how each of them works.

🚀 How does the ‘Space Flower Collection’ work?

The setup: visitors become astronauts
who crash-landed on an unknown planet.

It’s a tour where you
find the space flowers and collect stamps
hidden in 12 flower-farm booths,
with Goyang’s city character Goyang Goyangi
(고양고양이, the “Goyang cat”)
as your exploration guide.

The festival grounds are tied into one story
so that going from booth to booth
you meet all kinds of flowers and plants.

Goyang city pictures
picking out one plant you like
and taking it home as an everyday thing
as the festival’s final scene.

What you get for collecting them all
isn’t in the notice yet.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Parking at Starfield Goyang is free

The festival is on Starfield Goyang’s
1F east outdoor plaza.
The address is
1955 Goyang-daero, Deogyang-gu, Goyang.

How to get thereDetails
Subway500 m from Samsong Station (Line 3), exit 3 — about 8 min on foot
CarStarfield Goyang car park — parking is free
NavigationSearch for ‘Starfield Goyang’ (스타필드고양)
Car park entrancesNorth & west entrances lead to surface parking; south & annex entrances lead to underground parking

This is per Starfield Goyang’s own guide.
The notices don’t mention
any changes during the festival.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The stamp tour means going round
the 12 booths with your kids
looking for flowers.

According to Starfield Goyang,
you can borrow a stroller free at the 1F stroller rental desk
(1F, Hall 3).
They’re for children from 2 to under 36 months
and up to 15 kg.
The nursing room is in the 3F baby lounge.
If you lose sight of your child,
the 1F customer service center looks after lost children.

If you plan to take a pet into the mall,
only vaccinated dogs and cats
on a leash (1.5 m or shorter) or in a carrier
are allowed in.
Pets can’t go into cafés or restaurants.

The festival ends at 20:00,
and Starfield Goyang is open until 22:00.

🔑 KICKThe festival isn't inside the mall —
it's on the 1F east 'outdoor plaza'
There's no word yet on rainy days,
so check the weather before you set out
