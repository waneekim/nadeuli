Thomson Park Beach on Sullivan’s Island: We Saw Dolphins from the Shore, No Boat Needed! (US Business Trip, Day 7)

After looking around downtown Charleston,
we stopped by a small park by the sea,
Thomson Park 🐬

It was a really small park,
but we saw something amazing.
Dolphins!

A video from the beach: a dolphin's back glides across the water (a GIF zoomed in on the dolphin)

📍 Thomson Park
(Sullivan's Island, South Carolina, USA,
next to Breach Inlet)
🐬 Dolphins from the beach, no boat needed!
🏖️ A small, quiet seaside park
⚠️ Strong current: no swimming or wading

🏖️ A Small Park and a Quiet Beach

Thomson Park is at the end of
Sullivan’s Island,
next to Breach Inlet,
the channel between the island and the next one.

The park was really small,
and the beach was wide and quiet.

A wide, quiet stretch of sand with dry blades of grass scattered around

The beach next to the sand dunes: a big ship on the horizon and beach houses on the right

🐬 We Saw Dolphins from the Beach

When we went to Guam and Hawaii before,
we took a boat
really far out to see dolphins.

But here,
we could see dolphins from the beach!

At the start and the end of the video,
you can see two dolphins.

Their dark backs come up
out of the water like shark fins
and glide past.

Another dolphin passing by at the end of the video (a GIF zoomed in on the dolphin)

They showed up
much more often than what I caught on video.

We often even saw them rising up
with their whole tail showing.

KICKA dolphin spot where you can see dolphins
from the beach, no boat needed! 🐬

⚠️ Please Don’t Go into the Water

There is a DEADLY CURRENTS sign
on the beach, a warning about dangerous currents.

No swimming, and no wading either,
and the fine for breaking the rule is reportedly $1,040.

The DEADLY CURRENTS warning sign: no swimming or wading, $1,040 fine

📜 The Park Is Named After Col. Thomson

The story goes that the park’s name
comes from Col. William Thomson
of the American Revolutionary War.

On June 28, 1776,
the American troops led by Col. Thomson
reportedly held off the British who tried to cross this channel.

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone who wants to see dolphins
without taking a boat
✔ Anyone who likes
quiet walks by the sea
✔ Anyone looking for a small
spot near Charleston

It was a small park
with a dolphin spot!
