Icheon Rice Culture Festival 2026 at Bokhacheon Riverside Park — Dates, Shuttle Buses, Parking & Tips for Kids

It’s harvest season,
when the air smells of new rice 🌾

This year marks the 25th!
The 25th Icheon Rice Culture Festival
(제25회 이천쌀문화축제)
runs Wed, Oct 14 – Sun, Oct 18,
for five days.

This year it has moved
to a new venue:
around Bokhacheon Riverside Park
(복하천 수변공원).

We put together the schedule
and getting-there info
from the official festival website.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s slogan is
’Everything Made from Rice, Made In Icheon’.

The August 14 press release says
the festival will fill the roomier new venue
together with events like the Bubal Daljip (bonfire) Festival
and the Icheon Advanced Drone Expo.

The grounds are split into
several ‘madang’ (yards), such as the
Fairy Tale Yard (동화마당), Play Yard (놀이마당),
Sky Yard (하늘마당) and Cauldron Yard (가마솥마당).

are different on weekdays and weekends.

The activity booths are gathered
in the Harvest Yard (풍년마당):
Icheon rice lettering balloons, rice-grain hand warmers,
making scarecrows and jangseung (village totem poles),
face painting, dalgona,
making traditional kites and bows, and more.
Activity fees aren’t in the official info.

🍚 Food & market

At the Food Yard (먹거리마당), Icheon’s 14 towns and districts
serve up local dishes.
The listed menu includes sundae-guk (blood sausage soup), suyuk (boiled pork),
rice noodles, gimbap
and janchi-guksu (banquet noodles).

Rice you buy at the New Rice Market (햅쌀장터)
can be sent home from the free parcel delivery desk.
The Cauldron Yard
also lists injeolmi (rice cake) making.

🚌 Getting there & parking

There's no parking at the festival grounds

The website calls it a car-free, eco-friendly festival,
so there’s no parking inside the festival grounds.

Shuttle buses run daily from the 14th to the 18th,
08:30 to 18:30.
The first buses leave Icheon Station and the bus terminal at 08:30;
the last one leaves the festival at 18:30.

Shuttle routeRoute & frequency
① Icheon StationIcheon Station → Hyundai Hometown → Icheon City Hall → Iseopdaecheon Park → festival (Bokhacheon Campground); every 20 min on weekdays · every 10 min on weekends
② Bus terminalTerminal ↔ festival loop; every 20 min on weekdays · every 10 min on weekends
③ Songjeong-dong (Sinil Apartments)Sat & Sun only, every 10 to 20 min

The stops have moved this year,
so check the stop photos on the website first.

By subway, get off at Icheon Station (이천역) on the Gyeonggang Line
and change to the shuttle.

Parking fees aren’t in the official info.

The website gives the address as 17 Gyeongchung-daero 2422beon-gil;
on maps it shows up as Iseopdaecheon Park (이섭대천공원).

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Cauldron rice is served twice, at 12:00 and 14:00,
so lunch at the festival is easy.

On weekends the shuttle runs every 10 minutes,
and the Fairy Tale Yard bubble show is weekends only too.

From the shuttle drop-off it’s 100 steps to the entrance,
and electric cart buses run often too.

🔑 KICKDrone light show: the 17th at 18:00 (planned)
The last shuttle leaves the festival at 18:30
If you're staying for the show,
plan your way home first
