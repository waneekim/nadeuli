2026 Gwangju Seochang Silver Grass Festival Dates, Shuttle Bus & Activity Times – Before Walking the Yeongsan River Path with Kids

Silver grass along the Yeongsan River
is swaying silver right now 🌾

The 11th Gwangju Seochang Silver Grass Festival (광주서창억새축제)
runs October 15 (Thu) to 18 (Sun),
four days, daily 13:00–18:00,
along the Yeongsan riverside (Geungnakgyo to Seochanggyo Nanumnuri Forest).

I’ve summarized Seo-gu’s press releases and the festival website’s
timetable (updated October 8).

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

This year’s theme is
”An Autumn Stroll Brought by Silver Grass.”
It’s designed as a “strolling festival”
for walking, lingering and resting.
Admission is free,
while some activities and food charge a fee.

The roughly 400 m from the main stage entrance
to the small stage is a
Silver Grass Path with Melodies to walk with music.

Every day 13:00–18:00 there’s
the SG Silver Grass Lounge with LP music,
a Sound Walk Map that gives a souvenir
once you find 5 sound spots and fill in the map,
and four silver grass photo zones.

On the 15th, 16th and 18th, a sentimental trolley bus
loops festival site → Seochanggyo → Geungnakgyo
in 20 minutes.

✨ What’s On and When?

Here are the main stage events by date
from the festival website’s timetable.

Date · TimeProgram
10/15 (Thu) 17:00Opening ceremony (main stage)

10/17 (Sat) 16:00Silver Grass Sunset Marathon start (Sangmu Citizens’ Park → festival site)
10/18 (Sun) 13:00–14:30Wal Wal RUN walk with your dog
10/18 (Sun) 15:30–17:00Find the Rest King (main stage)

Every day 17:00–17:30 there’s
Sunset Live on the Seochang Gamseong Observatory,
and 17:30–18:00 is Noeul 530.
A professional photographer takes your photo
against the sunset for free and prints it, too.

Silver grass busking takes place
several times a day on the main and small stages.

Marathon registration
closed on September 28.
The September press release listed
Find the Rest King as 15:00–17:00.

🧒 Activities for Kids

Here are activities worth trying with kids.

ActivityTime · Details
Ttubeok Ttubeok Silver Grass Explorers14:00 · 15:00 · 16:00, 20 per session, 40-minute mission walk
Silver Grass Soundscape13:30 · 15:30 · 17:00, 10 per session (5,000 won if you choose a sound card)
1,000-won Picnic13:00–18:00 at Chakhan Seogu Zone, bring ID
Sea Glass Upcycling15th, 17th, 18th, coaster making, free
Forest Education15th, 16th, for families with toddlers and children
Kids’ pony riding17th–18th, on-site registration (Seo-gu press release)

On the 18th, the timetable shows the Explorers
only twice, at 15:00 and 16:00.

The 1,000-won picnic comes as a lounging set
(mat, air bed, tent), an outdoor play set
like bubble guns and badminton, and a “Fabre” set
with a digital microscope and insect kit.

Resident-run craft booths (ocarina, pottery wheel,
silver grass wreath making and more)
and an F&B flea market selling food and farm produce
are near the main stage.

🚌 Getting There & Parking

The shuttle runs every 20 minutes

To ease traffic and parking congestion,
Seo-gu runs a shuttle bus.

may move from near the Geungnak Riverside Park parking lot

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

The festival opens at 13:00.
If you arrive after lunch,
you can go 14:00 Explorers → 1,000-won picnic →
17:30 Noeul 530.

Show a receipt of 30,000 won or more
from a Seo-gu alley shopping district (from October 5)
with your ID at the festival verification booth
to get 10,000 won in Onnuri gift certificates.
It’s first come, first served, 500 people a day.

🔑 KICKPre-registration closes on the 11th (Sun) at 18:00
It covers the Explorers, Soundscape,
Rest King and Wal Wal RUN
After that, it's on-site, first come, first served
