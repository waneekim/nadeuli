Where to Eat Near Pyeongtaek Station: Beef Onmyeon at Hyundai Jeongyukjeom — Clean, Deep Broth, Garlic and Chili to Taste

A bowl of soup by Pyeongtaek Station 🍜

In June last year,
near Pyeongtaek Station,
I had beef onmyeon
(a warm noodle soup)
at Hyundai Jeongyukjeom (현대정육점),
the Pyeongtaek Station branch.

Beef onmyeon — beef, bean sprouts and green onion in a clear broth

📍 Pyeongtaek, Gyeonggi-do
27-1 Pyeongtaek 1-ro, 1F–2F
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
Last order 21:00
💰 Onmyeon 12,000 won
(brisket or beef tendon)
🅿 M Plaza across the street, basement
1-hour free parking ticket
📞 0507-1426-8054

It was a hearty meal
that really filled me up,
so here’s a short note.

🍲 A clean, deep broth

What stayed with me most
was the broth.

It’s clean, yet deep in flavor.

The beef was simmered
until soft and tender,
and the bean sprouts were crunchy —
a really nice texture.

Broth, beef and bean sprouts —
each one does its part.

🧄 Garlic and chili, as you like

The tray — a bowl of onmyeon with three side dishes; minced garlic and chili come separately

The side dishes
come neatly laid out.

Minced garlic and sliced chili
are served on the side,
so you can add them to taste.

For me,
it tasted better with them in.

🔑 KICKMinced garlic and chili
come on the side
I liked it better
with them stirred in

🤫 Quiet, so eating alone is easy

The restaurant is quiet and clean,
and I felt at ease
the whole meal.

Even if you come alone,
it’s a relaxed place to eat.

📋 Menu and prices

Brisket onmyeon (rice noodles) 12,000 won
Beef tendon onmyeon (rice noodles) 12,000 won
Special mixed onmyeon (rice noodles) 14,000 won
Naju-style Hanwoo beef head gomtang 12,000 won
Clear king galbitang (beef rib soup) 18,000 won

(as listed on Naver Place)

I didn’t note down
whether my ‘beef onmyeon’
was the brisket or the tendon one.

🚗 Parking

Park in the underground lot
of M Plaza (the Daiso building) across the street
and you get a 1-hour free parking ticket.

For longer stays,
they ask you to use
Jaran Public Parking (paid).

(per the notice on Naver Place)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone near Pyeongtaek Station
looking for a bowl to eat solo
✔ Anyone who loves
a clear, deep broth
✔ Anyone who likes adding
garlic and chili to taste

A place where I filled up

Next time I need a hearty meal
