Bucheon International Animation Festival (BIAF) 2026 — Schedule, Free Open Air Cinema & Ani-together Before You Take the Kids

It’s autumn in Bucheon,
and the animation film festival
is back in town 🎬

The 28th Bucheon International Animation Festival
(제28회 부천국제애니메이션페스티벌, BIAF2026)
runs Fri Oct 23 – Tue Oct 27
at the Webtoon Complex Center, CGV Bucheon,
the Korea Manhwa Museum, Bucheon City Hall
and other venues around Bucheon.

We’ve put this together
from BIAF’s official website,
focusing on programs to see with kids.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

BIAF is an animation film festival
built around its international competition.
It’s also Korea’s first
Academy Award®-qualifying festival.

This year’s slogan is
’Everyone’s Animation (모두의 애니메이션)’.

One thing to know first!
The main festival is made up of paid screenings.
You need a ticket for each film.

TicketPrice
Opening Ceremony (includes the opening film)30,000 won
General Screening · Program Class8,000 won
Off the Map (지역의 발견)15,000 won
Ani-together (애니투게더)4,000 won
Open Air Cinema (outdoor screenings)Free

The opening film, Tangles (〈탱글스〉),
is listed in the schedule
as rated 15+.

🌙 When is the free Open Air Cinema?

There are two evening outdoor screenings
on the Lawn Square at Bucheon City Hall.

They’re free to watch,
and the festival recommends
bringing a picnic mat.

Date & timeFilm (Bucheon City Hall, Lawn Square)
Oct 23 (Fri) 19:30–21:30David (〈다윗〉)
Oct 24 (Sat) 19:30–21:30Heartspring: Legend of the Whale Gem (〈사랑의 하츄핑: 고래보석의 전설〉)

The screenings may be canceled if it rains,
and the films may change
at the organizer’s discretion.

🐱 What’s showing at Ani-together?

Ani-together has 4 films,
all rated G (all ages).
In the schedule, all four are marked
’Korean dialogue’ (no English subtitles).

Date & time (venue)Film · length
Oct 23 (Fri) 10:00 (Bucheon City Hall)Wish Cat Magic Card (위시캣 매직카드) · 48 min
Oct 23 (Fri) 11:30 (Bucheon City Hall)Princess Catch! Teenieping (프린세스 캐치! 티니핑) · 48 min
Oct 26 (Mon) 10:30 (Bucheon City Hall)Metal Cardbot W (메탈카드봇W) · 48 min
Oct 26 (Mon) 10:30 (Korea Manhwa Museum)Sylvanian Families Freya’s Secret Tales (실바니안 패밀리: 프레야의 피스 오브 시크릿) · 59 min

The City Hall screenings are in
the Main Theater on the 2nd floor (어울마당).

All four are weekday-morning screenings —
there are none on the weekend (24th–25th).

🎟️ How do I get tickets?

Online booking opens Fri Oct 16 at 14:00.
All seats are sold online,
and only films that haven’t sold out
can also be bought on site.

Booking & cancellationWhen & where
OnlineFrom Oct 16 (Fri) 14:00 until each screening starts
On-site ticket boothsFrom Oct 23 (Fri) 09:30 · CGV Bucheon 6F (until the 25th), Korea Manhwa Museum 1F (until the 26th)
CancellationOnline up to 1 hour before, on site up to 30 min before · same-day cancellations cost 1,000 won per ticket
On-site discount for families with 2+ minor childrenGeneral Screenings only, 5,000 won (2 or more minor children, for the eligible person only, bring proof)

Mobile tickets are recommended,
and a screenshot of the QR code
won’t get you in.

🖼️ There are exhibitions too

On the 1st floor of the Korea Manhwa Museum,
BIAF exhibitions run
from Tue Oct 20 to Sun Nov 1.

There’s also an exhibition
(‘BIAF Special: Project D’)
that previews the characters, concept art
and making of Dokkaebi Bread (〈도깨비빵〉),
a feature film set for cinema release in 2027.

🚇 Getting there & parking

The Open Air Cinema is on the Bucheon City Hall Lawn Square
VenueSubway · parking
Bucheon City Hall (Open Air Cinema · 3 Ani-together films)Bucheon City Hall Station (Line 7), exit 1, 2 min walk · 454 parking spaces
Korea Manhwa Museum (exhibitions · Sylvanian Families)Samsan Gymnasium Station (Line 7), exit 5, 1 min walk · free at the Korea Manhwa Contents Agency (한국만화영상진흥원) lot; if full, the public lot is 400 won for 30 min

BIAF’s venue guide page
is still being prepared,
so it’s not yet known how parking
will work during the festival.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

If you’re going with kids on the weekend,
the Heartspring outdoor screening on Sat the 24th
is the best fit.
It ends at 21:30,
so bring a mat and a jacket.

Ani-together is on Friday and Monday mornings,
so check it against your daycare
and school schedules.

No outside food is allowed at CGV Bucheon,
and the other screening venues allow
only bottled water with a cap.

🔑 KICKMost festival screenings are paid
With kids, start with the free Open Air Cinema
and the 4,000-won Ani-together films
Booking opens October 16 at 14:00
