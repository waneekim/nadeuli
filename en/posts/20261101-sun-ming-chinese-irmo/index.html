Sun Ming, the First Chinese Restaurant I Tried in the US: Great Mapo Tofu, Huge Portions! (US Business Trip, Day 7)

Dinner on Day 7 was
at a Chinese restaurant near our hotel 🥢

Sun Ming!
It was the first Chinese restaurant I tried in the US,
and it was tastier than I expected.

The round table full of dishes (faces of our group are pixelated)

📍 Sun Ming Chinese Restaurant
(Irmo, South Carolina, USA
7509 St Andrews Rd)
🥢 The first Chinese restaurant I tried in the US
🌶️ Mapo tofu, stir-fry and rice noodles were excellent
🍽️ One dish was about a Korean medium for three
🍹 Drink: cranberry juice

🏮 An Entrance Guarded by Lion Statues

When we arrived at night,
a red sign in Chinese characters
was glowing on the pointed roof.

On both sides of the entrance,
white lion statues stood guard.

Sun Ming's entrance at night — the red sign 食為先 and lion statues (people's faces are pixelated)

A white lion statue guarding the entrance

🐟 A Spacious Hall Behind Fish-Etched Glass

Inside, you see a spacious hall
through glass etched with fish and lotus leaves.

The menu cover also had
SUN MING Chinese Restaurant
and two fish on it.

The hall behind glass etched with fish and lotus leaves (diners' faces are pixelated)

The SUN MING menu cover

🍽️ One Dish Each, and the Portions Were Huge

We ordered the way we do in Korea:
one dish per person.

But as expected in the US,
the portions were huge.
One dish came out at about the level of a Korean medium
size for about three people.

Mapo tofu, fried rice,
two plates of stir-fried wide rice noodles
and two pots of noodle soup
filled the round table.

🌶️ We Finished Every Bit of the Mapo Tofu

The mapo tofu, the stir-fry and the rice noodles
were especially excellent.

The mapo tofu was a spicy sauce
with tofu, ground meat and peppers,
and we finished it all without leaving any.

Mapo tofu — a spicy sauce with tofu, ground meat and peppers

🍜 The Rice Noodles Were Excellent Too

The plate of wide rice noodles
stir-fried with beef, bean sprouts and green onions
was really good too.

I think we ate
about half of each of the rice noodle dishes.

Stir-fried wide rice noodles — beef, bean sprouts and green onions

Noodles served in a pot — beef and bok choy

If we hadn’t been full,
honestly we could have eaten more,
but in the end we left a lot.

KICKAt US Chinese restaurants, skip one dish each:
order about three dishes
and share them all together! 🥢

So from now on,
we decided to order just three dishes.
From here on, we’ll always order
dishes to share.

🍹 The Drink Was Cranberry Juice

For a drink, I ordered cranberry juice.
It was a little tastier
than other drinks you can buy in stores.

A glass of cranberry juice (the face of the person behind is pixelated)

👍 Recommended For

✔ When you’re craving
Chinese food in the US
✔ Anyone who
loves mapo tofu
✔ Groups who want to
share several dishes

The portions are huge, but
it was a tastier Chinese restaurant than I expected!
