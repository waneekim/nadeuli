Where to Eat Sushi in Cheongna, Incheon — Sushi Katsu’s ‘Today’s Sushi’ Lunch with a Spicy Ramyeon

Our Cheongna sushi lunch 🍣

We went for lunch at
Sushi Katsu (스시카츠),
a sushi restaurant on the food street
by Cheongna’s Apartment Complex 4.

Sushi Katsu, Cheongna — the 'sushi restaurant' sign is huge

📍 15 Cheongnakeonael-ro 233beon-gil
Seohae-gu, Incheon
(in front of Complex 4's main gate)
⏰ Daily 11:30–22:00
Break time 15:00–17:00
(Sat & Sun: from 15:30)
💰 Today's Sushi, 10 pcs
16,900 won
🅿 Parking · takeout · delivery
📞 032-562-2245

🏠 ‘The Place with Great Sushi in Cheongna’

Right on the glass entrance door,
in big letters, it says
’the place with great sushi in Cheongna.’

To give you the verdict first:
it lived up to the sign!

The glass door under the sign says it too: 'the place with great sushi in Cheongna'

🥢 The basic table setting

As soon as you sit down,
the basics are laid out first:
rice porridge, cabbage salad, soup,
pickled ginger and rakkyo (pickled shallots).

The basic setting — porridge, salad, soup, pickled ginger, rakkyo

🍣 Today’s Sushi

We ordered the house basic,
Today’s Sushi (오늘의 초밥).

Egg, shrimp and salmon nigiri,
torch-seared sushi
and flying-fish-roe gunkan
come lined up on one plate.

Today's Sushi — from egg, shrimp and salmon to flying-fish-roe gunkan

This was seriously delicious!

Just this one basic plate
makes for a generous lunch.

A slow pan around Today's Sushi (GIF)

Per the Naver Place menu,
it’s 2 pieces of fresh fish, salmon,
garlic scallop, vinegared shrimp,
swordtip squid, raw shrimp,
thin-sliced beef brisket, egg
and a flying-fish-roe gunkan —
10 pieces for 16,900 won.

🎁 Hoe-muchim from the review event

Thanks to a review event,
we got hoe-muchim (spicy raw-fish salad)
as a free extra.

Raw fish, flying-fish roe and seaweed
on a bed of vegetables,
drizzled with sweet-and-sour chili sauce —
nice to nibble between pieces of sushi.

The free hoe-muchim from the review event — topped with flying-fish roe, too

Hoe-muchim close-up (GIF)

🔑 KICKJoin the review event
and the hoe-muchim is free!
(5,000 won if ordered on its own)

🍜 One spicy ramyeon

Sushi alone would have left us
wanting a little more,
so we ordered a ramyeon too.

A spicy-broth ramyeon
piled high with bean sprouts —
perfect to go back and forth with the sushi!

Spicy ramyeon — heaped with bean sprouts

The moment you lift the noodles, the steam really rises (GIF)

📋 Menu & prices

Today’s Sushi (10 pcs) 16,900 won
Special Sushi (12 pcs) 21,900 won

Today’s Sushi Set 21,900 won
(choose one: udon, cold soba
or hoedeopbap, a raw-fish rice bowl)

Hoe-muchim on its own 5,000 won

(per the Naver Place menu)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone in Cheongna looking for
an easy, no-fuss sushi lunch
✔ Anyone who wants
a spicy soup with their sushi
✔ Anyone who wants a place
that’s generous right from the basic spread

The words on the outside wall — 'Sushi Katsu: made with all our heart, good enough to serve our own family'

Just like the words on the wall —
sushi made with all their heart,
‘good enough to serve their own family’ —
we could taste that in one basic plate.
