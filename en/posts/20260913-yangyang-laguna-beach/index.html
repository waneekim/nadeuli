Yangyang Laguna Beach (Hajodae) — Half a Day by the Sea Even Without Surfing, Plus the Sand Sculptures in Front of Burger World

Our day by the sea in Yangyang 🏖

We went to Laguna Beach
on Yangyang’s Hajodae Beach.

It’s a surf beach,
but we didn’t surf —
we just watched.

Mermaid, sailing ship, lion and horse sand sculptures — on the sand in front of Burger World Laguna Beach

Hyeonbuk-myeon, Yangyang-gun, Gangwon-do

⛱ Seats under the parasols

On the beach there are thatched parasols,
sun loungers and tables.

Sit in the shade
and the sea is right in front of you.

Under a parasol — tables and chairs are set out

The sea from the shade of a parasol

🚗 Parking was easy

Parking was easy.

There was enough room
to stop briefly on the beach road,
and there’s a separate parking lot too.

According to Naver Place,
go 300 m straight on from Surfyy Beach
and the lot is on your left.

👍 Who this is for

of a surf beach without surfing
