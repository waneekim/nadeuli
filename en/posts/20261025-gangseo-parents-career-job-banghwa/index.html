Parents & Kids Career Job Review: AI Career Festival at Banghwa Neighborhood Park, Gangseo-gu, with Laser Survival and Bracelet Making

A review of the career experience festival
at Banghwa Neighborhood Park in Gangseo-gu 🤖

On Saturday we took the kids
to the 9th Parents & Kids
Career Job fair (제9회 자녀와 함께하는 학부모 진로잡).

This year’s slogan was
Connecting AI, Sensibility and K-Culture.

The event banner 'Connecting AI, Sensibility and K-Culture', with the AI Mirae ON booth behind it

📍 Banghwa Neighborhood Park, Gangseo-gu, Seoul
(Banghwa-dong · 178 Geumnanghwa-ro)
📅 Saturday, October 24, 2026
⏰ 09:30 – 14:00 (on-site poster)
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Elementary, middle and high school students in Gangseo and their parents
🎪 151 experience booths
(Friday and Saturday combined, per press reports)
📞 Gangseo Career and Job Experience Support Center
02-2699-8723

🎪 What Kind of Event Was It?

It’s a career experience festival
that Gangseo-gu held over two days.

Friday (10/23) was
the 13th Dream Job (제13회 드림잡),
a day for first-year middle school students.

Saturday (10/24) was
the 9th Parents & Kids Career Job fair,
a day for kids and parents together.

We went on Saturday.

The event poster on a booth table, with the dates for Dream Job (Fri) and Parents & Kids Career Job (Sat)

According to press reports,
there were 151 experience booths.

They were reportedly split into
9 special booths,
65 on AI and the future,
56 on sensibility,
and 21 on traditional Korean culture.

The tent banners at the venue
also carried theme names like
AI Mirae ON and Gamseong ON.

🔫 Laser Survival in the Forest

We played a survival war game
with the kids.

On the wooded slope,
‘LASER SURVIVAL’ panels
stood here and there.

The forest laser survival area, with 'LASER SURVIVAL' panels

🤖 Making an AI Bracelet · Brain-Wave Activities

After that,
we made an AI-themed bracelet,

and tried several
brain-wave-controlled activities.

At the AI Mirae ON tent,
booths like voice recognition
and a floor mat activity
stood in a row.

The AI Mirae ON booth, 'Can you hear me! Voice recognition experience'

A booth where you step on round mats

At the Gamseong ON tent,
there was an activity
where you make a one-of-a-kind ring
holding your dream.

The Gamseong ON booth, 'A one-of-a-kind ring made with my dream'

🔑 KICKSaturday's Parents & Kids Career Job
is a day for kids and parents
to experience things together
(Friday's Dream Job is for first-year middle schoolers)

🌳 The Park We Often Visit

Banghwa Neighborhood Park is
a place we often go
even on ordinary days.

So the kids played
comfortably in a familiar place.

A tree-lined walking path, with the event tents visible further in

It’s a park many Banghwa residents visit,
so the walking paths are well made,
and it’s an old but well-kept park.

A forest trail sign: they planted mainly ginkgo, the Gangseo-gu tree, along with Yoshino cherry and zelkova trees

The grounds are very large,
and there’s a hill behind,
so you can do a light hike too.

Inside the park there’s also
a yellow mud-wall house with a thatched roof.

A thatched house in the park, with vines climbing over the roof

The Saturday hours
on the on-site poster were
9:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.

For inquiries, press reports gave
the Gangseo Career and Job Experience Support Center
at 02-2699-8723 · 8746,
and the Gangseo-gu Office Education Support Division
at 02-2600-6984.

👍 Who We’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone in Gangseo-gu looking for
a career experience to do with kids
✔ Anyone looking for activities to try with kids,
like survival games, bracelet making and brain-wave experiences
✔ Anyone looking for a large park
with good walking paths

If you’re looking for a career experience festival
to go to with kids in Gangseo-gu,
remember Parents & Kids Career Job!
