Ulsan Whale Festival 2026 — Family Activities, Dates and Parking Checks

Whale-themed activities and performances meet
at the 30th Ulsan Whale Festival
(울산고래축제),
October 15 (Thu)–18 (Sun),
throughout Jangsaengpo Whale Culture Special Zone.

This guide uses the foundation website
and culture ministry information
checked on October 8.
Some dates absent from the overall timetable
appear on individual programme pages,
so family activities and stages were checked separately.

Let's take a quick look!

🎪 What kind of whale festival is it?

The festival marks its 30th anniversary,
with a whale invitation and birthday-party theme
and a slogan about joining in together.
Ulsan Nam-gu hosts it; the Whale Culture Foundation organizes it.

The introduction includes AI robots and media stages,
a citizen parade, a children’s musical,
family dance and singing contests, and activities.
The ministry lists 10:00–21:00 and free admission.
Check individual material costs and attraction fees separately.

📅 Family programmes by date

These dates and times come
from the organizer’s individual programme pages.
Stage and activity hours differ.

Date and timeProgramme and venue
10/15–18; individual times not listedWhale Dream AI Zone, lantern making, whale-riding challenge and Wish Whale Project
10/16 (Fri), 18:00–19:00Whale Family Dancing Star, Whale Theatre
10/17 (Sat), 18:00–19:00Whale Family Singing Contest, Whale Theatre at the multipurpose ground
10/18 (Sun), 16:00–18:00Whale Expedition Family Tour, family activity zone at the multipurpose ground

Watching a contest is different from entering it.
The official notices include a September 29 finalist announcement;
walk-in entries have not been confirmed.
Use the times above if planning to watch.

🐳 Make things and get moving

The Whale Dream AI Zone is at the multipurpose ground.
Its description includes smartphone AR searches
for whales and sea creatures, motion and touch activities,
a colouring screen and media photo zone.

At the same ground’s whale lantern workshop,
visitors assemble, colour and decorate a lantern
before switching on its LED.
Material fees and registration are not specified.

The whale-riding challenge is in front of the ecology centre.
Try staying on a whale-shaped ride for the time limit;
souvenirs depend on the result.
Age and height requirements are unconfirmed.

The Wish Whale Project uses the central fence
on the multipurpose ground’s car park side.
Attach heart-shaped wishes to a whale picture;
at night, UV and LED lighting form a photo zone.
That location description does not guarantee public parking there.

👨‍👩‍👧 What is Sunday’s family tour?

The 18th’s 16:00–18:00 programme is a stamp tour
with five cooperative family missions.
Activities include finding whales and sea friends,
opening a whale’s path and feeding the whale.

The guide says staff check completed stamps
and take a family completion photo.
Advance booking, capacity and participation fees
were not found on that detail page.
Ask how to join before planning Sunday around it.

🍢 Food and places to explore

The ministry introduces the Gorae Bapsang food zone,
a flea market and busking area,
plus “Whale Time”, a 30th-anniversary archive exhibition.
Menus and prices remain unconfirmed.

The whale-watching boat, Whale Ecology Experience Centre
and Jangsaengpo Culture Warehouse are linked attractions.
Treat their admission separately from the festival,
and check each attraction’s bookings, hours and fees.

🚇 Getting there and parking

The map centres on Jangsaengpo Multipurpose Ground,
home to family activities and Whale Theatre.
The Naver Place address checked on October 8 is
252 Jangsaengpogorae-ro, Nam-gu, Ulsan.

Official directions suggest searching for
울산고래축제 in navigation.
The festival spans several spaces,
so also check your chosen programme’s venue.

The ministry describes a transfer-style welcome gate
at Taehwagang Station Square.
However, shuttle times and stops, temporary car parks
and their fees were absent from the guidance checked.
Check transport announcements before setting off.

🔑 KICKAI and lantern workshops: multipurpose ground.
Whale-riding challenge: outside the ecology centre.
Choose the activity and its location together.

👍 Who might enjoy it?

✔ Families interested in whale-themed AR and crafts

Start with the date and venue of your chosen activity
when planning a Jangsaengpo outing.
