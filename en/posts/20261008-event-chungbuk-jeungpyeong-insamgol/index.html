2026 Jeungpyeong Insamgol Festival Schedule, Performances, Robot Experience — Before Visiting Bogangcheon Sports Park with Kids

It’s ginseng harvest season —
Jeungpyeong’s autumn festival has begun! 🌱

Now in its 33rd year!
The 33rd Jeungpyeong Insamgol Festival
kicks off today, Thursday, October 8,
and runs for four days until Sunday, October 11
at the Bogangcheon Sports Park area.

Admission is free.

I’ve summarized the official information
from Jeungpyeong-gun press releases (September 28 & October 7)
and the Chungbuk tourism website.

Want a quick overview?

🎉 Happening Now

Today, the 8th (Thursday), is the first day.
The remaining days are 9th (Friday), 10th (Saturday), and 11th (Sunday),
so there are two weekend days left.

The county press release doesn’t include a daily timetable,
so I’ve put together a table of daily programs
from a local news article (Gong News) on October 8.

DateMain Programs (per article)
8th (Thu) Opening DayDrawing Contest · Baekgok Kim Deuk-shin Writing Contest · County Talent Show · Evening Comedy Show & Fireworks
9th (Fri)Opening Ceremony · Insamgol Open Concert
10th (Sat)Insam Korean Beef Bulgogi Feast · K-POP International Youth Festival
11th (Sun)Community Harmony Program · National Insamgol Song Festival

The main stage County Talent Show on the 8th
is also mentioned in the September 14 press release.

The September 28 press release says
Bada, Huh Gak, Jinwook, and others will appear at the Insamgol Song Festival.
The article says Bada and Huh Gak
will perform at the Open Concert on the 9th.
Please check on-site for the exact day to see them.

There is no official announcement yet
of the specific times for each program.

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

This is Jeungpyeong’s signature festival
featuring its specialty, ginseng.

Jeungpyeong was selected as an ideal location for ginseng cultivation
in a national survey in the 1970s,
which led to the establishment of a ginseng research center.
Even in the 1530 book ‘Sinjeung Dongguk Yeoji Seungnam’,
ginseng is listed as a local product of this area.

At the festival, you’ll find Jeungpyeong ginseng and
a local produce market
where you can see and buy freshly harvested ginseng.

The festival is hosted by the Jeungpyeong Cultural Center
and organized by the Jeungpyeong Insamgol Festival Committee.

‘Inmaek Party’ is a combination of ginseng and beer.
It used to be held as a street performance,
but this year it’s moved to the main stage.

Yes, this is new for this year.
A future technology experience zone
showcasing ‘Smart Jeungpyeong’ will be set up.

The Military Song Contest, first held last year,
will be back again,
as well as the Traditional Ssireum Wrestling Event.
There is no official info yet

And for four nights straight,
the Bogangcheon Autumn Night Fireworks Festival will light up the sky.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

If you’re coming with children,
the Robot & VR Experience Zone
is a new highlight this year.

This year, the festival is eco-friendly with no disposable containers.
All food vendors will serve food in reusable containers.
When you’re done, return the dishes
to the dedicated reusable container return bins throughout the festival.

Since the fireworks are a nighttime event,
it’s a good idea to bring an extra jacket for your child.
