Uijeongbu Budae-jjigae Festival 2026 — Schedule, Free Tasting Time & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Budae-jjigae Street

When the cold wind starts to blow,
it’s budae-jjigae season 🍲
(budae = army base, jjigae = stew)

The 19th Uijeongbu Budae-jjigae Festival
(제19회 의정부부대찌개축제)
runs Sat, Oct 24 – Sun, Oct 25
around Budae-jjigae Street in Uijeongbu
(의정부 부대찌개거리).

We put this together from
Uijeongbu City’s event listing,
a notice (September 22) from the organizer,
the Uijeongbu Urban Corporation’s
Commercial District Promotion Center,
and the official poster.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Uijeongbu’s Budae-jjigae Street
is a designated ‘food culture’ street
lined with budae-jjigae restaurants.
It’s the alley across from the post office,
next to the old Yangju-gu office.

According to the city website,
the dish goes back about 50 years:
ham, sausage and bacon from the U.S. Army bases
were boiled up into a spicy stew
with kimchi, gochujang, rice cakes and vegetables.
Ham, sausage and minced beef are the main ingredients,
and you add sari (noodles and other add-ins)
while the stew is bubbling.

The festival is held around October every year,
and this year it centers on
a free tasting from 12 participating restaurants
and the 3rd Cooking Contest.
It’s hosted by the Commercial District Promotion Center,
organized by the Uijeongbu Budae-jjigae
Premium Brand Association,
and sponsored by Uijeongbu City
and NH NongHyup Bank’s Uijeongbu branch.
The festival mascots are
Sojji, Rajji, Haemjji and Chijji (소찌·라찌·햄찌·치찌).

✨ What’s on, and when?

The programs are all packed into
Saturday the 24th;
on Sunday the 25th,
only the restaurant discounts continue.

Date & timeProgram
24th (Sat) 12:00–17:00Activity booths · handmade flea market
24th (Sat) 13:00–15:003rd Cooking Contest — 6 food-business teams, contest dishes to taste on site
24th (Sat) 13:00–17:00Stilt-walking clown’s balloon art · free balloons
24th (Sat) 15:00–16:30Free budae-jjigae tasting from participating restaurants

The cooking contest is a battle
of creative fusion dishes
made with budae-jjigae ingredients.
It’s judged by expert judges
together with an on-site citizen panel,
according to the notice.

An African-rhythm street performance
by ‘Anyapo’ (아냐포)
and a magic show are also listed.
Their times aren’t out yet.

There’s no separate admission info in the notice,
and the tasting is free

🍲 How do the tasting and discounts work?

At the tasting you can try
budae-jjigae from the participating restaurants
all in one place.
The notice says there’ll be tasting rice
cooked with Uijeongbu’s ‘Hoeryong rice’ (회룡쌀)
and a table zone where you can sit and eat.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Kids’ activities run on Sat the 24th,
12:00–17:00 — about 10 kinds are listed,
including face painting, caricatures
and home gardening.
The flea market has handmade fabric goods,
interior décor items and food.

A stilt-walking clown does balloon art
and hands out balloons from 13:00 to 17:00.
Photos with the clown are on the list too.

The cooking contest starts at 13:00
and the tasting follows at 15:00,
so if you arrive around lunchtime
you can watch the contest → then the tasting.

The city’s annual events calendar also lists
a ‘Mental Health Day event’
(activity booths & information)
on the same street on the same day, the 24th.

🔑 KICKFor activity booth tickets
spend 20,000 won or more at a participating restaurant on the 24th
and hand in the receipt to get 2
Have lunch on the street and keep your receipt
