Where to Eat in Gyeyang, Incheon with Family — Birthday Bossam & Jeon at Hwanggeum Andong Guksi, the Egg Bomb with Dak-galbi at Gyeyang-ok

Our family meals out in Gyeyang, Incheon 🍽

Last July (2025),
we had two family meals
in Gyesan-dong, Gyeyang.

Bossam at Hwanggeum Andong Guksi — served with kimchi and chili peppers on the side

One was Hwanggeum Andong Guksi,
where we took my parents
for our child’s birthday;

the other was Gyeyang-ok,
where we went with the kids
for dak-galbi.

Both are in Gyesan-dong,
so we’ve put them
in one post.

🎂 Hwanggeum Andong Guksi (황금안동국시) — a birthday meal

📍 Gyeyang-gu, Incheon
Unit 101, 1F, 958 Gyeongmyeong-daero
⏰ Daily 8:00–21:00
Last order 20:30
💰 Hwanggeum Andong guksi (noodle soup) 16,000 won
Set meals 32,000–59,000 won
🅿 Free (next to the Mediful building)
👶 High chairs · groups welcome
📞 0507-1376-2648

It was our child’s birthday,
so we took my parents along.

For my parents,
it was their grandchild’s birthday spread.

🥩 Bossam that melts in your mouth

The meat was like Hanwoo beef —
tender and full of juices!
It melted in our mouths.

Eat it with the kimchi
and the savory flavor really pops.

The bossam (boiled sliced meat)
comes topped with kimchi, namul (seasoned greens)
and seaweed, so it looked great
and tasted rich too.

🥞 A plate of jeon to finish

The jeon (savory pancakes)
that came out last
were neat and generous.

There was a bit of everything —
zucchini, dongeurangttaeng, dumpling jeon —
so every generation
ate well.

A plate of jeon — zucchini jeon, dongeurangttaeng (meat patties) and dumpling jeon

My parents beamed,
saying, “This takes us back,”

and our child
really tucked into the meat.

🔑 KICKEat the bossam with kimchi!
The savory flavor really pops
Finish with a plate of jeon

📍 Gyeyang-ok (Gyesan Main Branch) — open in Naver Map

That same month,
we also took the kids
to Gyeyang-ok’s Gyesan main branch.

The star here is,
without question, the egg bomb!
Just the look of it is explosive.

Egg bomb — soft, fluffy egg topped with flying fish roe, corn and cheese

On the soft, fluffy egg:
flying fish roe that pops,
nutty corn,
and cheese that stretches and stretches.

The texture was fantastic.

Eaten with the dak-galbi (grilled chicken),
the combination was out of this world!

It melts softly in your mouth,
so the kids ate loads of it too.

We’re planning to go back.

🔑 KICKHave the egg bomb
with dak-galbi!
On the menu it's probably
'Bomb tricolor steamed egg' (폭탄 삼색 계란찜)
