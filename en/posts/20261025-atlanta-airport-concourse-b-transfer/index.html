Atlanta Airport Concourse B Layover: Taking the Plane Train, Browsing the Shops, and a Flipped Time Difference (US Business Trip, Day 1)

US business trip, Day 1,
the second story ✈️

This business trip was
a route from Korea through Atlanta
all the way to Columbia, South Carolina.

Atlanta airport was
a stop for a connection
along the way.

And
Atlanta airport was seriously big.

Concourse B hall — shops line both sides

🚇 Taking the Plane Train to B

After arriving,
we took the train inside the airport.

There were boarding stations
(concourses) from A to F,
and we got off at Station B.

Inside the airport train (the Plane Train) — the screen above shows the order of stops

This train is
the Plane Train.

The display inside the train — after B comes A

Between the concourses,
there was also an underground walkway you can walk through.

It is a light artwork called ‘Flight Paths’,
with a ceiling that sparkles like a forest.
(per the Atlanta airport guide — between Concourses A and B)

The underground walkway between concourses — a light artwork like a forest ceiling

🌙 Late-Night Snack Time in Korea

I wanted to eat
something else too,
but in Korean time it was late-night snack time,
so I wasn’t craving much.

The time difference was completely flipped,
so I wrote this post
feeling a little foggy.

Still,
having new experiences in a new place
is really fun.

🙂 People Weren’t as Big as I Expected

I thought there would be
lots of huge people
once I got to the US,

but I didn’t really feel
that people were overwhelmingly tall
or big.

👍 Recommended For

✔ Anyone connecting
at Atlanta airport
✔ Anyone catching Delta
on Concourse B
✔ Anyone who’ll browse
the shops during a layover

I hope it turns out
to be a fun business trip!
