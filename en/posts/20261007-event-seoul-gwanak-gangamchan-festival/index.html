Gwanak Gang Gam-chan Festival 2026 in Seoul — Schedule, Timetable & Parking, plus a History Talk Show and Aerial Fireworks at Nakseongdae Park

October — time to plan an autumn day out 🍂

In Nakseongdae, Gwanak-gu,
where General Gang Gam-chan,
a great commander of the Goryeo dynasty, was born,
a local history festival is coming up.

The Gwanak Gang Gam-chan Festival 2026
(관악강감찬축제)
runs for three days, Fri Oct 16 – Sun Oct 18,
around Nakseongdae Park.
This year marks its 10th anniversary.

We put this together from
the festival website
and Gwanak Cultural Foundation notices,
as of October 7.

Saturday the 17th is
'With Families!' day

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It started fresh in 2016,
when the ‘Gwanaksan Azalea Festival’ (held since 1998)
and the ‘Nakseongdae Inheonje’ (held since 1988)
were merged into one.

In January 2024,
Korea’s Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism
named it a ‘preliminary Culture & Tourism Festival’.

This year’s theme is
’TOMORROW — Opening the Future’.

On the website’s timetable,
each day has its own name:
16th — With Young People
17th — With Families
18th — With Residents

✨ What’s on each day?

Fri, Oct 16 — With Young People
10:00–12:00 Memorial rite for General Gang Gam-chan
(Anguksa Shrine 안국사)
13:00–17:00 Inheon calligraphy contest (Open Yard 열린마당)
15:00–16:00 Chungnam Youth Art Festa performance
19:00–21:00 Youth Dream Concert
(Main Plaza 대광장)

The concert features Dynamic Duo, rapper Sinseu (신스)
and hip-hop clubs from five Seoul universities.
The foundation’s press release says
both shows are free for anyone to watch.

Sat, Oct 17 — With Families
10:00–17:00 Children’s drawing contest
(Inheon Art Festival, Seoul Citizens’ University)
11:00–18:00 ‘Find the Little Gang Gam-chan’
(Anguksa Shrine)
13:00–14:00 Seol Min-seok’s Gang Gam-chan talk show
(Seol Min-seok 설민석 is a well-known Korean history lecturer)
14:30–16:00 Quiz show (planned, Main Plaza)
16:00–18:00 ‘Ullim (Resonance)’ intergenerational concert
(Open Yard)
19:30–20:30 10th-anniversary special show
’Gang Gam-chan, Who Came from the Stars’ (Main Plaza)

The special show is a 60-minute outdoor performance —
traditional performing arts, contemporary dance
and live music,
topped off with an aerial fireworks performance.

Sun, Oct 18 — With Residents
11:00–17:00 Community Arts Festival
performances & hands-on activities (Anguksa Shrine)
12:00–14:30 Citizens’ Pungnyu Festival (Main Plaza)
16:00–19:00 Community Arts Festival
performances (Main Plaza)

Under the timetable it says:
“Programs may change
depending on how things run on the day.”

🎡 Weekend all-day programs

On Saturday and Sunday,
the all-day programs run
daytime 12:00–19:00
evening 19:00–21:00.

🧒 Byeolbyeol Playground (별별놀이터) (Middle Plaza 중광장)
A family activity where you try out
Gang Gam-chan’s strategy and tactics
through play.

🏹 Nakseong Flower Garden (낙성꽃뜰정원)
Archery program by Gwanakjeong (traditional Korean archery),
instant photos for three-generation families,
10th-anniversary archive exhibition

📚 Goryeo Suseowon pop-up outdoor library
An outdoor library run with Seoul Library
(Anguksa Shrine, 17th–18th, 12:00–18:00)

For markets, there’s the ‘Goryeo Market’
in the Open Yard (from Friday 13:00)
and ‘Byeongnando 21’ along the walking trail.
There are also booths from Gwanak’s 21 neighborhoods,
a Wish Tunnel, and the permanent exhibition
at the Gang Gam-chan Exhibition Hall.

🚇 How do I get there? What about parking?

The official guide recommends
the subway or bus

From Nakseongdae Station (Line 2), exit 4,
it’s a 10-minute walk.

By bus, get off at the Nakseongdae Entrance stop
(낙성대입구)
and walk 5 minutes.

Here’s the website’s notice, word for word:
“Roads around Nakseongdae Park may be congested
during the festival.
Please use the subway or bus where possible.”

If the nearby public car parks fill up,
they say they’ll direct you to alternative parking.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids?

If you can pick only one day,
make it Saturday the 17th,
the day the website calls ‘With Families!’.
The afternoon talk show, Byeolbyeol Playground
and the 19:30 fireworks show are all on the same day.

On the 18th, the Community Arts Festival
sets up craft and flea-market booths at Anguksa Shrine.
Only knitting ‘Punggyeong (풍경)’ and pottery ‘Clay Crew (클레이크루)‘
need a reservation; the rest take sign-ups on site.

If you sign up with the ‘사전신청하기’ (pre-register) button on the website,
you get a ‘Gang Gam-chan Summons’ image.
Save it and show it on site to check in, they say.

The fireworks show is outdoors after dark,
so pack an extra jacket for the kids.

🔑 KICKJust watching the talk show?
No sign-up needed
Pre-registered for the quiz show?
Check in on site by 12:30 that day!
If you're late, your spot
goes to on-site participants

👍 Who this is for

✔ Families who want to hear Gang Gam-chan’s story with their kids
(Seol Min-seok’s talk show — no sign-up needed to watch)
✔ Families looking for play and making activities
(Byeolbyeol Playground, crafts on the 18th)
