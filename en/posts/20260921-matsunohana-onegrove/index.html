Where to Eat at Magok One Grove (Near Gimpo Airport): Matsunohana — Shoyu-Salmon Ebi-Tendon, Hormone Bowl and Conger Eel Tempura on a Date with My Wife

Our tendon review from One Grove, Magok 🍤

This past February,
my wife and I
stopped by Matsunohana (마츠노하나)
while out on a date.

The whole table — shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon and hormone bowl, plus the wild conger eel tempura we ordered on the side

📍 165 Gonghang-daero, Gangseo-gu
One Grove (원그로브), B1
🚇 Magok Station (Line 5), exit 1
about 3 min on foot
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
⏰ Daily 10:30–22:00
(last order 20:30)
💰 Shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon
18,800 won
🅿 One Grove underground parking
(free time based on what you spend)
📞 0507-1481-0071

To cut to the chase,
it was a clean, satisfying meal.

🍤 Shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon & hormone bowl

We ordered
one shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon
and one shoyu-salmon hormone bowl.

The moment the bowls arrived,
the looks alone blew us away!

Shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon — two pieces of shrimp tempura and soy-marinated salmon over rice

Shoyu-salmon hormone bowl — soy salmon and hormone (offal), with one egg yolk in the middle

As for the taste,
the nutty richness of the hormone bowl
and the soft umami
of the shoyu-sake (soy-marinated salmon)
went really well together.

🐟 Adding wild conger eel tempura

On top of that,
we ordered
wild conger eel tempura.

The crisp texture was superb,
and it made our table
feel far more lavish.

✨ Treated like honored guests

Every dish
came out neatly plated,
so we enjoyed it all,
feeling like honored guests.

The staff were kind
and the place was clean.

🔑 KICKAdd the conger eel tempura
and the table instantly feels fuller
(last order is at 20:30)

🚗 Parking at One Grove

Just use
One Grove’s underground car park.

✔ First 30 min free
✔ More free time the more you spend
20,000 won or more: 1 hour
30,000 won or more: 2 hours
50,000 won or more: 3 hours
100,000 won or more: 5 hours
✔ After that, 2,000 won per 30 min
(max 30,000 won a day)

This is the parking notice
on the Naver Place page
of Hoho Sikdang (One Grove),
in the same complex.
It may change,
so please check the signs on site too.

📋 Menu & prices

Shoyu-salmon ebi-tendon 18,800 won
(soy salmon, 2 shrimp, vegetable tempura)
Shoyu-salmon hormone bowl 19,800 won
(soy salmon + daechang, large intestine)

Ebi-tendon (4 shrimp) 16,800 won
Hormone bowl 18,800 won
Unagi-don (eel rice bowl) 28,000 won

(per the Naver Place menu)

The conger eel tempura on its own
isn’t on the Place menu,
so we haven’t listed a price.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for
a date-meal spot in Magok
✔ Anyone after
a good-looking Japanese rice bowl
✔ Anyone who wants one more
piece of tempura with their tendon

If you want a date meal in Magok
over a neat Japanese rice bowl,
we recommend it!

Both the food and the atmosphere
lived up to our expectations.
