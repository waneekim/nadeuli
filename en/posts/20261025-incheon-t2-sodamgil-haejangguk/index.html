Hansik Sodamgil, Incheon Airport Terminal 2 Basement 1: Seokkeotang (Spicy) at H Haejangguk for 17,900 Won, a Mild Airport Breakfast

A review of a breakfast
on basement level 1 of
Incheon Airport Terminal 2 🍲

I had seokkeotang
(mixed meat soup)
at H Haejangguk (H 해장국)
in the Korean food court
Hansik Sodamgil (한식소담길).

As for the taste,
it was very mild, with a rich pork broth.

The entrance to 'Hansik Sodamgil' on basement 1 of Incheon Airport Terminal 2

📍 Incheon Airport Terminal 2,
Transportation Center, basement 1
(446 Je2terminal-daero, Yeongjong-gu, Incheon)
⏰ Daily 06:00 – 22:00
(Naver Place)
🍲 H Haejangguk seokkeotang (spicy)
+ extra sliced boiled pork
💰 17,900 won (per the receipt)
🥡 No takeout on any menu item

🏮 A Korean food court with eight stalls, A to H

On the menu boards
in front of the entrance,
you can see eight stalls.

A Jeondongjib (전동집), B Dombeok (돔베옥),
C Gajok Hoegwan (가족회관), D Dubu Maeul (두부마을),
E Sinpo Sundae (신포순대), F Kodari Naengmyeon (코다리냉면),
G Hanokjib Kimchi-jjim (한옥집 김치찜),
and H Haejangguk (해장국).

The menu boards for the eight stalls A-H in front of the entrance

Of those, I picked
the Jeju-style haejangguk stall
(H Haejangguk).

The sign also reads
’JEJU PEOPLE’.

🖥 Order at the kiosk

You order at a kiosk.

There are tabs for each stall
at the top of the screen,
so you pick a stall and add menu items.

Order at the kiosk — a tab for each stall

Next to the kiosk,
there were notices saying
drinks are ordered at the counter and
no menu item can be taken out.

🍲 Spicy seokkeotang with extra sliced boiled pork

What I ordered was
seokkeotang (spicy) for 14,000 won
plus extra sliced boiled pork for 3,900 won,
17,900 won in total.

Seokkeotang (spicy) 14,000 won on the kiosk

Seokkeotang (spicy) with extra sliced boiled pork — 17,900 won

The taste was very mild,
and I could really taste
the pork broth.

I was so hungry that
I dug in the moment it came out,
so it’s a bit of a shame that
I have no photo of the food.

🪑 Roomy seating and the tray return

In the middle,
there’s roomy seating
shared by the stalls.

The central seating — E Sinpo Sundae in the back

The G Hanokjib Kimchi-jjim and H Haejangguk stalls and the tray return

Just bring your empty bowls
to the ‘bowl return’ (그릇 놓는 곳).

👍 Who I’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone looking for
breakfast before a flight
at Terminal 2
✔ Anyone who wants a mild broth
to settle the stomach
✔ Anyone who wants to pick
from several Korean stalls

If you need breakfast
at Incheon Airport Terminal 2,
remember H Haejangguk at Hansik Sodamgil!
