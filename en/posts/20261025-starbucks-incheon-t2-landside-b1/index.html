Starbucks Incheon Airport Terminal 2, Basement 1 Review: No App Orders or Coupons, Blonde Aerocano Venti to Go

A review of the Starbucks
on basement level 1
of Incheon Airport Terminal 2 ☕

On my way to the US
for a business trip,
I stopped by the
basement 1 Starbucks.

But this store
didn’t take app orders or coupons,
which was a letdown.

Blonde Aerocano venti to go, with the store entrance behind it

☕ Not as big as I expected

Head down to basement 1

The entrance to the basement 1 Starbucks

The Siren logo on the round wall next to the entrance

📱 No app orders or coupons

The perks were
not so great.

In the Starbucks app,
I couldn’t place an order,
and I couldn’t use coupons either,
which was very inconvenient.

I usually use lots of coupons
to get my Starbucks economically,

so it was a real shame
that coupons didn’t work here.

👍 Who I’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone who wants to grab
a coffee after checking in
at Terminal 2
✔ Anyone getting a
to-go drink on basement 1
✔ Anyone who needs to use
app orders or coupons

If you need a coffee
before departure at Incheon Airport Terminal 2,
try the basement 1 Starbucks,
but remember that
app orders and coupons don’t work there!
