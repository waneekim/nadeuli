2026 Cultural Heritage Moonlight Festival Schedule, Performance Timetable, Reservations—Before Visiting Uam Historic Park with Kids

On autumn nights, take a moonlit journey along cultural heritage sites 🌕

The 2026 Cultural Heritage Moonlight Festival will be held for two days, October 10 (Sat)~11 (Sun), from 18:00 to 22:00 each evening at Uam Historic Park (우암사적공원) in Gayang-dong, Dong-gu, Daejeon.

I’ve organized the official announcement, guide images, and layout map posted by the Daejeon Cultural Foundation on September 29.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

Uam Historic Park is where the late Joseon scholar Song Si-yeol (우암 송시열) studied. Historic buildings like Namganjeongsa, Jangpangak, Ijikdang, and Deokporu are located within the park.

The festival is described as a traditional culture festival where you can enjoy both these cultural heritage sites and the nighttime scenery.

At the entrance, Guards, Peddlers (Bobusang), and Damo (다모) will greet visitors, and moonlight photo zones will be set up at Namganjeongsa and throughout the festival grounds.

✨ What’s Happening When?

Performances take place at two locations: the main stage at Ijikdang and Deokporu by the pond. The schedule differs each day.

The Deokporu performances are open to all without reservation. The outdoor theater runs once each day, with mat seating as noted in the guide.

🏮 What Experiences Are There?

From 18:00 to 22:00, you can freely participate in experiences on site.

ExperienceDetails · Location

It is noted that experiences may end early if materials run out.

📝 Programs Requiring Reservation

Two programs require advance application via OnOffMix. Both are free.

ProgramTime · Details
Docent Tour ‘Path of Learning’18:00 · 19:00 · 20:00 · 21:00 (30 min each), 20 people per session — Namganjeongsa → Jangpangak → Ijikdang → Deokporu
Moonlight Tea Gathering18:00 · 21:00 (30 min each) — Enjoy chrysanthemum tea and traditional snacks while viewing Deokporu at night

Applications are open until October 9 (Fri) 23:59. As of October 8, all Moonlight Tea Gathering sessions are full, but there were still spots for the docent tour.

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your program starts. The meeting point listed for reservations is ‘the operations booth in front of Uam Historic Park.’

🚗 Directions & Parking

The address is 53 Chungjeong-ro, Dong-gu, Daejeon.

On the layout map, a parking lot (P) is marked near the main entrance and the general information center. There is no official information about the number of spaces, parking fees, or public transportation.

👨‍👩‍👧 If Visiting with Kids

If you’re bringing young children, it’s great to focus on the balloon show and hands-on experiences. The balloon show is at 19:30 on Saturday and 18:30 on Sunday.

The family movie ‘Moonlight Palace’ begins at 20:30 and ends at 21:50. Since seating is on mats, it’s a good idea to bring an extra jacket for your child after sunset.

On the layout map, first aid facilities and restrooms are marked next to the operations headquarters.
