Goyang Animal Healing Culture Festival 2026 at Ilsan Lake Park — Schedule, Parking & a Dog Show You Can Enjoy Without a Pet

It’s autumn — the perfect season
for a walk around the lake 🍂

The 2026 Goyang Animal Healing Culture Festival
(2026 고양동물교감치유문화제)
takes place October 24 (Sat), 10:00–17:00
around Theme Square in Ilsan Lake Park.

We put this together from
Goyang city’s event listing
and the official poster.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of event is it?

A one-day animal culture event
held by the Animal Administration Team
of Goyang Special City’s
Agricultural Products Distribution Division.

The venue, Theme Square (주제광장),
is the central plaza
right in the middle of Ilsan Lake Park.

The poster introduces the programs
split into three groups.

GroupPrograms
Join in (참여할개)Dog Sports Day (댕댕운동회 — running & limbo) · Doggy Star contest (댕댕스타) · dog behavior training & learning tricks · Animal Bonding (open to people without pets)
Try it (체험할개)Making pet food & ID tags · pet massage & hygiene grooming · pet caricatures · photo zone
Together (함께할개)DOG SHOW · autumn concert · taekwondo demonstration team · adoption campaign for abandoned animals

The city’s notice also lists
busking performances.

🙆 No pet? Is that OK?

On the poster, one program is marked
separately as ‘open to people without pets’:
Animal Bonding (동물교감).
How it works and from what age
aren’t in the official notices yet.

The ‘Together’ group is
stage shows — the dog show,
autumn concert and taekwondo team —
plus the adoption campaign.
If your family doesn’t have a pet,
start with this group and the photo zone.

The rest of ‘Join in’ and ‘Try it’
have dogs or pets in their names.
Whether you can do them without a pet
hasn’t been announced yet.

So far, the only times announced
are the festival hours and the ceremony.

🐶 Going with your dog

The very first of Ilsan Lake Park’s rules:
keep your dog on a leash and pick up after it.
Here are the park rules in one table.

RuleDetails
LeashRequired — 50,000 won fine if not
Dog wasteMust be picked up — 50,000 won fine if left behind
Dog playgroundFree · only for registered dogs wearing an ID tag
Kids at the playground12 and under must enter with a guardian

How to sign up for Dog Sports Day
and the Doggy Star contest
isn’t in the official notices yet.

By subway, it's Jeongbalsan Station
exit 1

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

At the park’s main information center (08:00–20:00)
you can borrow a stroller for free.
Just show your ID.

Inside the information center
there’s a nursing room and baby beds,
and 7 of the park’s restrooms
have diaper-changing tables.

Tents, sunshades and cooking
are not allowed in the park.

With so many dog programs that day,
if your child is scared of dogs,
it’s worth talking it over beforehand.

The park’s Nature Learning Center (자연학습원)
is described as an eco-learning site
home to more than 1,300 species.
