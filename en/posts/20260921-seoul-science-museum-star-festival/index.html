Seoul Science Center Starlight Festival with Kids — Telescopes and Star Talks, and We Went Two Days in a Row (Nowon, November 2025)

A place to go with kids in Nowon 🌙

On the last weekend
of November last year,
we went to the Starlight Festival
at Seoul Science Center
in Hagye-dong, Nowon.

We went on Saturday,
then went back again on Sunday!

A huge moon model set up outside the building — it stands out even more at night

📢 This is a record of the event
on November 29–30, 2025.
The event is already over.
The 2026 Starlight Festival dates
aren't on the official website yet.
(checked October 6, 2026)

📍 160 Hangeulbiseok-ro
Nowon-gu, Seoul
(Line 7 Hagye Station, exit 3)
⏰ Usually 09:30–17:30
Closed Mondays
(Tuesday if Monday is a holiday)
💰 Admission free
(some programs are paid)
🅿 150 won per 5 min (small lot)
max 14,400 won a day
📞 02-970-4500

🔭 Telescopes and star talks

It was an event
just right for families.

From hands-on telescope sessions
to constellation talks,
the lineup was packed.

Best of all, there were lots of
programs the kids take part in themselves,
so they stayed really focused.

It wasn’t just looking —
there was plenty of hands-on time.

Our child writing something by hand — many of the programs were hands-on

An observation scope in the exhibition's hands-on corner — the screen behind reads ‘자연과 도시가 어우러진 생태습지’ (an eco-wetland where nature and city meet)

🙋 Smooth running, friendly staff

Things ran fairly smoothly on site,
so waiting wasn’t much of a stress.

The staff guiding us were friendly, too.

Learning and hands-on fun
were nicely balanced —
a satisfying family day out.

☁️ The downside — weather

The night-sky viewing
was a little affected by the weather.
You’re looking at the real sky,
so it depends on that day’s weather.

The event notice also said
the stargazing program is canceled
in the rain and replaced
with a different program.

🔑 KICKNight-sky viewing
depends on the weather
If stargazing is the goal,
check the forecast first!

🔁 We went back the next day

After the first day,
we went back once more
the next day.

All we wrote on day two
was one line: “Want to come again — the best!”

Going two days in a row
says it all, we think.

🌟 The 2025 Starlight Festival at a glance

November 29 (Sat) – 30 (Sun), 2025
Night opening on the 29th, closing at 21:30
Entry to the event: free
(some workshops paid, 3,000–30,000 won)

There was telescope assembly, stargazing,
a relay of astronomy and space lectures,
a lunar lander workshop and more.

The museum website has
a separate Starlight Festival menu
under ‘과학행사’ (Science Events) —
check there for news of the next one.

🚗 Parking & getting there

The parking lot is small,
so public transport is recommended.

Parking is 150 won per 5 minutes
(1,800 won an hour, max 14,400 won a day).

From Line 7 Hagye Station, exit 3,
it’s a 1.2 km walk,
or take bus 1141 and get off at
Samik Seongyeong Apt. back gate
(삼익선경아파트 후문).

🏛 Worth a visit without the festival, too

Admission is free.
(Some hands-on and education programs are paid.)

Open 09:30–17:30, closed Mondays
(closed Tuesday if Monday is a public holiday).

Inside the museum
there’s also an observatory, ‘BOOT’.
Check the website
for its schedule.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Parents who want to try telescopes
and constellations with their kids
✔ Kids who’d rather do things
with their hands than just look
✔ Anyone looking for
a family outing in Nowon

Telescopes and constellations,
plus programs the kids do themselves.
Only the night-sky viewing depends on the weather;
the rest was a family outing
good enough to go two days in a row.
