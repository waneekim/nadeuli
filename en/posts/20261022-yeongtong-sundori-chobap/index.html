A review of a sushi restaurant near Yeongtong-gu Office, Suwon 🍣

🍣 Special Assorted Sushi with Springy Rice

We ordered the special sushi for one person.

Its name on the standing sign is
Sundori Teukmodeum,
and we paid the standing sign price,
16,900 won.

It was good value too.

The sushi rice
was tasty and springy.

The fish was fresh,
and the gunkan rolls were good too.
