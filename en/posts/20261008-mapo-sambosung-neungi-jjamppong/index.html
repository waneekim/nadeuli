Where to Eat Jjamppong in Mapo, Seoul: Samboseong (삼보성) — Neungi Mushroom Seafood Jjamppong for 13,000 Won, Front Parking and Menu Prices

A review I paid for myself 🍜

A Chinese restaurant I came across
while running an errand nearby:
Samboseong on Changjeon-ro, Mapo!

Neungi mushroom seafood jjamppong — packed with mushrooms and seafood

📍 32 Changjeon-ro, Mapo-gu
(창전로 32, Gusu-dong)
🚇 About 260 m in a straight line
from Gwangheungchang Station (Line 6)
🍜 Neungi mushroom seafood jjamppong 13,000 won
🅿️ Parking right out front (wide)
📞 02-717-0088
⏰ Please call to check opening hours

I dropped by
a little after 12 noon on a Thursday.

It felt old
and very much like a traditional Chinese restaurant.

🏮 An old-school, traditional Chinese restaurant

Big red letters on the first floor
read ‘전통중국요리’ (traditional Chinese cuisine),
over a long red awning.

Red '전통중국요리' letters and the awning

Above the entrance:
gold ‘Chinese Restaurant’ lettering
and the hanja sign 三寶成 (Samboseong).

The street sign next to it says ‘Changjeon-ro 32’.

The 三寶成 sign at the entrance — Changjeon-ro 32

On the entrance pillar
hang a Mapo-gu ‘Good Restaurant’ plaque,
a ‘좋은 식단’ (good meals) sign
and a ‘neighborhood sharing shop’ plaque.

Mapo-gu Good Restaurant, good-meals and sharing-shop plaques

🅿️ Parking right out front

There’s a wide parking area out front.
Green parking bays
with wheel stops in a row.

A note on the entrance glass says:
“If you park over the (line),
you’ll be fined!!”

Please park neatly inside the bay.

🥢 Lunchtime, and it was busy

When I went, too,
there were quite a lot of customers.
It felt like a really popular place!

Your table gets three small side dishes,
a water bottle and a cup.

Three side dishes and a water bottle at my seat

The sides: danmuji (yellow pickled radish),
pickled cabbage, and one red seasoned side dish.

Danmuji, pickled cabbage and a red seasoned side dish

A sign on the wall lists the jjamppong types:
samseon (three-seafood) · neungi mushroom seafood · chadol (beef brisket)

The leftover takeaway container fee
is 500 won (small) or 1,000 won (large).

Wall signs: takeaway fee, jjamppong types, self-service

Personal plates, wet towels, chili powder,
vinegar and scissors are at the self-service corner.

“Help yourself if you need them!”

The self-service corner with plates, towels and scissors

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for a traditional Chinese restaurant
near Changjeon-ro or Gwangheungchang Station
✔ Anyone craving jjamppong loaded with mushrooms and seafood
(neungi mushroom seafood jjamppong 13,000 won)
✔ Anyone driving there
(wide parking right out front)
✔ Anyone curious about the dishes and courses
(weekday lunch sets from 35,000 won per person)

If you’re looking for good jjamppong in Mapo,
I recommend Samboseong’s neungi mushroom seafood jjamppong!

With so many dishes on the menu,
it’s a place where I was sorry not to try them all.
