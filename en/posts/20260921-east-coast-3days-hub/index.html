Samcheok to Yangyang: A 3-Day East Coast Korea Itinerary — Daegeumgul Cave, a Galnam Port Guesthouse, Laguna Beach & Burger World (5 Families, 19 People, August 2026)

Our 3-day East Coast trip map 🗺

From Friday, August 28 to Sunday, August 30, 2026,
five families — 19 of us —
spent three days and two nights
on Korea’s east coast.

In front of the Daegeumgul marker stone, 10:53 am on day one

🗓 August 28–30, 2026
👪 5 families, 19 people
(9 adults · 10 elementary schoolers)
① Aug 28 morning: Daegeumgul Cave
② Aug 28–30: Galnam Port guesthouse
③ Aug 29: swimming at Galnam Port
④ Aug 30 afternoon: Laguna Beach
⑤ Aug 30 evening: Burger World
🏠 Base: Galnam Port, Samcheok

Our base was the ground floor of a guesthouse
at Galnam Port in Samcheok.
On the last day we drove north
along the east coast
and ended the day in Yangyang.

We’ve written up each stop separately,
so here we just sum up each post briefly
and link to it.

🚝 Aug 28 morning — Daegeumgul Cave, Samcheok

At 10:53 am,
our photos begin
in front of the Daegeumgul marker stone.

Tickets are booked in advance on the official website.
On the 1st of each month at 10:30 am,
the next month’s tickets open,
and individuals can book up to 10 people at a time.

Adults 12,000 won · children 6,000 won
(monorail included);
a visit takes 1 hour 40 minutes.

We have no photos from inside the cave,
so our Daegeumgul post is a record
of the marker stone, the forest path and an old house,
plus a summary of the official information.

🏠 Aug 28–30 — Jinsol Minbak, Galnam Port

📍 Jinsol Minbak (guesthouse) — open in Naver Map

7:05 pm on day one — Galnam Port seen from the guesthouse terrace in the rain

We stayed at Jinsol Minbak (진솔민박),
a guesthouse facing Galnam Port —
the five families took over
the whole ground floor.
(5 rooms · living room · shared kitchen)

The forecast said rain all three days
(a 70–80% chance),
but it only rained on the first day.

Per the notice we got when booking,
snorkels, life jackets and flippers rented
for 5,000 won each, and a parasol for 10,000 won (per day).

🏖 Aug 29 — Swimming at Galnam Port

📍 Galnam Port — open in Naver Map

12:41 pm on day two — the beach at Galnam Port

Day two: into the water at Galnam Port!

Our photos from 12:38 to 12:41 pm
show tents and parasols,
and people in life jackets
riding tubes between the coastal rocks.

At 7:07 pm the kids
set up camping chairs at the water’s edge
and sat side by side, looking at the sea.

Galnam Port is at 99-20 Galnam-ri, Wondeok-eup,
and has parking.
(per Gangwon State tourism information)

That day’s story is in the guesthouse post, too.

🏄 Aug 30 afternoon — Laguna Beach, Yangyang

📍 Laguna Beach (Yangyang) — open in Naver Map

Sand sculptures at Laguna Beach — a mermaid, a sailing ship, a lion and a horse; a permanent display in front of Burger World's Laguna Beach branch

On the last day we drove north
along the east coast
to Laguna Beach in Hyeonbuk-myeon, Yangyang.

It’s a surfing beach,
but we didn’t surf;
that day was cloudy, and the waves were fairly big.

The life-size sand sculptures on the beach —
a mermaid, a sailing ship, a lion and a horse —
are a permanent display by Burger World’s Laguna Beach branch.

In the end the kids went back to plain sand,
lying in it and digging, over and over.

🍔 Aug 30 evening — Burger World

📍 Burger World (Laguna Beach) — open in Naver Map

Two burgers on an orange tray — the US flag marks the New York Burger, the Korean flag the Yangyang Burger

After looking at the sand sculptures,
we had dinner at the restaurant
right in front of them.

One entire wall is open,
so from your seat you look straight out
at the sand sculptures and the sea.

The Yangyang Burger, 9,900 won, has onion chutney sauce;
the New York Burger, 10,900 won, has a double patty and cheese.
(per the restaurant’s notice)

Each burger comes with a little Korean or US flag stuck in it,
so you can tell them apart.

🗺 Our three days at a glance

① Aug 28: Daegeumgul → check in at the Galnam Port guesthouse
rain on the first evening only

19 of us on the guesthouse’s whole ground floor

Our pre-trip schedule also listed
a makguksu (buckwheat noodle) lunch, Yonghwa Land (용화랜드)
and the Chogok Yonggul Chotdaebawi Trail (초곡 용굴촛대바위길),
but we have no record of actually going,
so they’re not on this map.

✔ Anyone planning a 3-day trip
that combines Samcheok and Yangyang
✔ Anyone organizing a big group trip
with several families
✔ Anyone heading to the east coast
with kids for some beach time

Starting in Samcheok
and ending in Yangyang,
