Sea in the City Festival 2026 at Noryangjin Fish Market, Seoul — Dates, Lineup & Parking, Plus Shrimp Catching and a Wave Playground for Kids

For two days one autumn weekend,
Noryangjin Fish Market
becomes ‘Seoul’s sea’ 🌊

The 9th Sea in the City Festival
(제9회 도심 속 바다축제 —
“dosim sok bada” means “the sea in the city”)
runs Sat Oct 24 – Sun Oct 25
around Noryangjin Fish Market
and Noryangjin Football Field.

We put this together from
Seoul’s festival site Fun Seoul
and Dongjak-gu’s press release (September 18).

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Dongjak-gu, Suhyup
(the National Federation of Fisheries Cooperatives)
and Suhyup Noryangjin Susan
host it together,
with support from Seoul city.

This year’s theme is
’Noryangjin Becomes Seoul’s Sea’.

Fun Seoul describes it as
a festival that uses Noryangjin Fish Market,
Seoul’s largest wholesale seafood market,
so you can experience the sea
in the middle of the city.

There’s a direct-trade seafood market,
an affordable food market,
sea culture activities,
and seafood cooking demos with tastings.

A seafood purchase payback event
run with the market
is on too.
How much you get back
hasn’t been announced yet.

We’ve put the names as written
on the Fun Seoul page and the poster
into a table (the English is our translation).

The stage program is Opening Seoul’s Sea
(SEA STAGE).

The poster names
seven performers in all.
Here are the stages by date, with the performers
as they appear going by the poster layout.

🚇 Getting there & parking

From Line 1 exit 9,
it's 150 m to the market entrance

Fun Seoul’s map pin is on
Noryangjin Football Field (688 Nodeul-ro);
Noryangjin Fish Market is at 674 Nodeul-ro.

These directions follow
Suhyup Noryangjin’s
market location guide.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The activities kids do themselves —
A Round of Shrimp Catching,
the Little Kids’ Sea Fishing Pond
and the Wave Playground —
are all under SEA PLAY.

If you’ll do shrimp catching or fishing,
wet wipes and a change of clothes help.

If you’ll make mood lamps,
suncatchers or beach bags,
bring a bag to carry them home.

If you’ll shop at the market too,
bring a cooler bag or an ice box.
The market sells year-round with no days off;
the general-section stores (대중)
are open 01:30–22:00.

🔑 KICKDriving there to shop?
Ask for parking discount tickets when you buy
1,000 won per ticket, no limit on how many!
Some shops don't give them out
