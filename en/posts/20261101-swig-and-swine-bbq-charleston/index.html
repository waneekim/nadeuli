Swig & Swine BBQ in Charleston: The Best Beef I’ve Ever Had! Family Platter Review (US Business Trip, Day 7)

After leaving Cypress Gardens,
on the way to downtown Charleston,
we stopped at a local barbecue place 🍖

Swig & Swine BBQ!
I’d never had beef this good before.

Family platter — slices of white bread, coleslaw, corn pudding and mac and cheese, with the meats behind

📍 Swig & Swine BBQ West Ashley
(Charleston, South Carolina, USA
1217 Savannah Hwy)
🍖 Family platter $85 (from the menu)
🥩 Beef that was not steak but like Korean suyuk (tender sliced boiled meat)
💵 About $150 for six of us · about $180 with tip
😋 Everyone was satisfied!

🐷 The Place That Horrifies Vegetarians?

It’s a local restaurant on the
main road heading to downtown Charleston.

On the board under the sign,
a Halloween-style phrase was posted:
TRICK OR TREAT YOSELF TO BBQ. 🎃

The SWIG & SWINE BBQ sign by the road — HIBACHI GRILL next door

On the wall next to it, beside a big pig painting,
was a funny line:
horrifying vegetarians since 2013,
meaning “the place that has been scaring vegetarians since 2013.” 😆

The pig painting on the wall — horrifying vegetarians SINCE 2013

The words on the pig statue by the entrance

The blackboard at the entrance said
SEE HOST TO BE SEATED!!
You wait for a staff member to seat you.

The entrance blackboard — wait to be seated, Halloween special menu

Inside was a spacious room with red chairs
and big windows like garage doors.

Inside the restaurant, with rows of red chairs

📋 The Menu and the Family Platter

The Swig & Swine menu

I ordered the family set.
The $85 FAMILY PLATTER on the menu comes with
Pulled Pork, Beef Brisket, Smoked Turkey,
Smoked Pork Belly, Housemade Sausage,
1/3 rack of ribs and Smoked Chicken Wings,
plus 3 sides (pints).

KICKThe small print under the menu —
a 20% service charge for parties of 8 or more,
and paying by card adds another 3.75%!

🥩 The Best Beef I’ve Ever Had

We ate a lot of beef, but
I’d never had beef this good.

It wasn’t steak.
It was beef with a suyuk-like feel
(suyuk is Korean tender boiled sliced meat),
and it came with sausage
and several other meats.

Brisket charred black on the outside, and a plate of sides

🧴 Five Sauces

There were five sauces on the table.
Jalapeno · Sweet Red · Mustard ·
Alabama White · Vinegar!

The five sauces on the table

The whole Sweet Red sauce recipe
was written on the wall.
Orange, smoked onion and garlic, tomato paste,
molasses, ketchup, even rosemary!

The Sweet Red sauce recipe on the wall

💵 About $150 for Six of Us

Even though we ate this much,
it was only about $150 for the six of us,
and about $180 with tip.

Everyone was satisfied! 😋

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone who wants to try
local barbecue in Charleston
✔ Anyone going with a group who wants
to taste many meats at once
✔ Anyone who wants a hearty meal
on the way to downtown Charleston

I’d never had beef this good
before!
