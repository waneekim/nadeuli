Noljasup, Dongducheon — Indoor Climbing, Air Bounce and a Net Zone to Wear the Kids Out (Prices & Weekend Lines)

A place to go with kids in Dongducheon 🌲

On a Sunday in early November last year,
the kids needed
to burn off some energy,
so we headed to Noljasup (놀자숲)
in Dongducheon.

The net zone — kids run around a woven-net space like it's a trampoline

📍 1 Tapdonggasan-ro
Dongducheon, Gyeonggi-do
⏰ Indoor 10:00–18:00
Outdoor 10:00–17:00
Closed Mondays
(open on public holidays)
💰 Day pass, indoor 30,000 won
Afternoon pass, indoor 24,000 won
👶 Under 115 cm tall:
junior pass + guardian pass
(36 months and up)
🅿 Free parking
👟 Sneakers & socks required
📞 031-866-5560

In a word,
it’s an energy explosion zone!

From indoor climbing
to a giant air bounce
and a trampoline-like net zone.

While the kids play,
the parents end up watching
with their hearts in their mouths.

🧗 The climbing area — even kindergartners try it

We especially liked
the climbing area.

The kids wear safety gear
and a staff member
stays right beside them,
so even a first grader and a kindergartner
switched into challenge mode.

The climbing area — kids climb in a helmet and harness

Several kinds of tall walls, too — with safety staff close by

The colors are bright
and the space is big,
so the kids never got bored.

🎈 The outdoor air bounce — excitement at 200%

The air bounce outside,
with the sunshine on top of it,
had the kids’ excitement at 200%!

The outdoor air bounce — even more colorful in the sunshine

After a lap of the indoor area,
by the time you get here,
they’re totally worn out.

⏳ Weekends mean fairly long lines

It’s a popular place,
so on weekends
the lines tend to get fairly long.

Still, it’s the kind of place
that has kids shouting “Let’s come again!“,
so on a day you want them to burn energy,
we highly recommend it!

🔑 KICKBe ready for weekend lines!
Even so, the kids shout

📌 Good to know before you go

✔ Naver Place has
a ‘Booking’ (예매) tab,
but entry, junior and guardian passes
are sold on site only.
✔ For the activities,
sneakers and socks are a must
(no Crocs, sandals, slippers or skirts)
✔ Outside food
is restricted.
✔ Parking is free.
✔ Per the October notice,
outdoor areas close at 17:00,
which can change with the weather.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Days you really want
to tire the kids out
✔ Anyone who wants to try
indoor climbing with their kids
✔ Anyone who wants indoor and outdoor play
in a single day

On a day you really want
to wear the kids out,
it’s worth the trip to Dongducheon.
Just be ready for the weekend lines.
