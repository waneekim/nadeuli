Ansan Festa 2026 — Kim Hong-do Festival Programs, Schedule & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Hwarang Park

October, with its cool breeze —
Ansan’s autumn festivals
all come together in one place 🍂

Now in its 3rd year!
Ansan Festa 2026
(안산페스타 2026)
runs Fri, Oct 23 – Sun, Oct 25,
three days around Hwarang Park
and Ansan Arts Center.

We put this together from
Ansan city’s festival news (October 2)
and the festival pages
on the city website.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Ansan Festa bundles festivals and events
that used to be held separately
into Ansan’s combined autumn festival.
These are the 7 events this year.

EventAnnounced dates & venue
Kim Hong-do Festival (7th)Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun), around Hwarang Park
Ansan Food Culture FestivalOct 23 (Fri) – 24 (Sat), Hwarang Park
Lifelong Learning FestivalOct 24 (Sat) 10:00, around the Hwarang Park main stage
Disability Welfare ExpoDetails not announced yet
Urban Farming FestivalDetails not announced yet
Ansan Mayor’s Cup Danwon Kim Hong-do Ssireum (Korean wrestling) TournamentDetails not announced yet
Resident Autonomy “with” Village Community ExpoDetails not announced yet

This year the 7 events are linked
along a single route
so you can move from one to the next.

While the festival is on,
the Kim Hong-do Art Museum,
Gyeonggi MOMA
and the Ansan Industrial History Museum
also hold special exhibitions.

✨ What’s on when?

Different events run on different days.
Here are the dates announced so far.

DateEvents
Oct 23 (Fri)Kim Hong-do Festival · Food Culture Festival
Oct 24 (Sat)Kim Hong-do Festival · Food Culture Festival · Lifelong Learning Festival (10:00)
Oct 25 (Sun)Kim Hong-do Festival

The Food Culture Festival runs only on the 23rd and 24th.
So far, only the Kim Hong-do Festival
is listed for Sunday the 25th.

🎭 What’s at the Kim Hong-do Festival?

It’s a festival built around
Danwon Kim Hong-do (단원 김홍도),
the Joseon-era painter
who is one of Ansan’s signature cultural assets.
It’s billed as a citizen-led festival
made together by local artists and residents.

TypePrograms
ShowsKim Hong-do madang play (open-air theater) · traditional performances · local artists’ shows
ActivitiesHorse riding, tea ceremony & hanbok try-on · Kim Hong-do village activities
PlayTraditional playground
Old villageKim Hong-do Folk Village · Joseon-style tavern
Drawing & exhibitsKim Hong-do outdoor drawing contest · a media exhibition reimagining his works

The programs let you experience
the times and everyday life
seen in Kim Hong-do’s paintings.
It’s open to all ages.

🍡 Food Culture Festival & Lifelong Learning Festival

The Ansan Food Culture Festival
is billed as a food festival
’for the whole family to enjoy together’.

Hall / activityWhat’s there
Food Culture HallHealthy food culture · promoting good local restaurants
Food Safety HallEating habits for children & seniors, country-of-origin comparison activity
Tasting & Activity HallTasting Ansan’s signature local dishes
Excellent Companies HallProducts from outstanding local food makers, on display & to taste
Making activitiesMaking traditional rice cakes & grape gochujang

The Lifelong Learning Festival
(평생학습한마당)
starts at 10:00 on Saturday the 24th
around the Hwarang Park main stage.

An opening performance,
an awards ceremony for the adult literacy
poem-and-painting exhibition,
the ‘Challenge! Literacy Golden Bell’ quiz,
lifelong learning club showcases
and hands-on activities are listed.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The Food Culture Festival has
a Food Safety Hall
covering eating habits for children and seniors
and a country-of-origin comparison activity.
Traditional rice cake and grape gochujang making
is on too.

At the Kim Hong-do Festival,
a traditional playground
plus horse riding and hanbok try-ons are listed.

The only day
all three events overlap
is Saturday the 24th.

🔑 KICKThe Food Culture Festival runs only on the 23rd & 24th
If you want to make rice cakes or gochujang,
plan for Friday or Saturday
