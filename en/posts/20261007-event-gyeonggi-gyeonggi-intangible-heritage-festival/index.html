Gyeonggi-do Intangible Heritage Festival 2026 — Schedule & Workshop Sign-up Before You Take the Kids to Gapyeong Culture & Arts Center

Gyeonggi-do’s traditional arts
gather in autumn Gapyeong 🥁

The 28th edition this year!
The 28th Gyeonggi-do Intangible Heritage Festival
(제28회 경기도 무형유산 대축제)
runs for three days, Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, Oct 18,
at Gapyeong Culture & Arts Center
(가평문화예술회관).

We put this together from
Gyeonggi-do’s press release of October 6
and the workshop notice from the
Gyeonggi Institute of History and Cultural Heritage.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s subtitle is
’The Style and Joy of Gyeonggi —
Carried On Together, Here Together’.

Gyeonggi-do’s intangible heritage
masters and practitioners
come in person to perform,
show their work
and demonstrate how it’s made.
There are hands-on programs too.

This year there’s also
an international exchange program
introducing the traditional culture
of Poland’s Lower Silesian Voivodeship.

The head of Gyeonggi-do’s Cultural Heritage Division
described it as a festival where you meet everything
’from the performing arts on stage
to works born at the fingertips of master artisans’
in one place.

🥁 What performances are there?

Traditional arts handed down
across Gyeonggi-do take to
the indoor and outdoor stages
during the festival.
These are the shows named in the press release.

StagePerformances
Indoor & outdoor stages (16th–18th)Icheon Geobuknori (turtle play) · Gyeonggi Geommu (sword dance) · Gyeonggi Songseo & Yulchang · Yangju Nongak · Toegyewon Sandaenori and more
Polish guest performanceLegnica Song and Dance Ensemble · traditional Polish folk dance and singing
Ceremony, Sat 17thThe first-ever ‘Gyeonggi-do Intangible Heritage Transmission and Remembrance Day’ · tribute performance by transmission instructors, awards for contributors

Lots of unfamiliar names?
Here are short descriptions from
the Korea Heritage Service’s heritage portal.

PerformanceWhat it is
Icheon Geobuknori (이천거북놀이)A Chuseok folk game: a turtle is made from sorghum stalks, people get inside, and it goes around the village wishing everyone good fortune
Yangju Nongak (양주농악)‘Nongsa-puri’ farmers’ music that plays at imitating farm work · a classic of northern Gyeonggi nongak
Toegyewon Sandaenori (퇴계원산대놀이)A mask dance drama from Toegyewon, Namyangju · masks carved from logs; nobles, servants and others bring satire and laughs
Gyeonggi Geommu (경기검무)A dance performed with swords · the first half is calm, the second half, with swords in hand, is powerful
Gyeonggi Songseo · Yulchang (경기송서·율창)‘Reading sounds’ — old texts and poems read to a melody · like chanting the Thousand Character Classic as ‘haneul cheon, ttang ji’ (sky 天, earth 地)

Geobuknori is a village-wide game
that unfolds round by round —
a road parade, a well rite,
village play and a gate rite.

The sandaenori has 12 acts,
with songs such as
’Cheongchunga’ and ‘Changbu Taryeong’.

Gyeonggi Geommu has more moves
that look like sparring with a partner
than the court sword dance.

Times for each performance
haven’t been announced yet.

🏺 What’s on display?

In the exhibition hall,
the works of artisans who have carried on
traditional crafts show the past
and future of intangible heritage.

ExhibitionWhat’s in it
Memory Space (기억 공간)Works, photos, videos and instruments of late masters — a look back at the footsteps of past artisans and performers
Future Exhibition (미래전)Works by transmission instructors · traditional skills passing to the next generation
Polish crafts43 pieces of Bolesławiec pottery, a 700-year tradition · 13 pieces of traditional glasswork

📜 Shall we try papermaking with the kids?

The one to try with kids is
the traditional papermaking workshop
from the Paper Museum
in Duszniki-Zdrój, Poland.

The press release describes it as a chance
to experience Polish paper culture first-hand
in Gapyeong, a place that keeps
the tradition of hanji (Korean paper).

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

In the Gyeonggi Institute of History
and Cultural Heritage notice,
the Polish performance, exhibition and workshop
run on Fri the 16th and Sat the 17th —
one day shorter than the festival period
in the province’s press release (until the 18th).

For papermaking, plan for
the afternoon of the 16th or the 17th,
and sign up in advance.
The workshop is held in the outdoor plaza.

On Saturday the 17th there’s also the ceremony
and a tribute performance
by the transmission instructors —
a moment to honor the practitioners
who have kept intangible heritage alive.

🔑 KICKPapermaking is first come, first served — sign up in advance
Sign-up closes on the 16th
There's no papermaking on Sunday the 18th
