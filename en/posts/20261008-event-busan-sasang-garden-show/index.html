Busan Garden Show 2026 Dates, Stage Schedule & Shuttle – What to Know Before Visiting Samnak Ecological Park with Kids

It’s the season for strolling through gardens
in the breeze off the river 🌿

Busan Garden Show 2026 (부산 가든쇼)
runs from October 15 (Thu) to 18 (Sun)
at Busan Nakdong River Garden,
in the lotus pond area of Samnak Ecological Park, Sasang-gu.

This year’s theme is
”A Life with Gardens, Stories Linked by Gardens.”

I checked the City of Busan Park City Division notices
and the venue leaflet posted on October 8
again on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🌳 What gardens can you see?

Here is how the garden exhibits are organized,
according to the leaflet’s venue map.

Garden typeNumber
Local artist gardens6 (Yeonje-gu, Buk-gu, Busanjin-gu, Jung-gu, Dongnae-gu, Sasang-gu)
Special gardens2
ESG corporate partner gardens10
Citizen gardens21 (including 10 “Big Hand” gardens)
Exchange garden1 (Seoul)

The “Big Hand” gardens (큰손바닥정원) are small gardens
of 12㎡ each, chosen through a public contest.
The final awards ceremony is said to take place
at 11:00 on the 15th on the opening ceremony stage.

There’s also a stamp tour where you collect
5 stamps at five gardens.
Pick up your souvenir at Information Center 1 or 2;
it may end early if souvenirs run out.

🎤 What’s on stage, and when?

The stages run 10:00~17:00 every day.
Here are mainly the main-stage picks.

Date · TimeProgram
10/15 (Thu) 10:30Celebration show by a children’s cheerleading team
10/15 (Thu) 11:00Opening ceremony
10/15 (Thu) 13:00Garden concert
10/16 (Fri) 13:00Magic bubble show
10/16 (Fri) 15:00Garden concert (Korean traditional music band)
10/17 (Sat) 11:00 · 13:00Dancing Garden (random play dance, etc.)
10/17 (Sat) 16:00Garden OX quiz
10/18 (Sun) 13:00Raon Boys and Girls Choir
10/18 (Sun) 15:00Garden Golden Bell quiz

On the sub stage, garden busking
and garden talks continue through the days.

🪴 What activities can kids try?

In the activity zone, with advance or on-site sign-up,
you can plant flowerpots, decorate pebbles, make worry stones,
build moss terrariums, try flower face painting,
and make volcanic-rock orchid pieces.

Programs with set times are listed below.

ProgramTime · Capacity
Children’s English garden tour10/15~16 11:00 · 15:00, 10 per session, on-site sign-up
Garden exhibit tour with citizen gardenersDaily 10:30 · 14:30, 15 per session, on-site sign-up
Nakdong Galmaetgil walkDaily 10:30 · 14:30, 700 people, advance or on-site
Garden school (gardening)10/17~18 11:00 · 15:00, 40 per session
Garden healing yoga10/16 16:00, 10/17~18 10:00, 30 per session
Garden writing class10/17 12:00, 30 people
Kitchen soap from coffee grounds10/18 10:00~17:00, first 100 people

For garden school, yoga and writing, you can apply in advance
through the Google Forms in the City of Busan notice;
for on-site sign-up, ask at Information Center 1 or 2.

The leaflet notes that sign-up is first come, first served
and may close early.
Fees for each activity are not listed in the notices or the leaflet.

🍢 Is there food or a market?

Four food trucks sell drinks, bunsik snacks
(sundae, fish cakes, tteokbokki),
chicken skewers, and takoyaki and okonomiyaki.

The venue map also shows 13 Garden Market booths
selling houseplants, flowerpots and bonsai,
plus a flea market and a garden bookshop.

🚌 How do the shuttle and parking work?

The venue loop shuttle is free

The main entrance is on the Parking Lot P9 side.
A loop shuttle connects the parking lots and the venue.

Loop shuttleDetails
FareFree
Hours10:00~17:00, every 15 minutes
Stop ①In front of Parking Lot P9
Stop ②In front of Gangbyeon Nadeul Bridge
Stop ③In front of Parking Lot P5 (near the Soccer Field C temporary lot)

Parking fees and subway or bus directions
are not given in the notices.

👨‍👩‍👧 If you’re going with kids

The venue map marks
a children’s playground inside the activity zone.

The English garden tour runs only on Thu and Fri,
and garden school only on Sat and Sun.
Start by choosing the dates of the programs your kids want.

🔑 KICKBoth the stages and the shuttle end at 17:00.
Activity sign-up is first come, first served,
so arrive in the morning and stop by an information center first.

👍 Recommended for these families

✔ Families looking for flowerpot or terrarium crafts with kids
✔ Families interested in the English garden tour or garden school
✔ Anyone planning an autumn walk in a riverside park

A good event to combine garden strolls and activities
in one daytime outing.

Check the official notice once more before you go!
