Dasan Jeong Yak-yong Cultural Festival 2026 — Schedule, Programs & Parking Before You Take the Kids to the Jeong Yak-yong Historic Site

An autumn festival is coming
to the home village of
Joseon-era scholar Jeong Yak-yong 🍂

The 40th edition this year!
The 40th Dasan Jeong Yak-yong Cultural Festival
(제40회 다산정약용문화제)
runs for two days, Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, Oct 18,
10:00–17:30,
around the Jeong Yak-yong Historic Site
and Dasan Ecological Park in Namyangju.

We put this together from the schedule
on Namyangju’s festival website
(updated September 30), its program guide
and the city’s press release (October 6).

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Running since 1986,
it’s Namyangju City’s signature festival.
This year’s theme is ‘Jeong Yak-yong,
Who Designed His Era, Innovates Today’.

The festival grounds are
the Jeong Yak-yong Historic Site —
with Yeoyudang (여유당), the house where
he was born and grew up, his tomb
and the Dasan Memorial Hall —
and Dasan Ecological Park,
facing Paldang Lake.

The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism’s
regional festival listing
shows admission as free.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The hands-on booths, Dasan Farm Market
and ‘exile’ experience run
10:00–17:30 on both days.

into a table for each day.

is open 10:00–18:00 on both days.

🏗️ What activities are there?

Jeong Yak-yong’s inventions and ideas —
there are lots of activities to discover them through play.

ActivityWhat it is · Who it’s for
Dasan PlaygroundTry a giant geojunggi (crane), and make a nongno pulley, a yuhyeonggeo cart and a mini crane
Yeoyudang HuntersA mobile treasure hunt following clues around the festival grounds
Jeong Yak-yong Future WorkshopTry future tech like AI and robots
Digital-detox ‘exile’ experiencePut your phone down and unwind with books, games and music
Traditional games & relaxationHanbok & traditional games, tea from Dasan Dawon, booths run by local agencies
Challenge! Top ScholarA Jeong Yak-yong quiz · lower & upper elementary divisions, pre-registration
Arts & writing contestDrawing, essay writing & handwriting · kindergarten and school students in Namyangju
Dasan Farm MarketNamyangju produce, food & a flea market, Brand Partners pop-up zone

The Top Scholar lower division is for grades 1–3,
and the upper division for grades 4–6 of elementary school.

Entries for the arts & writing contest close
Thursday, October 8, per the Namyangju Cultural Center notice.

The Robot Creation Contest’s call for 60 family teams
already closed on September 30.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

If your kids are in elementary school,
on the 18th you can pair
the Top Scholar quiz
with the crane at Dasan Playground.

The booths open at 10:00,
and the big stage shows are all after 16:00.

The same week, Oct 15–17,
Namyangju City’s ‘2026 Book Festa’ also runs
at Dasan Culture Park in front of
Jeong Yak-yong Library in Dasan-dong.
On Sat the 17th it’s 11:00–18:00,
and it’s open to ‘any Namyangju resident’.

🔑 KICKThe opening & fashion show (17th)
and the concert (18th) are after 16:00
The concert ends at 17:30
Bus 58 runs every 30–60 min on Sunday
Check your bus home first
