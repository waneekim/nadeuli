Charleston, Part 4: Spice Shops, Local Artists’ Paintings and an Antique Shop, Around 300,000 Won for One Spoon? (US Business Trip, Day 7)

Part 4 of our Charleston downtown series is
the story of looking around the shops 🛍️

A spice and herb shop, a gallery of local artists’ paintings,
and even an antique shop with outrageous prices!
Just looking made us feel rich.

The downtown shopping hall, with clothes, bag and hat shops in a row (faces of people blurred with mosaic)

📍 Shop-hopping in downtown Charleston
(Charleston, South Carolina, USA)
🌿 Spices, herbs and teas filling the walls
🖼️ Local artists' paintings: prints from $40
🥄 Antique shop: around 300,000 won for one spoon?
💸 Just looking made us feel rich

🌿 A Colorful Spice and Herb Shop

The first place we stopped at was
a spice and herb shop.

Spices filled big glass jars and bags
all over the wall,
and there was a truly colorful variety.

Spice jars and bags filling the wall

On the brick wall next to it,
under a TEA sign,
tea leaves and herbal teas stood in rows.

A shelf of tea leaves under the TEA sign (faces of people in the distance blurred with mosaic)

🖼️ A Gallery of Local Artists’ Paintings

Next was a gallery of local artists’ paintings.
It was full of colorful paintings
of Charleston’s streets.

Some of the paintings showed places like Rainbow Row and St. Philip’s Church,
so it was even nicer to see
the places we had walked that day.

Detta Cutting Zimmerman's paintings of Charleston streets; the white paper below is the price tag

On the price tags,
canvas and ink prints were $40 to $125,
and the hand-painted originals were $275 to $1,400.

I wanted to buy one,
but it felt a little expensive, so I passed.

Flower and sea paintings on another wall; the artist's name tag reads amelia whaley

KICKThe gallery has everything from prints ($40 to $125)
to originals.
If an original is too much, go for a print! 🖼️

🥄 An Outrageous Antique Shop

But the price of the paintings was
nothing
compared to the antique shop.

Geo. C. Birlant & Co. on King St
is an antique shop
that I heard opened in 1922.

The GEO. C. BIRLANT & CO. ANTIQUES display window, with plates and a chandelier (faces of passers-by and license plates blurred)

Inside, shiny silverware
filled every display case.

A display case full of silver pots, cups and trays

A silver-plated tea and coffee set on a table, with a handwritten tag

Even the old golf clubs had tags on them

The silver spoons on red velvet were,
as I remember, around 300,000 won each (Korean money). 😳

Just looking made us feel
like we had become rich!

Silver spoons and serving tools on red velvet, each with a handwritten tag

👍 Who I’d Recommend It To

✔ Anyone who likes looking at
spices and tea
✔ Anyone who wants to bring home
one painting from a trip
✔ Anyone who wants to feel rich
by looking at antiques

Just looking made it
a time when we felt rich!
