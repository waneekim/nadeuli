Daemeori Kalguksu, Mangpo (Suwon) — Clam Noodle Soup for 8,500 Won with the Review Event, and a Chewy, Crispy Cuttlefish Pancake

Our Mangpo kalguksu review 🍜

We had an errand in Mangpo-dong,
so on the way
we filled up first
at Daemeori Kalguksu (대머리칼국수).

We only stopped in
because it was near where we were going,
but this meal is what stuck with us.

A bowl of clam kalguksu — clams in a milky broth topped with seaweed

59 Yeongtong-ro 200beon-gil
⏰ 11:00–22:00
Mondays from 17:00
Last order 21:00

🅿 Free (out front & underground)
📞 031-273-2338

🍜 The kalguksu: cheap and tasty

In a word: great value!

Kalguksu is Korean
knife-cut noodle soup.

The broth is milky white,
it comes topped with seaweed (gim),
and there are clams in it.

Clams in the broth — still in their shells

Lift the noodles
and this is what you get.

Lifting the noodles (GIF)

🦑 The cuttlefish pancake: chewy and crispy

The cuttlefish and chive pancake
we ordered with it
is the second thing we remember.

Packed with chives and fried thin,
its edges are crispy,

while the pieces of cuttlefish
you bite into here and there
are chewy.

You get both textures
together in one bite.

Cuttlefish and chive pancake — packed with chives and fried thin, so the edges are crispy

Cutting it with scissors — you can see the texture (GIF)

🎟 8,500 won with the review event

The price is the whole point here.

Clam kalguksu is 9,500 won at full price,
and with 1,000 won off
from the review event,
a bowl comes to 8,500 won!

(Many Korean restaurants run
’review events’:
post a review, get a small discount.)

Given what kalguksu costs these days,
that price stands out.

🔑 KICKJoin the review event

📋 Menu & prices

The listing says that if you ask for
’lots of clams’ (바지락 많이),
they add more at no extra cost.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking for
a good-value meal in Mangpo
✔ Anyone who wants a pancake
alongside their clam kalguksu
✔ Anyone coming by car
(free parking, underground too)

The kalguksu stays with you for its price,
the cuttlefish pancake
for its texture.

If you have something to do around Mangpo,
you won’t regret
having your meal here.
