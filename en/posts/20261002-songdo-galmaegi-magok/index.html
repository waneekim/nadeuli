Where to Eat in Magok with Kids: Songdo Galmaegi at K-Square — Tender Beef Rib First, Sweet Marinated Pork Skirt Next, Two Bowls Gone in No Time

Our family BBQ dinner out in Magok 🍖

On a Friday evening,
at Songdo Galmaegi (송도갈매기) Magok
on K-Square’s second basement floor,
the whole family had dinner together.

Marinated galmaegi-sal, grilled glossy over charcoal

📍 105-7 Magokjungang-ro
K-Square Magok Tower 1
B2 (second basement floor)
⏰ Daily 11:00–22:00
Last order 21:00
💰 Traditional galmaegi, 1 serving
20,000 won (180 g)
🅿 K-Square parking
2 hours free evenings & weekends
👶 High chairs · groups · reservations
📞 02-6989-5299

We had the beef rib meat first,
then the marinated galmaegi-sal
(pork skirt meat).

The beef rib was tender,
and the galmaegi-sal was sweet and delicious.

🏮 EST 1988, a ‘100-year shop’

The sign shows ‘EST 1988’
together with ‘백년가게’
(Baengnyeon Gage, ‘100-year shop’).

The screen by the entrance says,
“We will be a Songdo Galmaegi
that carries on for 100 years.”

Songdo Galmaegi Magok's entrance — EST 1988, '100-year shop'

The board at the entrance shows
three kinds of ‘charcoal galmaegi

🥬 Lettuce from the smart farm

Right beside your seat,
behind a glass wall,
you can see the smart farm.

On shelves several tiers high,
lettuce grows
under LED lights.

The smart farm behind the glass wall next to our table

Lettuce growing under LED lights

The glass wall says,
“We bring it to your table.”

Handwritten shelf labels
showed vegetable names like ‘Butter Red’
and dates.

Grown right here in the restaurant,
the greens were incredibly fresh!

Leafy greens growing tier upon tier

📱 Order on the table tablet

Every table has an ordering tablet.

For meat, your first order
must add up to at least 2 servings,
but you can order 1 serving of each item.

Our cart had
1 serving of fresh beef rib meat,
2 servings of traditional galmaegi
and 1 abalone galbitang (after-meal menu) —
87,000 won in total.

The table ordering tablet — fresh beef rib meat, traditional galmaegi, abalone galbitang

In the middle of the table

🥩 Beef rib first

First, the fresh beef rib meat
went on the charcoal.

We ate it golden outside, a little pink inside,
and it was really tender.

Beef rib meat over the charcoal

Once cooked, the meat

Next, the marinated traditional galmaegi.

🍲 Galbitang — and a bowl of rice

Last came the galbitang (short rib soup).

It’s full of a big rib bone, meat, jujubes,
glass noodles and green onion.
(On the ordering screen: ‘abalone galbitang’, after-meal menu)

The galbitang was so good
the grown-ups each finished
a bowl of rice.

🚗 Parking

Parking in the K-Square building
starts at B3,
and the pillar rows numbered in the 50s
are said to be closest to the restaurant.

✔ Weekday lunch: 1 hour free
✔ Weekday evenings & weekends: 2 hours free
✔ After that, 2,000 won per 30 min

(per Naver Place)
