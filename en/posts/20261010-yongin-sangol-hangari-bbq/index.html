Sangol Hangari Barbecue in Yongin: Camping Atmosphere, Ready-to-Eat BBQ, Prices and Parking

For a taste of camping,
with the cooking taken care of.

I visited Sangol Hangari Barbecue
(산골항아리바베큐) in Gogi-ri, Yongin.

I’d originally gone for
buckwheat noodles at Gogi-ri Makguksu,
but the wait was over 2 hours 30 minutes.

That felt too long to wait,
so I headed to the barbecue restaurant
right across the way, which looked like a campsite.

🏕 A pleasant taste of camping

I was disappointed to miss the noodles,
but I liked the atmosphere and theme
more than I’d expected.

It offers a little of that camping feeling
without packing up camping gear.
It struck me as a nice place for autumn.

For a relaxed taste of the camping atmosphere,
it seemed like an easy option.

The deck beneath the trees

There was a spacious parking lot to one side,
so parking was easy.

I liked that even arriving and parking
felt straightforward that day.

🍖 The meat arrives already cooked

There was no need to sit by the fire
and grill the meat myself here.

The meat arrived already cooked,
ready to eat.

Being able to focus on the meal
without tending the fire or turning the meat
was certainly convenient.

💬 What I missed as someone who camps

I appreciated the easy meal,
but there were a few downsides.

As someone who already goes camping,
I found it fairly expensive.
There was a time limit, too.

It’s worth checking with the restaurant
before booking or ordering
how long you can stay.

The meat’s texture may also divide opinions.

To me, it had very little fattiness
and felt like suyuk, Korean boiled meat.
That’s how it tasted to me that day.

It might feel different
from the meat flavor you imagine
when you hear “barbecue.”

Then there’s the fun of grilling.

Having everything cooked for me was handy,
but I enjoy grilling my own meat
when I camp, so
it was a little less fun for me.

How you feel may depend
on whether you want an easy meal
or enjoy the cooking itself.

👍 When this place might suit you

✔ When you want a light taste of camping
✔ When you want barbecue without grilling it yourself
✔ When you’re looking for a Gogi-ri restaurant where I found parking easy

I liked the atmosphere and convenience;
the price and meat texture came down to preference.

If I wanted an easy meal
in a camping setting,
Sangol Hangari Barbecue would come to mind.
