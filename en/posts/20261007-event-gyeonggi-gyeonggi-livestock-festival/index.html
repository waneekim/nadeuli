2026 Gyeonggi-do Livestock Promotion Festival — Schedule, Grill Master Demo & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Imjingak

An autumn livestock festival
is coming to Imjingak
Pyeonghwa-Nuri Park 🐂

The 58th edition!
The 58th Gyeonggi-do Livestock Promotion Festival
(제58회 경기도 축산진흥대회)
runs Sat, Oct 24 – Sun, Oct 25,
two days in the parking lot area
of Imjingak Pyeonghwa-Nuri Park in Paju.

We put this together from
the event pages on Gyeonggi-do’s website,
the October issue of ‘My Gyeonggi’ (나의 경기도)
and Gyeonggi-do’s September 16 press release.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Gyeonggi-do describes it as
’a livestock culture festival
made together by livestock farmers and consumers’.

It began in 1969
as a Hanwoo (Korean beef cattle) contest,
and this is its 58th year.
This year’s slogan is
’Eat-多 (잇-다): carrying on
the value of Gyeonggi livestock’
(a pun: 잇다 means ‘to carry on’ and sounds like ‘eat’).

The festival’s stated purpose calls it
a ‘Farm To Table’ event
linking farmers’ production
to city dwellers’ consumption.

This year it’s being run as
a hands-on festival centered on consumers.
It’s hosted and organized by NongHyup Agribusiness Group’s
Gyeonggi headquarters, sponsored by
Gyeonggi-do, Paju-si and others,
and some 10,000 livestock farmers
and city residents are expected.

✨ What’s on when?

The stage programs differ by day.
We’ve put the timetable
from Gyeonggi-do’s website into a table.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 24 (Sat) 13:40Opening performance · event guide
Oct 24 (Sat) 14:00Opening ceremony · Handon (Korean pork) donation ceremony (livestock product sharing event)
Oct 24 (Sat) 15:00Hanwoo Cattle Contest: watching the judging videos and reviewing the cattle, awards for the winning cattle
Oct 24 (Sat) 15:50–18:00Celebration show & residents’ talent show (guest singers, Gyeonggi residents)
Oct 25 (Sun) 10:30–12:00Cities & counties fun sports day
Oct 25 (Sun) 13:30–14:30Grill Master Demo — 4 Hanwoo grill masters on stage
Oct 25 (Sun) 14:30–16:00Premium Beef Evaluation Contest video & review, awards and commendations

In the Hanwoo Cattle Contest segment,
everyone watches the remote judging videos together,
reviews the cattle,
and the winning cattle get their awards.

The final awards on day two
also include commendations for grill masters
and awards for the overall evaluation
of Gyeonggi’s cities and counties.

🍖 What’s in the grill zone & activities?

Besides the stage, the grounds have
spaces to taste and try things.

SpaceWhat’s there
Grill zoneA space to taste quality livestock products for yourself
Family activity spaceLivestock activity zone · event zone
Crop-livestock recycling boothPlant a pot using compost & liquid fertilizer made from livestock manure (Clean Livestock Fair, round 3, 24th–25th)

The G-Mark is a label
on livestock products certified
by the Governor of Gyeonggi-do.

Hours and prices for the grill zone
and activity spaces
aren’t in the official notices yet.

🚗 Getting there & parking

The venue is the parking lot area itself

Gyeonggi-do lists the venue as
the Imjingak Pyeonghwa-Nuri Park parking lot area.
How parking will work on festival days
isn’t in the official notices yet.

These are the regular parking fees
at the Imjingak tourist site,
set by Paju city ordinance.

VehicleImjingak parking fee
Compact car1,000 won
Small & mid-size car2,000 won
Large vehicle5,000 won

Paju’s culture & tourism site
lists the Imjingak tourist site’s address as
148-53 Imjingak-ro, Munsan-eup.

Public transport and shuttle information
isn’t in the official notices yet.
For general Imjingak information,
call the Imjingak Tourist Information Center (031-953-4744).

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The demo where 4 grill masters
show their Hanwoo grilling skills on stage
runs for an hour from 13:30 on Sunday the 25th.

The compost potting booth,
round 3 of the Gyeonggi Clean Livestock Fair,
is listed for both days, the 24th and 25th.
How to take part will be announced later
by Gyeonggi-do’s Livestock Policy Division
or on the Eco-Friendly Livestock Association website.

The Imjingak tourist site has
the Bell of Peace, the Freedom Bridge
and the steam locomotive
from Jangdan Station on the Gyeongui Line.
You can look around before or after the festival.
