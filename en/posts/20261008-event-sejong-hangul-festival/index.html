2026 Sejong Hangeul Festival Schedule · Drone Show Times · Parking, Before Visiting Jungang Park and Lake Park with Kids

In the autumn marking the 100th anniversary of Hangeul Day,
two parks in Sejong become the stage for a Hangeul festival 🔤

The 2026 Sejong Hangeul Festival
will be held from October 9 (Fri) to 11 (Sun)
at Sejong Jungang Park and Sejong Lake Park.
Admission is free.

I’ve organized the information from Sejong City’s press release (October 6)
and the festival website’s FAQ and leaflet.

Shall we take a quick look first?

🎪 About the Festival

This is a Sejong City festival held since 2013.

This year’s slogan is ‘Hangeul to the World’.
There are a total of 82 performances and experience programs.

During the day, you can enjoy activities at Lake Park and Jungang Park,
and in the evening, the main stage performances at Jungang Park continue.

✨ What’s Happening on the Main Stage?

Here’s the main stage schedule at Jungang Park Urban Festival Square,
collected according to the leaflet timetable.

The Black Eagles will fly over the park for about 5 minutes.
This may change depending on the weather.

The drone and fireworks shows near the main stage last about 10 minutes,
and the theme of the drone show on the 9th is the 580th anniversary of Hunminjeongeum’s proclamation.

🚇 Getting There · Parking

The organizers recommend public transport

They ask visitors to use public transport and walk
because parking lots will be very crowded.

Departure & BusDrop-off Location
Osong Station BRT B2·B3·A3Government Sejong Complex South, about 15 minutes on foot
Jochiwon Station Bus 801Office for Government Policy Coordination (Hantteul Village 3rd Complex), about 7 minutes on foot
Sejong Express/Intercity Bus Terminal Bus 203·205Sejong Arts High School, about 2 minutes on foot
Sejong Express/Intercity Bus Terminal Bus 222National Library of Korea, Sejong, about 2 minutes on foot

Parking: 11 parking lots are available.
It’s recommended to search by parking lot name
rather than address in navigation apps.

Lake Park Parking Lots 1, 2, 3; Eojin C47;
14-2 Temporary, 15-1 Outdoor, Urban Symbol Square;
Sejong Arts Center; Jungang Park LH Temporary;
Jungang Park Parking Lot 1, National Sejong Arboretum;
Ieung Bridge North Parking Lot.

Among these, Sejong Arts Center, Jungang Park LH Temporary,
and Ieung Bridge North Parking Lot will be closed for entry/exit
until 11:40 on the morning of the 9th during the Hangeul Run.
(The start time varies between 08:20 and 08:40 depending on the guide)

The car-free street will operate from 19:00 on the 8th
until 07:00 on the 12th.

There is no shuttle bus,
but the paid circulating Hangeul Train runs.

Hangeul TrainInfo

👨‍👩‍👧 Tips for Visiting with Kids

Strollers can be rented at the Lake Park Management Center.
There are 3 strollers and 2 twin strollers,
and rentals are expected to stop after sunset.

If a child is lost, go to the information center.
They are located at Lake Park Central Plaza, Green Field,
and Jungang Park Jangnamdeul Plaza.

During the festival, both parks are pedestrian-only,
so bicycles and kickboards are not allowed.

🔑 KICKLake Park programs end at 18:00
The leaflet also recommends Lake Park during the day,
and Jungang Park at night
Do the activities first, then the main stage in the evening!
