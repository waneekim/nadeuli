How We Pack Our IONIQ 9 for Car Camping: Half the Mat Set Up, 3 Boxes for the Rest

Long chairs and the board go in first,
the mat only half, the rest in 3 boxes.

Our family’s car camping
always starts as a very simple camp-nic
(a simple camping picnic).

We bring just enough
to unfold chairs and make a bed,
and we buy food whenever we can.

That’s how
the order for loading the IONIQ 9
got settled.

The packed IONIQ 9 trunk, with 3 boxes placed at the back

🚙 IONIQ 9 · simple car camping prep
🪑 4 chairs · small table · picnic mat
🛏 Leveling board · car camping mattress · bedding
👕 Clothes · toiletries · towels · snacks
📦 Small items go in 3 boxes
🧾 Gear we actually use · no purchase or affiliate links

The photos show
the gear packed while the car was parked.

🧺 Packing List: Camp-nic Level

The basics are
4 chairs, a small table and a picnic mat.

For sleeping in the car,
we bring a leveling board, window shades, a leveler,
the car camping mattress and a comforter.

On top of that go the kids’ clothes,
spare clothes for the adults,
simple toiletries and towels.

Sometimes we borrow the EV’s power
and bring a fridge and a microwave,
but they aren’t part of the usual load.

We try to prepare
so that we can buy food when we can.

🪑 Long Chairs and the Board Go Behind the 2nd Row

First, 2 long chairs and
the leveling board go right behind the 2nd row,
slotted in ahead of time.

The chair and board cases
stay at home.

We bring 4 chairs in all,
but the ones that go into this spot first
are the 2 long chairs.

We settle the long items first,
then get the mat ready.

🛏 Set Up Only Half the Mat in Advance

The mat has to be laid out
on site anyway.

So we use the trunk space
to set up about half of the car camping mattress
in advance.

The mat’s case bag
stays at home too.

It’s a way to cut down
the hassle of folding and unfolding
every time we go car camping.

Putting the mat in the IONIQ 9 trunk and arranging it

A close-up of the mat and its edge buckles in the IONIQ 9 trunk

Set up like this,
the folded mat sits right behind the 2nd row.

When we arrive and park
and level the floor,
we only have to unfold the rest.

🧺 Bedding and a Picnic Mat on the Mattress

On the mattress go
soft, irregularly shaped items
like bedding or a picnic mat.

The mat itself is soft,
so we tend to gather this kind of gear
on top of it.

The gear gathered behind the 2nd row, with room left near the trunk opening for the boxes

Our rule when loading is
soft items on the mat, boxes behind the mat.

Dividing the space this way
makes it easy to decide
what goes where next time.

📦 3 Boxes in the Folded 3rd Row Space

Behind the mat,
in the space where the 3rd row is folded,
we put 3 boxes.

Inside the boxes we split up
car camping gear like the 1st-row air mattress,
the window shades and the leveler,
plus the kids’ clothes
and snacks.

Midway through organizing, with folded parts and items gathered by box

Bundling things in boxes
beats leaving them scattered one by one.

It’s also easy to sort them
by type: camping gear, clothes or snacks.

💡 Five Reasons We Bundle Things in Boxes

① Loose items go into a box,
so packing gets easier.

② When we stack things up and down,
it’s easier to match their shapes.

③ It’s easy to separate them by type:
camping gear, clothes, snacks.

④ Soft items gather inside the box,
so they feel a bit more compressed.

⑤ Empty boxes we no longer need
can be folded and brought home.

🔑 KICKSet up half the mat in advance,
and leave its case at home
Bundle small items into 3 boxes

Stacking boxes
and securing cargo while driving are separate matters.

Before you leave,
please check the
cargo loading and securing guidance
in your vehicle owner’s manual.

Owner’s manuals for each model
can be found in
Hyundai’s official download center.

✅ Our car camping loading order
① 2 long chairs and the leveling board behind the 2nd row
② Only half the mat set up in advance
③ Bedding and a picnic mat on the mat
④ 3 boxes in the folded 3rd row space
⑤ After arriving, level the floor and unfold the mat

👍 Who We’d Recommend This To

✔ Anyone preparing
simple car camping or a camp-nic
with the IONIQ 9

✔ Anyone who finds
folding and unfolding the mat
again on arrival a hassle

✔ Anyone who wants to load
trunk items sorted by type

For us, it’s easier
to cut down on reopening cases on site
and sorting gear one piece at a time.

Prepare the mat in advance,
and bundle small items into boxes.

That’s the loading method
our family uses again and again
when we go car camping in the IONIQ 9.
