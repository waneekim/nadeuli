What Should You Buy on Your First Trader Joe’s Visit? The Bags I Picked Instead of the Tote, Plus Gift Shopping (US Business Trip, Day 3)

Day 3 of my US business trip,
an evening story 🛍

Honestly, I didn’t really
know much about Trader Joe’s.

But the people on the trip with me said
you absolutely have to go to Trader Joe’s,
and that the tote bag is famous,
so I stopped by.

A moon-print box-style bag — Greetings from Trader Joe's

📍 Trader Joe's, USA
(on a business trip to Columbia, South Carolina)
👜 Box-style and zip bags instead of the tote
🍫 Dark Chocolate Roasted Pistachio Toffee
🤝 A budget hand cream a colleague told me about
💄 Lip masks as gifts for my mother-in-law and wife
🎃 Gummies for the kids' Halloween party
⭐ Store-brand items you can only buy here

👜 The bags next to the tote

The famous tote bags
were hanging in big numbers, of course.

But they didn’t quite grab me,
and I liked the
box-style bag and zip bag
next to them,
so I picked up two for now.

A zip bag with red handles and a box-style bag open below it

🍫 I mostly skipped GPT’s snack picks

For the rest, I just bought
whatever caught my eye.

I asked GPT too,
but it only recommended
all kinds of American snacks,
so I skipped almost all of them.

Dark Chocolate Peanut Butter Cups seen in the store — a handwritten 'TJ's ESSENTIAL' sign

Pecan Pralines seen in the store

What I picked instead
was the pistachio chocolate.

The exact name is
Dark Chocolate Roasted Pistachio Toffee,
and the sign said
”Buttery, nutty, sweet & great on ice cream”.

Dark Chocolate Roasted Pistachio Toffee (212 g)

🤝 A budget hand cream a colleague told me about

I also bought that ‘weird’
hand cream my colleague told me about.
They said it was a good-value hand cream.

The box had a handshake picture
and the line
”The perfect precursor to your next greeting”.

Two Ultra Moisturizing Hand Creams (85 g) — coconut oil, hemp oil, 20% shea butter

💄 Gifts for my mother-in-law and wife: Cinnamon Roll Lip Mask

I also bought lip masks
as gifts for my mother-in-law and wife.
They’re cinnamon roll flavored.

Three Cinnamon Roll Lip Masks (20 g)

🎃 Getting ready for the kids’ Halloween party

And to get ready for a Halloween party
for our kids,
I bought some Halloween candy too.

They’re Halloween Gummies,
with several small bags inside.

Halloween Gummies — jelly candies divided into small bags

Under a sign with a ghost drawing,
Ghost Pepper Potato Chips
were piled high.
One bag followed me back to the lodging.

Ghost Pepper Potato Chips piled under a ghost-drawing sign

What I bought, spread out in the lodging — the almonds at the top right are from Walmart on day 1

⭐ Why is it so popular?

Once I got there, I saw that Trader Joe’s was
a really popular place.

There are brands you can only get here,
and that’s how I understood
why it’s so popular.
Every item in my photos
had the TRADER JOE’S name on it.

🔑 KICKDon't just look at the tote
Check the box-style and zip bags next to it
For gifts, pick items with the 'TRADER JOE'S' name
that you can only get here

👍 Recommended for

✔ Anyone buying gifts
for family on a US
business trip or vacation
✔ Anyone looking for a bag
other than the tote
✔ Anyone choosing Halloween
snacks for the kids
