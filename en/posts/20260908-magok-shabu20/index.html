Our Magok shabu buffet review 🍲

For a team lunch at work,
we went to
Shabu20 Balsan-Magok.

The meat bar — unlimited beef and pork! A life-size Won Bin cutout guards the entrance

It’s the shabu buffet
that actor Won Bin advertises.

It was so good
we only remembered the camera
after we’d finished eating.

So most of these photos

🥩 Unlimited meat

The main event:
unlimited beef and pork!

Take whole plates from the meat bar,

The broth is self-refill as well.

🚗 Parking

If you drive,
use the underground car park
of the Balsan Park Plaza building.

Per the sign in the restaurant,
parking is up to 90 min at lunch
and up to 120 min at dinner.

It’s not the Queens Park Nine building next door —
it’s the building with IBK Industrial Bank in it.
(per Naver Place)

📋 Menu & prices

Weekday lunch 22,900 won
Weekday dinner, weekends & holidays 26,900 won
Elementary schoolers 13,900 won
Preschoolers (36 months–7 years) 7,500 won

Along with unlimited beef and pork,
rice-paper wraps, dakgangjeong (sweet crispy chicken),
takoyaki, tteokbokki and shrimp fried rice
are free

(per the menu on Naver Place)
