2026 Gwangju Kimchi Festival Dates, Kimchi School, Directions – City Hall Kimchi Playground with Kids

Ahead of kimjang (kimchi-making) season,
a festival for making and tasting kimchi is coming 🥬

The 33rd Gwangju Kimchi Festival
(광주김치축제) runs
October 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun)
around the Gwangju Office of Jeonnam-Gwangju Special City.

I’ve put together the official festival website
and the Ministry of Culture’s festival listing.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

It is hosted by Jeonnam-Gwangju Special City
and organized by the Gwangju Kimchi Festival Committee.
This year’s subtitle is “Overwhelm! Kimchi Party.”

Kimchi activities, exhibitions, markets and contests
fill the city hall grounds for three days.

Admission is free, from 10:00 to 20:00 every day.
Kimchi School charges a separate fee.

🎤 When Are the Big Stage Shows?

All of them are on the main stage of the outdoor music hall.

Date & timeProgram
10/23 (Fri) 14:00Opening show · Gwangju Metropolitan Opera
10/23 (Fri) 18:00Kimchi Party RED NIGHT · opera & crossover
10/24 (Sat) 15:00Kimchi master demonstration · Presidential Award-winning masters
10/24 (Sat) 18:00Kimchi Party K-HEUNG NIGHT · traditional performance & band

The Kimchi Party runs until 20:00 on both nights,
an evening stage where the audience joins in.

At the opening ceremony on the 23rd,
the Korea Kimchi Contest awards are also presented.

🎮 Things to Try with Kids

These activities need no booking.

ActivityOfficial information

Arcade gamesMasters of salting, seasoning and mixing · reward booth
Kimjang sports dayDaily 16:00~17:00 · join on the spot, no sign-up
Sports day eventsArm wrestling · balloon distance · jegichagi (shuttlecock kicking)

Win at the sports day
and you can get festival prizes, too.

🥬 Kimchi School: Book Before You Go

These are paid activities in the plaza in front of the council building.

ItemOfficial information
Kimchi making & cookingStarts at :30 past each hour from 13:30 · 5 sessions a day · 30 min
What you doMix 1kg of kimchi · rice balls with Mudeungsan watermelon pickles
WhoAll ages
Master classDaily 11:00 · 60 min · adults
BookingNaver advance booking · remaining spots taken on site

You get a container to take your kimchi home.

The master class changes master and kimchi every day:
deodeok (bonnet bellflower root) white kimchi on the 23rd,
kohlrabi with ogeurakji (dried radish kimchi) on the 24th,
and whole-wheat seokbakji (chunky radish kimchi) on the 25th.

Because ingredients are prepared in advance,
there may be a cancellation fee.

👨‍👩‍👧 Visiting with Kids

Children’s activities are clustered in the afternoon.
Kimchi School starts at 13:30,
and the kimjang sports day is at 16:00.

The arcade games end at 18:00,
so it’s a good idea to stop by the lawn plaza first.

🔑 KICKKimchi School is booked in advance on Naver
Only remaining spots are taken on site
The master class is for adults
