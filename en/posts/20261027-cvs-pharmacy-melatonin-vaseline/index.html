What Should You Buy at a CVS Pharmacy? Melatonin for Jet Lag and a Giant ‘Only-in-America-Size’ Tub of Vaseline (US Business Trip, Day 3)

Day 3 of my US business trip,
the second story of the evening 💊

After Trader Joe’s,
I stopped by CVS Pharmacy.

Every aisle was full of
all kinds of supplements
and medicine.

A long aisle of supplements at CVS

📍 CVS Pharmacy, USA
(on a business trip to Columbia, South Carolina)
💊 Supplements and medicine in every aisle
😴 What I bought: CVS melatonin 5 mg ·
magnesium glycinate ·
a giant "only-in-America-size" Vaseline
💵 Didn't feel much pricier than Korea

💊 The aisle full of supplements

Vitamins, probiotics,
even nutrition drinks,
packed onto every shelf.

OLLY and Centrum supplement shelf — packed with price tags

Ensure and Boost nutrition drink display

😴 Melatonin, because of jet lag

I’m not adjusting well
to the jet lag right now,
and I’ve been waking up a lot at night.

And here they had
melatonin, which is
like a sleeping pill.

Melatonin shelf — CVS 10 mg 60 tablets $10.99, Natrol 10 mg 100 tablets $20.59

Nature's Bounty melatonin 5 mg twin pack

Even bottles labeled 'Sleep-Aid' have different ingredients — this one is diphenhydramine

The one I picked is
CVS melatonin 5 mg (120 tablets).

I need to get over the jet lag fast,
so I bought it to take that night
and try to sleep soundly.

The CVS melatonin 5 mg (120 tablets) I picked

⚠ Check first before bringing it to Korea
Melatonin is sold like a supplement in the US,
but in Korea it is classified as an item requiring import-requirement checks,
so it may be restricted even within the duty-free allowance
(Yonhap News fact check, June 3, 2026)
Ask the Korea Customs Service call center at 125 first

🧲 One magnesium, too

Along with the melatonin,
I bought one magnesium as well.
It’s magnesium glycinate.

The CVS melatonin 5 mg and magnesium glycinate (60 capsules) left at the hotel

🧴 A giant ‘only-in-America-size’ Vaseline

And here I spotted
an enormously big tub of Vaseline.

My older son keeps getting
chapped lips,
so I bought this only-in-America Vaseline
to slather generously on his lips.

Vaseline 368 g next to my palm — magnesium on the right

💵 Korean prices, on the other hand…

It didn’t make me feel
that it was pricier than Korea
as much as I expected.

So I actually had
the opposite thought:
prices in Korea have gone up a lot.
