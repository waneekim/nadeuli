Where to Get Coffee in Magok: Hausly (haüsly) — a Nutty 3,800-Won Americano, Hand-Drip Beans, Financiers and FacePay

Lunchtime coffee in Magok ☕

One Wednesday lunchtime,
we stopped by Hausly (하우슬리)
on Magokdong-ro 3-gil.

The Americano was nutty and delicious.

Iced Americano — nutty and delicious

📍 26 Magokdong-ro 3-gil, Gangseo-gu
Unit 111, 1F
(about 330 m from Magok Station exit 3)
🚇 Magok Station is on Line 5
(2 stops from Gimpo Airport)
⏰ Mon–Thu 08:00–18:00
Closed Sundays
(Fri & Sat: check Naver Place)
💰 Americano 3,800 won
Financiers from 3,000 won
🅿 Parking available
🐾 Pets welcome
📞 0507-1424-5681

🏠 A sky-blue hand-drip café

A small café
with a ‘Coffee & Hand Drip’ sign
on a sky-blue wall.

Out front there’s
a wooden deck and a bench,
and the windows open outward.

Hausly — the 'Coffee & Hand Drip' sign on the sky-blue wall

🥤 The nutty Americano

The Americano is 3,800 won.

The iced one comes
in a kraft paper cup with a clear lid,
stamped ‘haüsly — Specialty Coffee Haus’.

A small round table by the bench out front

A hot Americano, too!
It came in a paper cup
with a white sleeve.

Hot Americano — a Hausly stamp on the white sleeve

💳 A FacePay partner store

Hausly is
a FacePay (페이스페이) partner store.

To the right of the counter
is a Toss payment terminal,
and on top of the display case
stands a ‘Hausly QR’ sign.

📜 The coffee & hand-drip menu

The menu board at the counter
is split into coffee, hand drip,
non-coffee and tea.

The Special Latte (6,500 won)
is brewed with one single-origin bean
you pick — the board calls it
a latte that isn’t sweet.

Switching milk is free —
choose regular, oat or soy.

The counter menu board — coffee, hand drip, non-coffee, tea

There are nine hand-drip beans,
5,000–9,500 won.

Two of them —
Brazil Cerrado Chocolate Infused
and Brazil Santa Lucia —
carry a ‘nutty’ tag.

If you’d like to buy beans or drip bags,
the board says to ask the barista.

🧁 Six kinds of financier

In the glass case
in front of the counter
sit the financiers.

Salty choco, white choco, fig,
hazelnut, Maldon salt and plain!

The financier case — salty choco, white choco, fig, hazelnut, Maldon salt, plain

A note on the case says
the white choco and salty choco ones
are kept in the fridge,
so just ask if you want one.

The storage guide:
2 days at room temperature,
5 days in the fridge, 2 weeks frozen
(chocolate ones best refrigerated).

Along the top of the case
stands a row of Tom and Jerry figures.

🔑 KICKIf you like nutty coffee
try the two Brazilian beans
with the 'nutty' tag!
(hand drip from 5,000 won)
