Paju Gaeseong Ginseng Festival 2026 — Schedule, Timetable & Getting There Before You Take the Kids to Imjingak

Autumn sunshine falls on Imjingak —
it’s the third weekend of October 🍂

The 21st edition this year!
The 21st Paju Gaeseong Insam (Ginseng) Festival
(제21회 파주개성인삼축제)
runs for two days, Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, Oct 18,
at Imjingak Plaza
and around Pyeonghwa Nuri (평화누리).

We put this together from
Paju City’s press releases (September 30 & October 7)
and the 2026 timetable on
Paju City’s culture & tourism festival page.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It’s a farm-produce festival
starring Paju’s signature crops,
Jangdan Sambaek (장단삼백 — rice, soybeans & ginseng),
and 6-year-old Paju Gaeseong ginseng.

This year’s theme is ‘Run Together, Enjoy Together —
the Paju Gaeseong Ginseng Festival Runs Again’.

Only 6-year-old ginseng is picked for the festival,
and the city manages it all the way —
safety tests before harvest,
then digging, sorting and sealing
with city officials present.

The city says that every year during the festival
it sells 10 tons of ginseng and farm produce
direct from the farms
at 15–20% below market prices.

and Sunday has the ginseng auction

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

So families with strollers can get around easily,
there’s a little train that loops the festival grounds,
the Touring Car.
It circles the main spots at a slow pace.

With kids, put these on your list:
the free ginseng bibimbap tasting,
the herbal village with its herb-trimming demos,
and the evening Moonlight Theater.

The Moonlight Theater starts at 17:30
and runs until 19:30 — after sunset.
Pack an extra layer for the kids.

🔑 KICKThe free bibimbap tasting is only
30 min from 15:00 on Sat the 17th
and the Moonlight Theater at 17:30 that day —
it's all packed into Saturday afternoon
