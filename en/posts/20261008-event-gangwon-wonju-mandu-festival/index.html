Wonju Mandu Festival 2026 — Dates, Stage Timetable & Parking Before You Take the Kids to the Nakhwa-nori Fire Show on Wonjucheon Stream

It’s the season when
steaming-hot mandu
(Korean dumplings) feel just right 🥟

The 2026 Wonju Mandu Festival
(2026 원주만두축제)
runs for three days,
Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun),
around Wonjucheon Stream (Dawn Market)
and Culture Street in Wonju (원주), Gangwon,
through to the Jungang-dong traditional markets.

We put this together from
the official website’s timetable and venue map
and press releases from Wonju City
and the Wonju Cultural Foundation.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

The slogan is
’시장에서 한 판, 즐거울 만두!’ —
roughly ‘A round at the market,
no wonder it’s fun!’, with a pun on mandu.

This year the festival grounds,
which used to center on the roads,
now center on Wonjucheon Stream.
They stretch from the stream to Culture Street
and on to the Jungang-dong traditional markets.

The ‘Wonju Mandu Zone’ (원주만두존),
the main food area,
is made up of 15 vendors picked by judges
from 32 teams at a tasting contest in September.
To cut down on long lines,
they’re also bringing in
QR real-time ordering & booking
and kiosks, the city says.

There’s also a Mandu Road leading into the markets,
receipt rewards, a stamp tour,
a traditional-market ‘extra helping’ festa (덤 페스타)
and a Global Mandu Zone.

✨ What’s on, and when?

There are two stages: Wonjucheon Stream
and Culture Street.

The Youth Market opens on
Thursday the 22nd, the day before the festival.
The 22nd is market day at the
Folk Pungmul Market (민속풍물시장),
so that day it’s only in the plaza
inside the Culture Sharing Platform.

This year’s menus and prices
haven’t been announced yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The mandu contests are at 15:00 on the 24th & 25th,
and the cooking classes at 17:00 on the 23rd & 24th.
If you go on Saturday or Sunday afternoon,
you can catch contest → stage shows → evening concert
one after another.

Pets are welcome too.
A leash or a carrier is a must.

The venue map shows restrooms
in the Doremi Market, Jayu Market
and Jungang Market buildings
and by Wonjucheon Stream.

The Daegu Tteokbokki Festival
runs during the same days.
Visit both and show proof,
and you get festival goods, they say.

🔑 KICKThe nakhwa-nori fire show is only
on opening day, Fri the 23rd
It's part of the 18:00–21:00 concert
and the exact time isn't out yet

👍 Recommended for these families

✔ Kids who want to try folding mandu
✔ Families who like browsing markets & eating
✔ Families who want to stay for the evening fire show & concerts
