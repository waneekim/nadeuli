Jeongdong Culture Night 2026 in Seoul — Schedule, Pre-Registration & Craft Zone for a Night Walk Along Deoksugung’s Stone-Wall Road with Kids

On autumn nights,
along Deoksugung’s stone-wall road (덕수궁 돌담길),
Jeongdong’s historic buildings
open their doors after dark 🌙

Jeongdong Culture Night 2026
(정동야행 Jeongdong Yahaeng — ‘night trip’)
runs for two days, Fri Oct 30 & Sat Oct 31,
in the Jeongdong area around Deoksugung Palace
(Deoksugung-gil and Jeongdong-gil).

Programs that need pre-registration,
like guided tours and embassy tours,
take sign-ups until Fri Oct 16, 18:00 (Korea time)!

We put this together from
the official festival website of Seoul’s Jung-gu district
and Seoul city’s Fun Seoul (펀서울) listing,
as of October 7.

First, the key facts at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Jeongdong Culture Night is a festival
that Seoul’s Jung-gu district started in 2015.
News articles call it ‘Korea’s first
night festival at cultural heritage sites’.

Last year it was held in May,
and about 133,000 people came over two days.
This year it moves to autumn nights in October.

This year’s theme is
’Jeongdong, In a New Light’.

The program has seven strands,
each named with ‘ya (夜)’, meaning night:
night openings of venues (Yahwa),
guided tours (Yaro), hands-on activities (Yasa),
plus performances, night views,
a Moonlight Market and Jeongdong Yacham (night snacks).

If a program is oversubscribed, you may miss out,
and they won’t contact you separately if you do.

Programs include tours of the British and Canadian embassies,
a guided tour inside Ewha Girls’ High School,
an XR experience at the Appenzeller Noble Memorial Museum
(배재학당역사박물관, the Paichai Hakdang history museum),
and a Paichai Hakdang tour + planter making.

🚇 Getting there & parking

Official directions aren't out yet

The ‘Location’ menu on the official website
wasn’t open yet as of October 7.

The festival site map shows
City Hall Station (Lines 1 & 2)
and Seodaemun Station (Line 5),
plus an arrow pointing the way to Gwanghwamun Station (Line 5).

The main information booth is on Deoksugung-gil,
in front of the Seoul City Hall Seosomun Building.

There’s no parking or road-closure notice for this year yet.
For each festival from 2023 to 2025,
a separate ‘traffic control’ notice was posted.
If you’re driving, be sure to check the notice just before the festival.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

On Saturday the craft zone opens at 14:00,
and the Royal Guard ceremony and the brass band
are around the same time.
Crafts in the afternoon, then ‘Jeongdong Banquet’
after dark makes a good plan.

👍 Who we’d recommend it for

✔ Families looking for free craft activities
✔ Families who want to see the Royal Guard ceremony and an outdoor folk show
✔ Families curious to look inside embassies and schools

Stone-wall crafts by day, a media facade show by night —
Jeongdong’s autumn night, for just two days.

Pre-registration closes Oct 16, 18:00!
