Magok Café Near Balsan Station: Connects Coffee — Nutty Coffee at Lunch, 1,000 Won Off for Takeout and Order-Ahead Pickup

A lunchtime coffee in Magok ☕

One Monday lunchtime
I stopped by
Connects Coffee Magok (커넥츠커피 마곡점)
on Magokdong-ro.

The coffee was nutty and delicious.

The iced coffee I got at lunch — in a Connects Coffee sleeve

📍 61 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul
1F Unit 110
(about 460 m from Balsan Station
exit 9, Line 5)
⏰ Mon 08:00–18:00
Tue–Thu 08:00–20:00
Fri 08:00–21:00
Sat & Sun 11:00–21:00
Public holidays: posted separately
(last order 30 min before closing)
💰 Americano 4,500 won
1,000 won off for takeout
🅿 Parking: first 30 min free
📞 070-7807-1215

When I visited,
its Naver Place page
had a ‘Newly opened’ tag.

🧾 The counter and the menu boards

At the counter
there’s an ordering terminal showing ‘Order here’
and vibrating pagers.

In front of it, two menu boards!
On the left: coffee of the day, espresso,
cold brew and other drinks;
on the right: hand-brewed coffee
and bakery.

The counter — two menu boards, three bean cards, the ordering terminal and pagers

On the menu, the description
of the Coffee of the Day Classic
calls it ‘a nutty type’.

🫘 Pick from three beans

For espresso drinks,
you can pick from three beans.

The three bean cards under the menu:
Valentine Blend Plus,
Fruity Blend ‘Peach Cream’
and Decaf Blend ‘Weekend’.

The Valentine Blend card says
it mixes Indonesia, Guatemala and Costa Rica,
and describes the Guatemala
as having a ‘toasty roasted-nut’ tone.

🍰 The dessert case

Dessert case — cookies, egg tarts, pudding, Basque cheesecake, brownies and two kinds of pound cake

Cookies, egg tarts, pudding,
Basque cheesecake, brownies,
and even two kinds of pound cake.

Under the bakery section of the menu,
it says that once you pick a dessert,
the barista will recommend a coffee
to go with it.

🥤 1,000 won off for takeout

On the glass entrance door
there’s a ‘TAKE OUT BENEFIT’
poster.

Coffee drinks (except the signature)
are 1,000 won off for takeout!

From 08:00 to 10:00,
americano, café latte,
coffee of the day and cold brew
get another 500 won off.
(takeout only)

The takeout discount poster at the entrance, with opening hours by day

The top of the menu board says the same thing.

An americano (R) at 4,500 won
is 3,500 won to take out.

📱 Order ahead, pick up right away

On the glass next to it,
an ‘Order ahead, pick up right away’ poster!

✔ Toss — search ‘Toss Order’ (토스오더) in KakaoTalk
→ pre-order → choose Connects Coffee
✔ Naver — search ‘커넥츠커피 마곡’
→ Menu → Order

If you’re only grabbing takeout at lunch,
it’s worth remembering.

Next to the counter
there was also a sign for
’Gangseo Local Brand: Magok Misul-gil’ (Magok Art Street).

Spend 20,000 won or more in the area
and get 7,000 won off as a billing discount
(from September 1, 2026, until the budget runs out).
Check the QR code on the sign
for which payment methods qualify.

👍 Who we’d recommend it to

✔ Anyone in Magok who wants
a nutty coffee after lunch
✔ Anyone who often gets takeout
(1,000 won off coffee)
✔ Anyone who hates wasting lunch in line
(pre-order on Toss or Naver)
