Hanseong Baekje Culture Festival 2026 in Seoul — Schedule, Lineup, Drone Light Show & Parking at Olympic Park

On the lawns of Olympic Park,
an autumn festival runs for three days 🍂

The 26th Hanseong Baekje Culture Festival
(한성백제문화제 — Hanseong was the early capital
of the ancient Baekje kingdom)
runs Fri Oct 23 – Sun Oct 25
around the 88 Lawn (88잔디마당) in Olympic Park.

We put this together from
the event guide, full schedule and site map
on the Songpa-gu festival website,
as of October 7.

Shall we start with a quick overview?

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

First held in 1994,
it’s Songpa-gu’s history and culture festival.
Songpa-gu hosts it,
and the Hanseong Baekje Culture Festival Committee
organizes it.

Since last year’s 25th edition,
it has moved to
the 88 Lawn area of Olympic Park.

By day, the hands-on zone and the market;
in the evening, shows on the main stage.
On the first night there’s even
a drone light show!

✨ What’s on all three days?

The hands-on zone and the market are open all three days.
Here are their hours in a table.

Date & timeProgram
Daily 10:00–19:00Hanseong Baekje Hands-on Zone — a family activity zone with a kids’ café, all sorts of hands-on activities, photo zones and places to rest
Daily 11:00–21:00Hanseong Baekje Market — a food market run by resident groups from 12 neighborhoods (dong) and 5 trade associations

Dishes listed on the market menu include
dubu-kimchi (tofu with stir-fried kimchi) ·
hongeo-muchim (spicy skate salad) ·
janchi-guksu (warm noodle soup) ·
modum-jeon (assorted Korean pancakes) ·
gukbap (rice in hot soup).

The site map shows
activity booths (nos. 01–25),
the Hanseong Baekje kids’ café, photo zones,
a shaded rest area, food trucks
and promotional booths.

🎤 When is each stage show?

The official running orders
list the main-stage shows like this.

Date & timeStage
Oct 23 (Fri) 19:00–20:50Opening performance — 19:00 drone welcome ceremony, 19:25 orchestra & classical collaboration, 19:55 live band
Oct 23 (Fri) 20:10Celebration sets — Loco · Davichi
Oct 23 (Fri) 20:40Drone light show
Oct 24 (Sat) 16:50–20:10Hanmaeum Eoulmadang — a showcase by students of community-center classes from 26 neighborhoods (dong): nanta drumming · dance · guitar · pungmul folk percussion and more; 19:30 celebration performance, 19:50 awards ceremony
Oct 25 (Sun) 19:00–20:40Hanseong Culture Concert (closing performance) — daebuk big-drum percussion, geomungo (Korean zither) with a band, pansori, and fusion gugak by Lee Bong-geun
Oct 25 (Sun) 20:40Celebration sets — Kep1er · Dynamic Duo

The full schedule says it ends at 20:40,
but the running order
puts the celebration sets at 20:40,
so it may finish a little later.

There are daytime special performances too.

Date & timeDaytime special performance
Oct 23 (Fri) · Oct 25 (Sun) 17:30‘Songpa Sandae-noli’ mask dance drama — Songpa Folk Preservation Society
Oct 24 (Sat) 11:00–11:30Musical gala ‘Standing by the River’ (강가에 서서) — Songpa Cultural Foundation

For big events at Olympic Park,
public transit is recommended

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The hands-on zone closes at 19:00,
and on Friday and Sunday
the main stage starts at 19:00.
Activities by day → dinner at the market → the show
is an easy order to follow.

Last year’s hands-on zone had
rock climbing · a rice-straw slide
an obstacle playground · traditional games
and Baekje costume try-ons, all free.
This year’s page still shows last year’s list.

The information center
(lost children & lost and found)
is next to the main gate.
Agree on it as your meeting spot with the kids.
The first-aid station is on the activity-booth side.

🔑 KICKIn the official running orders, drones appear
