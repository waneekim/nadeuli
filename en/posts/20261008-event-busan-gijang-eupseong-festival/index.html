2026 Gijang Eupseong Festival Schedule, Timetable, Parking — Before Visiting Gijang Modern History Museum with Kids

A brand new autumn festival debuts 🍂
by the 600-year-old Gijang Eupseong fortress.

2026 Gijang Eupseong Festival
will be held for three days, October 9 (Fri) to 11 (Sun),
on the lawn in front of the Gijang Modern History Museum
and around the Gijang Government Office (Janggwancheong).

This festival continues the tradition of the previous Chaseong Cultural Festival,
but now takes place at Gijang Eupseong.

Here’s a summary of the daily schedule
from the official Gijang-gun press release (October 6)
and the county blog.

Want a quick overview?

🎪 What Kind of Event Is It?

This year’s theme is ‘Let’s Play at the Fortress’.
You can walk, sit, read, and relax throughout the fortress,
with the historic space set up for everyone to enjoy.

Instead of the usual music and trot performances
of past festivals,
there are more programs for reading, hands-on activities, talks, movies, and music
that you can join and linger at.

Gijang Eupseong is a cultural heritage site built in 1425,
during the 7th year of King Sejong’s reign,
so it’s about 600 years old.
The county also aims to connect the festival
with Gijang Market and nearby businesses.

✨ What’s Happening Each Day?

Here’s the schedule for the lawn stage
in front of the Gijang Modern History Museum.
The first day, the 9th (Fri),
kicks off with ‘Serious Spacing Out’.

the Chwita Band, and the Challenge Dance.

and BIFF on the 11th running until 21:00.

🎨 When Are the Museum Activities?

At Gijang Modern History Museum,
activities run at the same times all three days,
but what you make changes each day.

TimeMuseum Activities
13:00 · 14:00 · 15:00Pop-up Studio — 9th: Bookmarks, 10th: Ocean Keyrings, 11th: Bead Plants
13:00 · 15:00 · 17:00Gijang Eupseong Walk Docent Tour
17:00~18:00Slow Down & Relax — 9th: Aroma Singing Bowl, 10th: Mini Garden, 11th: A Cup of Tea

Ongoing experiences available all three days include
Gijang Amulet Lab, Pet Rock Adoption Center,
Gijang Eupseong Goods Shop, Anything Rental,
and Gijang Eupseong Gentlemen’s Style.

The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism’s festival guide
lists the fee as free.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

If you’re visiting with children,
pay special attention to the Saturday the 10th afternoon
stage events: 13:00 Traditional Performance,
14:00 Chwita Band, and 15:00 Challenge Dance.

The pop-up studio offers different crafts each day,
so you can pick the day your child wants to make
a bookmark, keyring, or bead plant.

The county tourism site’s festival page
has a separate ‘Participation Reservation’ button.

🔑 KICKPrograms start daily at 13:00
The pop-up studio runs only at 13:00, 14:00, and 15:00
so head to the museum on time
Evening movies are at 18:30 on the 9th, 18:00 on the 11th
