2026 Tamna Cultural Festival Dates, Night Theater & Road Closures – Before Visiting Jeju’s Citizen Welfare Town Plaza with Kids

Jeju’s signature autumn culture festival
moves to a new main stage this year 🎭

The 65th Tamna Cultural Festival (탐라문화제)
runs for five days,
October 17 (Sat) to 21 (Wed),
around Citizen Welfare Town Plaza in Jeju-si.

It was planned so visitors can enjoy it
together with the National Sports Festival
held in Jeju over the same period (16th–22nd).

I checked the official festival website
and the Jeju provincial press release (October 2)
again on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

It began in 1962 as the ‘Jeju Arts Festival’,
became the Halla Cultural Festival,
and has been called the Tamna Cultural Festival
since 2002.

This year’s theme is
’The breath of Tamna, the passion of the Games,
one Jeju’.

This year the main stage moved
to Citizen Welfare Town Plaza,
close to Jeju Sports Complex,
where National Sports Festival events are held.

Across four zones and 16 programs
(Ppuri, Nori, Eoul and Kkumbit),
there are performances, exhibitions, hands-on activities and markets.

Here is the stage schedule by date
from the ‘Main Programs’ page of the official website.

The Night Theater’s theme show is the original musical dance drama
’Daughter of the Wind, Becoming an Island’ (바람의 딸, 섬이 되다).
It retells Jeju’s myths and history
as a stage performance.
Hanchungeun Band joins on the 18th,
and a master dancers’ showcase (명무전) on the 19th and 20th.

The opening ceremony features pansori master Seo Myeong-hui,
and the closing ceremony has a celebration show
with singer An Ye-eun and a children’s choir.

From the 17th to the 21st, every day
13:00–22:00 on the plaza.

Tamna Intangible Heritage FestivalCraft exhibits & demonstrations by heritage holders

Tamna Mendorong MarketJeju local food & snack market

Due to on-site conditions, the Mendorong Market
will run at a minimal scale,
centered on a local food showcase (model displays & some tastings)
and a snack bar.

The Open Mic runs from the 19th to the 21st,
13:00–16:30,
an open stage where any resident or visitor
can perform.

🚌 Getting There & Road Closures

Roads close on the afternoon of the 17th

The venue is around Jeju Citizen Welfare Town Plaza
(286 Yeonsam-ro, Jeju-si).
It is close to Jeju Sports Complex,
where National Sports Festival events are held.

Because of the parade on the 17th,
there are road closures and bus route changes.

TypeDetails
Road closure 15:00–19:00Aehyang Stadium → Jonghap Gymnasium Intersection → Sports Complex Intersection → Health Center Intersection → Citizen Welfare Town Plaza
Temporary city bus changes 17:00–19:305 stops closed (Gateball Court (West), Jeju Health Center back gate, Government Complex), temporary stops in use

Bus numbers for each closed stop
are shown as an image in the website notice
(October 5).

There is no official information yet
on festival parking or shuttles.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

At the Tamna Sports Arena,
folk games handed down in Jeju
are set up as activities and matches
that anyone can easily try.

Tamna Yuram is a participatory program
where you become an explorer
and solve missions around the festival grounds.

On the evening of the 18th at the Kkumbit Stage,
the Student Culture Festival (18:00–19:00)
is followed by the youth contest ‘Kkumbit Rising Star’,
which runs until 22:00.

The Night Theater ends at 21:30.
If you plan to stay for the evening show with young kids,
decide how you’ll get home in advance.

🔑 KICKOn the 17th (Sat), 15:00–19:00,
roads on the parade route are closed
From 17:00–19:30 that day,
buses stop at temporary stops
