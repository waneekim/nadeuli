Dongjak Ppangdodong Bread Festival 2026 in Noryangjin, Seoul — Date & Parking, 22 Local Bakeries and a Bread Barbershop Sing-Along

On the last day of October,
a bread festival comes to Noryangjin 🍞

The 2026 Dongjak Ppangdodong Festival
(2026 동작 빵도동 축제)
runs Sat Oct 31, 12:00–17:00
at Noryangjin Football Field.
(The official notice marks the hours ‘tentative’,
and the poster only gives the start, ‘12:00’)

We put this together from
the festival announcement
the organizer, Dongjak Cultural Foundation,
posted on September 7,
and its bread booth notices.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What is Ppangdodong?

The Sangdo-dong area (상도동),
packed with bakeries,
is nicknamed ‘Ppangdodong’
(빵도동, also spelled Bbangdodong) —
‘ppang’ (빵) means bread.
This is Dongjak-gu’s bread festival,
named after that nickname.

This year’s slogan is
”A Sweet Day, a Bread-Packed Dongjak!”

The foundation describes it
as a cultural event that combines
bread sales, performances
and hands-on activities.
An April news article says
the festival was planned last year
on a proposal from local young people.

You don’t have to live in Dongjak-gu —
anyone in Seoul
can join on site for free.

🥐 How many bakeries are coming?

22 Dongjak-gu bakeries.

Only shops located in Dongjak-gu
that bake and sell their own bread
could apply.

The foundation called for 20 shops;
21 applied and all were selected,
and ‘Dongjak Ppang’ (동작빵),
the grand prize winner
of the ‘Ppang Ppang Contest’,
was added on top.
(The ‘21 shops’ in the announcement text
is the number picked through the call)

Bread booths are open 12:00–17:00 throughout,
and each shop sells up to 10 kinds
of breads and pastries,
including its signature item.

The foundation says it will publish
the list of bakeries later.

✨ What shows and activities are there?

On stage
Bread Barbershop Sing-Along Concert
& photo time
(Bread Barbershop, 브레드이발소,
is a Korean kids’ cartoon)
Busking by Dongjak-gu young artists
’Tune Around’ (튠어라운드)
and ‘Artist Comma’ (예술가 콤마)
Brass band ‘Boom Beat’ (붐비트)

Activities (all day)
Baking · keyring making
Bread Mini Sports Day · Ppangdodong Playground
Make Your Own AI Character
(with Chung-Ang University’s
Anchor Project team)
Reading · art programs

Ppangdodong Lounge
Coffee & drink food trucks
Bread flea market · Ppangdodong events

Start times for each show
haven’t been announced yet.
The foundation notes that
performers and programs may change
depending on how things run.

🚇 Getting there & parking

It's right outside
Noryangjin Station exit 9

Subway Noryangjin Station (Lines 1 & 9) —
come out of exit 9
and the sports grounds are right there.
(per Seoul city’s Media Hub)

Parking The football and baseball field
car parks hold 65 cars combined.
(per Seoul city’s 2021 description)
Parking arrangements on festival day
aren’t in the official notice.

Questions: Dongjak Cultural Foundation
070-7204-3250, 3251

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The Bread Barbershop concert
comes with photo time.
Once the show time is out,
it’s worth timing your visit to it.

Activities run all day,
so it’s easy to fit in baking,
keyring making or the Bread Mini Sports Day
before or after the show.

The football field has artificial turf
(per Seoul city’s 2021 description),
so shoes kids can run around in are a good idea.

If you plan to buy from several bakeries,
bring a shopping bag or a big tote.
It’s an outdoor event,
so an extra layer for the kids
for the afternoon is handy too.

🔑 KICKEvery bread booth is required
to have a card payment terminal
Bread booths don't sell coffee or drinks —
get those at the lounge food trucks

a district office notice.
Dongjak-gu’s football field schedule (October 6)
also lists Sunday, November 1
as ‘Ppangdodong Festival’ (빵도동축제),
and the August schedule
had it down for October 28–31.

That table lists the days
the field is given over to events,
so it may differ from the festival dates.
The foundation’s announcement and Fun Seoul
both give the festival date as
Saturday, October 31 only.

When any of this shows up
in the official notices,
we’ll update this post.

👍 Recommended for these families

✔ Families who want to pick from
local bakeries all in one place
✔ Families with kids
who love Bread Barbershop
✔ Families looking for baking

Bread shopping, a show and activities —
easy to wrap into
one Saturday afternoon.
