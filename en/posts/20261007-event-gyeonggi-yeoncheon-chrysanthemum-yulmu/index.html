Yeoncheon Chrysanthemum Festival & Yulmu Festival 2026 — Dates, Admission & Shuttle Before You Take the Kids to the Jeongok-ri Prehistoric Site

The entrance to the Jeongok-ri site
is turning every color of chrysanthemum 🌼

The 8th Yeoncheon Chrysanthemum Festival
(제8회 연천국화축제)
runs for 10 days, Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, Oct 25,
at the Jeongok-ri Prehistoric Site in Yeoncheon
(연천 전곡리 유적).

On the last three days, Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun),
the 5th Yeoncheon Yulmu Festival
(제5회 연천율무축제) is held at the same place too.
(Yulmu is Job’s tears, a grain.)

We put this together from
Yeoncheon County’s culture & tourism website,
the Korea Tourism Organization’s festival info
(updated October 6)
and the official poster.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

This year’s Chrysanthemum Festival theme is
’The Breath of Goguryeo,
Blooming as Chrysanthemums’.

At the site entrance, across 50,000 m²,
40,000 potted chrysanthemums
and chrysanthemum sculptures
are set to make a panoramic landscape,
according to the announcement.

The Yulmu Festival is
a market for Yeoncheon yulmu and farm produce.
Alongside direct farm sales
there are farming-culture activities,
exhibitions, performances and food.
The county website says it’s held
’every year on the Friday, Saturday and Sunday
of the fourth week of October’.

✨ What’s on, and when?

The Chrysanthemum Festival runs all ten days;
the Yulmu Festival, only the last three.

Date & timeProgram
10/16 (Fri) – 25 (Sun)Yeoncheon Chrysanthemum Festival — chrysanthemum displays, photo zones, activity zone
Daily 09:00–18:00Chrysanthemum Festival hours (Gyeonggi Tourism Organization festival info)

A day-by-day stage timetable
hasn’t been released yet.

💰 How much is admission?

The Chrysanthemum Festival is paid;
entry to the Yulmu Festival is free

Chrysanthemum FestivalAdmission
Individual (general)5,000 won
Group (general)4,000 won
Individual (discounted)3,000 won
Group (discounted)2,000 won

Paying visitors get the amount they paid
back in Yeoncheon Love gift certificates
(the poster: ‘full amount given
in Yeoncheon Love gift certificates’)

Who qualifies for the discount
and where the certificates can be used
aren’t in the official info yet.

🚇 Getting there & parking

By subway, take the shuttle from Jeongok Station!

Here’s the transport info
from the official poster.

HowWhere to get off / how to get there

Car1510 Yangyeon-ro, Jeongok-eup, Yeoncheon-gun (Jeongok-ri Prehistoric Site)

The shuttle bus timetable
and pickup point
haven’t been released yet.

Yeoncheon County’s tourist site info
says the Jeongok-ri site has a parking lot.
There’s no parking info
for the festival period yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

The Chrysanthemum Festival activities
include a mini train
and potting a plant,
and the Yulmu Festival has
yulmu face painting.

The Jeongok-ri site is where
Paleolithic people lived
about 300,000 years ago.
The first hand axe in East Asia
was found here,
and along a walking trail of about 2 km
there are exhibition halls and sculptures.

The county’s tourist site info
marks a nursing room,
restrooms and accessible restrooms.

The festival description lists
a ‘dog playground’,
but the site’s regular info says
dogs are ‘not allowed’.
If you plan to bring yours, ask first.

🔑 KICKTo see both festivals in one trip
go on Oct 23 (Fri) – 25 (Sun)
The chrysanthemum show charges admission
but entry to the yulmu market is free
