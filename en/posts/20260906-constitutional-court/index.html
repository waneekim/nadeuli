Constitutional Court of Korea Tour with Kids (Seoul) — Sitting in the Grand Courtroom We’d Only Seen on the News

We took our kids
on a tour of the Constitutional Court ⚖️

To be honest,
it was my first time inside, too.

The Constitutional Court under a blue sky — the moment we arrived, one of our kids ran on ahead

📍 15 Bukchon-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul
(100 m from exit 2 of
Anguk Station, Line 3)
🗓 Weekdays 10:00 · 14:00 · 16:00
Saturdays: 2 days a month
at 11:00 · 15:00
⏱ Courses: 30 / 60 / 90 min
✍ Apply in advance on the website
Individuals & families can apply
from 9:00 on the first Monday
of the month before
📞 02-708-3416 (school tours)

📺 A place we’d only seen on the news

Over the past few years,
amid the impeachment cases,
we saw it on the news countless times.

Walking into the place
where those decisions are made,
seeing it with our own eyes
and learning about it with our kids —
that alone meant a lot.

By the main gate — the main building beyond the pine trees

The lobby — the space itself carries a different weight

📜 The constitution in the lobby

High up in the lobby,
a line from the constitution
is carved in large letters.

All citizens shall be assured of human worth and dignity and have the right to pursue happiness.

A sentence well worth
reading aloud to your kids,
right in the middle of the building.

The constitutional article high up in the lobby

⚖️ The highlight: the Grand Courtroom

Grand Courtroom, Room 123 — the very room from the news

The very room
we’d only seen on TV
during the impeachment trials!

We sat right in the gallery
and looked around
while listening to the guide.

From the gallery of the Grand Courtroom — the justices' bench and the Korean flag right in front of us

In the hallway,
photos of the court’s past presidents
and justices
fill an entire wall.

The wall of past presidents and justices of the Constitutional Court

🔑 KICKApply for the tour on the website first!
Individuals & families can apply

of the month before the tour

🗺 Tour courses (per the Constitutional Court)

Course 1 · 30 min
Grand Courtroom, photos in judges’ robes, the back garden

Course 2 · 60 min
A video, the central lobby, Grand Courtroom,
the back garden & Baeksong (white pine) Sky Park,
the library and the exhibition hall

Course 3 · 90 min
Course 2 + a lecture and a talk
with a constitutional research officer

Saturday tours follow Course 2
and are for elementary to high school students
and their families.

Once you’re approved,
you’ll get a text message.
Tours may be cancelled
because of the court’s trial schedule.
