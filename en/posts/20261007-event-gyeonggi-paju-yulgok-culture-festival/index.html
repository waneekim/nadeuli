Yulgok Culture Festival 2026 in Paju — Schedule, Crafts & Getting There Before You Take the Kids to the Yi I Historic Site

It’s the last weekend of October,
with autumn deepening in Paju 🍂

The 36th one this year!
The 36th Yulgok Culture Festival
(제36회 율곡문화제)
runs October 24 (Sat) – 25 (Sun),
two days at the Yi I Historic Site in Paju
(파주이이유적).

We put this together from
the Paju Cultural Foundation’s press release (September 23),
the festival’s official website
and Paju city’s culture & tourism listing.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of event is it?

Paju’s autumn culture festival
honoring the Joseon scholar
Yulgok Yi I (율곡 이이).

This year’s theme is
’Gyeokmong (擊蒙): Awaken and Begin Again’.
It comes from Yulgok’s best-known book,
Gyeokmong Yogyeol (격몽요결),
and carries the idea of awakening yourself
and opening a new path.

Entry to the festival grounds is free.
Some activities, though,
may charge a fee, the notice says.

⏰ What are the hours?

The opening hours differ
from one official notice to another —
even two spots on the same website
list them differently.

Where it’s listedHours
Official website banner ①11:00–18:00
Official website banner ②11:00–17:00
Paju city’s ‘This Month’s Cultural Events’10:00–18:00

By the program schedule, on day one
the writing & art contests start at 10:00.

Whether it ends at 17:00 or 18:00,
a quick call to the Paju Cultural Foundation
(031-950-8424, 8426) before you go will settle it.

✨ What’s on, and when?

Day one centers on the memorial rite and the opening stage,
day two on family shows.
We’ve only put in the table what has a set time.

Date & timeProgram
Oct 24 (Sat) 10:00–14:00Yulgok Writing Contest & National Yulgok Art Contest — in front of the Yulgok statue
Oct 24 (Sat) 11:00–12:00Yulgok Yi I Memorial Rite (추향제) — the rite that opens the festival
Oct 24 (Sat) 13:00Yulgok Cup Baduk (Go) Tournament — main hall, Paju Civic Center
Oct 24 (Sat) 15:00–15:10Opening — Paju City Boys & Girls Choir

Oct 25 (Sun) 11:00–12:00Shin Saimdang Memorial Rite — honoring Yulgok’s mother

Oct 25 (Sun) 12:40–13:10Traditional magic show
Oct 25 (Sun) 13:20–13:50Nongak concert ‘Young-in-jeon’ (영YOUNG인전) — plate spinning (beona) & Korean diabolo (jukbangul)
Oct 25 (Sun)Yulgok Reading Day — humanities talks by Shim Yong-hwan and Song Gil-young
Oct 17 (Sat), 23 (Fri), 24 (Sat)Yuga Procession — Unjeong Lake Park, Geumneung Station, Yi I Historic Site

The mask play is a stage show
about grandma and grandpa
getting a family photo taken.

The Yuga Procession celebrates
Yulgok placing first in the state exams nine times,
and it’s held three times.
The place and time for each date
haven’t been announced separately yet.

The foundation’s press release
introduces the Baduk tournament
together with the programs on the 25th.
Our table follows the date on the website’s program page.

✂️ What activities are there?

All the regular activities are walk-in (sign up on site).
We’ve sorted the 11 by who they’re for.

ForActivities
EveryoneDMZ mini terrarium · sea-glass phone grip · comb & mirror decorating, keycaps · face painting · shrink-plastic lucky charms · resin mother-of-pearl norigae (hanbok pendant) · hanbok try-on · seonbi traditional tea ceremony
Elementary school age and upJoseon-era ID tag (hopae) making (wood burning) · decorating rubber shoes (gomusin) with hanji paper
Whole family togetherFamily garden making — building a garden through gardening and drama play
Yulgok programs (join on site)Guyong Training Ground (play activities) · Yulgok Tamtam (historic-site tour)

Feeling peckish? There’s a chestnut dessert booth 🌰
Rice-and-chestnut walnut cakes, chestnut pound cake
and handmade chestnut gelato are on the list.

🚌 Getting there and parking

They say the shuttle will be announced before the festival

The official website gives a route
from Munsan Station on the Gyeongui–Jungang Line
that takes two buses.

BusBoard → Get off
No. 11Across from Munsan Station exit 1 → Beobwon Intersection (법원사거리)
No. 19-1Beobwon Intersection stop (30580) across the road → Jaunseowon (자운서원)

A shuttle bus is planned.
Details will be posted
in the website’s notices before the festival.

Festival parking information
isn’t in the official notices yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

With 11 walk-in activities,
it’s easy to plan a whole day around crafts.

Hopae making and gomusin decorating
are for elementary school age and up.
For younger siblings, look at
the terrarium, phone grips
and face painting.

If you want to catch the shows,
on the 25th (Sun), 12:00–13:50
the mask play → traditional magic → nongak
run back to back.

Hanbok try-on is walk-in too,
so you can tour the historic site in hanbok.

🔑 KICKThe closing time is listed
two ways: 17:00 and 18:00
Plan on finishing your activities
before 17:00

👍 Recommended for…

✔ Families who want to try lots of crafts
✔ Families who want a taste of tradition
through hopae, hanbok and a tea ceremony
✔ Families looking for a family show
on a Sunday afternoon

From crafts to family shows,
it’s a culture festival where you can spend the whole day without paying admission.

Check the official notice once more before you go!
