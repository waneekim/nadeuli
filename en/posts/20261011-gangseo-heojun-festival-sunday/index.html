2026 Heo Jun Festival Review: Last Day, Sunday Afternoon at Seoul Botanic Park, Magok with Kids

No celebrity stage on the last day,
so the Heo Jun Festival felt calmer.

The 24th Heo Jun Festival
ran for two days, Saturday and Sunday.
We only dropped in late
on Sunday afternoon.

We joined a few of the last
remaining programs,
made a keyring,
and bought snacks like tteokbokki.

🌙 From Sunset into the Night Stage

A Heo Jun character doll on the stage screen at dusk

We watched the stage screen
from the lawn until the sun set
and it got dark.

The night stage screen · a person holding a microphone and a clarinet player (faces are covered)

there would be a closing ceremony
and the Heo Jun Concert of Music
