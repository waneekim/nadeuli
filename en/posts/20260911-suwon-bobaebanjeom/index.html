Where to Eat Jjamppong in Suwon: Bobae Banjeom (Yeongtong) — Whole Squid on Top, Quick for Lunch or a Work Dinner

Our Suwon jjamppong review 🍜

We went to
Bobae Banjeom in Suwon —
home of what we’d call
the jjamppong of our lives.

Jjamppong — squid legs and body rings cover the broth

📍 104 Bandal-ro, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon
(영통상가 Yeongtong arcade, 1F)
⏰ Daily 11:00–22:00
💰 Bobae jjamppong 9,500 won
Bobae ganjjajang 11,000 won
🎁 Flower buns on the house
🅿 1–2 spaces behind the restaurant
📞 0507-1465-8326

It’s a good spot for work dinners,
and lots of students
from the hagwons (academies) nearby
come here too.

Rather than lingering for hours,
it’s for eating quickly and lightly.

🦑 Jjamppong — no skimping on squid

The first thing we noticed
was the squid on top
of the jjamppong (spicy seafood noodle soup).

Not chopped into little bits —
the legs stay whole,
and the body comes in thick rings!

Lift the noodles and the broth comes up with them (GIF)

Wood ear mushrooms, mussels, chives
and sliced Cheongyang chilies on top —
all in a red broth.

One chopstick-lift of noodles — you can see a whole squid ring

🔑 KICKStart with the jjamppong here!
Big squid legs and rings
right on top

🍳 Ganjjajang — with a fried egg

Ganjjajang is black-bean noodles
with the sauce stir-fried separately
and spooned over the noodles —
and on top, a fried egg!

The meat and onion are in big pieces,
with shrimp peeking out here and there.

Ganjjajang with a fried egg — one shot before mixing

It’s the kind of dish
you have to photograph
before you mix it.

Mixing the ganjjajang — big pieces of meat and onion (GIF)

🥢 Tangsuyuk, sauce on the side

The tangsuyuk (sweet-and-sour pork)
doesn’t come with the sauce poured on —
it’s served in a small dish on the side.

So there’s no need to fight
the great “pour or dip” debate.

Tangsuyuk — the sauce comes separately in a small dish

🎁 Flower buns on the house

The flower buns (kkotppang, steamed buns)
came on the house.

Dip them in chunjang (black bean paste)
or soak them in the jjamppong broth.

Flower buns and chunjang, on the house

📋 What we ate that day

Jjamppong — squid legs and body rings
Ganjjajang — topped with a fried egg
Tangsuyuk — sauce in a dish on the side
Flower buns — on the house

📋 Menu & prices

Bobae ganjjajang 11,000 won
Bobae jjajangmyeon 7,500 won

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone looking
for a work-dinner spot
✔ Anyone who wants to finish
a bowl quickly and lightly
✔ Anyone who loves
jjamppong loaded with squid

A bowl of jjamppong
that doesn’t skimp on squid —
that’s this place’s signature.

Work dinner or lunch,
when you want to eat quickly and lightly,
keep this one in mind!
