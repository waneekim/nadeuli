2026 Byeolbada Busan Night Market Dates, Hours, Kids Cooking Class at Hwamyeong Ecological Park with Children

As the sun sets over the Nakdong River,
a market opens at Hwamyeong Ecological Park 🌅

2026 Byeolbada Busan Night Market
’Golden Autumn Season’
is being held every Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday
from September 26 (Sat) to October 18 (Sun)
at the Hwamyeong Ecological Park Lotus Complex.

I’ve organized the official info from
Busan City’s press release (September 21) and
the Visit Busan and reservation pages.

Let’s take a quick look first!

🔴 It’s Happening Now

Including today, Thursday, October 8,
there are 8 days left to visit.
It’s not open on Mondays, Tuesdays, or Wednesdays.

Remaining DatesHours & Highlights
10/8 (Thu) · 10/9 (Fri)17:00~22:00
10/10 (Sat)17:00~22:00 · Kids Cooking Class 18:00 (fully booked as of 10/8)
10/11 (Sun)16:00~21:00 · Kids Cooking Class 18:00 final session
10/15 (Thu)17:00~22:00
10/16 (Fri) · 10/17 (Sat)17:00~22:00 · Gupo Naru Festival at the same lotus complex
10/18 (Sun)16:00~21:00 · Final day, Gupo Naru Festival also held

The Busan City press poster
only lists 17:00~22:00.
The Visit Busan and reservation pages
say Sundays are 16:00~21:00.
Some details may change depending on the situation on site.

🎪 What Kind of Event Is This?

Hosted by Busan City and
organized by the Busan Tourism Organization,
this is Busan’s signature nighttime tourism event.
Last year, about 100,000 people visited
over 12 days.

This year, the event is centered around a traditional liquor pop-up booth
and features three main themes.

ThemeDetails
Starlight TavernTraditional liquor pop-up ‘SPIRIT Busan’, sunset picnic platforms, small business food stalls & food trucks ‘Starlight Food Zone’
Starlight KitchenJeon (savory pancake) making & makgeolli brewing experiences, yoga & running, kids cooking class
Starlight PlazaJangdolbaengi Market (flea market & kids flea market), stage performances & on-site events

For the first time, a delivery zone is also being piloted.
Busan City announced that you can order food from Gupo Market and nearby shops
and pick it up near the event site.

🧁 What Can You Do with Kids?

The Kids Cooking Class
has a different menu each week.
This weekend is week 3,
Character Pretzel making.

Kids Cooking ClassReservation Page Info
TimeSat & Sun 18:00~19:00
Fee8,000 won
Remaining Sessions10/11 (Sun) · 10/10 (Sat) were fully booked as of 10/8
How to ApplyEventus ‘Byeolbada Busan Night Market’ channel, until 10/11 17:30

According to Visit Busan,
you can join experience programs
by advance reservation or on-site registration.

The Kids Flea Market
is a little market where children bring things from home
to share and sell themselves.
The seller (kid seller) fee is 5,000 won,
and the reservation page shows dates
up to October 11 (Sun).

🚇 Directions & Parking

You can walk from the station

The event is at the Hwamyeong Ecological Park Lotus Complex,
right next to the Hwamyeong Auto Camping Site.
The Visit Busan address is
1425 Nakdonggang Bicycle Road, Buk-gu.

Here are the subway directions
from the Gupo Naru Festival guide (Buk-gu),
which is held at the same lotus complex.

Subway LineWalking Directions
Line 2 Deokcheon StationExit 5, walk to ‘Geumbit Noeul Bridge’, about 10 minutes
Line 3 Gupo StationInside the station, take ‘Gamdon Narutgil Riverwalk’, about 5 minutes on foot

Parking is not mentioned in the official night market info.
The Gupo Naru Festival guide
lists Hwamyeong Ecological Park P5~P2 parking lots,
but notes that parking is limited and recommends public transport.

From October 16 (Fri) to 18 (Sun),
the Gupo Naru Festival shuttle bus
runs between Line 2 Hwamyeong Station Exit 1 (in front of Lotte Mart)
and Hwamyeong Ecological Park P5 parking lot.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

The sunset picnic platforms are
rest areas set up for you to enjoy the sunset and night view.
They are free to use,
with only a few spots as paid packages.

If you’ve reserved the cooking class (18:00),
you can arrive a bit early
and explore the market first.

Since it’s an evening event by the river,
it’s a good idea to bring an extra jacket for your child.

🔑 KICKThere are only a few mats and camping items on site,
so seats may run out.
You’re allowed to bring your own mat or camping chair
and set it up at the event.

👍 Recommended for These Families

✔ Families who want to enjoy a sunset picnic dinner with their kids
✔ Families whose children love cooking experiences or flea markets
✔ Families looking for an evening food market by the Nakdong River

With sunset picnic platforms and little chef experiences,
it’s a great market for a family evening outing on any of the 8 remaining days.
