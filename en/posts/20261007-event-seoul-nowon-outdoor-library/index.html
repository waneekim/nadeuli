Nowon Outdoor Library 2026 ‘Nangman Bojang’ in Seoul — Schedule, Parking & Programs for a Book Picnic with Kids on the Gyeongchun Line Forest Park

A fall weekend when books
come out onto the grass
of a forest trail 📚

The 2026 Nowon Outdoor Library ‘Nangman Bojang’
(노원 야외도서관 낭만보장 — “romance guaranteed”)
runs for two days,
Sat Oct 17 – Sun Oct 18.

It’s set along the Gyeongchun Line Forest Park
in front of the Hagye 2-dong
Community Service Center,
from noon to 8 pm on both days.

We put this together from
Nowon-gu’s press release (October 7)
and the poster attached to it.

Shall we start with the overview?

🎪 What kind of event is it?

It’s an outdoor reading festival
put on by Nowon-gu.
Nowon-gu hosts it,
and the Nowon-gu Public Library runs it.

This year’s theme is
’In the AI Era, After All, People’.
The focus is on people
meeting and talking
with books between them.

The Lawn Plaza,
Gyeongchun Station Book & Coffee
and the Gyeongchun Line railway tracks —
about 8,800 m² in all —
become the festival grounds.

Lawn Plaza
Main stage · Camping zone · Book rest area

Gyeongchun Line tracks
’Nowon Neighborhood Bookstore Trip’
and a ‘bulmeong’ fire-gazing zone
Four local bookstores bring
their recommended books
(Bronte Salon 브론테살롱 · Jigu Bulsichak 지구불시착 ·
Chaekingam 책인감 · Ttaengttaengseom 땡땡섬)

Bookshelves & book baskets
About 1,000 books
picked by the Nowon-gu Public Library.
Insects, art, nature, ecology, rest, health —
chosen by theme and by age group,
so just pull one out and read!

✨ What’s on, and when?

Here are the programs with set times.

Sat Oct 17
13:00 Meet author Kim A-young
(bookstore & author booth, Lawn Plaza)
15:00 Book concert with Professor Kim Sang-geun
’Reading People in the AI Era’
16:00 Meet author Heo Ji-na
17:20 Opening ceremony
18:10 ‘Ssolleolle’ (쏠레올레) opera gala show

Sun Oct 18
15:00 Humanities concert by docent Lee Ji-an
’Comfort and Romance Found
in the Stories of Italian Masterpieces’
16:00 ‘Chaekmeong time’ — resting with a book

The featured book for
Professor Kim’s book concert
is The Odyssey.

The 18:10 opera gala time is from the poster;
the press release says it comes
’right after the opening ceremony’.

The opening ceremony offers
sign-language interpretation,
guidance for visually impaired visitors
and wheelchair seating.

🫧 What shows and activities are there?

Family shows
Family shows such as bubble and magic shows,
plus string performances,
run throughout the festival.
(Times aren’t out yet)

Reading activities
Make a mini book, an emotion doll
or a picture book;
make a bookmark or a mini-book key ring

There’s also mood-lamp making
with Words I Want to Hear (듣고 싶은 말),
Nowon-gu’s ‘One Book of the Year’.

Nangman Theater: Beer Cinema
An open-air cinema
in the afternoon on both days.
(The films haven’t been announced yet)

Food & booths
Food trucks, a CGV pop-up store
and a craft beer cooperative take part.
The poster also lists exhibitions
and a local market.

🚇 Getting there & parking

No parking at all at the venue

The poster puts it like this:
“Parking at the venue is completely prohibited,
so please use public transportation.”

From Hagye Station (Line 7), exit 4
it’s a 7-minute walk
to Gyeongchun Station Book & Coffee
(경춘스테이션 북앤커피, 53 Gongneung-ro 55-gil).
(Seoul city’s ‘Seoul in My Hand’ article, July)

Gyeongchun Station Book & Coffee
is part of the festival grounds.

Bus stops and entrances
aren’t in the official notice yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Times for the bubble and magic shows
haven’t been announced yet.
If the shows are what you’re after,
it’s easiest to check the times
as soon as you arrive.

The shelves also have books on themes
like insects, nature and ecology.
Pick one and read it
at the book rest area on the Lawn Plaza.

The festival runs until 8 pm.
On the 17th, the opening ceremony is at 17:20,
followed by the opera gala show.

🔑 KICKOn festival days, all of Nowon-gu's
public and small libraries
are closed (per the poster),
except the Nowon Lifelong Learning Center
Check your library plans for that weekend!
