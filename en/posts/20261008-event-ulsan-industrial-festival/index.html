2026 Ulsan Industrial Festival Schedule · Drone Show · AI Experience Zone, Before Visiting Taehwagang Namgu Riverside with Kids

An autumn festival is opening
at the Taehwagang riverside 🏭

2026 Ulsan Industrial Festival
will be held for four days from
October 8 (Thu) to 11 (Sun),
centered around Taehwagang National Garden Namgu Riverside,
with events throughout Ulsan.

This is a summary of the Ulsan city press release (October 6)
and information from the Ulsan Culture & Tourism Foundation and Waterworks Headquarters.

Let's take a quick look first!

🔴 Happening Now

Today, Thursday, October 8 is the opening day.
There are four days left until the 11th (Sun).

Since the schedule continues from Hangeul Day (9th) through the weekend,
I’ve organized the remaining days in a table.

Remaining DaysMain Programs
10/8 (Thu) EveningOpening Ceremony 18:30 Welcome Performance, 19:00 Themed Performance · Celebration Performance · 1,000-Drone & Fireworks Show
10/9 (Fri, Hangeul Day)Ulsan Citizen Festival 11:30~13:00 · Tap Water Cafe
10/10 (Sat)Ulsan Citizen Festival 11:30~13:00 · Tap Water Cafe · Evening U-Culture Festival
10/11 (Sun)13:00~16:00 Silla Pop Concert · Dancing Carnival, 19:00 Closing · Traditional Performance
Every day 8~11AI Experience Zone · Ulsan Future Expo · PR/Experience Booths

On the 8th, there is the Labor-Management-Civil-Government Unity Walking Event,
on the 9th, the Workplace Song Festival King of Kings,
on the 10th, the Workers’ Sports Festival
are all held alongside the festival.

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is This?

This year, the festival for the industrial city of Ulsan
has added more history and culture.

This year’s vision is
’AI Transformation (AX) Grand Shift,
All In! Ulsan.’

It connects 60 years of Ulsan’s industrial growth
and 7,000 years of Ulsan’s history
into one story,
and is designed as a festival where citizens take the stage.

All the scattered event venues
have been gathered into one place at
Taehwagang National Garden Namgu Riverside.

🚁 What’s Happening on Opening Night?

The opening ceremony’s themed performance
flows through 6 acts, from
’Time Asks’ to
’Land of Envy! Ulsan.’

Citizen performers, local artists,
and professional performers will all take the stage together.

FollowingCelebration Performances (Cha Ji-yeon, Baek In-tae, Park Hye-kyung, Woongsan Band, Soran, Band ONEWE)
With Celebration Performances1,000-Drone Swarm Show · Fireworks Show

The exact start time for the drone and fireworks show
is not specified in the official information.

🤖 Experiences to Try with Kids

The AI Experience Zone at Namgu Riverside
is open all four days from the 8th to the 11th.
It is open to everyone, regardless of age or gender,
according to the information provided.

WhereWhat to Do
AI Experience Zone (8~11)Create Your Own Storybook (Generative AI) · VR Racing · AI Caricature · AI Health Experience · Robot Soccer · 3D Printing
Ulsan Future Expo (8~11)AI · Robot · Extended Reality (XR) Exhibitions · Demonstrations · Experiences
Whale Water PR (9~11)Water Purification VR Experience Zone, Keyring & Bottled Whale Water for video ‘likes’ or comments
Tap Water Cafe (9,10)Coffee & Iced Tea made with Whale Water, Reusable Cups
Ulsan Tourism (Foundation)Tourism Souvenir Exhibition · ‘Came to Ulsan’ App Roulette · Travel Play Market

For food, the organizing committee has selected
5 food trucks and
14 food rest areas.

🥁 What About the Last Day, the 11th (Sun)?

From 13:00 to 16:00,
young artists will perform at the
Gaeunpo Silla Pop Concert
and there will be a citizen Dancing Carnival.

After the SeoDo Band’s special performance at 19:00,
there will be a closing speech and the announcement of the vision for the 2027 Ulsan Representative Festival.

The closing performance will feature traditional acts such as dance, samulnori, Namsadang Nori,
tightrope walking, and Ganggangsullae.

🚗 Directions & Parking

The main venue is Namgu Riverside

The main venue is
Taehwagang National Garden Namgu Riverside
(Sinjeong-dong, Nam-gu, Ulsan).

There is no official announcement yet
about festival shuttle buses, traffic control, or parking.

According to Naver Place (checked October 8),
the nearest Taehwagang Riverside Public Parking Lot
(1513 Sinjeong-dong, Nam-gu)
is listed as 500 won for the first 30 minutes.
There is no information on whether this will be available during the festival.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

At the Ulsan Culture & Tourism Foundation’s Barrier-Free Tourism PR Center,
there are restrooms for people with disabilities and nursing rooms,
and wheelchair and stroller rentals are also available.

Ulsan City has emphasized festival safety rules:
follow the guidance of safety staff,
familiarize yourself with emergency exits in advance,
and do not enter restricted areas.

🔑 KICKThe 1,000-drone & fireworks show
is officially scheduled only for the opening day, the 8th
If you want to focus on experiences,
you can visit during the holiday from the 9th to 11th
