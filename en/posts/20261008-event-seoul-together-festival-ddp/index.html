2026 Seoul Dahamkke Eoullim Hanmadang Schedule, Experience Zones, Parking – Free Hangeul Day Activities at DDP

On Hangeul Day afternoon, a public holiday,
you can experience the cultures of several countries
in one place at Dongdaemun 🌏

Now in its second year!
The 2nd Seoul Dahamkke Eoullim Hanmadang
will be held on October 9 (Fri) from 13:30 to 17:00
at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) Eoullim Square (동대문디자인플라자 어울림광장).

Participation is free.

I’ve organized the information from
Seoul City’s press release (October 6) and
the Seoul festival website (Fun Seoul).

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Event

This is a cultural and health exchange event
where Korean and international residents of Seoul
come together through experiences and performances.

It is hosted by the Seoul Global Center,
organized by K-Health Media,
and sponsored by the Seoul Metropolitan Government.

According to the press release, embassies of Colombia, Vietnam,
Uzbekistan, and the Spanish Cultural Center will participate,
and international student associations will help run
the World Culture Zone booths.

There will also be a team of 30 volunteer interpreters
on site to assist foreign residents who are not fluent in Korean.
They will help with interpretation, event guidance,
and running the experience booths.

Seoul City stated that they have prepared
interactive programs so that people can
naturally experience different cultures.

The official target participants are
’Korean and international residents of Seoul
(multicultural families, international students, citizens, etc.)’.

✨ What Experiences Are There?

There are five experience zones in total.
Here’s a table of what you can do at each zone.

Experience ZoneActivities
Information ZoneEvent info · Life counseling for foreign residents
K-Beauty ZoneAI skin analysis · Personal color diagnosis · Photo zone · Face painting
K-Health ZoneBlood pressure/blood sugar check · Seoul fitness test · VR safety experience · CPR
K-Culture ZoneGat keyring & traditional headband making · Traditional calligraphy · Jegi, Tuho, Ddakji games
World Culture Zone6 countries’ culture experience: Vietnam, Spain, Uzbekistan, India, Peru, Philippines

This year, the Mobile Fitness Test is new.
You can measure your strength and flexibility
and get exercise advice based on your results.

According to the info,
adults aged 18 and over who participate
will receive 1,000 points for Wrist Doctor 9988.

🎤 What’s on Stage?

The official guides list the stage programs as follows:

A new Seoul City Taekwondo performance

have not yet been announced.

🚇 Directions & Parking

Eoullim Square is near Exit 1

Directions are based on the DDP guide.
The Fun Seoul transportation guide
also says it’s 26m from Exit 1.

TransportWhere to Get Off/Route
Subway Lines 2, 4, 5Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station Exit 1 (closest to Eoullim Square)
CarEnter via Eulji-ro 45-gil to DDP underground parking

The DDP parking lot is open 24 hours.
The fees are as follows:

Parking TypeFee
Hourly4,800 won per hour (400 won per 5 min)
Daily Maximum50,000 won
Seoul City Multi-child Card & Compact Cars50% discount (personal card, no double discounts)

There is no mention of event participant
parking discounts in the notice.
For parking inquiries, contact the DDP Parking Customer Center
at 02-2153-0141.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Bringing Kids

Fun activities for kids include
face painting & photo zone,
jegi, tuho, ddakji games,
gat keyring & traditional headband making,
and VR safety experience.

The event starts at 13:30
and ends at 17:00.
If you leave after lunch,
you’ll arrive at a good time.

There is no information about
the minimum age or number of materials
for each booth, so please check on site.

The next day (October 10),
there will be a Kids’ Economy Market
at the same DDP.
See the summary → DDP Kids’ Economy Market

and the first 100 on site
will receive a gift
(per Fun Seoul guide)
