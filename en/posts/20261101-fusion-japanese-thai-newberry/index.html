Fusion Japanese & Thai in Newberry: Pho with Amazing Beef and Broth (US Business Trip, Day 8)

Lunch on Day 8 was at
a Japanese and Thai restaurant
in Newberry 🍜

We had pho and a roll, and…
as expected, we misjudged how much to order! 😅

Pho with amazing beef and broth

📍 Fusion Japanese & Thai Cuisine
(Newberry, South Carolina, USA
1266 Wilson Rd)
⏰ Mon–Thu 11:00–21:30
Fri–Sat 11:00–22:30 · Sun 11:00–22:00
(restaurant website)

A pink FUSION sign sits on the roof,

The FUSION sign on the roof — the street number by the door is 1266

Inside there’s a bar counter,

🍜 Pho: The Beef and Broth Were Amazing

The pho came with thin slices
of beef piled
on top of the broth.

According to the menu on the restaurant’s website,
it’s a beef bone broth simmered for a long time
with thin rice noodles, bean sprouts, red onion and cilantro,
topped with rare tenderloin.

And truly,
the beef and broth were amazing!

Beef and noodles lifted with chopsticks

The only thing was the noodles that kept
breaking apart, which felt typical of the US —
not so great.

🍣 A Roll Topped with Tuna

Along with the pho, we ordered a roll.

It was topped with tuna,
and two sauces, one orange and one brown,
were drizzled over it in a zigzag.

A roll topped with tuna and two sauces (part of a companion's face is covered)

A bowl of peanuts in front of the roll

😅 As Expected, We Misjudged the Amount

There was also a plate of
beef stir-fried with wide noodles on the table.

Just the evening before at Sun Ming,
the portions got the better of us…

A bowl of pho plus a roll —
this time, too, as expected, we misjudged how much to order.

A plate of stir-fried beef and wide noodles on the table

KICKPortions at US restaurants are big!
Before you order,
think about the amount once more 🍜

👍 Recommended For

✔ When you’re craving a warm broth
in the US
✔ Anyone who loves pho
loaded with beef
✔ Anyone looking for Japanese or Thai food
around Newberry

The noodles were a letdown, but
the beef and broth were the best — a great lunch!
