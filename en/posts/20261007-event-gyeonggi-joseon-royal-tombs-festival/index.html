Joseon Royal Tombs Festival 2026 — Schedule, Booking & Parking Before You Take the Kids to Yungneung & Geolleung and Donggureung

A festival comes to the forest paths
of the royal tombs this autumn 🍂

The 7th edition this year!
The 2026 World Heritage Joseon Royal Tombs Festival
(2026 세계유산 조선왕릉축전)
starts with the opening ceremony on Fri, Oct 16
and runs until Sun, Oct 25
at 12 Joseon royal tombs.

The opening ceremony and the musical
are staged at Yungneung and Geolleung in Hwaseong
(융릉·건릉, also called Yunggeolleung).
This post rounds up the 8 royal tombs
in Gyeonggi-do,
grouped so they’re easy to visit with kids.

We put this together from the festival website,
the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism’s festival listing
and the royal tombs’ visitor information.

First, the essentials at a glance

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

It’s a festival of shows, tours,
hands-on activities and exhibitions
that brings you up close to the Joseon royal tombs,
a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
This year’s slogan is ‘See Them Anew, the Joseon Royal Tombs’.

The main festival runs Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, Oct 25.
Monday the 19th is the royal tombs’ closing day,
so only some programs run that day.

The ministry’s festival listing gives
Guri as the host city,
but the map in this post is pinned to
Yungneung and Geolleung in Hwaseong,
where the opening ceremony and the musical take place.

🗺️ The 8 royal tombs in Gyeonggi-do — what’s on where?

We picked out just the Gyeonggi-do royal tombs for this table.
The festival also runs at 3 royal tombs in Seoul
and at Jangneung in Yeongwol.

✨ When are the evening shows and bookable programs?

The opening ceremony and the evening shows
need advance booking.
Booking opened in stages from September 15
on Ticketlink (티켓링크).

‘Stories of the Royal Tombs by the Tomb Keeper’ (능참봉 이야기)
is a walking show: the audience
becomes the new tomb keeper,
strolling the forest paths
and solving quizzes and missions.

🔍 What’s on during the day?

Joseon Detective (조선명탐정) is
an outdoor escape game to find the missing words of the royal annals,
collecting clues all around the royal tombs.
No booking — just join on site.

ActivityWhen & where

At Donggureung, the Book Garden teams up
with Guri City Library
for fairy-tale activities and bookmark making.
The notice says activities may end early
depending on conditions on site.

🎫 How much is admission?

There’s an admission fee to enter the royal tombs.
(The ministry’s festival listing also says ‘admission charged’.)
Kids get in free: anyone aged 24 or under is free.

CategoryAdmission
Ages 25 to 641,000 won (500 won at Yeongneung and Nyeongneung, Yeoju)
Ages 24 and under · 65 and overFree (bring ID)
Parents with a Dadungi (multi-child family) cardFree (show the card)
Anyone wearing hanbokFree

Ticket prices for the shows and activities
aren’t listed separately on the website;
the ministry’s festival listing just says
’some performances and hands-on programs are paid’.

🚗 Getting to Yungneung and Geolleung & parking

Parking at Yungneung and Geolleung is free

Yungneung and Geolleung,
home to the opening ceremony and the musical,
is at 21 Hyohaeng-ro 481beon-gil,
Byeongjeom-gu, Hwaseong-si.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

Most daytime activities start at 10:00
and end at 17:00.
Arrive in the morning and you can do
the escape game and a craft back-to-back
at the same royal tomb.

Under the royal tombs’ visitor rules,
picnic mats, sunshades,
and sports or play equipment
may have to be left in storage,
or you may be refused entry.
Packed lunches can only be eaten in designated areas,
and pets aren’t allowed in.

The musical tells the story
of the death of Crown Prince Sado,
so read the synopsis first
and then decide whether to watch it with your kids.

go on a weekend or from Tuesday on
