2026 Gunsan Jjamppong Festival Dates, Chef Schedule, Parking—Before Visiting Baeknyeon Square with Kids

It’s autumn, the season when you crave a spicy, hearty broth 🍜

The 2026 Gunsan Jjamppong Festival will be held for three days, from October 9 (Fri) to 11 (Sun), 12:00~22:00, at the Baeknyeon Square area in Jangmi-dong, Gunsan.

I’ve organized the information from the Gunsan City press release (September 28) and the festival website.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

Gunsan City introduces Gunsan as the ‘nation’s representative jjamppong city.’

In the early 1970s, Chinese immigrants made ‘chomamyeon’ with pork, beef, and chicken bone broth, and with the addition of fresh seafood from the port city and chili powder, it became today’s Gunsan jjamppong.

At the festival, there will be a total of 20 booths combining local traditional Chinese restaurants and chef collaboration content. There will be various menus like meat jjamppong and seafood jjamppong.

All Chinese food booths will sell jjamppong and jjajangmyeon in 0.5 servings. This way, you can try several dishes at reasonable prices.

👨‍🍳 When Are the Chefs Coming?

Three chefs known from TV will participate. These are the dates listed in the chef promotion images on the website.

DateChef
10/9(Fri)~11(Sun)Chef Mikael (‘Please Take Care of My Refrigerator’)
10/9(Fri)Chef David Lee (‘Black and White Chef’), The Born Korea tasting booth
10/10(Sat)Chef Lee Mi-young (‘Black and White Chef’), The Born Korea tasting booth

On the festival map, the ‘Chef Mikael’ booth and ‘The Born Korea’ booth are drawn separately. The exact times the chefs will appear have not been officially announced yet.

🎯 What Experiences Are There?

Anyone, young or old, can participate in jjamppong-themed experiences. Here are the experience booths listed on the website.

Experience BoothOperation
Bubbling Jjamppong SoupAll day
Beat the K-Tourism IslandAll day
Pick the K-Tourism IslandAll day

The press release also introduces ‘The Three Iron Delivery Brothers’ as an experience program. There is no official information yet about participation fees.

In the evening, a premium program called ‘Jjamppong Restaurant’ will be held. It will fill the festival night with performances. On the map, the seating is next to the stage.

There is also a Jjamppong Specialty Street near the festival venue. The city announced plans to introduce old Chinese restaurants and famous spots, such as Bin Hae Won, known as a movie filming location.

On the festival map, the Gunsan Modern History Museum, Modern Art Museum, and Modern Architecture Museum are marked right next to the venue.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

Gunsan jjamppong has a spicy broth with chili powder. If your child can’t eat spicy food, you can choose jjajangmyeon 0.5 serving.

On the festival map, operation/medical booths and the integrated information center are marked.

Since the festival runs until 22:00, if you plan to stay for the evening stage, it’s a good idea to bring an extra jacket for your child.
