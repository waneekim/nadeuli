2026 Seogwipo Chilsimni Festival Schedule, Drone Show & Parking – Cheonjiyeon Falls with Kids

As the sea breeze turns cool,
Seogwipo becomes a festival ground for three days 🌊

The 32nd Seogwipo Chilsimni Festival
runs October 23 (Fri) to 25 (Sun)
around the Cheonjiyeon Falls parking lot in Seogwipo.

I checked the official festival website’s
overview, program, performance and transport pages
and the Seogwipo City and Ministry of Culture notices
on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

It’s Seogwipo’s signature local festival,
bringing together the city’s nature, culture
and citizen participation.
It was first held in 1995.

The official website explains that the name “Chilsimni” (70 ri)
goes back to the 1653 gazetteer Tamnaji (탐라지),
which recorded Seogwipo as lying 70 ri to the west
of Seongeup, where the Jeong-ui county office stood.

This year’s slogan is
’동GO동樂 볼거인, 놀거인, 재미인’ (original Korean slogan).
It is hosted by Seogwipo City
and organized by the Seogwipo Chilsimni Festival Promotion Committee,
and according to the Ministry of Culture’s festival listing, it’s free.

🎆 The Drone Light Show and Night Stages

The opening ceremony on the 23rd features an opening declaration and performance,
a guest concert,
and then a drone light show
capturing Seogwipo’s charms.
It’s linked with Seogwipo City’s drone light show
’Shining Seogwipo Nights’ (빛나는 서귀포의 밤).

The night of the 24th brings a guest concert
and a retro DJ party,
and the 25th wraps up the festival
with Crying Nut and a laser beam show.

🗺️ What Can Kids Try?

ActivityWhat it is
Chilsimni ExplorersOutdoor adventure solving missions with a map and clues · join on site
Chilsimni Mini Sports DaySimple games for the whole family · open to all visitors
Always-on activitiesRetro stamp workroom · sea-glass candles & vintage lettering cakes
MarketFlea market & food trucks · local brand pop-ups

The Explorers route covers the festival site, Saeyeongyo Bridge,
Jaguri Park and Chilsimni Poetry Park.
Mini Sports Day takes place at the Chilsimni Outdoor Theater.

The Horror Maze is a night activity with a horror maze, photo zones
and a horror costume runway,
so check the mood first before going in with young kids.

On the 23rd from 16:00 to 19:00,
the parade passes along Jungjeong-ro → Jeongbang-ro →
Chilsimni-ro → the Cheonjiyeon Falls parking lot.
Nearby roads may be crowded at this time,
so please follow the on-site traffic guidance.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

Kids’ activities are busiest on Saturday the 24th.
The Explorers start at 12:00,
followed by Mini Sports Day from 16:00.

On Sunday the 25th, there’s Mini Sports Day at 13:00,
and at 16:00 Jeju children take the stage
for the Haesuni & Seomdori contest.
(Applications close October 11,
for Jeju residents aged 6–12)

🔑 KICKThe festival parking lot has only about 230 spaces
Especially during the parade on the 23rd,
take the bus to the Cheonjiyeon Falls terminus
