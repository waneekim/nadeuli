Blu Shaak Coffee Magok (Seoul) — A Delicious Signature Shaak Latte: 8 oz for 3,400 Won, and Picking Your Beans

My Magok lunchtime coffee review 🦈

At lunch on a Tuesday,
I stopped by Blu Shaak Coffee
on Magokdong-ro.

The signature Shaak Latte was delicious.

Shaak Latte — in the 8 oz signature cup, coffee and milk in layers under the cream

📍 55 Magokdong-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul
1F, #102
(about 430 m from Balsan Station exit 9, Line 5)
⏰ Weekdays 08:00–22:00
Sat 10:00–21:00
Sun 10:00–20:00
(per Naver Place)
💰 Shaak Latte 3,400 won
Americano 2,500 won
🅿 Parking available
📞 0507-1380-3099

🏠 The blue fin logo

It’s a white shop
with a blue shark-fin logo.

The window by the entrance
is covered in menu posters,
and there’s a bench out front.

Blu Shaak Coffee Seoul Magok — the blue fin logo and the awning

🥛 The signature comes in 8 oz

The Shaak Latte I had
came in a small
8 oz signature cup with the logo on it.

According to the menu board, signature drinks
come in ‘8 oz, the size that tastes best’,
and there are no size-ups — they’re iced only.

The signature section has two drinks.

Shaak Latte 3,400 won
Peanut Latte 3,800 won

The iced drink I got along with it
came in a big cup with a sleeve.

The sleeve says
’Vacation in the City’.

The iced drink I got along with it — 'Vacation in the City' on the sleeve

📋 The menu board — Americano 2,500 won

The menu board hangs in front of the counter.

An Americano is 2,500 won,
and it also comes in a 1 L bottle.

Sizes are signature (8 oz),
16 oz (hot drinks),
20 oz (iced)
and the 1 L bottle (iced, 4 shots).

The menu board in front of the counter — coffee, non-coffee, blended drinks, tea

🫘 Three beans to choose from

Inside there’s an ordering kiosk,
with a note about the beans stuck beside it.

✔ Night Blend
rich aroma, full body, nutty taste
✔ Summer Blend (NEW)
fruity, fresh and sweet, with some acidity
✔ Decaf
clean and smoky

Seasonal beans and decaf
cost 1,000 won extra.

The ordering kiosk — the bean note on the left

The Night Blend poster on the wall
lists five tasting notes —
dark chocolate, cacao nibs,
malt, peanut and brown sugar.

⏰ Check the hours first

On the day I visited, the glass entrance door said
weekdays 07:00–22:00,
Sat, Sun & public holidays 10:00–21:00.

That differs from Naver Place (opening at 8:00 on weekdays,
closing at 20:00 on Sundays),
so if you’re going early in the morning or on a Sunday evening,
check before you go.

The hours on the glass entrance door (as of our visit)
