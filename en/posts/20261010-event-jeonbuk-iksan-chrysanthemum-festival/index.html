2026 Iksan Chrysanthemum Festival Dates, Kids’ Crafts, Parking – Before Visiting Jungang Sports Park with Kids

23rd Iksan Ten-Million Chrysanthemum Festival (익산 천만송이 국화축제) Poster — Oct 23 (Fri)-Nov 1 (Sun), 2026 (10 days), Jungang Sports Park, Iksan (익산 중앙체육공원) (Photo: Korea Tourism Organization · KOGL Type 3)

Late autumn is coming,
and the chrysanthemums are at their most fragrant 🌼

The 23rd Iksan Ten-Million Chrysanthemum Festival (익산 천만송이 국화축제)
runs October 23 (Fri) to November 1 (Sun)
for 10 days at Jungang Sports Park in Iksan.

This year’s theme is
”GLOW IKSAN – Iksan shining anew like a jewel.”

I rechecked Iksan City’s festival website
and the city’s event notice (October 2)
on October 10 and summarized them here.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 What Kind of Festival Is It?

past festival — A festival entrance sculpture decorated with flowers (Photo: Korea Tourism Organization · KOGL Type 3)

past festival — An exhibition walkway lined with chrysanthemum bonsai (Photo: Korea Tourism Organization · KOGL Type 3)

It’s Iksan’s autumn chrysanthemum festival,
hosted by Iksan City and organized
by the Iksan Agricultural Technology Center.

Chrysanthemum works such as
multi-bloom, cascade, and bonsai styles are on display,
and there’s a farm-products market with chrysanthemum tea and more.

Here’s what the festival website lists as exhibits.

ExhibitSize
Chrysanthemum sculpturesAbout 600
Chrysanthemums and other autumn flowers60,000 plants
Chrysanthemum bonsai worksAbout 200
Light installationsAbout 100

🎨 What Crafts Can Kids Do?

Sixteen crafts run all 10 days,
10:00–21:00.
Eight are free and eight are paid.

CraftType
Chrysanthemum bead bracelet · Name braceletFree
Baekje crown · Baekje mother-of-pearl kids’ jewelry boxFree
Balsam nail dyeing · Four Gracious Plants D-form blocksFree
Autumn jewel cross-stitch keyringFree
Keycap keyringFree · stamp tour participants only
Hands-on insect experiencePaid · stag beetle & rhinoceros beetle houses, insect specimens
Chrysanthemum slime & diary decorating · Pressed flowers · Mood lampPaid
Chrysanthemum & Maryong coloring playground · Goods makingPaid

Fees for the paid crafts
are not in the official information yet.

🎁 What Events Can You Join?

EventHow to join
Ten-Million Blossoms Craft YardFamilies & kids: post a festival visit on social media, then do crafts like bead bracelets (about 600 on weekdays, 900 on weekends)
Stamp tour “Kkot-kkot Sumeora”Visit key spots like the festival site and Sinheung Park + mobile missions, prize draw
“Drink, Eat, Stay in Iksan”Spend 30,000 won or more at local restaurants/lodging or 15,000 won at cafés, post on social media → bouquet (100 people daily)

Sign up for the bouquet event
at the operations booth on site.

Discount coupons for the city’s selected
partner shops are handed out only at the festival site.

still says “in preparation,“
so times may change.

takes about 1 hour 20 minutes.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

The venue has two women’s restrooms
with a nursing room.
Wheelchair rental and a lost-child center are also available.

The kids’ puppet show “The Brother-Sister Pagoda”
has 10 a.m. sessions on the 26th, 28th, and 30th,
so you can plan a weekday morning visit.

If you join the “Kkot-kkot Sumeora” stamp tour,
you can also make a keycap keyring.

🔑 KICKCraft booths stay open until 9 p.m.
With about 100 light installations,
plan to stay into the evening
