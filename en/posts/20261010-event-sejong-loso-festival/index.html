2026 Sejong LoSo Festival Schedule, Bubble Show & Movies – City Symbol Plaza with Kids, Before You Go

On a cool autumn evening,
Sejong’s City Symbol Plaza starts buzzing 🎈

The 2026 Sejong LoSo Festival “Sejong Deulsseok” (세종 들썩)
runs on October 16 (Fri) and 17 (Sat)
for two days around the City Symbol Plaza.

This guide is based on Sejong City’s “event notice” (October 8)
and the event plan attached to the September call for vendors.

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

It is held to open up new sales channels for local small businesses.
The slogan is “Local values come together for a bigger tomorrow!”

At its heart is a small-business win-win market,
where you can see, buy and try products from Sejong’s small businesses,
with shows and games the whole family can enjoy.

The event plan describes it as a festival
that links after-work and weekend leisure to local shops.

✨ What’s On Friday Evening (16th)?

Friday runs from 16:00 to 22:00.

20:30–22:00Movie screening: Zootopia

🫧 And on Saturday (17th)?

Saturday runs from 11:00 to 22:00,
with lots of programs to enjoy with kids.

On both days, the evening movie
is introduced as “movie time with the family.”

🎁 Spend 30,000 won, get 10,000 won back

During the festival, spend 30,000 won or more
anywhere in Sejong (except large supermarts and adult entertainment bars)
and receive a 10,000 won Onnuri gift certificate.

Receipts from 2 or more shops can be combined,
and you must bring your ID.
Certificates are handed out from 18:00 to 22:00 at the event HQ,
to the first 400 people each day, 1 per person per day.

🚗 Getting There & Parking

No parking info has been announced yet

The City Symbol Plaza is in Naseong-dong, Sejong.
Its Naver Place address is 30 Eoullnuri-ro.

Neither the city notices nor the event plan
include parking or public transit information.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

With kids, Saturday afternoon works well:
Recreation (15:00) → Treasure hunt (17:00) →
Bubble show (18:00) run one after another.

On Friday there is the tall-man performance (16:00)
and the balloon art show (19:30).

The September poster and the event plan say that on the 17th
the festival links up with a downtown camping event with 100 camper vans.

🔑 KICKThe gift certificate event starts at 18:00 each day
for the first 400 people
Bring your receipts and ID!
