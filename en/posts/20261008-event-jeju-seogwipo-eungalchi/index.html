2026 Seogwipo Silver Hairtail Festival Schedule · Fireworks Show · Conch Fishing Experience, Before Visiting Seogwipo Port with Kids

A festival where you can meet
the silver hairtail fresh from the Seogwipo sea 🐟

The 7th Seogwipo Silver Hairtail Festival
will be held for three days from October 8 (Thu) to 10 (Sat)
at the special stage area of Seogwipo Port East Pier.

On the first day, October 8, the opening ceremony is at 18:00,
and at 21:00, there will be a Seogwipo Port Night Sea Fireworks Show!

I’ve organized the schedule and program information
from Jeju’s official press release (October 7)
and the festival website.

Shall we take a quick look first?

🔥 Happening Now: Three Main Days

Free hairtail tasting, conch fishing,
and hairtail auction experiences are available all three days.
The stage programs change daily.

DateMain Stage Events
10/8 (Thu)Haenyeo Parade 17:00 → Opening Ceremony 18:00 → Celebration Performance → Fireworks Show 21:00
10/9 (Fri)Song Festival · Arm Wrestling · Yut Game Preliminaries → Silver Hairtail Song Festival 18:20 → Guest Singer · Awards 20:30
10/10 (Sat)Arm Wrestling · Yut Game Finals → Fishermen’s Song Festival 16:30 → Guest Singer Performance 17:30

🎪 Festival Highlights

This is a seafood festival in Seogwipo
where you can taste Jeju’s signature silver hairtail
and experience auctions and fishing culture firsthand.

Hosted and organized by the Seogwipo Fisheries Cooperative,
this year’s slogan is
”Blue Sea, Silver Waves, Breath of Fishermen.”

There will be hairtail food events and
a Seogwipo Fisheries Cooperative seafood market,
with seafood sold at up to 30% off.

🎆 First Day, October 8 (Thu): Opening Ceremony and Fireworks Show

The first day starts with afternoon experiences,
followed by the haenyeo parade, opening ceremony,
and ends with the night fireworks show.

October 9 (Fri) is Hangeul Day.
There are preliminary competitions during the day,
and the Silver Hairtail Song Festival in the evening.

🎣 Experiences to Try with Kids

The conch fishing experience lets you catch conch with a fishing rod
and grill them at the bulteok (outdoor stone grill).
The auction experience is held with a real auctioneer.

ExperienceTime & Participation
Gujaengi Conch Fishing8th at 13:00, 14:00, 15:00; 9th & 10th every hour from 11:00–16:00 · 10,000 won per person · On-site registration
Hairtail AuctionAt least 1 child (age 5–high school senior) + at least 1 parent, up to 4 per team · Advance registration closed, 5 teams per session on-site, first come first served
Dawn Auction9th & 10th at 08:00 · Ages 5 and up, up to 4 children/youth + at least 1 parent · Advance registration closed
Free Hairtail TastingHairtail & hairtail fish cakes · All three days
Silver Hairtail Pangpang EventSurprise roulette event during the festival

The yut game was for 5th graders and up,
with teams of 4 including 1 adult,
but registration for the song festival and arm wrestling
has already closed.

The festival stage, experiences, and tastings
are all at the Seogwipo Port East Pier Special Stage Area.
The address from the Seogwipo city event guide is
16, Chilsimniri-ro 72beon-gil.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Going with Kids

If you’re with kids,
make sure to try the conch fishing.
Since it’s on-site registration, on the 9th and 10th
it runs every hour from 11:00 to 16:00.

Auction experience advance registration is closed,
but they plan to accept 5 teams per session
on a first-come, first-served basis.
There is no information on where or how to register on-site.

Free hairtail and hairtail fish cake tastings
are available all three days.

🔑 KICKThe fireworks show is only once, at 21:00 on the 8th (Thu)
If you visit on the 9th or 10th,
plan for daytime experiences and evening song festivals or performances
