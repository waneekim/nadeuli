Where to Eat Gopchang in Mangpo, Suwon — Byeokjeokgol Hwangso Gopchang: a Mixed Platter, Liver and Tripe First, and the Heart Was the Star

Our Mangpo, Suwon gopchang review 🔥

When a friend and I
crave gopchang,
this is where we always go —
Byeokjeokgol Hwangso Gopchang (벽적골 황소곱창).

We were there again
on the evening of September 21.

The mixed platter — onion, potato, enoki mushrooms and even egg

📍 Yeongtong-gu, Suwon
Seorin Plaza, 9 Yeongtong-ro 214beon-gil
⏰ 16:00–24:00
Closed Sundays
💰 Mixed platter for 3 (690 g)
67,000 won
Gopchang · daechang · makchang 230 g
23,000 won each
🅿 Mangpo public car park in front
7,000 won a day (EVs 3,000 won)
⏳ Waitlist via CatchTable
📞 0507-1321-5366

🐂 100% Korean fresh gopchang

The banner at the entrance says,
“This restaurant serves
only 100% Korean fresh gopchang.”

The sign — Byeokjeokgol Hwangso Gopchang, with the entrance banner: '100% Korean fresh gopchang only'

The standing sign by the street
says “The original Byeokjeokgol Hwangso Gopchang”,

and on the glass door
a notice said opens at 4 pm.

The standing sign and the notice on the door — OPEN PM 4:00

🥩 Gopchang, daechang, makchang — or a mix

This place sells
gopchang, daechang and makchang — all three
(beef small intestine, large intestine
and fourth stomach).

You can pick just one,
or get them as a mixed platter.

When there are two of you
and you want to try a bit of everything,
the platter is the easy choice.

🥢 Liver and tripe come first

Once you order and sit down,
the table gets set like this.

Seasoned chives, napa cabbage kimchi,
a dipping sauce
with Cheongyang chili floating in it,
and in the ttukbaegi (clay pot)
in the middle of the grill,
doenjang stew is bubbling away.

The table setting — doenjang stew bubbling in the ttukbaegi in the middle

Then liver and cheonyeop (omasum tripe)
come out first.

They’re my favorite special cuts!

Liver and cheonyeop, served first

🔥 The mixed platter

The platter goes onto the grill.

Onion, potato, enoki mushrooms
and even egg are arranged around it.

The platter cooking on the grill (GIF)

Picking up a piece (GIF)

❤️ The heart was the best

To be honest,
the gopchang isn’t the plump ‘al-gopchang’ kind.
Still, it was plenty tasty.

The best thing that day
was the yeomtong (beef heart)!

Crunchy yet tender —
it was really delicious.

It’s a bit funny
to leave a gopchang place
remembering the heart,
but that’s how it was.

With the bubbling doenjang stew

🔑 KICKLiver and tripe come first,
and the heart is the best!
Crunchy
yet tender

📋 Menu & prices

Mixed platter for 3 (690 g) 67,000 won
(one portion each of gopchang, makchang and daechang)

Hwangso gopchang 230 g 23,000 won
Hwangso daechang 230 g 23,000 won
Grilled hwangso makchang 230 g 23,000 won

Ramyeon · fried rice 3,000 won each

(per the menu on Naver Place)

🅿 Parking & waiting

Right in front of the restaurant:
the Mangpo public car park!
It’s 7,000 won for the whole day,
and EVs get a discount to 3,000 won.

Naver Place says
you can also use the underground car park
in the Seorin Plaza building.

The waitlist runs on CatchTable (a Korean booking app).
I guess that tells you how busy it gets.

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone in Mangpo looking for
a gopchang · daechang · makchang platter
✔ Anyone who loves special cuts
like liver and tripe
✔ Anyone who wants a gopchang place
with parking right outside

An evening that started
with liver and tripe
and ended with the heart.

A good place for two
when a gopchang craving hits,
and with the car park right in front,
it’s especially easy.
