Where to Eat Kalguksu in Gwanggyo, Suwon — Dalin Daebo Kalguksu: A Clean, Mild Work Lunch with Portions That Filled Everyone Up

Our Gwanggyo kalguksu lunch 🍜

Last December, for a work lunch,
we each had a bowl of kalguksu
(Korean knife-cut noodle soup) at
Dalin Daebo Kalguksu (달인대보칼국수)
in Gwanggyo.

Kalguksu — thick noodles in a clear broth, with carrot, green onion and sesame

📍 Unit 114, 9 Daehak 4-ro
Yeongtong-gu, Suwon
⏰ Daily 11:00–20:00
Last order 19:45
💰 Original kalguksu 8,000 won
🅿 Underground parking, 1 hour free
📞 0507-1463-1454

🍜 Mild, but with a quiet depth

The broth was clean
and the noodles were chewy.

Nothing harsh — light and mild,
yet with a quiet depth,
so we kept going back for another spoonful.

There was just the right amount of vegetables,
so it felt well balanced.

⏱ A refresh on a busy day

A hot bowl of kalguksu
in the middle of a hectic schedule
felt like a little “pull yourself together!”

For a short break during work,
it was the perfect refresh.

🍚 Portions that filled everyone up

Every coworker who came along
said “I’m full” —
the portions were that generous.

📋 Menu & prices

Original kalguksu 8,000 won
(anchovy-broth base)

Eolkeuni jang-kalguksu 9,000 won
(a spicy, chili-paste broth)

Sanma kongguksu (May–Aug) 10,000 won
(cold soy-milk noodles with Korean yam)

Wang-mandu (big dumplings): 2 for 3,000 won
4 for 5,000 won

Rice is free (self-serve)

(per the Naver Place menu)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone after a work lunch

In a word,
a clean, solid lunch option,
