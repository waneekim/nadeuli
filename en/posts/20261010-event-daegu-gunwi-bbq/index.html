2026 Gunwi BBQ Festival Dates, Zones & Directions – Self-Grill Barbecue at Samgukyusa Theme Park with Kids

In late October, as autumn deepens,
a barbecue festival comes to
Samgukyusa Theme Park in Gunwi 🍖

The 2026 Gunwi BBQ Festival (군위 바베큐 축제)
runs for two days, October 31 (Sat) to November 1 (Sun),
across all of Samgukyusa Theme Park.

This guide is based on the Gunwi-gun press release (September 14)
and the theme park’s notice (October 9).

Let's take a quick look first!

🎪 About the Festival

The slogan announced by Gunwi-gun is
’Meat is good, Gunwi’ (고기 좋다 군위).
The theme park’s notice says
’Grill Gunwi meat! Enjoy it together!’

Along with self-grill hanwoo beef and pork,
Gunwi’s premium local meat,
there will be barbecue from Korea and abroad, local food,
an opening show and a parade,
plus hands-on activities and a flea market.

Gunwi-gun expects
about 10,000 visitors.
The festival name appears as ‘바베큐’ in the county press release
and ‘바비큐’ in the theme park notice — both mean barbecue.

the theme park posted on October 9 into a table.

Which day and what time
the opening show and parade take place,
and who will perform,
have not been announced yet.

According to the Gunwi-gun press release,
the festival runs 10:00 to 18:00.

Whether it’s the same during the festival is not known yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 If You’re Visiting with Kids

The ‘At a Glance’ illustration shows
an air bounce and craft activities
in the Gui-Play Zone.

As side events, kids can draw with chalk on the ground,
and you can browse the flea market.

During the festival, a general control room runs at Hanul Plaza (한울광장),
and a first-aid booth runs at the venue.

The theme park usually restricts pets (except guide dogs),
burners, cooking equipment and alcohol.
No festival-specific rule has been announced yet.

🔑 KICKThe zone that uses open fire is the Gunwi Meat zone
The air bounce and crafts are in the Gui-Play Zone
Gunwi-gun specially checked fire safety there
