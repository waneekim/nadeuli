Wangsukcheon Culture Festival 2026 in Namyangju — Schedule, Activities & Rides at Donong Sports Park Before You Go With Kids

The autumn breeze has cooled,
and a neighborhood party is coming
to the Wangsukcheon stream 🍂

The 13th Wangsukcheon Culture Festival
(제13회 왕숙천문화제)
is on Sat, Oct 31, 10:00–18:00
at Donong Sports Park in Namyangju.

This year’s theme is
’Rest and stay a while, like a king’
(왕처럼 쉬다 머물다 가소서).

We put this together from
Namyangju city’s cultural events notice
and the official flyer
posted along with it.

Let's start with the overview!

🎪 What kind of festival is it?

Namyangju city introduces it as
”As autumn turns cool,
come spend a special day
by the Wangsukcheon.”

It’s organized and run by
the Wangsukcheon Culture Festival Organizing Committee.
This is its 13th year.

According to the flyer,
local groups such as
the Dasan 1-dong residents’ council,
the Saemaeul Women’s Association
and the volunteer neighborhood patrol
run booths together.

Activities, shows,
rides and food
all come together in one park
for one day.

✨ What’s on, and when?

Only two times have been announced.
Everything else has no timetable yet.

🐟 What activities are there?

The first activities in Namyangju city’s notice
are loach catching and rice-cake (tteok) making.
There are especially many craft activities.

The flyer ends the list with ‘etc.’,
so there may be more.
Activity fees and whether
it’s first-come, first-served
haven’t been announced yet.

👨‍👩‍👧 Going with kids

There are activities
where you put your hands in water,
like loach catching.
A towel and a change of clothes
for the kids are a good idea.

The festival starts at 10:00,
so you could do activities
in the morning first
and then watch the 14:00 official ceremony.

With plenty of food booths,
you can have lunch in the park too.

🔑 KICKNo parking information yet
By bus, the Dasan River Palliche Apt. stop
is 6 min on foot —
the closer of the two listed stops
