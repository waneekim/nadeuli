Maillard87, Iksan — Dry-Aged Korean Pork BBQ: Belly and Neck, Then Jowl and Skirt, and a 49,000-Won Set for Two with Kimchi Stew

Our Iksan pork belly review 🥩

One evening over Chuseok
(the Korean harvest holiday),
the whole family went to
Maillard87 in Iksan.

The grill packed with cut-up meat

📍 16-3 Seonhwa-ro 3-gil, Iksan
(선화로3길 16-3, Mohyeon-dong)
⏰ Weekdays 16:30–01:00
Sat & Sun 10:00–01:00
💰 Set for two 49,000 won
(2 belly + 1 neck + stew)
🅿 Free parking behind the building
📞 0507-1353-4745

😋 The short version

We ordered pork belly and neck,
then added jowl
and skirt meat too,

and it was so good we nearly flipped
right off our chairs!

Kimchi stew comes with it,
the set for two is great value,
and the table setting is neat.

🐷 Dry-aged Korean pork barbecue

The sign reads Sunjin Pork Handon
(Handon = Korean-raised pork)
and “The moment of perfect taste,
made by time!”

The Sunjin Pork Handon Maillard sign

The top of the menu board says
’Premium dry-aged
Korean pork barbecue specialist’.

A sign further inside
lists the aging conditions:
dry aging, temperature 1℃,
humidity 75–85%

The MAILLARD neon sign, the vent pipes along the ceiling, and the dry-aging sign at the back

There’s even a poster on the wall:
‘What is the Maillard reaction?’

🥗 A neat table setting

A black divided tray
with shredded green onion, onion,
and four little wells
of salt, seasoning powder and sauces.

Lettuce, perilla leaves and chili peppers
come separately in a basket.

Shredded green onion, onion, the four-well sauce tray, and a basket of lettuce and perilla leaves

The side dishes come
one to each white compartment dish:
potato salad, pickled radish wraps,
a few pickles and seasoned greens,
and two dipping sauces.

The side dishes in white divided dishes

The basket of leafy greens for wraps

🔥 It goes on in thick slabs

At first the meat goes on the grill
in thick slabs,
with kimchi, mushrooms, onion and potato
grilling alongside.

Thick meat just put on the grill, with kimchi and mushrooms

Once it was cooked,
we snipped it into bite-size pieces
with scissors as we went.

Cutting with scissors — the kimchi and seasoned greens grill too

Thick pieces
with pink centers
went on separately as well.

Thick-cut meat cooking

🍲 Kimchi stew and steamed egg

Kimchi stew comes too.
It has enoki mushrooms, tofu
and bok choy in it.

Kimchi stew — enoki mushrooms, tofu and bok choy

The steamed egg
in a ttukbaegi (earthenware pot)
came out puffed up high.

Steamed egg in a ttukbaegi

💰 The set for two is the deal

The wall menu — sets, meat and meals

By the menu’s own math,
the meat in the set for two is 48,000 won,
or 54,000 won with the stew,

but the set price is 49,000 won.
That’s why the set for two
felt like such good value.

🔑 KICKThe set for two is the answer!
Its meat alone is 48,000 won
yet with stew it's 49,000 won

📋 Menu & prices

Signature set for two 49,000 won
(belly 300 g + neck 150 g\

  • your choice of kimchi or doenjang stew)

Meat-only set 59,000 won
(belly 300 g + neck 300 g)

Belly · neck · jowl · gabeuri (collar cap) ·
skirt · marinated galbi 16,000 won each

Stew 6,000 won · steamed egg 4,000 won

(per the Naver Place menu)

👍 Who this is for

✔ Anyone in Iksan looking for
a barbecue place for a family meal
✔ Anyone who wants to try
special cuts beyond belly and neck
✔ Anyone after a filling set for two
that comes with stew

We started with belly and neck
and went on to jowl and skirt —
a dinner the whole family shared.

A place we’d happily come back to!
